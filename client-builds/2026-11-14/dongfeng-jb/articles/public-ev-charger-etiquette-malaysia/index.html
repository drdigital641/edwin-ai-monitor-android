What is the right etiquette at a public EV charger?

Updated 2026-11-14 · By the Dongfeng Johor Bahru team

Park at a public charger only to charge, and move your car when you have enough charge for your next stop. Do not block a charger you are not using, leave the cable tidy, and never unplug another car. If the charger is busy, wait your turn. If it is faulty, report it to the operator.

Who can park at a charger bay?

A charger bay is for a car that is charging. Do not park there just because it is close to the entrance, and do not leave a car in the bay once it has finished charging. Read the signs for any time limit or parking rule, because the car park or the charger operator sets those, not us.

When should I stop charging and move?

Move when you have enough charge to reach your next stop, with some to spare. You do not have to fill the battery every time. Charging often slows as the battery fills, so staying for the last few percent can keep another driver waiting. Set a phone reminder so you come back when you planned to.

What if the charger is already in use?

Wait your turn and do not crowd the bay. Do not unplug another driver's car, even if it looks finished: the connector is normally locked to the car, and it is not yours to remove. If you have waited a long time, you can leave a polite note or contact the operator or the car park staff.

What should I do if a charger is faulty?

Do not force the connector or keep retrying. Stop, return the cable to its holder and report the fault to the charger operator using the contact on the charger. Telling the operator helps the next driver as well. If the charger shows damage, do not use it.

  • Ask how to start and stop a charge in the car, so you can stop it when you are done
  • Ask how the car shows the charge level so you know when you have enough
  • Ask how to release the charging cable at the end of a charge
  • Check the charger signs and the operator's rules before you leave your car

Questions

Can I leave my car at a charger after it is fully charged?

You should not. A charger bay is for a car that is charging, so move once you have enough charge, so the next driver can use it. Follow any time limit shown on site.

Can I unplug another car if it looks finished?

No. Do not touch another driver's cable. The connector is normally locked to the car, and unplugging it is not your decision. Wait, leave a polite note or contact the operator.

Do I have to charge to 100 percent?

No. Charge as much as you need for your next stop. Charging often slows as the battery fills, so stopping earlier can be kinder to the next driver in the queue.

Who should I tell if a charger does not work?

Tell the charger operator, using the contact shown on the charger, and the car park staff if there are any. Do not force the connector.
