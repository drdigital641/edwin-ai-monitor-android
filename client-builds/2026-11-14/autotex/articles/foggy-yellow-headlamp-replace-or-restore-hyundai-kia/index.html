Foggy or yellow headlamp: replace or restore?

A foggy or yellow headlamp is usually the outer plastic lens clouding or yellowing with age and sun. If it is only hazy on the outside, a workshop can often polish and re-coat it. If there is moisture inside, a crack, a broken mounting tab or a burnt housing, replacing the headlamp unit is the safer fix.

By Autotex · Published 14 Nov 2026 · Last updated 14 Nov 2026

Why does a headlamp turn foggy or yellow?

Most headlamp lenses are plastic with a clear protective coating. Years of sun, heat, rain and car washing can wear the coating down, so the plastic turns hazy or yellow and less light gets through at night. This is a surface problem on the outside of the lens.

Can a foggy headlamp be restored?

Often, yes, when the haze is only on the outside. A workshop sands and polishes the lens, then adds a new protective coat. Ask the workshop to explain what it will do and how long the result is expected to last, because a lens restored without a new coating can cloud again.

When should I replace the headlamp unit instead?

  • Moisture or water droplets inside the lamp: the seal has failed, and the fog is on the inside where polishing cannot reach.
  • A crack or chip in the lens, or a missing piece.
  • Broken mounting tabs, so the lamp no longer sits firmly in the body.
  • A melted or burnt housing or reflector, or a reflector that has gone dull.
  • Damage from an accident.

How can I tell if the fog is inside or outside?

Wipe the outside of the lens with a clean damp cloth. If the haze stays, check from the side. A cloudy film that you can feel as rough or sticky on the outside is a surface problem. Beads or a misty film that you cannot touch means moisture inside the lamp, which usually points to a failed seal or a cracked housing. If you are unsure, ask a workshop to look at it.

Should I replace both headlamps?

Not always. Replace only the side that is damaged. If the other lamp is also cloudy, restoring or replacing both gives an even beam and even colour, but that is your choice. Left and right units are different parts, so tell us which side you need.
