Where to stay in Johor Bahru for Singaporeans: KSL or Iskandar Puteri?

By JB Staycations team · Published 14 Nov 2026 · Updated 14 Nov 2026

Stay at Landak in Taman Abad if you want a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall and a short drive from the Causeway (host estimates). Stay at Prima in Gelang Patah, Iskandar Puteri, if you come via the Second Link, need space for up to 12 guests, or have more cars. Both are private houses, priced per night.

Which area is better for a short JB stay?

It depends on how you travel and what you plan to do. We run two private houses in two different bases, so this is a comparison of those two, not of every area in Johor Bahru. One is a walk from a mall in the city, the other is on the Second Link side of Iskandar Puteri.

Base 1: walk to KSL City Mall (Landak, Taman Abad)

Landak Kawan Fun House is a newly built September 2026 house in Taman Abad. It is about a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall and about 12 minutes by car from the Causeway CIQ (host estimates, depending on traffic). It sleeps up to 10 guests in 3 bedrooms with 5 beds, with 3 bathrooms (1 en-suite, 2 shared) that have water heaters.

It suits a group that wants food and shopping within walking distance. Evenings can be spent on the 8ft pool table, foosball, darts and mahjong. See what is within 5 to 15 minutes of KSL.

Base 2: Second Link side (Prima, Gelang Patah)

Prima is a landed house in Taman Nusantara Prima, Gelang Patah, Iskandar Puteri. The host estimates about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link on the Malaysian side, excluding border queues. It sleeps up to 12 guests in 3 rooms, each with 1 queen bed and 2 single floor mattresses, with 3 bathrooms (1 en-suite, 2 shared) that have water heaters, and a ground-floor bedroom for elderly guests.

It suits families driving in via the Second Link who want a larger house. See Prima and LEGOLAND for drive times to that side of Iskandar Puteri.

Parking and group size compared

Prima has parking for 2 cars inside and 6 outside, 8 in all, and takes up to 12 guests. Landak has parking for 2 cars inside the gate and takes up to 10 guests. If your group travels in three or four cars, Prima is the easier fit. Parking is not a guarantee against loss or damage.

What each house costs

Prima is RM 450 weekday, RM 650 weekend, RM 750 public holiday (about S$142 / S$205 / S$237) a night for the whole house, cleaning included (about S$11.84 per person on a weekday at 12 guests; an estimate). Landak is RM 550 weekday, RM 750 weekend, RM 1,000 public holiday (about S$174 / S$237 / S$316) a night, cleaning included (about S$17.37 per person on a weekday at 10 guests; an estimate).

SGD estimate only; exchange rates vary. Based on RM1 = S$0.3158 (6 Nov 2026; checked 9 Nov 2026 SGT). The final total depends on your dates and group size. See the prices page for both houses.

So which should you choose?

Choose Landak if walking to KSL City Mall and a short drive to the Causeway matter most and your group is 10 or fewer. Choose Prima if you cross by the Second Link, have up to 12 guests or several cars. Border queues can be long on weekends and holidays, so read which way to cross before you decide.

House rules that apply to both

Check-in is 3pm and check-out 11am. Quiet hours are 11pm to 7am. No pets, no BBQ or grilling, and no sticking decorations on walls. Gatherings need the host's approval. No deposit for a normal gathering without food catering. A catered gathering (food catering, for example Lok Lok, or a birthday or shower gathering with catering) has a RM 1,000 deposit, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents.
