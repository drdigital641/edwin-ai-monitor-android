Eternal flowers for a niche at Nirvana Memorial Garden

Fresh flowers wilt within days. Many families want something that still looks cared for on the next visit.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-10-27 · 3 min read
Eternal flowers (永生花) are real flowers that are carefully selected, processed and dried so they keep their look for a long time. Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2025 booklet offers a range of eternal-flower products so families can personalise a niche, and calls such decoration "the warmest gift you can offer your ancestors". Benjamin Poon can show you the current range and confirm what is allowed at your niche.

What are eternal flowers?

According to Nirvana's booklet, eternal flowers are fresh-cut flowers that go through a series of processes: careful selection, processing, drying and beautifying. The aim is to keep their beauty and freshness for an extended period. The booklet says they symbolise love that never fades, and a promise of love, hope and remembrance.

Why do families choose them for a niche?

  • Fresh flowers need replacing often; eternal flowers are chosen for lasting looks.
  • Relatives who live far away, or visit only on Qing Ming and anniversaries, can still leave something that stays.
  • The booklet describes personalised niche decoration as a way for the family to arrange the loved one's resting place with care.
  • It can be chosen together by the whole family, instead of one person deciding alone.

What does Nirvana offer?

The 2025 booklet shows a niche decorated with an urn and an eternal-flower arrangement, and lists eternal flower decorations among its ceremonial services, together with 安灵 (placing the soul) and memorial engraving. The booklet does not list the styles, sizes or prices, so please ask Benjamin for the current range.

Details here come from Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2025 booklet. Benjamin will confirm anything that may have changed.

What to ask before you choose

  1. Which styles are available now, and how they look on the niche you are considering.
  2. Whether there are limits on size or on what may be placed at that niche.
  3. How the decoration is placed, and whether it can be changed later.
  4. How it fits with other ceremonies, such as Qing Ming and Zhong Yuan prayers.

For the niche itself, see Nirvana's columbarium suites and the one-time maintenance fee.
