EV diagnostic tools

More electric cars are coming into Singapore workshops. These tools read their high-voltage systems and battery packs.

For electric cars, CSKA supplies the THINKCAR Expert 394 EV, the Autel MaxiSYS Ultra EV and the Autel MS909 S2 EV, which read high-voltage systems and show battery State of Charge and State of Health on supported cars. For battery pack work we also supply the EVP501 lift, rated to 1,500 kg.

Questions

Can a scan tool check an EV battery's health?

On supported cars, EV-capable tools such as the Autel MaxiSYS Ultra EV show the high-voltage battery's State of Charge and State of Health, and the Ultra EV can also test the pack directly with its cables.

Which EV tool is right for my workshop?

It depends on the EV brands you see and whether you remove battery packs. Tell us the cars you service on WhatsApp and we will compare the Expert 394 EV, Ultra EV and MS909 S2 EV for you.

Do you supply equipment to lift EV battery packs?

Yes, the EVP501 lift is made for EV battery replacement and maintenance, lifting up to 1,500 kg to a height of 1,785 mm.

Do I need a special scan tool for electric cars?

A normal OBD reader cannot analyse the high-voltage system. EV-capable tools such as the Expert 394 EV, Ultra EV and MS909 S2 EV are made for it. Which one fits depends on the EV brands you service.
