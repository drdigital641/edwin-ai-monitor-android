Questions

Where can I buy Autel MaxiSys in Singapore?

CSKA Auto Enterprise in Woodlands supplies Autel MaxiSys tablets to workshops. WhatsApp Steven on 9822 2629.

Which Autel tablet supports J2534 programming?

The MaxiSYS MS909 and MS909 S2 come with a J2534 pass-thru VCI.

Which Autel tablet supports DoIP and CAN FD?

The MaxiSys MS908S3 supports DoIP and CAN FD, and the MaxiSYS MS909 VCI supports DoIP, D-PDU and J2534. Newer cars increasingly need DoIP or CAN FD for a full scan.

Which Autel tool is for electric cars?

The MaxiSYS Ultra EV reads high-voltage systems, shows State of Charge and State of Health and can test the battery pack directly with its cables. The MS909 S2 EV adds EV functions to the MS909 S2 tablet.

Can my Autel MS909 S2 do ADAS calibration?

Yes, the MS909 S2 can be upgraded for ADAS calibration with software and the target package. WhatsApp us your tablet model and we will tell you what you need.
