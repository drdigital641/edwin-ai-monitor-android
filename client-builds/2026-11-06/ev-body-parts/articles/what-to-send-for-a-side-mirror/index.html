Side mirror for a China or EV car: what to send us

To get a side mirror matched, send the model, the build month and year, left or right as seen from the driver's seat, and photos from outside and inside the door. Say what the mirror does, such as folding, heating, a camera or a blind-spot light, and whether you need the whole unit, the glass or the cover.

By EV Body Parts · Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

Send the brand and model, the build month and year, which side, and a photo of the mirror from outside and from the inside of the door. Tell us what the mirror does: folding, heated, camera or a blind-spot light. Say whether you need the whole mirror, the glass or only the cover.

1. Say left or right

Left and right are as seen from the driver's seat, not from outside the car. If you are not sure, a photo of the whole side of the car shows which mirror is damaged.

2. Whole mirror, glass or cover?

A side mirror is made of the housing and motor, the mirror glass and the outer cover. Tell us which part is broken. If the cover is cracked but the glass and motor work, a cover alone may be enough, but we confirm what is available before we reply.

3. List what the mirror does

Modern mirrors can fold electrically, heat the glass, carry a turn signal or a blind-spot light, hold a camera or lower when you reverse. Mirrors for the same model can come with different features, so tell us which of these yours has.

4. Take two photos

Take one photo from outside the car showing the whole mirror, and one from inside the car looking at the door panel and the mirror controls. If the mirror is already off, add a photo of the back, where any markings or numbers are.

5. Give the car details

Brand, model and the build month and year from your car documents. Say if the car has been repaired, repainted or fitted with a different mirror before, and whether the cover needs a colour match.

What happens next

We compare your photos with the part and confirm fitment before we reply. We do not guess from the model name alone, because versions and markets can differ. You can also browse mirrors by car and tap Get a quote.

FAQ

Is the left mirror the one on my left as I face the car?

No. Left and right are as seen from the driver's seat, not from outside the car. A photo of the whole side of the car shows which mirror is damaged.

Can I replace only the mirror glass or cover?

Sometimes. If the motor and housing work, the glass or the cover alone may be enough. Tell us which part is broken, and we confirm what is available before we reply.

Why do you ask what features the mirror has?

Mirrors for the same model can differ: folding, heated glass, turn signal, blind-spot light or camera. Knowing yours helps us match the right version.

What photos should I send for a side mirror?

One from outside showing the whole mirror, one from inside showing the door panel and mirror controls, and, if the mirror is off the car, one of the back.
