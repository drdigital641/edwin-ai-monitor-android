How to wash and care for a wrapped or PPF car

By Wrapstar Autostyle · 17 October 2026

The basics

Hand wash your wrapped or PPF car with a pH-neutral shampoo, keep pressure washers away from film edges, remove bird droppings and sap quickly, and avoid polishes and waxes on satin or matte film. Park in shade when you can.

Six habits that make film last

  1. Wait a few days after the job before the first wash, so the film settles.
  2. Hand wash with a soft mitt and a pH-neutral car shampoo.
  3. If you use a pressure washer, stand back and never aim at seams or edges.
  4. Clean bird droppings, sap and fuel spills the same day.
  5. Skip abrasive polishes and cutting compounds, and on satin or matte film, skip wax.
  6. Park in shade or a covered car park when you can: sun is the biggest ager of any film.

Automatic car washes

Brush tunnels can scratch film and catch edges. A hand wash, or a touchless wash used gently, is kinder to a wrap or PPF.

Colour PPF self-heals, within limits

On colour PPF, light swirls fade under hot water or a hot afternoon. Deeper cuts through the film stay, and a damaged panel can usually be re-filmed on its own.

Come back for a check

One owner who had coloured PPF fitted with us wrote that they were asked to come back after a week so we could check for air bubbles or defects. If anything lifts or bubbles, WhatsApp us a photo.

Questions

When can I wash my car after a wrap?

Give the film a few days to settle before the first wash, then hand wash gently. Ask us for the exact timing for your film.

Can I use a car wash after PPF?

Hand washing is best. Avoid brush tunnels, and keep pressure washers well back from film edges.
