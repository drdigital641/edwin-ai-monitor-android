New car workshop equipment checklist

By CSKA Auto Enterprise · Updated 19 October 2026

A new car workshop usually needs a lift for each bay, a full-system diagnostic scanner, a battery tester, a low-profile floor jack and stands, an air impact wrench and compressor line, a waste oil drainer and a tool trolley with a full set. Add an aircon machine, TPMS tool or programming power supply for the services you plan.

For every bay

  • Lift a two-post lift suits most cars; check ceiling height and floor.
  • Jack and stands a low-profile floor jack reaches lowered cars.
  • Tool trolley and set sockets, wrenches, screwdrivers and pliers in one place.
  • Air impact wrench with a hose reel to the bay.

For the workshop

  • Diagnostic scanner full-system, with the resets you use most.
  • Battery tester ideally one that prints a report.
  • Waste oil drainer mobile suction or gravity type.
  • Service kits ATF extractor, coolant tester, brake bleeder, torque wrench.

If you offer these services

  • Aircon an all-in-one recovery and flushing machine.
  • Tyres a TPMS tool to program and relearn sensors.
  • Programming a stable power supply such as the THINKPPS 150.
  • EVs an EV-capable scanner and an EV battery lift.

Buy in the right order

Lifts and air lines go in before the opening; scanners and testers can follow. One supplier for the whole list keeps delivery and set-up on one schedule.

Questions

What should I buy first for a new workshop?

The lifts and compressed air system, because they are installed into the unit. Then the scanner, battery tester, jacks and tool trolleys.
