SP ZONE in Singapore

SPZ-FA1 and SPZ6000 all-in-one car aircon flushing and recovery machines.

CSKA Auto Enterprise supplies SP ZONE car aircon machines in Singapore: the SPZ-FA1 and SPZ6000 refrigerant flushing and recovery machines, which recover, recycle, flush, leak test, vacuum and inject oil in one unit. What is the difference between the SPZ-FA1 and SPZ6000?

Both are all-in-one recovery, flushing and recharge machines. The SPZ-FA1 has an 8.4-inch screen and PAG or hybrid POE oil selection; the SPZ6000 has a 9-inch screen, a bigger cylinder and three fans with a condenser.
