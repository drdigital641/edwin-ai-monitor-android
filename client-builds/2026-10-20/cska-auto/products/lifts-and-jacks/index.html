Lifts, jacks and stands

The lifting equipment a workshop bay needs, from the two-post lift to the stands under the car.

CSKA supplies lifting equipment to Singapore workshops: 4-ton two-post lifts (including top-connection models), the THINKCAR TFJ 330 dual-pump low-profile jack with an 85 mm minimum height, 3-ton floor jacks and THINKCAR TFJ103 3-ton jack stands, 500 kg double-stage transmission jacks, underhoist stands and the EVP501 EV battery lift.

What we supply

LiftTwo-post lift, 4 ton

Supplied and set up for new workshops; top-connection models with one-side lock also available.

THINKCARTFJ 330 dual pump jack

Dual pumps for faster lifting, 85 mm minimum pad height, magnetic tool tray, 360-degree wheels.

THINKCARTFJ103 jack stand (3 ton)

Pairs with the 3-ton THINKCAR floor jack 330.

JackTECH THJ-75 low-profile floor jack

3 ton, double pump, low profile.

JackDouble-stage transmission jack

500 kg hydraulic jack for gearbox work.

Stand3/4 underhoist stand

Supports components while the car is on the lift.

LiftEVP501 EV battery lift

Up to 1,500 kg and 1,785 mm, electric raise and lower with a mechanical safety lock.

Questions

Do you supply two-post lifts for new workshops?

Yes. Two-post lifts are part of the new workshop set-ups we supply. WhatsApp Steven with your floor space and the vehicles you work on.

What is a low-profile jack for?

Lowered cars and sports cars sit too low for a normal jack. The THINKCAR TFJ 330 goes down to 85 mm, so it fits under them.

Do you have a jack for gearbox removal?

Yes, a 500 kg double-stage hydraulic transmission jack.
