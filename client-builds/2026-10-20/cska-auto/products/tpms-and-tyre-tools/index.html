TPMS and tyre shop tools

Tools for tyre shops: TPMS sensor work and quick tyre and brake disc checks.

CSKA supplies the Autel MaxiTPMS TS508WF to tyre shops and workshops in Singapore. It activates TPMS sensors, reads sensor status, checks the TPMS system, programs Autel MX-sensors and runs relearns, with updates over Wi-Fi. THINKCAR scanners such as the Master X2 also include a TPMS module.

Questions

What does a TPMS tool do?

It wakes up each tyre pressure sensor to read its ID, pressure and battery, programs replacement sensors, and runs the relearn so the car recognises the new sensor.

Do you have a TPMS tool that programs sensors?

Yes. The Autel MaxiTPMS TS508WF programs Autel MX-sensors, and the THINKTOOL Master X2 has a TPMS module that programs and relearns sensors.

Is there a quick way to check tread depth for customers?

The laser tyre tread and brake disc examiner reads wear without taking the wheel off and shares a report by QR code. Ask us for a demo.
