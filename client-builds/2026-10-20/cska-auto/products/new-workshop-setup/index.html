Questions

What equipment does a new car workshop need?

Most start with a lift per bay, a diagnostic scanner, a battery tester, a floor jack and stands, an impact wrench, a waste oil drainer and a tool trolley. An aircon machine is added if you will do aircon service.

Can you supply everything in one order?

Yes. Recent new workshops took their lift, scanner, battery tester, aircon machine and tools from us together.

How do I start?

WhatsApp Steven on 9822 2629 with your unit size, number of bays and the services you plan, and we will send an equipment list and quote.
