Questions

What is a smoke leak detector used for?

It fills the intake, vacuum or EVAP system with harmless smoke at low pressure so you can see exactly where it escapes. The THINKCAR PLD 212 also works with THINKCAR scan tools on leak-related faults.

Do you sell complete tool trolleys?

Yes, seven- and eight-drawer trolleys with 360 to 376-piece tool sets, including the THINKCAR TTK 333.

Do you carry workshop chemicals?

Yes, including Stirling brake cleaner and ThreeBond products. Ask us for the item you need.
