Battery testers, chargers and jump starters

Test the battery, charging and starting system in a minute, and keep voltage steady while you program.

CSKA supplies battery tools to Singapore workshops: THINKCAR TBT 360 and TBT 360 Pro testers (3 to 360 Ah), the TOPDON BT600 tester with a built-in printer, the TOPDON V4500 Plus 12V jump starter (4500A peak), the TOPDON Tornado 30000 charger and the THINKPPS 150, which keeps a stable supply during module programming.

What we supply

THINKCARTBT 360 / TBT 360 Pro

Tests batteries from 3 to 360 Ah plus the charging and starting system. 2.8-inch screen, Kelvin clamps, cuts off above 35V. The Pro prints reports.

12V tester with a 3.5-inch colour screen and built-in thermal printer. Measures real cold cranking amps and state of health.

12V, up to 4500A peak and up to 45 jump starts per charge. PD100W fast charging, about 90 minutes to full, 3-minute boost mode.

Questions

Why use a power supply when programming a car?

Programming can take a long time with the ignition on. If battery voltage drops, the update can fail. A programming power supply such as the THINKPPS 150 keeps the voltage steady.

Which battery tester prints a report for the customer?

The THINKCAR TBT 360 Pro and the TOPDON BT600 both print test reports.

Is the V4500 Plus only for big vehicles?

It is a 12V jump starter built for high-displacement vehicles, trucks, off-road vehicles and boats, so it also has plenty of power for cars.
