Christmas staycation JB 2026: 2-night minimum and public holiday rates

By JB Staycations team · Published 10 Nov 2026 · Updated 10 Nov 2026

For a Christmas staycation in JB, our two private houses need a minimum of 2 nights over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, and both nights are charged at the public holiday rate: Prima RM 750 and Landak Kawan Fun House RM 1,000 a night, whole house, cleaning included.

Ask about dates on WhatsApp

What is different over Christmas

Over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day (24 and 25 December) the minimum stay is 2 nights instead of 1, and both nights are charged at the public holiday rate. The usual check-in is 3pm and check-out 11am. There is no cancellation, but you can change your dates once.

Christmas prices for the whole house

Prices are per night for the whole house, cleaning included. For 24 and 25 December together: Prima is 2 x RM 750 = RM 1,500 (S$474 estimated), and Landak Kawan Fun House is 2 x RM 1,000 = RM 2,000 (S$632 estimated).

At full occupancy that is about S$39.48 per person at Prima (12 guests) and about S$63.16 per person at Landak (10 guests) for the two nights. SGD estimate only; exchange rates vary. Based on RM1 = S$0.3158 (6 Nov 2026; checked 9 Nov 2026 SGT). The final total depends on your dates and group size. Normal weekday, weekend and public holiday rates are on the prices page.

Which house for a Christmas group

Prima takes up to 12 guests in 3 rooms with 5 queen beds and 2 single floor mattresses, and has a ground-floor bedroom for elderly guests, a 7ft pool table, a mahjong table and parking for 2 cars inside and 6 outside.

Landak Kawan Fun House takes up to 10 guests in 3 bedrooms, has an 8ft pool table, foosball, 2 dart boards, a mahjong table, a TV arcade and a children's playroom, and is about a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall.

Rules to know before you book

Quiet hours are 11pm to 7am, and there is a RM 500 penalty for smoking, vaping or eating durian indoors. No BBQ or grilling, no pets, and no sticking decorations on walls, so bring tabletop decorations only. Gatherings need the host's approval.

No deposit for a normal gathering without food catering. A catered gathering (food catering, for example Lok Lok, or a birthday or shower gathering with catering) has a RM 1,000 deposit, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents. Singapore guests pay by PayNow or PayLah to the host's mobile, and a receipt is issued. Malaysian guests pay in RM by bank transfer or TNG eWallet. Bank details are shared once your booking and dates are confirmed. You can also book through the Airbnb listing.

Getting there at Christmas

The host estimates Prima is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link and Landak about 12 minutes from the Causeway CIQ, excluding border queues. Queues over holidays can be much longer, so check the ICA traffic updates before you leave. See getting to Prima and Landak and which way to cross.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum stay over Christmas at JB Staycations?

The minimum is 2 nights over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Outside that period the minimum is 1 night.

Which rate applies on 24 and 25 December?

Both nights are charged at the public holiday rate: RM 750 a night at Prima and RM 1,000 a night at Landak Kawan Fun House, whole house, cleaning included.

How many guests can stay?

Prima takes up to 12 guests and Landak Kawan Fun House up to 10.

Is a deposit needed?

No deposit for a normal gathering without food catering. A catered gathering (food catering, for example Lok Lok, or a birthday or shower gathering with catering) has a RM 1,000 deposit, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents.
