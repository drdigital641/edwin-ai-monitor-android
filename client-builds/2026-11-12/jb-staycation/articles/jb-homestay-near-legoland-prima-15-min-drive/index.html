JB homestay near LEGOLAND: Prima, about 15 minutes by car

By JB Staycations team · Published 12 Nov 2026 · Updated 12 Nov 2026

Prima, a private landed house in Gelang Patah, Iskandar Puteri, is about a 15-minute drive from LEGOLAND Malaysia (host estimate, depending on traffic). It sleeps up to 12 guests, has a ground-floor bedroom for grandparents, and costs from RM 450 a night for the whole house, cleaning included.

How far is Prima from LEGOLAND Malaysia?

The host estimates about a 15-minute drive from Prima to LEGOLAND Malaysia. Mall of Medini, next to LEGOLAND, is also about 15 minutes, and Puteri Harbour about 15 to 20 minutes. These are drive times from the house on the Malaysian side. They are estimates and depend on traffic, so check Google Maps on the day.

The Around the house section of the Prima page has a map link for each place.

Why Prima suits a family trip to LEGOLAND

Prima takes up to 12 guests in 3 rooms with 5 queen beds and 2 single floor mattresses, with 3 bathrooms (1 en-suite, 2 shared) that have water heaters. One bedroom is on the ground floor, so grandparents do not need the stairs.

The house has about 2,500 sq ft of living space with a dining area and kitchen, two TVs with Netflix, Wi-Fi, a washer, air conditioning and a covered balcony. A 7ft pool table and a mahjong table are there for the evenings. Parking is for 2 cars inside and 6 outside.

What does it cost?

Prima is RM 450 weekday, RM 650 weekend, RM 750 public holiday (about S$142 / S$205 / S$237) a night for the whole house, cleaning included. A weekday night at 12 guests works out to about S$11.84 per person (an estimate).

SGD estimate only; exchange rates vary. Based on RM1 = S$0.3158 (6 Nov 2026; checked 9 Nov 2026 SGT). The final total depends on your dates and group size. See the prices page for both houses. We do not sell LEGOLAND tickets and do not quote their prices or opening days, so check the official LEGOLAND Malaysia site before you plan.

A LEGOLAND weekend plan from the host

A plan the host suggests for a family weekend: breakfast at Wee Hoi Kopitiam, a full day at LEGOLAND, groceries at Mall of Medini, then mahjong and pool back at the house. On the second day, the Puteri Harbour playground, lunch at Chua Kee Seafood Restaurant and home via the Second Link.

Opening hours change, so check each place on Google Maps before you go.

Coming from Singapore

The host estimates Prima is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link on the Malaysian side, excluding border queues, which suits families driving in via the Second Link. Border queues can be long on weekends and holidays, so read which way to cross and getting to Prima and Landak before you leave.

House rules to know

Check-in is 3pm and check-out 11am. Quiet hours are 11pm to 7am. No pets, no BBQ or grilling, and no sticking decorations on walls. Smoking, vaping or eating durian indoors carries a RM 500 penalty. Gatherings need the host's approval.

No deposit for a normal gathering without food catering. A catered gathering (food catering, for example Lok Lok, or a birthday or shower gathering with catering) has a RM 1,000 deposit, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents. There is no cancellation, but you can change your dates once.
