Bumper damage on an EV or China car: what else to check before you order

A cracked bumper cover can hide damage behind it. The brackets, the reinforcement beam, the grille or lip and the small clips can all be bent or broken by the same knock. Have the area checked first, then send us the car model, build month and year, and photos so we can check the right parts together.

By EV Body Parts · Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

A cracked or scuffed bumper cover is only the part you can see. Behind it sit brackets, a reinforcement beam and clips that a knock can bend or break. Have the area inspected before you order, then send us photos of the damage so we can check the right pieces together.

Why the cover is not the whole story

The bumper cover is the painted outer shell. It is held on by brackets and clips and sits in front of a reinforcement beam and the car's front or rear structure. A knock that cracks the cover can also bend a bracket or break the tabs that hold it, so a new cover may not sit properly if those are left damaged.

What to look at behind and around the cover

Check the bumper brackets, which hold the cover to the car, and the reinforcement beam behind it. Look at the lower grille, front lip or rear diffuser, the trim strips, and the small clips and fittings. If the cover had a fog lamp, a reflector or a number plate bracket, check those too.

Who should check the hidden parts

A bumper reinforcement or anything near the crash structure is a specialist check, so have a workshop or body shop look at it before you order. We do not guess at structure damage from a photo. If the workshop names a part, send us its name and your car details and we check what we can source.

What to send us

The brand and model, the build month and year, and whether it is the front or rear. Add photos of the whole bumper, of the damaged corner, and of any sensor or camera openings. If a workshop has listed the parts needed, send that list too.

Check sensors, paint and the version first

Many EVs have parking sensors, cameras or radar near the bumper, so read the guide on which bumper version has the sensor holes. Then see whether the bumper comes bare, primed or painted, and our checklist for China EV body parts.

FAQ

Is a cracked bumper cover the only thing I need to replace?

Not always. A knock that cracks the cover can also bend the brackets, break the clips or damage the reinforcement beam behind it. Have the area checked before you order.

Who should check for hidden bumper damage?

A workshop or body shop. Anything near the reinforcement beam or crash structure is a specialist check, and we do not judge structure damage from a photo.

What should I send to get a bumper matched?

The brand and model, the build month and year, front or rear, and photos of the whole bumper, the damaged corner and any sensor or camera openings.

Do the brackets and clips come with a new bumper cover?

Not necessarily. Brackets, clips and fittings can be listed as separate parts, so say what you need and we check what is included before we quote.
