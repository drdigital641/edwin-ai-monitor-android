Nirvana Memorial Garden facilities: what is there for visitors, and is it open every day?

Before a family visit, it helps to know where you can sit, pray and talk. Here is what Nirvana's own booklet lists.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-11-03 · 3 min read
Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2025 booklet lists a 2U Zone with a payment counter and private consultation rooms, a Level 5 lounge, a Level 9 prayer space and a Level 9 cafe. It also says the garden is open every day of the year, including weekends and public holidays, with extended openings on special occasions, and has 24-hour security. Benjamin will confirm current hours before you go.

What facilities does Nirvana list for visitors?

  • 2U Zone: a payment counter and private consultation rooms.
  • Level 5 lounge: a lounge area. The booklet shows it as the Block B Level 5 lounge (贵宾休息厅).
  • Level 9 prayer space: an area for prayers.
  • Level 9 cafe: a place for visitors to sit and have a drink or meal.

Nirvana describes these as facilities "thoughtfully designed to balance solemnity and comfort". The booklet does not give opening times or menus for the cafe, so ask before you plan around it.

Is Nirvana open every day?

The booklet says Nirvana Memorial Garden is open every day of the year, including weekends and public holidays, with consistent daily hours and extended openings during special occasions. It also says the garden has 24-hour security services. These are Nirvana's own statements. The booklet does not print the hours, so we do not quote any here.

How do you plan a visit around these facilities?

  1. Ask Benjamin for the current opening hours on the day you want to go.
  2. Plan the viewing and any talk about niches or tablets for the consultation rooms, so the family can sit down together.
  3. If someone tires easily, ask where the lounge is and plan a break there.
  4. If your family wants to pray, ask about the Level 9 prayer space and any ceremonies on that day.

For getting there, see how to get to Nirvana Memorial Garden. For taking older parents, see visiting a columbarium with elderly parents.
