Does a dashcam drain the car battery?

By Battery Rescue SG (Motoserv) · Last updated 13 Nov 2026 · First published 13 Nov 2026

QUICK ANSWER
A dashcam draws only a little power, so it will not flatten a healthy battery on a normal day. Trouble starts with parking mode or a hardwired kit that records while the engine is off, especially on an older battery or a car that sits for days. A low-voltage cut-off protects the battery; a failing battery is the usual real cause.

How much power does a dashcam use?

A dashcam draws a small current compared with the car's own electronics. While you drive, the alternator supplies it, so the battery is not affected. The question only matters when the engine is off and the dashcam is still powered, because the battery then has to supply every minute of it.

When can a dashcam drain the battery?

  • Parking mode: the dashcam stays awake to record bumps or motion while the car is parked.
  • Constant-live hardwiring: a kit connected to a fuse that stays on, so the dashcam never fully turns off.
  • No low-voltage cut-off, or one set too low: the dashcam keeps recording until the battery is too weak to crank the engine.
  • An ageing battery: a battery near the end of its life holds less charge, so a small constant draw is enough to leave you stuck. See signs a car battery is dying.
  • Short trips and long parking: the battery is not fully recharged between uses. See how long a car can sit before the battery dies.

How do you set up a dashcam to protect the battery?

  1. If you do not need parking mode, power it from a socket that switches off with the ignition.
  2. If you want parking mode, use a proper hardwire kit with a fuse tap and set a low-voltage cut-off so enough charge is left to start the car.
  3. Do not tap a wire at random. Ask a technician to fit it if you are unsure; a wrong connection can trip fuses or cause other faults.
  4. Turn parking mode off when the car will sit for several days, for example when you travel.

How do you tell if the dashcam is the cause?

Unplug the dashcam and see whether the problem stops. If the car still starts slowly or goes flat again, the dashcam was not the main cause. Check the terminals for corrosion (see white powder on battery terminals) and compare with our guide on a battery that goes flat every morning, which covers other drains.

Frequently asked questions

Will a dashcam drain my car battery overnight?

Not on a healthy battery if it only runs when the engine is on. A parking-mode dashcam or one wired to a constant live feed keeps drawing power with the car off, which a weak battery may not cover.

What is a low-voltage cut-off on a dashcam?

It is a setting or hardwire-kit feature that switches the dashcam off when the battery voltage falls to a set level, so enough charge stays to start the car. Check your dashcam manual for how to set it.

Should I use the cigarette lighter or hardwire the dashcam?

A socket that switches off with the ignition records only while driving and is the lowest-risk choice for the battery. Hardwiring allows parking mode but needs a proper kit and fuse tap, ideally fitted by a technician.

My car will not start and I have a dashcam. Is that the cause?

It can be, but a worn battery is more common. Unplug the dashcam, check the terminals, and test the battery. If the car still will not start, a jump-start may get you going.

Does a dashcam shorten the life of a car battery?

Repeated deep discharge shortens any battery's life. A dashcam that keeps pulling power until the battery is very low can add to that, so use parking mode only with a working cut-off and a healthy battery.
