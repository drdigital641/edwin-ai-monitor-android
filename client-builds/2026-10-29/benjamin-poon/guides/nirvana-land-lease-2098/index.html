Nirvana Memorial Garden land lease: extended to 2098

Families choosing a niche often ask how long the site will be there. Nirvana has published a statement on its land lease.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-10-29 · 3 min read
Nirvana Memorial Garden says it extended its land lease in June 2016, from an original end date of 2029 to 2098, completing a 99-year lease. This comes from its 2025 booklet, which lists it among eight reasons to choose Nirvana. Benjamin Poon can show you the booklet page and confirm the latest position.

What does Nirvana say?

  • Original land lease end date: 2029.
  • Extended in June 2016 to 2098.
  • The booklet describes this as a 99-year land lease, listed as reason 8 of its "Why choose Nirvana" page.
  • The booklet also lists the lease extension in its milestones, next to the start of operations in January 2009.

Why do families ask about it?

A niche is meant to be visited for generations, so many families want to understand the site's tenure before they decide. The lease date is one piece of that picture. It is not the whole picture, and it is not advice on your own contract.

Questions to ask before you choose

  1. Can I see the booklet page or document that states the lease position?
  2. What does the niche agreement I would sign say about the length of the arrangement?
  3. How is the one-time maintenance fee and trust fund explained in writing?
  4. Is anything in the booklet out of date since 2025?
