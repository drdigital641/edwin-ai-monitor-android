Hyundai or Kia aircon not cold: which parts usually cause it

If a Hyundai or Kia aircon is not cold, the usual causes are low refrigerant from a leak, a compressor that is not engaging, a blocked or damaged condenser, a cooling fan that is not running, or a clogged cabin filter. A workshop checks gas pressure and the compressor first, then the parts around them.

By Autotex · Published 8 Nov 2026 · Last updated 8 Nov 2026

Why is my Hyundai or Kia aircon not cold?

The aircon needs three things to cool: enough refrigerant, a compressor that pumps it, and a condenser with airflow to release the heat. If any of the three fails, the air from the vents turns lukewarm or warm. Weak airflow is a different problem and usually points to the cabin filter or the blower.

What are the usual causes?

  • Low refrigerant. The system is sealed, so low gas normally means a leak somewhere in the pipes, joints or condenser.
  • A compressor that does not engage, or that is noisy or worn.
  • A condenser that is blocked with dirt, or damaged by stones or a minor front impact.
  • A radiator or condenser fan that does not run, so the heat cannot escape, especially in slow traffic.
  • A choked cabin air filter, which cuts airflow rather than temperature.

Is it warm air or weak airflow?

Note which one you have. If the fan is strong but the air is not cold, look at the refrigerant, compressor, condenser and cooling fans. If the air is cold but weak, the cabin filter is the first thing to check, then the blower. Cold at highway speed but warm at traffic lights often points to a cooling fan that is not working.

Will a gas top-up fix it?

Only if the gas was low, and low gas means the leak is still there. A top-up without a leak test may last days or a few weeks before the aircon warms up again. Ask the workshop to find the leak, and replace the part that is leaking, often a condenser, a hose or a compressor seal.

Can I keep driving with the aircon off?

Usually yes, but get a noisy or seizing compressor checked soon. On belt-driven systems a failing compressor can load the drive belt that also turns other parts. If the engine temperature gauge rises as well, stop in a safe place: that is an engine cooling problem, not only an aircon one.
