Several Engine Fault Codes at Once on a Mercedes: Could It Be the ECU?

When Camtronic, camshaft and sensor codes appear together, the engine control unit itself may be the fault. Here is how to tell, and what to check first.

By The ECU Clinic · 11 Nov 2026

Short answer: yes, it can be. When a scan shows several unrelated circuit faults at the same time, for example Camtronic solenoids, camshaft position, intake air pressure and charge air temperature, it is unlikely that all of those parts failed together. The common point is the engine control unit (ECU) that drives and reads them. Check the battery, the wiring and the plugs first, then have the ECU tested.

Why do many codes at once point to the ECU?

The engine ECU supplies power to many sensors and switches many solenoids. If a driver circuit or a sensor supply inside the ECU fails, every part on that circuit can report a fault together, even though the parts themselves may be fine. Codes such as an open circuit on a solenoid, or a short circuit to positive on a sensor, describe the wiring or the ECU side as much as the part.

What did this Mercedes C-Class show?

A C-Class (C205) with the M274 engine came to us with these codes stored in the engine ECU:

  • P001A13 Camtronic solenoid 1 "Intake": open circuit
  • P002A13 Camtronic solenoid 1 "Exhaust": open circuit
  • P261612 Camshaft position output signal: short circuit to positive
  • P119012 Intake air pressure sensor: short circuit to positive
  • P007D12 Charge air temperature sensor: short circuit to positive

Five unrelated circuits were faulty together. We tested the ECU itself, found the fault inside the car's own Bosch MED17.7.2 unit, repaired it on the bench, refitted it and cleared the codes. The job is also shown on our Mercedes engine ECU repair page.

What should you check before blaming the ECU?

  1. Have the battery and charging checked. Low or unstable voltage can set several codes at once.
  2. Check the wiring and plugs at the affected parts for water, corrosion, damage or a loose connector, especially after engine work.
  3. Check the parts the codes name, such as the solenoids and sensors, so that a simple fault is not missed.
  4. Read and keep every code. Take a photo of the scan screen before anything is cleared.
  5. If the same circuits fail again after parts or wiring are cleared, ask for the ECU to be tested.

Do new parts fix it?

Not if the fault is in the ECU. A sensor or solenoid that is replaced on a guess can leave the same codes returning, which is one of the signs that points back to the control unit. Our guide to sensor or unit: which is faulty explains the same thinking for ABS, where the code decides the next step.

Questions

Can an ECU fault cause many fault codes at once?
Yes. If a driver circuit or sensor supply inside the engine ECU fails, several unrelated parts can report faults together even though the parts themselves may be fine.
What are Camtronic codes on a Mercedes M274 engine?
Camtronic is the valve lift system on the M274 engine, and its solenoids are controlled by the engine ECU. Codes such as P001A13 and P002A13 describe an open circuit on the intake and exhaust solenoids.
Should I replace the sensors first when the codes show short circuits?
Not before checking the wiring, the plugs, the battery and the other codes. If the same codes return after the simple checks, ask for the ECU to be tested.
