Best locations for a vending machine in Singapore

The best location is where your exact customer already waits for a few minutes. AIVS's 13 AI Printbox machines sit at mall entrances, MRT linkways and Changi Airport Terminal 3; its 6 Topi Express machines sit at motorcycle workshops, an accessory shop, a dealer and a laundromat. Match the product to the crowd, then check footfall at the hours that matter.

Malls: entrances, atriums and linkways

AI Printbox machines are placed at the entrances of specific shops (Bukit Panjang Plaza, Eastpoint Mall, Tampines 1, Sembawang Shopping Centre), in atriums and lobbies (AMK Hub Level 3 Lobby B), and in the Hillion Mall linkway to Bukit Panjang MRT. People slow down at these points.

The airport

Changi Airport Terminal 3 has two AI Printbox machines: one in the Arrival Hall and one in Basement 2. Travellers waiting for pickups and families meeting arrivals have time and a souvenir in mind.

Where riders stop

Topi Express works at the places riders already visit: Hodaka Motoworld and Candy Motor in Kaki Bukit, BW Motorsports in Old Toh Tuck Road, Wheels & Cycles in Woodlands. A laundromat (345 Wash in Telok Blangah) works too, because a wash cycle is a natural wait.

How to judge a site

Count people at the hours you expect to sell. Check there is a power point and a clear line of sight. Agree who looks after the space. Both AIVS machines are cashless, which hosts prefer.
