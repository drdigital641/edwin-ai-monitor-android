Is a vending machine profitable in Singapore?

A vending machine can be profitable in Singapore, but nobody can promise it. Whether it makes money depends on four things: the machine, the location, what it sells, and how many people use it each day.

This guide shows how to think it through before you buy, using AIVS's own published prices. It also covers what you still need to find out, because AIVS does not publish sales figures for its machines and will not guess them for you.

What is the short answer?

  • Yes, it can make money when enough people use the machine at a price they're happy to pay, and the costs stay below what it takes in.
  • No, it isn't automatic. A machine in a quiet corner can earn very little, even if the machine itself is good.
  • What you control: which machine you buy, where it goes, and how well it's kept stocked and working.

3. Your running costs

Write these down before you decide:

  • Location fee or revenue share agreed with the host (it varies by site).
  • Consumables: blank phone cases, or disinfectant for helmets. AIVS supplies stock at cost.
  • Support plan: from S$99/month with AIVS.
  • Electricity, if the host doesn't include it. See how much electricity a vending machine uses.
  • Your time for restocking and checks.

4. Royalties and fees

Many franchise models take a cut of every sale. With AIVS you own the machine, with no franchise fee and 0% royalties, so the sales are yours after your costs. See franchise vs owning your own machine.

How do you work out your own numbers?

You don't need AIVS's figures to test a site. Use your own estimate:

  1. Pick a daily sales estimate for the site, and be careful with it. Use a low number, a middle number and a high number.
  2. Multiply by the price per use (S$15 or S$5) to get daily sales.
  3. Take off monthly costs: location share, stock, the S$99/month support plan and anything else on your list.
  4. Compare with the machine price (from S$5,000 or S$1,800) to see how long it might take to cover it.

If the low estimate only works with a long wait to cover the machine, that site is a risk. Read the risks of a vending machine business before you commit.
