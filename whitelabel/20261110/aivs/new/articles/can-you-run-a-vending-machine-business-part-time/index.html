Can you run a vending machine business part-time in Singapore?

It can be, because AIVS machines are self-service and cashless, so no shop or staff is needed at the machine, and the owner dashboard shows revenue, device status and inventory on your phone. How much time it takes depends on how many machines you run, how far they are from you and how often they need restocking. AIVS does not publish an hours-per-week figure, so ask before you buy.

What does the machine do without you?

Both AIVS machines are self-service. A customer chooses, pays by QR code and collects, with no staff at the machine. AI Printbox prints a custom phone case in about 3 minutes; Topi Express cleans a helmet in about 5 minutes. Because payment is cashless, there is no cash to collect from the machine.

What still needs a person?

A vending machine is low-effort rather than hands-off. Someone still restocks it (phone cases for AI Printbox, disinfectant for Topi Express), checks it, and keeps a good relationship with the host of the space. The owner dashboard helps by showing revenue, device status and inventory, so you can restock before a slot runs empty instead of visiting on a fixed schedule.

Who looks after faults?

The optional support plan from S$99 a month covers maintenance and repairs, system support and 24/7 technical help, so a fault does not have to be fixed by you in the evening. Read what it covers on the support and maintenance page.

Why the location matters for a part-time owner

You choose and secure the location, and AIVS can help with site sourcing. A site close to your home or workplace makes restocking easier to fit around a job, and a site with steady footfall matters more than a convenient one that nobody walks past. See best locations for a vending machine in Singapore.

What to ask AIVS before you buy

Ask how often a machine like yours is usually restocked, how stock is delivered to you, what the support plan includes and how quickly help responds. AIVS does not publish earnings figures, so ask for real usage numbers from comparable sites rather than relying on a promise. The full ownership steps are in how to start a vending machine business in Singapore, and the model is on the own a vending machine page.
