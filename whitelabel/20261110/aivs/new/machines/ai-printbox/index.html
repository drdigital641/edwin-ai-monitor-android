What is the AI Printbox by AIVS?

The AI Printbox is a self-service kiosk that prints a customer's own photo or a chosen design onto a phone case in about 3 minutes. Customers pick their phone model, choose a design or upload a photo, pay cashlessly and collect the printed case. See it in action

What customers get

A durable, protective phone case with their design printed into it, not a sticker or a peel-off decal. They choose a design from the collection, add an AI filter, or upload their own photo of a pet, a group or a holiday. The machine supports the latest iPhone and Samsung models and the list grows as new phones launch.

Typical customers include secondary school students and working adults who want a case nobody else has, and people looking for a last-minute personal gift.

What you get as the owner

A refurbished AI Printbox machine from S$5,000 with a 1-year hardware warranty (terms apply). You own it outright and pay no royalties. You can run it under the AI Printbox brand that shoppers already see in 13 locations, or re-wrap it with your own branding.

Phone case stock comes from AIVS at partner pricing. A monthly support plan from S$99 covers maintenance and system support, and training is included so you can start without any vending experience.

You choose and secure your own location. No retail shop or staff is needed: the machine is self-service and takes cashless payment around the clock.

Questions from future owners

How much does an AI Printbox machine cost?

A refurbished AI Printbox machine is from S$5,000, with a 1-year hardware warranty (terms apply) and an optional monthly support plan from S$99. WhatsApp 8335 4865 for the current price and what it includes.

Do I own the machine?

Yes. You buy and own the machine outright, and there are no royalty fees. You can run it under the AI Printbox brand or re-wrap it with your own branding. The artwork, branding and mascot designs on the AIVS wrap remain AIVS intellectual property.

Do I need to manage it every day?

No. The machine is self-service and cashless. Under the monthly support plan AIVS handles maintenance and system support, and you top up phone case stock, which you buy from AIVS at partner pricing.

Who finds the location?

You choose and secure your own location, which keeps the business yours. AIVS shares what it has learned from its own mall placements, such as high-footfall entrances, linkways to MRT stations and airport arrival halls, and can help with site sourcing.

Which phones does AI Printbox support?

The latest iPhone and Samsung models, and the list is updated as new phones are released. The machine interface shows the models available at the time of printing.

Why is it called "AI" when there is no artificial intelligence in the machine?

In the AIVS name, "AI" stands for Automated Intelligence: smart, automated vending that makes a business simple to run. It does not refer to artificial intelligence.

Questions from customers

How does AI Printbox work?

Select your phone model on the touchscreen, pick a design from the collection, add an AI filter, or upload your own photo, then pay. The machine prints your case in about 3 minutes.

How much is a printed phone case?

A custom printed case is S$15 (price published on aivs.sg in 2025; check the machine for the current price). Each case is a durable, protective case with the design printed into it, not a sticker.

Is my uploaded photo kept?

No. Uploaded images are deleted from the system within 48 hours and are not stored, shared or used for anything else.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

No. Purchases are non-refundable and non-exchangeable, because each case is printed to your order. You are responsible for the content you print.
