Your machine, your brand

Every AIVS machine can carry the established AI Printbox or Topi Express look, or a wrap of your own design.

Two ways to brand

Keep the AIVS brand: AI Printbox's pink bear and Topi Express's blue-green helmet wrap are already seen in 19 locations, which helps a new site feel familiar on day one. Or re-wrap the machine with your own branding at your own cost, for a corporate site, an event or a brand that wants the machine to match its shop.

What stays with AIVS

The artwork, branding and mascot designs on the AIVS wraps remain AIVS intellectual property, so they are licensed to you rather than sold. Your own wrap is yours.

Events and pop-ups

AI Printbox has drawn crowds at franchise exhibitions and roadshows. A branded machine can print event-themed cases on the spot, which makes it a popular activation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove the AI Printbox or Topi Express branding?

Yes. You can re-wrap the machine with your own branding at your own cost.

Can I use the AIVS mascots on my own marketing?

The AIVS artwork, branding and mascots stay AIVS intellectual property. Ask AIVS before using them outside the machine.

Can the printed designs be branded too?

Ask AIVS. The AI Printbox design collection is managed centrally, and event or brand designs can be discussed.
