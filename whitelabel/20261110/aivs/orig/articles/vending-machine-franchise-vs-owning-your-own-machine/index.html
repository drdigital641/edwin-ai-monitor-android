Vending machine franchise vs owning your own machine

In a franchise you pay an entry fee and a royalty on sales for the right to use a brand and system. With AIVS you buy the machine outright, pay no royalties, can use the AI Printbox or Topi Express brand or your own, and add a support plan from S$99 a month only if you want it. You own the asset either way you brand it.

When a franchise makes sense

When you want a fully prescribed system and are happy to pay for it on every sale. Some operators prefer that trade.

When ownership makes sense

When you want to keep what the machine earns, control the location and the branding, and still have support on call. AIVS's model is built for this: you own the machine, AIVS supplies stock at cost price, trains you and maintains the machine under a monthly plan.

What AIVS keeps

The AI Printbox and Topi Express artwork, branding and mascots stay AIVS intellectual property. If you re-wrap in your own brand, that wrap is yours.

Questions to ask before you sign a vending machine franchise in Singapore

Whichever route you take, get answers to these five questions before you pay anything:

  1. What is the entry fee, and what does it include? Machine, branding, training and first stock should be listed separately.
  2. What royalty or share of sales do you pay? Ask whether it is taken from sales or from profit.
  3. Who sets the stock price? Ask whether you can buy stock anywhere else.
  4. Who chooses the location? Ask what happens if the spot does not work out.
  5. How do you exit? Ask what the contract says about ending it, selling the machine or changing the brand.

AIVS's answers are in the table above: machine price from S$1,800, 0% royalties, stock at cost price, location chosen by you with AIVS's help, and a re-wrap in your own brand if you want one. Ask AIVS to confirm them for the machine you are considering.

Questions to ask before you choose either route

Ask what you pay upfront, what you pay on every sale, who sets the price of stock, who chooses the location and who owns the machine if you stop. Get the answers in writing. The table above shows how AIVS ownership answers each one, and you can put the same questions to any franchisor to compare.
