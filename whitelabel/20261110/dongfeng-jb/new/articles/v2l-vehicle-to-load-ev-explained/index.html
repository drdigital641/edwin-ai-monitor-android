What is V2L in an EV, and what should you ask before you rely on it?

Updated 2026-11-10 · By the Dongfeng Johor Bahru team

V2L, short for vehicle-to-load, lets an EV supply electric power to outside devices from its battery. Our Vigo page lists a 3.3 kW V2L output. Check the watts printed on any device before you plan to use it, and ask us on a test drive how to switch it on and how it is used.

What does V2L do?

Normally power flows into an EV from a charger. With V2L, the car can also send power out, so a device that needs mains electricity can run from the car's battery. It is useful when you are away from a socket, for example on a family outing.

What does our Vigo page list?

The Dongfeng Vigo page lists V2L at 3.3 kW. The Dongfeng 007 page lists V2L as a feature, and we do not state a figure for it in this guide. Ask us for the details of the car you are interested in.

Why do watts matter?

Every device draws a certain amount of power, shown in watts (W) on its label or power adapter. The total of the devices you plug in has to stay within the car's V2L output, and some devices draw more power when they start than when they run. A device that needs more power than the car supplies will not work properly, so check the label first.

What does it do to the battery and range?

The power comes from the car's battery, so using V2L uses up charge that you would otherwise keep for driving. How long a device can run depends on its power use and how much charge is in the battery. We make no run-time claim here. Keep enough charge for your drive home, and follow the owner's manual for how to use V2L.

Questions

Does the Dongfeng Vigo have V2L?

Yes. The Dongfeng Vigo page lists a 3.3 kW V2L output.

Does the Dongfeng 007 have V2L?

The Dongfeng 007 page lists V2L as a feature. We do not state an output figure here, so ask us for the details.

Can V2L run any device I own?

Not necessarily. A device must stay within the car's V2L output, and some draw more power at start-up. Check the watts on its label, and ask us if you are unsure.

Does using V2L reduce my driving range?

Yes, because the power comes from the same battery. How much depends on what you run and for how long. Keep enough charge to drive home.
