Signs your Hyundai or Kia needs new spark plugs

Worn spark plugs usually show as a slow or hard start, a rough idle, hesitation when you accelerate and higher fuel use. A misfire code can also appear. Follow the interval in your owner's manual, and have a workshop check the plugs and coils together, as both cause similar symptoms.

By Autotex · Published 4 Nov 2026 · Last updated 4 Nov 2026

What does a spark plug do?

A spark plug creates the spark that ignites the fuel and air in each cylinder of a petrol engine. The electrodes wear down with use, so over time the spark gets weaker and the engine burns fuel less cleanly.

What are the common signs of worn spark plugs?

  • The engine is slower to start, or cranks for longer than before.
  • A rough or uneven idle, or a slight shake when stopped at a traffic light.
  • Hesitation or a flat spot when you accelerate.
  • Higher fuel use than usual.
  • A check-engine light that comes on with a misfire code.

When should spark plugs be changed?

Follow the service schedule in your owner's manual, because the interval depends on the engine and the type of plug fitted. Your workshop can also pull the plugs, check the electrode wear and tell you whether they are due.

Is it the spark plug or the ignition coil?

Both cause a misfire, and they sit together in most engines. A workshop reads the fault code to find the cylinder, then checks the plug and the coil on that cylinder. If you are changing plugs on an older car, ask the workshop to look at the coils at the same time.

Do all cars need spark plugs?

No. Spark plugs are for petrol engines. Diesel engines do not use them and electric cars have no engine, so they are not listed for those models in our shop.

Which spark plugs do I need?

Plugs differ by engine, not just by model. Send us your car plate or chassis number and we will match the right set. Plugs are sold as a set for the number of cylinders your engine has.
