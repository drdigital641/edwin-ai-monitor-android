What decides the right thermostat

The thermostat opens at a set temperature to let hot coolant to the radiator, then closes to hold the engine at working temperature. Which one fits depends on engine, and the opening temperature; the part may come with its housing.

Signs the Kia thermostat needs replacing

  • An engine that overheats or runs cold.
  • A temperature gauge that swings.
  • Poor heater output because the engine never warms up.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket thermostat

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
