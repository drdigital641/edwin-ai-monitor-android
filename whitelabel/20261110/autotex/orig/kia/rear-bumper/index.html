What decides the right rear bumper

The rear bumper covers the back of the car and carries the reflectors, parking sensors and the exhaust cut-out on some trims. Which one fits depends on generation and facelift; parking sensor holes and exhaust cut-outs differ by trim.

Signs the Kia rear bumper needs replacing

  • Cracks or split mounting tabs after a reversing knock.
  • A bumper edge that has popped out of the tail lamp or boot gap.
  • Paint damage that has exposed the plastic.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket rear bumper

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
