What decides the right water pump

The water pump circulates coolant through the engine and radiator. Which one fits depends on engine; the pump may be driven by the drive belt or the timing belt, and some come with a housing.

Signs the Kia water pump needs replacing

  • Coolant dripping from the pump weep hole.
  • A whine or grinding from the front of the engine.
  • The engine overheating with no other cause.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket water pump

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
