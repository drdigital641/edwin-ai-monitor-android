What decides the right wiper blades

The wiper blades clear rain from the windscreen with a rubber edge pressed against the glass. Which one fits depends on blade length for each side and the type of arm connector; the driver and passenger blades are often different lengths.

Signs the Hyundai wiper blades need replacing

  • Streaks or smears that do not clear.
  • Chattering or squeaking as the blade crosses the glass.
  • Split or hardened rubber.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket wiper blades

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
