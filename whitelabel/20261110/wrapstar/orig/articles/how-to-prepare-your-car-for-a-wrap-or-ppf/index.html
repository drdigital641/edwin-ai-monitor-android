How do you prepare your car for a wrap or PPF job?

By Wrapstar Autostyle · Last updated 24 October 2026

The short answer

Before a wrap or PPF job, tell the studio about any past respray, accident repair, chips, dents or rust, and let them check the paint before they start. Film follows the surface it sits on, so paint problems can show through it. Then plan the day with the studio, and plan the first wash: wait a few days, then hand wash gently.

Why does the condition of your paint matter?

Wrap and PPF films sit on top of your paint. On healthy factory paint a good film comes off cleanly later. Chips and bad resprays can show through the film, and panels that were badly resprayed can lift paint when film is removed. That is why we check the paint before we start and tell you if there is a risk. The same point comes up in should you remove a wrap before selling.

What should you tell the studio?

  • Any respray, accident repair or touch-up, and which panels.
  • Chips, dents, scratches or rust spots you already know about.
  • Aftermarket parts, trims or accessories on the outside of the car.
  • Whether the car is leased, company-owned or will be sold or returned later.
  • Which panels you care most about, if you are not wrapping the whole car.

Should you fix chips or dents first?

Film does not fill a chip or a dent: it follows the shape underneath. Ask the studio before you book any repair, because they can tell you whether a mark will show through the film you are choosing and which repairs are worth doing first. Do not start polishing or touching up paint yourself in the days before.

What should you do on the day?

Ask the studio when to bring the car, when to collect it, and what to take out of the car first. Arrive with the car reasonably clean if you can, and ask whether they would like to clean it themselves. When the job is done, walk round the car with the studio and look at every panel in good light before you drive away.

What happens after the job?

Give the film a few days to settle before the first wash, then hand wash gently. If anything bubbles or lifts, WhatsApp us a photo and we will take a look. Our guide to caring for a wrapped car covers the first wash and everyday habits, and you can talk to us about a colour change wrap if you are still choosing.

Questions

Do I need to wash my car before a wrap?

The surface has to be clean before film goes on, so arrive with the car reasonably clean if you can and ask the studio whether they prefer to clean it themselves.

Can a car with chips or a respray be wrapped?

Often yes, but chips and bad resprays can show through the film, and badly resprayed panels can lift paint when film is removed later. We check the paint before we start and tell you if there is a risk.

When can I wash the car after a wrap or PPF job?

Give the film a few days to settle before the first wash, then hand wash gently. Ask us for the exact timing for your film.
