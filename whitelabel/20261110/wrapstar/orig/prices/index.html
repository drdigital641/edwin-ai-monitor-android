What changes the price of a wrap or PPF?

  • Car size. Hatchbacks and sedans cost less than SUVs and MPVs.
  • Film. Vinyl, colour PPF and clear PPF are different materials at different prices.
  • Coverage. Full car, front only, door shuts, roof, trims.
  • Paint condition. Chips or old repairs may need attention before film goes on.

Price questions

Is colour PPF more expensive than a vinyl wrap?

Yes. Our posted wrap price starts from $1,588, while our recent colour PPF offers were $2,588 (August 2026, hatchbacks and sedans) and $2,788 nett for a Tesla Model 3 (October 2026). Colour PPF is thicker TPU film that also absorbs stone chips.

Do SUVs cost more to wrap?

Usually, yes. SUVs and MPVs have more panels to cover, so they take more film and more time. Our National Day offers in August 2026 were for hatchbacks and sedans, with SUVs and MPVs priced by quote.
