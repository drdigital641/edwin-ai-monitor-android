Clear paint protection film (PPF)

Love your car's colour as it is? Clear PPF is a transparent, tough film that keeps the factory paint looking new by taking the chips and scratches for it.

What is clear ppf?

Clear PPF is a transparent polyurethane film fitted over the paint. It keeps your original colour and finish, takes the stone chips and light scratches that would otherwise mark the paint, and makes the car easier to keep clean.

Why choose clear PPF?

  • Keeps your colour. Clear film over the factory paint, gloss stays gloss.
  • Front-end defence. Bonnet, bumper and mirrors take most stone chips on expressways.
  • Headlights too. Clear film on headlamps helps against chips and haze.
  • Easier washing. Dirt and droppings come off the film with less effort.

Which parts of the car should you cover with clear PPF first?

Start with the front: bonnet, front bumper, fenders and mirrors take most of the stone chips on expressways. Headlights can be covered too. You can add the rest of the car later, and we quote front-only coverage separately.

  • Bonnet and front bumper face the road and take the most chips.
  • Fenders and mirrors sit at the front corners where chips also land.
  • Headlights can be covered with clear film. LTA does not allow tinting or masking of vehicle lamps, so keep headlamp film clear.

Clear PPF questions

Is clear PPF worth it in Singapore?

If your car does a lot of expressway driving or you plan to keep it for years, clear PPF saves the paint from chips that would otherwise need touch-ups or a respray.

Can I do PPF on only the front of the car?

Yes. Many owners protect the bonnet, front bumper, fenders and mirrors first, as those take most of the chips. WhatsApp us for a front-only quote.

Will clear PPF turn yellow?

Film quality decides how it ages. Ask us which film we fit on your car and what its warranty covers before you book.

Does clear PPF change my car's colour?

No. Clear PPF is transparent, so it keeps your original colour and finish. Gloss stays gloss. If you want a new colour, see colour PPF or a colour change wrap.

Can PPF go on headlights?

Yes, clear film can protect headlamps. LTA does not allow tinting or masking of vehicle lamps, so headlamp film should stay clear.
