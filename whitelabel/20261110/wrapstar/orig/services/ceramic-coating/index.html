Ceramic coating

A ceramic coating is a liquid layer that bonds to your paint and cures hard. Water beads off, dirt lets go more easily, and the gloss stays deeper between washes.

What is ceramic coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid coating applied by hand to a clean, polished car. It bonds to the paint and cures into a thin, hard, glossy layer that repels water and makes dirt easier to wash off. It does not stop stone chips: that is what paint protection film is for.

Why choose ceramic coating?

  • Easier washing. Water beads and runs off, and grime rinses away more easily.
  • Deeper gloss. The coating goes on after a polish, so the shine you see is locked in.
  • Works with film. Can go on paint, and on wraps or PPF, to keep them easier to clean.

Ceramic coating questions

What is car ceramic coating?

A liquid coating that bonds to your car paint and cures into a hard, glossy layer. It repels water and makes the car easier to keep clean.

Does ceramic coating protect against stone chips?

No. A coating is very thin. For stone chips and scratches, clear PPF or colour PPF is the right choice, and a coating can go on top.

Do I need a polish before ceramic coating?

Yes. The paint is cleaned and polished first, so the coating locks in a clean, glossy finish rather than old swirls.
