Colour PPF: change the colour and armour the paint

Colour PPF is paint protection film with the colour built in. It changes the look like a wrap, but it is thicker and tougher, so it takes stone chips, light scratches and road rash for your paint.

What is colour ppf?

Colour PPF (paint protection film) is a thermoplastic polyurethane film that is coloured. The film we posted for the Tesla Model 3 is 7.5mil TPU: it absorbs stone chips, light swirls heal under hot water or afternoon sun, and it can be removed later, leaving the factory paint underneath.

Why choose colour PPF?

  • Real TPU armour. 7.5mil thermoplastic polyurethane film that absorbs stone chips, scratches and road rash.
  • Heat self-healing. Minor swirls and light scratches fade under hot water or Singapore's afternoon heat.
  • Hydrophobic topcoat. Water, bird droppings, grime and tree sap slide off, so washing is quicker.
  • Resale value kept. Removable later, with the original factory paint preserved underneath.

Colour PPF questions

What is the difference between colour PPF and a vinyl wrap?

Both change the colour. Colour PPF is a thicker TPU film that also absorbs stone chips and heals light swirls in heat; vinyl is thinner and costs less, but protects less. Our guide compares them side by side.

Does colour PPF really self-heal?

Light swirls and fine surface scratches in the top layer fade with heat, such as hot water or a hot afternoon. Deep cuts through the film do not heal.

Can colour PPF be removed?

Yes. It is designed to come off later and leave the factory paint underneath in the condition it was protected in.
