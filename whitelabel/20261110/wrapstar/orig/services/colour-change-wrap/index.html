Car wrap in Singapore: colour change wraps in Yishun

A full vinyl wrap changes your car's colour in days, not weeks, and the factory paint stays underneath, protected and untouched.

What is colour change wrap?

A colour change wrap is a thin vinyl film laid over every painted panel of your car. It gives a new colour and finish, guards the paint from light scratches and fading, and can be removed later without a respray.

Why choose a colour change wrap?

  • New colour, original paint kept. The factory paint stays under the film, which helps when you sell.
  • Any finish. Gloss, satin and matte, from quiet greys to bright yellow.
  • Easier upkeep. Wrap film shrugs off light marks and bird droppings wipe away.
  • Reversible. Bored of the colour later? It can be peeled off and changed.

Colour change wrap questions

How much does a full car wrap cost in Singapore?

Our posted price for a full car colour wrap starts from $1,588. The final price depends on the size of the car, the film and how much of the door shuts you want covered. WhatsApp a photo of your car for an exact quote.

Will a wrap damage my car paint?

A good film on healthy factory paint comes off cleanly. Panels that were badly resprayed can lift paint when film is removed, so we check the paint before we start and tell you if there is a risk.

Do I need to tell LTA if I wrap my car?

Rules on colour changes are set by LTA. Check the current requirements on OneMotoring or with LTA before your job, and ask us what other owners have done.

Can I wrap a leased or company car?

Yes, many owners wrap cars they plan to return or resell, because the film can be removed and the original colour is still there underneath.
