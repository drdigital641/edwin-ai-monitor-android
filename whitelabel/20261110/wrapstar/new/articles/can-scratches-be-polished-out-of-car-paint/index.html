Can scratches be polished out of car paint in Singapore?

By Wrapstar Autostyle · 6 November 2026

The short answer

Light scratches, swirls and wash marks that sit in the top layer of clear coat can be polished out, because a machine polish levels that layer. Scratches that go through the clear coat to the colour or primer cannot be polished out and need a touch-up or respray instead. Send us a photo and we will tell you which yours is.

Which scratches can polishing remove?

Polishing uses a machine and a fine abrasive polish to level the top layer of clear coat. That reduces light swirls, wash marks and dullness and brings the gloss back. Light scratches that stay inside the clear coat are the ones that can come out.

Which scratches cannot be polished out?

Scratches that go through the clear coat to the colour or primer. Polishing only works on the clear coat, so these need a touch-up or a respray instead.

How can you tell which kind you have?

  1. Wash and dry the car so dirt is not mistaken for a scratch.
  2. Run a fingernail lightly across the scratch. A common rule of thumb: if your nail catches, it is probably too deep to polish out. It is a guide, not a guarantee.
  3. Look at the colour inside the scratch. White, grey or a different colour usually means it has gone through the clear coat.
  4. WhatsApp us a photo of the paint and the car model, and we will say what we would do.

Should you polish a wrapped or matte car?

No. Matte and satin wraps should never be polished, because polish adds uneven shine. Paint is polished before a wrap or a coating, not the film itself. See caring for a wrapped car.

How do you stop new scratches after a polish?

Polishing is also the first step before a ceramic coating, which locks in a clean finish. A coating is thin and does not stop chips; clear PPF is the film that takes chips and light scratches for the paint. Our guide PPF or ceramic coating explains the difference, and which car panels need PPF first covers where to start.
