PPF or ceramic coating: which does your car need?

By Wrapstar Autostyle · 4 November 2026

The short answer

They do different jobs. PPF is a transparent, tough film that takes stone chips and light scratches for your paint. A ceramic coating is a thin liquid layer that cures hard and glossy, so water beads off and dirt washes away more easily, but it does not stop chips. If chips are your worry, choose PPF; if easier washing and gloss matter most, choose a coating; many owners put a coating on top of PPF.

When is PPF the better choice?

When your car does a lot of expressway driving or you plan to keep it for years. The bonnet, front bumper, fenders and mirrors take most of the chips, so many owners protect the front first. See which car panels need PPF first and our clear PPF service.

When is a ceramic coating the better choice?

When you want the car easier to keep clean and glossy between washes and chips are not your main worry. The paint is polished first, so the coating seals in a clean finish rather than old swirls. Read about our ceramic coating and polishing.

Can you have both?

Yes. Film takes the impacts and the coating on top keeps the surface easier to wash. If you have wrapped the car, a coating can go on the wrap too, but matte and satin wraps should never be polished. Our guide to caring for a wrapped car covers washing.

Not sure which suits your car? WhatsApp us the model and a photo of the paint and we will tell you what we would do and quote it.

Questions

Does ceramic coating replace PPF?

No. A coating is very thin and does not stop stone chips. For chips and scratches, clear PPF or colour PPF is the right choice, and a coating can go on top.

Can I have both PPF and a ceramic coating?

Yes. A ceramic coating can go on paint, on wraps and on PPF, to keep the surface easier to clean.

Do I need a polish before a ceramic coating?

Yes. The paint is cleaned and polished first, so the coating locks in a clean, glossy finish rather than old swirls.
