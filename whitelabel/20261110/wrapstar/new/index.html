How much does a car wrap cost at Wrapstar?

Our posted price for a full car colour wrap starts from $1,588. Colour PPF, clear PPF, window film and dechrome are priced by car and film. See our prices page for recent offers, or WhatsApp your car model for a quote.

Should I choose a vinyl wrap or colour PPF?

A vinyl wrap is the lower-cost way to change colour. Colour PPF also changes the colour, but it is thick TPU that absorbs stone chips and heals light swirls in heat. Pick colour PPF if you want protection as well as looks.
