Services

Seven services, all done in our own studio at North Spring Bizhub, Yishun.

Wraps, PPF and window film in Yishun

Wrapstar Autostyle is a car wrap and paint protection studio at North Spring Bizhub, Yishun. We change the colour of your car with gloss, satin or matte vinyl that comes off when you want it to, add stone-chip protection with clear or colour PPF, and cut heat with LTA compliant nano ceramic window film.

We also dechrome shiny trims and badges, polish paint to bring back gloss, and apply ceramic coating for a harder, glossier layer that is easier to wash. Pick the service below to see how it works, or check the prices page for our dated offers. WhatsApp +65 9754 1833 with your car model and we will reply with options.
