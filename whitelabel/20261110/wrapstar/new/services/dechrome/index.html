Dechrome in Singapore: chrome delete for trims, grilles and badges

Chrome window lines, grille surrounds and badges date a car fast. Dechrome covers them in gloss or satin black film, so the car looks lower, cleaner and newer.

What is dechrome?

Dechrome, or chrome delete, means covering a car's chrome trims, such as window surrounds, grille bars, roof rails and badges, with black film. It gives a stealthier, sportier look, costs far less than replacing the parts, and the film can be removed later.

Why dechrome your car?

  • Window trims. Chrome window lines in gloss or satin black.
  • Grille and badges. Grille surrounds, emblems and lettering blacked out.
  • Pairs with a wrap. Common with a colour change or colour PPF to finish the look.
  • Reversible. The chrome is still there under the film if you want it back.

Which chrome parts can be dechromed?

The parts most often covered are window surrounds, grille bars and surrounds, roof rails, and badges and lettering. Send us a photo of your car on WhatsApp and say which chrome parts you want in black film; we quote for the parts you choose and tell you if any part is not a good fit for film.

Dechrome questions

What is dechrome?

Dechrome, or chrome delete, is covering the chrome parts of a car with black film so they match a stealth or sporty look.

Which parts of a car can be dechromed?

Commonly window surrounds, grille bars and surrounds, roof rails, and badges and lettering. Send a photo on WhatsApp and say which parts you want covered; we quote for those parts.

How much does dechrome cost?

It depends on how many chrome parts your car has. Send a photo of your car on WhatsApp and we will quote you.

Can dechrome be removed?

Yes. The film can be peeled off later and the chrome underneath is still there.

Can I dechrome without wrapping the whole car?

Yes. Dechrome is often done on its own, as a quick way to change how a car looks.
