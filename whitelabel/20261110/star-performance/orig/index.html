Quick answers

What does Star Performance Motor do?

We are a used car dealer in Yishun, Singapore. We sell quality pre-owned cars, buy cars outright or as trade-ins, consign cars for owners, find specific cars on request, and arrange exports for cars near the end of their COE.

Where is your showroom?

9 Yishun Industrial Street 1, #01-82 North Spring Bizhub, Singapore 768163. Walk-ins are welcome, or WhatsApp +65 9742 7491 to book a time.

What are your opening hours?

We are open 10am to 8pm, every day at 9 Yishun Industrial Street 1, #01-82 North Spring Bizhub. Walk-ins are welcome, and you are also welcome to WhatsApp +65 9742 7491 to book a time.

Where can I see the cars you have for sale?

Our current cars, with prices and photos, are listed on our sgcarmart dealer page. You can also WhatsApp us for what is in stock today.

Are your customers happy?

Yes. Our sister business Wrapstar Autostyle shares our unit at North Spring Bizhub and does colour change wraps, colour PPF, clear paint protection film, window film and dechrome. See wrapstarautostyle.com for its colours and past works.
