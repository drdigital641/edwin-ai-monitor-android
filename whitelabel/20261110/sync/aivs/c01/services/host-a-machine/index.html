Host a machine at your location

AIVS places machines at sites with the right footfall and is open to new placement partners. If you manage a mall, shop, kiosk or workshop, we would like to talk.

Sites that work

AI Printbox does well at mall entrances, atriums, MRT linkways and the airport arrival hall, where people have a few minutes to spare. Topi Express belongs where riders stop: motorcycle workshops and accessory shops, petrol kiosks, detailing centres, and even a laundromat in Telok Blangah.

What a host provides

Floor space for a machine roughly the size of a drinks vending machine, a standard power point, and agreement on the placement. The machines are cashless, so no cash handling is involved.

Current partners

AI Printbox is in 13 locations including AMK Hub, Hillion Mall, Parkway Parade, Tampines 1, The Centrepoint and Changi Airport Terminal 3. Topi Express is at six motorcycle-related sites including Hodaka Motoworld, BW Motorsports and Candy Motor.

Next step

Send us the address, the spot you have in mind and the kind of visitors you get. The team will arrange a meeting at your convenience to discuss how a placement could work.

Frequently asked questions

Who looks after a hosted machine?

AIVS or the machine owner handles stock, maintenance and support. The host provides the space and power.

How big is the machine?

About the footprint of a standard drinks vending machine. Ask for exact dimensions for the model you are interested in.

Does the machine need cash collection?

No. Both machines are cashless.
