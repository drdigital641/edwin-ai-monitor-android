Quick answers

What does AIVS sell?

Two self-service machines you can own: AI Printbox, which prints custom phone cases in 3 minutes, and Topi Express, which cleans and disinfects a helmet in 5 minutes. Both are sold refurbished with a warranty and 0% royalties.

How much does it cost to own a machine?

Topi Express from S$1,800 and AI Printbox from S$5,000, refurbished, plus an optional support plan from S$99 a month. Stock is supplied at cost price.

Do I need experience or staff?

No. The machines are self-service and cashless, training is included, and AIVS handles maintenance under the support plan.

Where are AIVS machines today?

19 locations in Singapore: 13 AI Printbox machines in malls and Changi Airport Terminal 3, and 6 Topi Express machines at motorcycle shops, workshops and a laundromat.

Is there a franchise fee or royalty?

No. You buy the machine outright, there is no franchise fee and AIVS takes 0% royalties on your sales. You pay for stock, which AIVS supplies at cost price, and an optional support plan from S$99 a month.

What warranty comes with a machine?

AI Printbox comes with a 1-year hardware warranty and Topi Express with a 6-month hardware warranty. Warranty terms apply, so ask on WhatsApp for the details before you buy.

Who finds the location for my machine?

You choose and secure the location. AIVS shares what works from its own placements, such as AMK Hub and Changi Airport Terminal 3, and can help with site finding.

Ready to own a machine?

WhatsApp the machine you want and the location you have in mind. From S$1,800, 0% royalties, training included.
