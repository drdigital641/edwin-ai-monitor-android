What is OMV on a used car in Singapore?

OMV means Open Market Value: the value of a car that Singapore Customs assesses for tax purposes, based on its cost, insurance and freight. It is not the price on a used car listing. The ARF (Additional Registration Fee) is worked out as a percentage of the OMV, which is why you may see the OMV mentioned when cars are compared.

What does OMV mean on a used car?

OMV is short for Open Market Value. LTA OneMotoring describes it as assessed by Singapore Customs and based on the cost, insurance and freight costs of the vehicle. It is a figure fixed when the car was brought in, so it stays with that car for life. It is separate from the COE, which is a certificate you bid for, and separate from what a seller asks today.

Is OMV the price you pay?

No. The asking price of a used car reflects its age, mileage, condition and how much COE is left, as explained in our guide to depreciation on a car listing. Two cars with the same OMV can be priced very differently if one has years of COE left and the other does not.

How does OMV set the ARF?

The ARF is charged as a percentage of OMV, and the percentage rises in bands. For cars with COEs obtained from the second COE bidding exercise in February 2023 onwards, LTA OneMotoring lists these rates:

OMV bandARF rate
First $20,000100%
$20,001 to $40,000140%
$40,001 to $60,000190%
$60,001 to $80,000250%
$80,001 and above320%

Cars with COEs obtained before February 2023 follow an earlier schedule, so check which one applies to the car you are looking at on LTA OneMotoring (page read 4 November 2026).

Why does OMV matter when you buy a used car?

Because the ARF paid when a car was first registered feeds into the PARF rebate, a car with a higher OMV can carry a bigger rebate when it is deregistered early. We explain the schedules in what the PARF rebate is and how it is calculated. OMV also appears in the MAS rules on used-car loans, covered in how much car loan you can get for a used car. For the certificate itself, see what a COE is.

What should you ask the seller?

  • The car's OMV, if you want to compare the ARF and PARF position of two cars.
  • The registration date and COE expiry date, which matter more to the price than the OMV.
  • The yearly depreciation figure, so you can compare cars on cost per year rather than asking price.

Frequently asked questions

What does OMV stand for?

OMV stands for Open Market Value. Singapore Customs assesses it, based on the cost, insurance and freight costs of the vehicle.

Is OMV the price I pay for a used car?

No. OMV is an assessed import value used to work out tax, not the dealer's asking price. The price of a used car also reflects its age, mileage and remaining COE.

How is the ARF worked out from OMV?

The ARF is a percentage of OMV in bands. For cars with COEs from the second COE bidding exercise in February 2023 onwards, it is 100% on the first $20,000, 140% on the next $20,000, 190% on the next $20,000, 250% on the next $20,000 and 320% above $80,000, per LTA OneMotoring.
