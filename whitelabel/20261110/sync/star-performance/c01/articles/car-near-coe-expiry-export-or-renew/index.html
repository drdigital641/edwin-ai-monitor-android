Car near the end of its COE: export, scrap or renew?

When a car's COE is close to expiry you can renew the COE and keep driving, or deregister the car by exporting or scrapping it. Exporting can pay more than scrapping for a car in good condition. Compare the export offer, the rebates due on deregistration and the cost of renewing before you decide.

Option 1: renew the COE

You pay the Prevailing Quota Premium to extend the COE for 5 or 10 years. It suits a car you know well and want to keep. Check the current premium on LTA's OneMotoring site.

Option 2: export the car

An export buyer pays for the car and ships it overseas, and the car is deregistered in Singapore. Popular models in good condition often fetch more this way than through a local sale near COE expiry.

Option 3: scrap the car

The car is deregistered and scrapped. This is usually the choice for cars that are not worth exporting.

Rebates on deregistration

When a car is deregistered, LTA pays any PARF rebate (for eligible cars) and a COE rebate for the unused part of the COE to the owner. Your car's figures are on LTA's OneMotoring site.

What to gather before you compare

Have your registration date, COE expiry date, mileage and a short description of the car's condition ready. With these a dealer that handles exports can give you an offer, and you can look up your rebates and the renewal cost on LTA OneMotoring. Ask for any offer in writing so you can compare the options on the same terms.

When renewing makes sense

Renewing suits a car you know well, that is in good condition and that you plan to keep for the extra years. Think about the costs of keeping an older car, such as servicing and repairs, and compare the total with the net amount you would receive from exporting. If you plan to change cars soon anyway, exporting and buying another may be simpler.

Timing and paperwork

Deregistration is done with LTA, and any outstanding car loan has to be settled with the bank first, so start early rather than in the last days of the COE. Our guide on what you need to sell your car in Singapore lists the documents, and you can read how we handle this on our car export page or WhatsApp us your car details.

A simple way to decide

Put the three options side by side on one page: the net amount from exporting, the rebates from deregistering and scrapping, and the renewal cost plus what you expect to spend keeping the car running. The option with the best result for your own plans is usually clear once the numbers are written down. If you are unsure, WhatsApp us your car details and we will explain what an export would involve before you commit to anything.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know my car's export value?

Ask a dealer that handles exports for an offer. Send the model, registration date, COE expiry and mileage.

Can I export my car and buy another at the same time?

Yes. In August 2026 one of our customers exported his old car through us and bought a Toyota Vellfire.

Where can I check my rebates?

On LTA's OneMotoring website, using your vehicle details.
