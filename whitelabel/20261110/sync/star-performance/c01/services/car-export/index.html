Car export in Singapore: export your car near the end of its COE

When a car is close to COE expiry, an export buyer can pay more than a local one. We find the offer and help you move to your next car.

When your car is near the end of its COE, Star Performance Motor can find an export buyer for it. An exported car is deregistered in Singapore, so any rebates due to you under LTA rules are paid on deregistration. We compare the export offer with a local sale and help you upgrade.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your car. Model, registration date, COE expiry and condition, on WhatsApp.
  2. Export offer. We find an export offer and compare it with what the car would fetch locally.
  3. Deregistration. The car is deregistered with LTA when it leaves Singapore. Rebates due to you are paid under LTA rules.
  4. Your next car. If you are upgrading, we line up your next car so you are not left without one.

What should you have ready to get a car export offer?

WhatsApp us your car's model, registration date, COE expiry date and condition, with a few photos. We compare an export offer with what the car would fetch locally, so you can decide which is better before you commit.

  • Model and variant, so we can value the right car.
  • Registration date and COE expiry date, because the time left decides whether export or a local sale is likelier to pay more. See export or renew a car near COE expiry.
  • Condition, including any faults, so the offer matches the car we inspect.
  • Your next car, if you are upgrading, so we can line it up. Rebates paid on deregistration are explained in the PARF rebate guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to export or renew my COE?

It depends on the car's condition, its export value and the current COE premium. Send us the details and we will compare the options for you.

What rebates do I get when my car is exported?

When a car is deregistered, LTA pays the PARF rebate (for eligible cars) and the COE rebate for unused COE to the owner. Check your car's figures on LTA's OneMotoring site.

What do I need to tell you to get an export offer?

Your car's model, registration date, COE expiry date and condition, with a few photos, on WhatsApp 9742 7491. We compare the export offer with a local sale and help you upgrade.
