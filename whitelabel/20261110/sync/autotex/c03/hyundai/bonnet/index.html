What decides the right bonnet

The bonnet is the hinged panel over the engine bay, made of steel or aluminium depending on the car. Which one fits depends on generation and facelift; the shape of the front edge follows the headlamp and grille design.

Signs the Hyundai bonnet needs replacing

  • Dents or creases after an accident or a fallen branch.
  • Rust or bubbling paint along the front edge.
  • A latch that does not close or a bonnet that sits uneven against the fenders.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket bonnet

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
