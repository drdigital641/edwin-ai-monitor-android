About New Zen Werkz

An independent car workshop at Eunos Technolink, Kaki Bukit.

New Zen Werkz Pte Ltd is an independent car repair and servicing workshop at Eunos Technolink in Kaki Bukit, Singapore. New Zen Werkz Pte Ltd was incorporated in January 2019. The workshop is at Blk 3 Kaki Bukit Road 1, #03-04.

What we do

Routine servicing and oil changes, diagnostics, brake work and battery replacement, through to the jobs many workshops send elsewhere: taking engines and automatic gearboxes out of the car, stripping them down and rebuilding them.

On any given day the workshop floor might have a Bentley, a Mercedes-Benz E-Class and a Hummer side by side, with a Mazda in the next bay. The photos on this site are from our own workshop.

How we work

Customers know the boss as Ah Mun, and several have followed him for years. We find the cause before we fix anything. You hear what we found in plain language, with the options and a quote, before any repair starts.
