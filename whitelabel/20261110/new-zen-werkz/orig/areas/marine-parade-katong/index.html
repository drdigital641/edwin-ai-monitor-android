From Marine Parade and Katong, the route to Kaki Bukit runs north: Still Road South becomes Jalan Eunos, and leads straight to Eunos Link and our street.

It is a direct drive with no expressway, so it suits a routine service as well as a bigger repair.

Getting here from Marine Parade & Katong

4.3 kmby road
about 6 minoutside peak hours

From Marine Parade Road near Marine Parade MRT. Route and time from OpenStreetMap routing (free-flowing traffic); peak-hour traffic takes longer.

  1. From Marine Parade Road near Parkway Parade, turn left onto Still Road South and stay on it for about 1.5 km.
  2. Still Road South becomes Jalan Eunos. Carry straight on for about 1.3 km, then onto Eunos Link.
  3. Turn right onto Kaki Bukit Avenue 1, then left onto Kaki Bukit Road 1.
  4. Eunos Technolink is Blk 3. The workshop is on the third floor, unit #03-04.
