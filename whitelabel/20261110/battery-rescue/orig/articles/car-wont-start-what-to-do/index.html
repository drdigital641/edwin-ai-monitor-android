Car won't start in Singapore: what to do in the next 5 minutes

By Battery Rescue SG (Motoserv) · Last updated 2 Nov 2026 · First published 26 Oct 2026

Most no-start problems in a car park are a flat battery. Here is how to tell, and what to do.

1. Listen when you turn the key or press start

  • Rapid clicking, or nothing at all: almost always a weak or flat battery.
  • Slow, heavy cranking: the battery is weak; it may start once and fail again.
  • Normal cranking but no start: probably not the battery (fuel, ignition or a sensor). Tell us before we come down.

2. Stay safe

If you are on a road, switch on the hazard lights and wait in a safe place. Don't try to jump-start a battery that is swollen, leaking or smells of rotten eggs.

3. Get help

Call 8125 9169 (24 hours) or WhatsApp your location from our home page. Tell us your car model so we bring the right Delkor battery.
