JB Staycations offers two private houses in Johor Bahru for Singapore groups. Prima, in Taman Nusantara Prima, Gelang Patah, Iskandar Puteri, sleeps up to 12. JB Staycations

JB Staycations offers private holiday houses in Johor Bahru for families and groups, many of them travelling from Singapore. Our idea is simple: a quieter escape, just across the causeway.

We have two houses, Prima in Taman Nusantara Prima, Iskandar Puteri, and Landak Kawan Fun House in Taman Abad, Johor Bahru.

Your host is Daniel, based in Singapore and JB. He understands both cultures and speaks English, Malay and Chinese. Ask him anything on WhatsApp at +65 9006 2198. Singapore guests pay by PayNow or PayLah to the host's mobile, and a receipt is issued. Malaysian guests pay in RM by bank transfer or TNG eWallet. Bank details are shared once your booking and dates are confirmed. You can also book through the Airbnb listing.
