Why Is My ABS Light On After a Brake Pad or Tyre Change?

A new ABS light right after a brake or tyre job is usually a disturbed sensor or plug, not the pads. Here is what to check.

By The ECU Clinic · 9 Nov 2026

Short answer: an ABS light that comes on right after brake pads, discs, wheels or tyres were changed is usually caused by the work, not by the pads. The most common causes are a wheel speed sensor that was knocked or not clipped back, a wire or plug that was strained or left loose, or dirt or damage on the sensor or its ring. Have the fault code read to see which wheel is reporting. If the same code returns after the sensor and wiring are checked, the ABS unit may be at fault.

Why can the ABS light come on after a brake or tyre job?

Each wheel has a speed sensor close to the hub or brake disc, with a wire and plug that run to the ABS unit. Brake, wheel and tyre work happens right next to these parts. If a sensor is moved, a plug is not fully clicked in, or a wire is pulled, the ABS unit loses that wheel's signal, stores a code and lights the ABS warning.

What are the common causes?

  • Sensor knocked or not refitted properly: a sensor that sits too far from its ring, or is left loose, can give a weak or missing signal.
  • Wire or plug disturbed: a connector that is not fully seated, or a wire that was pinched or stretched during the job.
  • Dirt, rust or damage on the sensor or ring: brake dust, debris or a damaged sensor ring can corrupt the signal. See ABS wheel speed sensor or ABS pump.
  • Battery disconnected during the job: some cars store a code or need a check afterwards. See ABS light on, then off by itself.
  • Brake fluid or hydraulic work: some cars need the ABS unit bled with a scan tool after the system has been opened. Follow the car maker's procedure.

Is the brake pad warning light the same as the ABS light?

No. The brake pad warning is a separate light, linked to a pad wear sensor, and it can stay on if that sensor wire was not reconnected. The ABS light points to the anti-lock system. If a red BRAKE light is also on, read ABS light vs brake warning light and stop driving if it is red.

What should you do first?

  1. Tell the workshop that did the job, and ask them to check the wheel speed sensors, wires and plugs on the wheels they worked on.
  2. Have the fault code read before it is cleared. The code usually names the wheel.
  3. Look for a loose plug, a hanging wire or a sensor that is not fitted flush.
  4. If the light stays on after that, send us the car model, chassis number and fault code.

Is it safe to keep driving?

Often the normal brakes still work, but the anti-lock function may be off while the light is on, so the wheels can lock in a hard stop on a wet road. Drive gently, keep extra distance and get it checked soon. The usual causes of a steady light are listed in ABS warning light on? Common causes.

When is the ABS pump or module the cause?

When the sensors, wiring and connectors check out and the same code keeps returning, or the code points to the pump motor, a valve or the control unit. That is the part we repair. If the scan points to a sensor, a plug or a brake part, we will tell you so and you can have it fixed at a workshop. See repair, used or reconditioned unit for your options if the unit is at fault.

What should you send us?

WhatsApp us the car model, the chassis number and the fault code, with a photo of the dashboard or the scan tool screen. Tell us what work was done just before the light came on. Questions

Why did my ABS light come on after changing brake pads?
Usually because a wheel speed sensor, wire or plug was disturbed during the job, or the ABS unit stored a code. Have the code read to see which wheel reports it, and ask the workshop to check that wheel.
Can changing tyres turn on the ABS light?
It can. Removing and refitting wheels can knock a sensor, or strain a wire or plug, or leave dirt on the sensor ring. Have the fault code read before clearing it.
Is the brake pad warning light the same as the ABS light?
No. The pad warning is linked to a pad wear sensor and the ABS light to the anti-lock system. A red BRAKE light is different again: stop driving if it is on.
When does an ABS light after brake work mean the ABS unit is faulty?
When the sensors, wiring and connectors are fine and the same code keeps returning, or the code names the pump, a valve or the control unit. We repair ABS pumps and modules, so send us the car model, chassis number and fault code.
