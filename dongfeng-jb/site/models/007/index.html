Dongfeng 007 electric sedan

Sleek power in silence. Two versions: Premium with rear-wheel drive, Prime with all-wheel drive.

Electric fastback sedan

What is the Dongfeng 007?

The Dongfeng 007 is an electric fastback sedan with a 73.48 kWh LFP battery and DC charging up to 200 kW. The rear-wheel-drive Premium makes 200 kW (268 hp) with up to 650 km of range (CLTC; 530 km WLTP) and 0-100 km/h in 5.8 seconds; the dual-motor Prime makes 400 kW (544 PS), goes up to 565 km (CLTC; 389 km WLTP) and does 0-100 km/h in 3.9 seconds. Both have a 19-speaker WANOS sound system and an 8-year unlimited-mileage powertrain warranty. See it at our Johor Bahru 3S centre.

Questions

Questions about the Dongfeng 007

What is the difference between the 007 Premium and 007 Prime?

Premium has one rear motor (200 kW, 268 hp) and the longer range, up to 650 km. Prime has two motors and all-wheel drive, 400 kW (544 PS), 0-100 km/h in 3.9 seconds and up to 565 km (CLTC), plus 19-inch wheels with performance calipers and an optional white dashboard.

How fast does the Dongfeng 007 charge?

It accepts DC fast charging up to 200 kW and AC charging at 6.6 kW. Actual charging time depends on the charger, the battery temperature and how full the battery is.

What colours does the 007 come in?

Four, for both versions: Celestial White Metallic, Eclipse Grey Metallic, Obsidian Black Metallic and Starlit Silver Metallic. WhatsApp us to see which colours are in the showroom now.

What warranty does the 007 have?

8 years with unlimited mileage on the powertrain, and 6 years with unlimited mileage on the vehicle, for both Premium and Prime. Ask us for the full terms.
