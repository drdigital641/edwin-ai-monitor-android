Dongfeng Vigo electric SUV

Smart, spacious and fully electric. See it, sit in it and drive it in Johor Bahru.

Electric SUV · 5 seats

What is the Dongfeng Vigo?

The Dongfeng Vigo is a five-seat electric SUV with a 51.87 kWh LFP battery, a 120 kW (161 hp) front motor and a claimed 471 km range on the CLTC cycle. It has a 500-litre boot (1,040 litres with the rear seats folded), a split tailgate whose lower half folds out as a bench, and a 3.3 kW V2L socket to power devices from the battery. You can see it and book a test drive at our 3S centre in Taman Ekoperniagaan, Johor Bahru.

Questions

Questions about the Dongfeng Vigo

How far can the Dongfeng Vigo go on one charge?

Dongfeng quotes 471 km on the CLTC test cycle. Real driving in Johor, with highway speeds and air-con, gives less, so plan on a lower everyday figure. We can talk you through your own daily distance.

How many people and how much luggage fit in the Vigo?

It seats five. The boot holds 500 litres, or 1,040 litres with the rear seats folded, and the rear seats fold flat into a 2-metre bed. Bring your stroller or golf bag to the test drive and try it.

What colours does the Vigo come in?

Six: Mosswood Green (the launch colour), Canyon Orange, Arctic White, Sandstone Beige, Titanium Silver and Cyber Black. The cabin is light grey and black dual-tone vegan leather. WhatsApp us to see which colours are in the showroom now.

What warranty does the Vigo have?

8 years with unlimited mileage on the powertrain, and 6 years with unlimited mileage on the vehicle. Ask us for the full terms.
