Questions

Good to know

Where is the Dongfeng showroom in Johor Bahru?

Our 3S centre is at No. 28, Jalan Ekoperniagaan 3/11, Taman Ekoperniagaan, 81100 Johor Bahru. WhatsApp +60 18-762 0828 before you come so we can have the car you want ready.

Which Dongfeng models can I buy in Johor?

We sell Dongfeng passenger EVs: the Dongfeng Vigo electric SUV and the Dongfeng 007 electric sedan.

How do I book a test drive?

WhatsApp us with the model and a day and time that suit you. We confirm the slot and have the car charged and ready.

Why are prices not on the website?

Prices and offers change, so we give you the current figure directly. WhatsApp us and ask.
