Dongfeng Vigo or Dongfeng 007: which one suits you?

Updated 2026-10-20 · By the Dongfeng Johor Bahru team

Choose the Dongfeng Vigo if you want a higher seat, a 500-litre boot and an easy family SUV. Choose the Dongfeng 007 if you drive long distances or want more performance: its 73.48 kWh battery gives up to 650 km in the Premium, and the Prime does 0-100 km/h in 3.9 seconds.

Range is the manufacturer's test figure; real-world range is lower and depends on speed, load, weather and air-con. Ask us to confirm the figures for the exact car you are buying.

Who usually picks which

Families who do school runs and weekend trips around Johor often like the Vigo for its height, boot and split tailgate. Drivers who cover long distances, or simply want a quiet, fast sedan, lean to the 007. The best way to decide is to drive both back to back: WhatsApp us and we will line them up.

Questions

Which is bigger, the Vigo or the 007?

The 007 is longer (4,880 mm vs 4,306 mm) with a longer wheelbase, but the Vigo is taller (1,645 mm vs 1,465 mm) and has the bigger boot (500 L vs 452 L).
