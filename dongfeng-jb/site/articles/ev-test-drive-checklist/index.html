Your first EV test drive in Johor Bahru: what to check

Updated 2026-10-23 · By the Dongfeng Johor Bahru team

On an EV test drive, check four things: how the car pulls away and slows when you lift off (regenerative braking), how quiet the cabin is at speed, whether the screens and phone connection are easy for you, and how you would charge it at home or near work. Book a slot on WhatsApp so the car is charged and ready.

Before you come

Bring your driving licence. Think about your usual week: daily distance, where you park at night and whether you can fit a home charger. Those three answers decide which model and which charging plan suit you.

On the drive

Try a stretch at highway speed, a U-turn and parking. Feel the regenerative braking, switch drive modes, and test the camera views when reversing. Sit in the back seat too.

Questions to ask us

Ask about warranty terms, service at our 3S centre, home charger installation, delivery time and the current price. We answer on the spot or on WhatsApp.
