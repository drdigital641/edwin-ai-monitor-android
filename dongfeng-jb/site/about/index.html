About us

Who runs Dongfeng in Johor Bahru?

Dongfeng Johor Bahru is run by MyKar World Sdn. Bhd. (202501050786, 1652194-X). Our showroom at No. 28, Jalan Ekoperniagaan 3/11, Taman Ekoperniagaan, 81100 Johor Bahru, is the first Dongfeng 3S centre in Johor Bahru, handling sales, service and spare parts for Dongfeng passenger EVs.

We want Johor drivers to know there is a full Dongfeng centre right here, and we want to build the brand in the state together with dealers and partners.
