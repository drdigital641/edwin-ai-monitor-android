Plantation Edge I & II flat types and renovation ideas

2-room Flexi (Type 1)

Approx. 38 sq m (36 sq m internal)

Keep the flat open and light. A full-height wardrobe wall, a bench seat with drawers and a sliding or fluted-glass partition give a bedroom feel without closing the space. For senior owners, plan step-free floors, slip-resistant tiles and blocking in the walls for grab bars.

3-room

Approx. 69 sq m (66 sq m internal)

Treat living and dining as one room with a shoe cabinet at the entry and a slim TV and display wall. In the main bedroom a wardrobe along the longest wall frees the most floor. If the second bedroom doubles as a study, a fold-down or wall-hung desk keeps it flexible.

4-room

Approx. 93 sq m (90 sq m internal)

Most 4-room owners ask for four things: a kitchen with a tall pantry run, a main bedroom wardrobe wall, a living-room feature wall with hidden storage, and a built-in desk in a common bedroom. Use the service yard for a laundry cabinet and keep the walkway from the door to the living area clear.

Approx. 113 sq m (110 sq m internal)

The extra space suits a defined dining zone, a proper study nook and a longer feature wall that carries both display and closed storage. In the main bedroom a wardrobe wall plus a dressing counter works well. A full-height shoe and bag cabinet at the entry helps a larger household.

Layout Ideas for the 2-room Flexi, 3-room and 5-room types are in the same brochure and are not shown here. The brochure gives no count by flat type and no completion or key month, and we do not split facts between phase I and phase II. Stack orientation and views are not verified.

The 4-room plan, room by room

  1. Living and dining

    The living and dining area runs along the window side of the 4-room plan and carries the household shelter. Zone it into an entry cabinet at the door, a dining bench near the kitchen and a TV and storage wall at the living end, with the walkway kept clear from the door.

  2. Kitchen and service yard

    The kitchen is enclosed and opens to a service yard. Keep the working run continuous, put a tall fridge and pantry column at the end, and move laundry to the yard so the counter stays free for cooking.

  3. Main bedroom and bath

    The main bedroom has its own bath. A full-width bed-head wall with lit niches, a platform bed with drawers and a full-height wardrobe on the longest wall give the most storage without crowding the window.

  4. Common bedrooms

    The two common bedrooms take a platform bed and a wall-to-wall window desk with knee space well. Plan one as a child's room and one as a study or guest room, and change the desk later rather than the wardrobe.

  5. Common bath

    The common bath serves the common bedrooms and guests. For the first three years, toilet finishes in a new BTO flat cannot be removed, so plan overlays, a vanity cabinet and niches rather than hacking.

Zoning and traffic flow

  • Public zone: entry, living and dining. Keep the line from the door to the kitchen clear.
  • Service zone: kitchen and service yard, close to the dining table and the household shelter.
  • Private zone: bedrooms and baths, away from the entry so the living area works as a buffer.

Keep or hack?

  • Keep: the household shelter walls. HDB does not allow them to be hacked or drilled.
  • Check first: any other wall. We confirm with your approved plan and the permit rules before we quote.
  • Prefer joinery over hacking: a fluted wall, bench or niche changes the room without touching the structure.

The design thinking behind the four concepts

Living room: Obsidian Batten Walnut Living

A full-height almost-black battened walnut TV wall with cove lighting and a floating console, a built-in dining bench along the kitchen-side wall, and handleless storage. Window view illustrative.

Why it suits this flat. It is drawn for the living and dining with a household shelter on HDB's 4-room plan. Think of the living room as one long elevation. A tall, fluted or reeded wall breaks the glare of a flat surface, hides cabling and shelving behind doors, and lets cove light wash the wall. Warm timber and stone keep it calm, and handleless doors stop the wall from looking busy.

Kitchen: Malachite Enamel Galley

Deep malachite enamel inset-panel cabinets with aged-brass knobs, dark honed quartz and under-cabinet lighting, sized for the enclosed 4-room kitchen that opens to a service yard.

Why it suits this flat. It is drawn for the enclosed kitchen that opens to a service yard. A compact HDB kitchen works when every surface earns its place. One continuous run keeps the working triangle short, a tall pantry column takes the bulk storage off the counter, and lit niches or a lit shelf give light where the work happens. A single matte colour on the doors keeps a small room from feeling cut up.

Main bedroom: Lapis Velvet Dusk Chamber

A recessed lapis-blue velvet headboard wall with lit bedside niches, a built-in platform bed, and a full-height smoked-glass wardrobe in ebonised walnut. Evening view illustrative.

Why it suits this flat. It is drawn for the main bedroom with its own bath. A bedroom should feel lower, darker and quieter than the living room. A full-width bed-head wall with lit side niches replaces bedside tables, a built-in platform bed hides drawers, and a full-height wardrobe in one joinery set keeps lines continuous. Dim, warm light does the rest.

Common bedroom: Teal Sapele Homework Cabin

A platform bed with drawers, a sapele wardrobe with deep teal accent doors, and a wall-to-wall study desk with open knee space under the window.

Why it suits this flat. It is drawn for a common bedroom of the official 4-room plan. A shared or child's room has to change with age. Put the bed on a platform with drawers, a wall-to-wall desk under the window with open knee space, and a tall wardrobe. A single accent colour on a few doors gives the room character that can be repainted later.

Practical design rules we use

  • Light

    Use the window wall for the desk or the dining bench so natural light does the work, then add cove, niche and shelf lighting for the evening. Mixing warm light in the living room and bedrooms keeps the home restful.

  • Flow

    Keep a clear walking line from the door to the living area and from the kitchen to the dining table. Furniture that sits inside the joinery, not in front of it, keeps that line open.

  • Storage

    Plan storage along whole walls, floor to ceiling, rather than in small pieces. Shoe and bag storage at the door, a pantry column in the kitchen and a wardrobe wall in every bedroom carry most households.

  • Small-space tricks

    One material family across rooms, handleless doors, mirrors or reeded glass to bounce light, and built-in benches or platform beds with drawers all make a flat feel larger.

Checklist before key collection

  1. Confirm your unit type and keep a copy of the approved plan from HDB.
  2. List what you need built in: kitchen run, wardrobes, shoe cabinet, living-room wall, study desk.
  3. Ask for an itemised quotation and 3D drawings so you can compare scopes line by line.
  4. Choose a contractor from HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors so the permit can be applied for properly.
  5. After keys, report defects within 30 days, then book the start of works.

Planning your Plantation Edge I & II renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Report defects within 30 days of key collection, before renovation starts (HDB move-in guide). The Defects Liability Period runs 1 year from key collection.
  • Check floor and wall tiles for hollow or chipped pieces, plaster for cracks, and paint for stains.
  • Test every door, window, lock, tap, floor trap, light point and power socket.
  • Report through the QR code in your Welcome Kit or your project's Building Service Centre, with photos.

What the rules allow

  • Use a contractor listed in HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors; they can apply for the permit for you.
  • A permit is needed for works such as removing floor finishes, raising floor levels in wet areas, or hacking or altering walls.
  • For the first 3 years, toilet wall and floor finishes in a new BTO flat cannot be removed. New tiles can be overlaid.
  • General works: Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Noisy works: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. No works on Sundays or public holidays.
  • Household shelter walls must not be hacked or drilled. Windows need a BCA-approved window contractor; air-conditioners need a BCA-trained installer.

Rules change, so we check the current version for your unit before we quote. Full bto key collection renovation guide

About Plantation Edge I & II

Plantation Edge I & II is an HDB BTO project at the edge of Tengah's Plantation district, bounded by Plantation Crescent and Plantation Close. HDB offered it in the October 2023 BTO sales launch as 1,010 units of 2-room Flexi, 3-, 4- and 5-room flats in eight residential blocks of 14 to 15 storeys. Two of those blocks also have some rental flats, and one more block of rental flats brings the total to nine residential blocks. HDB's Layout Ideas give approximate floor areas of 38 and 48 sq m for the two 2-room Flexi types, 69 sq m for the 3-room, 93 sq m for the 4-room and 113 sq m for the 5-room. 2-room Flexi flats are on a 99-year lease or a short lease. The brochure says the projects are well connected to the adjacent JE2 MRT station. It does not publish a project-wide completion or key month, so we do not give one.

Plantation Edge I & II renovation FAQ

Is the completion or key date for Plantation Edge I & II confirmed?

No. HDB's official October 2023 sales brochure does not publish a project-wide completion or key-collection month, and we do not use third-party estimates. If you have an HDB key-appointment letter, use that, and check which phase it names.

When was Plantation Edge I & II launched?

It was offered in HDB's October 2023 BTO sales launch, as stated on the cover of HDB's Plantation Edge I & II sales brochure.

Do you have the Plantation Edge I & II floor plan?

Yes. HDB's official 4-room Layout Ideas plan (approx. 93 sq m, including 90 sq m internal floor area and the air-con ledge) is shown on this page, copied as-is from the October 2023 sales brochure. It is a typical type plan, not your stack. Layout Ideas for the 2-room Flexi, 3-room and 5-room types are in the same brochure. Please send your approved HDB unit plan.

How many units and which flat types are in Plantation Edge I & II?

The brochure lists 1,010 units of 2-room Flexi, 3-, 4- and 5-room flats in 8 residential blocks of 14 to 15 storeys, plus one block of rental flats. It does not give a count for each flat type, so we do not publish one.

What are the Plantation Edge I & II flat sizes?

HDB's Layout Ideas give approx. 38 sq m and 48 sq m for the two 2-room Flexi types, 69 sq m for the 3-room, 93 sq m for the 4-room and 113 sq m for the 5-room, each including internal floor area and the air-con ledge.

Which MRT station is near Plantation Edge I & II?

The official brochure says the projects are well connected to the adjacent JE2 MRT station. We do not publish walking times to that station or any other.

What renovation ideas suit a 4-room at Plantation Edge I & II?

Plan a fitted kitchen with a tall pantry or fridge run, a laundry cabinet in the service yard, a wardrobe wall in the main bedroom, a living-room storage wall and a built-in desk in a common bedroom. Keep the walkway from the door to the living area clear.

How much does Plantation Edge I & II renovation cost?

The cost depends on your unit type and scope, mainly the amount of built-in carpentry. We have not published package prices, so WhatsApp us for a quote for your unit type. We give an itemised quotation so you can see what each part costs.

Do I need an HDB renovation permit?

Yes, for the usual HDB renovation works. After keys, inspect defects, then apply for the permit with a registered contractor before carpentry is fabricated.

Can I remove the toilet tiles in a new BTO flat?

Not in the first three years. HDB's renovation guide says toilet wall and floor finishes in a new BTO flat cannot be removed in that period, but new tiles can be overlaid. Plan vanity cabinets, niches and overlays instead.

Your approved floor plan, unit type if you have it, the phase if it is named on your documents, and a key date if one is already in writing. WhatsApp us at https://wa.me/6593391185.
