FAQs

Can I just use a regular vacuum to clean up baby powder?

A standard vacuum is often insufficient because the particles are fine enough to pass through or stay trapped deep in the fabric backing. Professional high-suction extraction is required to reach these particles.

Will an air purifier help with the baby powder smell?

An air purifier may mask or temporarily filter ambient dust, but it will not remove the source of the odour trapped in your carpets or seats.
