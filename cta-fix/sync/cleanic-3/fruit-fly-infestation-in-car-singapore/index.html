Car Hygiene Tips

How to Get Rid of Fruit Fly Infestation in Your Car Singapore

Fruit fly infestations in cars are typically caused by decaying food or moisture. We provide a step-by-step guide to clearing these pests effectively.

Sep 30, 2026 · Cleanic Detailing Team · Singapore
Short answer: To get rid of fruit flies, first identify and remove the food source (e.g., dropped fruit or rotting spills). Deep clean the affected area with an automotive-safe cleaner, vacuum thoroughly, and consider professional fumigation for stubborn infestations.

Why Do I Have Fruit Flies in My Car?

Finding tiny flies in your vehicle can be frustrating. Typically, these are fruit flies attracted to fermenting organic matter or lingering moisture.

Common culprits include a piece of fruit left under the seat, a spilled sugary beverage, or even damp floor mats that trap organic debris. Even a small amount of residue can provide enough nutrients for a small colony.

  • Decaying organic waste (fruit, vegetables, food wrappers)
  • Spilled drinks (especially fruit juices or sodas)
  • High humidity levels attracting pests
  • Accumulation of dirt and organic crumbs in hard-to-reach areas

Steps to Clear the Infestation

The first step is a thorough inspection. Remove all trash and inspect under seats, inside the glove box, and within the door pockets. Once the source is removed, a deep clean is necessary to ensure no eggs or larvae remain.

  • Remove all personal belongings and trash from the car.
  • Perform a deep vacuuming of all carpeted areas and fabric seats.
  • Wipe down surfaces with a professional-grade interior cleaner.
  • If the flies persist, consider a professional fumigation treatment.

FAQs

Can I use household fly spray in my car?

It is not recommended, as household sprays can damage sensitive dashboard plastics, leather upholstery, and leave harmful chemical residues.
