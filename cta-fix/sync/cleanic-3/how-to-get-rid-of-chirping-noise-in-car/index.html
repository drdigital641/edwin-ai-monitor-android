Chirping Noise in Car? How to Locate and Remove Hidden Pests

A persistent chirping noise in your car is often caused by trapped insects like crickets or grasshoppers. This guide helps you identify the source and resolve it through professional cleaning and fumigation.

Nov 13, 2026 · Cleanic Detailing Team · Singapore
Short answer: If you hear a chirping noise in your car, it is likely a trapped insect like a cricket. To stop it, first park in a quiet area, remove all loose items, and inspect the dashboard and floorboards. If you cannot spot the source, professional pest removal and interior fumigation is the most effective solution to ensure the pest is gone.

Why Is There a Chirping Noise in My Car?

If you have ruled out mechanical issues like loose fan belts, a high-pitched chirping or clicking noise inside your cabin is almost certainly biological. Insects like crickets, grasshoppers, or even certain beetles seek refuge in dark, narrow crevices within vehicle interiors during the day.

In Singapore's humid climate, these pests often enter through open windows or door gaps, finding comfort under dashboard panels, floor mats, or inside seat rails.

  • Nocturnal insects are most active at night or in dark parking garages.
  • The sound resonates through the air vents, making it difficult to pinpoint the exact location.
  • Trapped insects may eventually die in inaccessible areas, leading to further odour issues.

Steps to Locate the Source

Before calling for help, you can attempt to locate the pest by turning off the engine and air conditioning in a quiet, parked location. Listen carefully to where the sound is loudest.

Check beneath the seats, inside the glove compartment, and along the door storage bins. However, if the sound continues to move or is coming from deep within the dashboard, it is likely hidden inside the air ducting or electrical housing.

  • Use a flashlight to check tight gaps between seat rails.
  • Remove all floor mats and inspect the footwell carpet for entry points.
  • Avoid using household insecticide sprays, which can stain upholstery and leave a sticky residue.

FAQs

Can I use insect spray to get rid of the chirping?

We do not recommend household insect sprays as they can stain leather and fabric upholstery and leave a sticky chemical residue that attracts dust.

Will the chirping noise go away on its own?

If the insect is trapped and cannot find an exit, it will eventually die. However, this may lead to an unpleasant rotting odour and can cause further issues if it dies deep within the ventilation or electrical system.
