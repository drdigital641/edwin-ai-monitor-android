Car Interior Hygiene

Mushrooms Growing in Car? Why It Happens and How to Remove Them Safely

Mushrooms in a car indicate extreme humidity and trapped moisture, usually beneath carpets or upholstery. Immediate professional intervention is required to stop the spread of spores and prevent health risks.

Oct 15, 2026 · Cleanic Detailing Team · Singapore
Short answer: Mushrooms grow in cars due to long-term water ingress, persistent dampness, and poor air circulation. To remove them, you must eliminate the moisture source, deep clean the affected area with professional-grade biocides, and perform air treatment to kill airborne spores.

Why Are Mushrooms Growing in My Car?

Seeing mushrooms growing inside your vehicle is a major red flag. Unlike minor dust or common grime, mushrooms require specific, persistent conditions to thrive. This typically happens because your car's interior has become a sustained breeding ground for fungi.

In Singapore's humid environment, even a small leak can lead to catastrophic moisture buildup if left untreated. If water pools under your floor mats or gets trapped in the foam padding under your carpets, it provides the perfect damp, dark ecosystem for fungal spores to germinate and eventually produce mushrooms.

  • Hidden leaks from sunroofs, windows, or door seals.
  • Spilled liquids that soaked deep into upholstery foam.
  • Long periods of car inactivity in humid, shaded parking.
  • Blocked air-conditioning drainage pipes leading to floor dampness.

The Hidden Risks of Fungal Infestations

Beyond the unpleasant sight and smell, mushrooms and associated moulds release spores into the cabin air. Breathing in these spores can trigger respiratory issues, allergies, or asthma, especially for vulnerable passengers like children or seniors.

Relying on store-bought sprays is often ineffective because the root system (mycelium) remains embedded deep within the car's fabrics and padding. A professional approach is necessary to ensure the source is extracted and the air quality is restored.

  • Potential for allergic reactions and respiratory irritation.
  • Risk of accelerated corrosion of hidden metal components under the carpet.
  • Persistent 'musty' odour that standard air fresheners cannot mask.
  • Damage to the structural integrity of interior foam and fabric padding.

FAQs

Are mushrooms in a car dangerous to my health?

Yes, they can be. Fungi release spores that can cause respiratory irritation, trigger asthma, and cause skin allergies. It is best to avoid driving the car until it has been professionally cleaned.

Can I just pull the mushrooms out?

No. Pulling the visible mushroom only removes the 'fruit,' not the 'roots' (mycelium) growing deep in the carpet or foam. Without professional remediation, they will simply grow back.
