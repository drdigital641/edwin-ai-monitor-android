New launch preview to booking day: the steps in Singapore

By Jayven Ang, PropNex, CEA R063222A · 16 November 2026

A new launch preview is the stage before a project opens to the public. The usual path runs from the showflat visit, to the Property Details Information (PDI), to the booking fee and Option to Purchase (OTP), and then to the Sale and Purchase Agreement (S&PA). The official rules say what the developer must show you first.

Facts below come from URA circular URA/COH/Circular-2026-03 (dated 30 October 2026, effective 31 December 2026) and the CEA page on buying a private residential property. Both read 16 November 2026.

What a new launch preview is

A preview is when a developer lets invited buyers see the showflat and the project details before the public launch. Preview dates move. On this site they come from the PropNex new-launch list, tentative as of 7 October 2026. See the launch calendar and the 2027 launches for the current list.

The steps from preview to booking day

  1. Check the target preview. Treat any date as tentative until the developer confirms it.
  2. Visit the showflat. Look at the unit layouts and the site model, and ask which unit types are open for booking.
  3. Ask for the PDI and the floor plans. The URA circular says developers must give you the PDI (prescribed Form 3) before collecting the booking fee and issuing the OTP. You must also receive scaled unit and site floor plans before the booking fee is collected.
  4. Pay the booking fee and receive the OTP. The PDI footnote says there is no fee forfeiture if you decide not to proceed after viewing the PDI.
  5. Sign the Sale and Purchase Agreement. The CEA page offers standard templates for the OTP and for the agreement for the sale and purchase of a private residential property, and separate checklists for uncompleted property.

What the official sources say

  • Projects launched for sale on or after 31 December 2026 must use the newly prescribed OTP and S&PA (URA circular).
  • Maximum maintenance charge rates must be disclosed to buyers through the PDI, the OTP and the S&PA (URA circular).
  • CEA suggests going through the checklist for your type of transaction before entering a contract, and considering a property agent if you are unfamiliar with the process (CEA).

What to ask at the showflat

  • Can I have the PDI and the scaled floor plans for the unit I am interested in?
  • Which OTP and S&PA forms apply to this project?
  • Where do I find the maximum maintenance charge rates?
  • Is the preview date confirmed, or still tentative?

Budget and loan checks come before booking day. This guide covers the process only. For the project list, see the new launches calendar.

FAQ

What is the PDI at a new launch?

The PDI is the Property Details Information, a prescribed form (Form 3) that the developer must give you before collecting the booking fee and issuing the Option to Purchase. Source: URA circular dated 30 October 2026, read 16 November 2026.

Do I lose the booking fee if I decide not to proceed after reading the PDI?

The PDI form carries this footnote: there is no fee forfeiture if a home buyer decides not to proceed with the purchase after viewing the PDI. Source: URA circular dated 30 October 2026, read 16 November 2026. Read the Option to Purchase terms before you sign.

Is the preview date of a new launch fixed?

No. On this site the dates come from the PropNex new-launch list, which is tentative as of 7 October 2026. Check the launch calendar and WhatsApp Jayven for the latest.

Which Option to Purchase and S&PA forms apply?

Per the URA circular dated 30 October 2026 (read 16 November 2026), projects launched for sale on or after 31 December 2026 must use the newly prescribed OTP and Sale and Purchase Agreement. Projects with OTPs issued before that date may keep using the prescribed forms for their remaining unsold units.
