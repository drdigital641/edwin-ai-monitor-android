Silverfish in Car: How to Detect and Remove These Pests in Singapore

Silverfish are attracted to damp, dark environments and can damage car upholstery. This guide explains how to identify an infestation and why professional fumigation is the safest solution for your vehicle.

Oct 3, 2026 · Cleanic Detailing Team · Singapore
Short answer: Silverfish in cars often thrive in humid conditions where paper, fabric, or damp materials are present. To remove them, it is essential to keep the interior dry and use professional-grade fumigation services like MIRAKN to ensure all hidden areas are treated.

Why Silverfish Choose Your Car

Silverfish are nocturnal insects that thrive in high humidity and moderate temperatures—conditions often found in parked cars in Singapore. They feed on starches, polysaccharides, and synthetic fibers, making your car upholstery, paper documents in the glove box, and even glue in the dashboard potential food sources.

When your car interior remains damp or is cluttered with paper, it creates an ideal nesting ground. Identifying the signs early is critical to preventing damage to your car's interior trim.

  • Presence of unexplained small holes in fabric or carpet
  • Yellow stains on paper items or documents
  • Small, pepper-like droppings in dark crevices
  • Musty odour indicating high moisture levels

The Risks of Ignoring Silverfish

While silverfish do not bite humans, they are destructive to car interiors. Their feeding habits can lead to unsightly damage to seat fabrics, floor mats, and headliners. In more severe cases, they can settle in hidden areas near electronics or behind door panels, making them difficult to reach without specialized tools.

Persistent humidity is often the underlying cause. If you notice persistent dampness, it is important to address both the moisture source and the insect presence.

  • Physical damage to upholstery and interior materials
  • Contamination of interior surfaces
  • Potential for the infestation to spread to other vehicle components
  • Increased difficulty of removal as population grows

FAQs

Can I get rid of silverfish with just a vacuum?

Vacuuming helps remove surface-level pests and debris, but it is rarely effective for a full silverfish infestation, as they hide deep within car seat foam and dashboard components.

Is silverfish fumigation safe for my car?

Yes, our professional fumigation methods are designed to be safe for vehicle interior materials, including leather and synthetic fabrics, when performed by trained technicians.
