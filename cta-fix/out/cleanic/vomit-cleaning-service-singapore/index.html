What to do immediately

  • Remove solid residue carefully without pushing it deeper into fabric or carpet.
  • Blot liquid with clean absorbent material; avoid aggressive rubbing.
  • Do not cover the smell with perfume before the source is cleaned.
  • Tell Cleanic exactly which area is affected and whether the liquid reached seat foam, carpet underlay or child-seat areas.

Why does the vomit smell keep coming back after cleaning?

The smell usually returns because residue is still trapped below the surface, in seat foam, seams or carpet underlay. Wiping the top layer or covering it with air freshener does not remove it. The fix is to clean the affected material at the source, let it dry properly and then check whether any odour remains.
