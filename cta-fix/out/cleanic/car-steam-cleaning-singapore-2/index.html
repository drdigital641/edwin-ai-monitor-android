The Problem: Why Your Car Interior Needs Professional Cleaning

Your car interior faces constant attack from Singapore's heat and humidity. Sweat from seats, food spills, pet odours and moisture from the tropical climate create the perfect breeding ground for bacteria, mould spores and allergens.

Regular vacuuming and air fresheners only mask the problem on the surface. They don't reach the bacteria trapped deep inside seat foam, under carpets, or lurking in air-conditioning vents. Over weeks, your car begins to smell musty, stale or unpleasant.

Even worse, these hidden organisms can trigger allergies, asthma and respiratory issues, especially for children and elderly passengers. A musty or stale car also signals poor maintenance when you're trying to sell or impress clients.

You need professional cleaning that reaches where regular cleaning cannot.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does car steam cleaning take?

Most interior jobs take about 1 to 2 hours on-site, depending on your car size and condition. Colin will give you an exact time estimate when you call.

Will steam damage my car's electronics or leather seats?

No. Professional steam cleaning is calibrated to clean fabrics and leather without water pooling. Modern cars are built to handle moisture. Colin has treated hundreds of vehicles, from economy cars to luxury vehicles, with zero damage.

Can steam cleaning remove stubborn stains and odours?

Yes. High-temperature steam penetrates deep into carpet and fabric fibres to dissolve dried stains and eliminate odour-causing bacteria. However, extremely old or set-in stains may require pre-treatment with mild enzymatic cleaners.

What's the difference between car steam cleaning and regular car washing?

Regular car washing cleans the exterior and surface-level interiors. Car steam cleaning targets deep interiors, such as seat foam, carpets, vents and hidden crevices, where bacteria and odours actually live. Steam is the only method that truly sanitises and deodorises.
