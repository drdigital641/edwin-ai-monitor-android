Many Google Reviews but No Website in Singapore: Is That Enough?

Strong Google reviews are a great start, but no website means you are missing enquiries from Google Search. Here is what you are leaving on the table.

Published 25 October 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

You have built something real. Customers love your work, they leave five-star Google reviews, and your Google Business Profile looks busy. But you have no website. Every time someone searches your business name or your trade on Google, they see your reviews, your phone number and your location on the map. Is that enough to keep the enquiries coming?

This is one of the most common situations for Singapore small businesses in 2026: great social proof on Google, strong word of mouth, but no dedicated website. The short answer is that reviews alone are powerful, but they leave a gap that a website fills.

Many Google Reviews but No Website in Singapore: Is It Enough?

Google reviews build trust, but they do not help you appear in Google Search results beyond your Maps listing. A website gives Google and AI tools like ChatGPT hundreds of pages to read and rank. Without one, every customer who searches 'car detailing Toa Payoh' or 'sofa cleaning near me Singapore' finds the businesses with websites above your Maps pin.

Key takeaways

  • Google reviews show up in Maps and your Business Profile, not in the main Google Search results.
  • A website gives you a second, larger surface for Google to rank: one page per service, per question, per neighbourhood.
  • AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity read websites, not Google Business Profiles, when recommending businesses.
  • Your Google Business Profile can be suspended or edited by anyone; your website is yours.
  • Reviews and a website compound each other: a site that shows your reviews is more convincing than either alone.
  • Getting a website does not have to mean a big upfront cost or a long agency contract.

What Does a Google Business Profile Actually Do?

A Google Business Profile (GBP) is the card that appears on Google Maps and in the panel on the right side of search results when someone searches your business name. It shows your address, opening hours, photos and reviews.

It is excellent at one thing: helping customers who already know your name or are searching 'near me' on Maps to find your location and check your credibility. Your five-star reviews live here and they absolutely matter.

What it does not do is compete in the main Google Search results. When someone types 'HDB aircon servicing Jurong' or 'home baker customised cake Singapore' into Google, the ranked pages that appear are mostly websites. A GBP alone rarely appears in those organic results.

What a Website Adds That Reviews Cannot

Think of it this way. Your GBP is a shopfront sign. Your website is the shop itself.

Here is what a website gives you that a Google Business Profile alone cannot:

  1. A page for every service. A workshop with ten services can have ten pages, each targeting a different search. Your GBP has one listing.
  2. Pages that answer customer questions. When someone searches 'how much does sofa cleaning cost Singapore', a page on your site can rank for that. Your GBP cannot.
  3. AI visibility. Tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity read websites when they recommend local businesses. They cannot read a GBP in the same way. If you want to be recommended by AI search, you need a website with clear, structured content. Read more about how to get your business recommended by ChatGPT in Singapore.
  4. Trust when customers land on your name. If a customer googles your business name and finds only a Maps card with no website link, some will hesitate. A website confirms you are a real, established operation.
  5. You own it. Your GBP can be suspended by Google or have details changed by a third party. Your website belongs to you.

What Happens When a Competitor Has a Website and You Do Not?

Imagine two sofa cleaning businesses in Tampines. Both have 80 five-star Google reviews. One has a website with pages for 'fabric sofa cleaning Tampines', 'leather sofa stain removal Singapore' and 'how long does sofa cleaning take'. The other has only a GBP.

When a Tampines resident searches any of those phrases, Google serves the website. The business with no website never appears. The reviews do not help because the customer never reached the GBP.

This is the gap. Reviews build trust after a customer finds you. A website helps customers find you in the first place.

What About Home-Based Businesses and Small Trades?

Customers in Singapore still search Google before WhatsApp-ing a stranger. A simple site with your services, your service areas, your prices and your reviews embedded gives them the confidence to contact you.

How to Turn Your Google Reviews into a Website That Compounds Your Visibility

Here is a practical path from 'many reviews, no website' to 'reviews plus a website that ranks':

  1. Register a domain in your name. Something like yourbusinessname.com.sg or .com. Usually S$15-30 a year.
  2. Build a page for each core service. Not one page that lists everything. One page per service, each targeting the search a customer would type.
  3. Display your Google reviews on the site. A widget or embedded link connects the trust you have already built to the new site.
  4. Set up structured data. This is code that tells Google and AI tools your business name, address, opening hours and services clearly. It is the technical layer that makes your site readable to search engines.
  5. Connect Google Search Console. This lets you see which searches bring visitors to your site and track progress over time.
  6. Keep publishing. A new article every couple of weeks, each targeting a real customer question, compounds your visibility over months.

That is what a well-managed site does. You can read about how getting found on Google Search beyond Maps works in practice.

Is My Google Business Profile Already Optimised?

Before adding a website, it is worth checking your GBP is complete. A strong profile and a website work together. Check: every service listed, photos added, opening hours correct, questions answered, and your business description using the words customers actually search. Frequently asked questions

Do Google reviews help me rank in Google Search, or only in Maps?

Google reviews primarily boost your visibility and credibility within Google Maps and your Business Profile panel. They are a ranking factor for Maps results, but they do not directly earn you a position in the main Google Search results. A website with relevant pages is what competes in those organic search results.

Can I add my Google reviews to my website?

Yes. You can embed a Google reviews widget on your site so visitors see your star rating and written reviews without leaving the page. This connects the trust you have built on Google to your website and can increase the number of people who contact you.

What if someone tries to contact me through my Google Business Profile and I have no website link?

They can still call you or get directions via the profile. However, some customers hesitate when there is no website link, as they use it to confirm the business is legitimate and to learn more before committing. A website removes that friction.

Will a new website hurt my Google Business Profile ranking?

No. Adding a website to your GBP listing by linking it in your profile details generally strengthens your overall Google presence. It gives Google more information about your business and can improve how confidently it displays your listing.

How quickly can I get a website if I have none right now?

With Page One's Get Found plan, the site is usually live within seven days. The domain is registered in your name, the site is built for you at no extra charge, and it is connected to Google Search Console and your Google Business Profile from day one.

Is it safe to rely only on Google for my business visibility?

Relying on any single platform carries risk. Google can suspend a Business Profile, and policy changes can affect visibility. A website you own is a stable asset: even if your GBP were suspended, customers could still find and contact you through your site.

What searches will a website help me appear for that my GBP cannot?

Informational and service-specific searches such as 'how much does X cost in Singapore', 'best Y near Tampines' or 'Y service for HDB' are typically won by websites with dedicated pages, not by GBP listings. Each page you add to your site can target a different search your customers use.
