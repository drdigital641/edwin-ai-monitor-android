Does a Google Business Profile Need a Website in Singapore?

A Google Business Profile works without a website, but it only reaches people on Maps. Here is what a site adds and how to get one without a big bill.

Published 27 October 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

You have set up your Google Business Profile. It shows your address, your opening hours, your photos and maybe a few good reviews. Then a thought lands: does a Google Business Profile need a website in Singapore, or is the profile enough on its own?

The short answer is that a profile does not need a website to exist, but it works much harder with one. This guide explains what the profile can do alone, where it stops, and how to add a website without a big bill.

Does a Google Business Profile need a website in Singapore?

No. Google lets you create and verify a Business Profile without a website, and the website field is optional. But a profile alone only helps people who already know your name or who browse Google Maps. It does not give Google a set of pages to show in normal search results, and it gives AI tools very little to read.

Key takeaways

  • A Google Business Profile is free and works without a website. Set it up first.
  • The profile mainly serves Google Maps and the panel that appears when someone searches your business name.
  • A website lets you appear in normal Google Search for specific services, questions and neighbourhoods.
  • AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity read web pages, so a website gives them something to quote.
  • You own a website. A profile can be edited, suspended or removed by Google.
  • Adding a website later is easy: paste its address into your profile.

What can a Google Business Profile do without a website?

A Google Business Profile is a free listing that shows your business on Google Maps and Google Search. It is very good at a few jobs:

  • Showing your address, phone number and opening hours to people nearby.
  • Collecting Google reviews, which build trust quickly.
  • Letting people tap to call, get directions or message you.
  • Appearing for ‘near me’ searches on Google Maps when you are close to the searcher.

For a shop in an HDB estate or a mall unit, that is already valuable. Many Singapore owners get calls and walk-ins from the profile alone. If you have not finished yours, start with our guide on how to appear on Google Maps for a small business in Singapore.

What can a profile not do without a website?

A profile is one card. It cannot carry a page for every service, answer detailed questions or explain your prices. Here is what is missing:

  1. No pages for specific searches. A person searching ‘how much does sofa cleaning cost in Singapore’ or ‘aircon servicing for HDB flat’ is usually shown web pages, not profiles.
  2. Little for AI tools to read. AI assistants build answers from pages they can read. A profile gives them an address and a star rating, not an explanation of what you do.
  3. No home you control. Google can edit details, merge listings or suspend a profile. A website stays yours.
  4. A weaker first impression. Some customers tap the website link to check that a business is real. A blank link makes them hesitate.
  5. No way to measure. A website connected to Google Search Console shows which searches bring visitors. A profile gives you far less detail.

If you already have strong reviews and no site, our article on many Google reviews but no website in Singapore explains what a website adds on top of them.

Does having a website help my Google Business Profile?

Yes, in a practical way. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence. A website helps with relevance and prominence because it gives Google more information about what you offer and where you work. No one can promise a ranking, but a clear website and a complete profile give Google more to work with than a profile alone.

A website also gives the profile a useful link to send people to: your services, your prices, your service areas and a WhatsApp button.

Can I use my Facebook or Instagram page instead of a website?

You can put a Facebook or Instagram link in the website field, but a social page is not the same as a website. Social pages are built for scrolling, not for being found by a new customer searching Google. Posts disappear down the feed and accounts can be locked or banned. We cover the Facebook case in do I need a website if I have a Facebook page in Singapore.

Google also retired its old free website builder for Business Profiles in 2024, so there is no longer a one-click site that comes with the profile.

How do I add a website to my Google Business Profile?

It takes a few minutes once the site exists. Follow these steps:

  1. Get a domain name in your own name. A domain usually costs S$15 to S$30 a year.
  2. Build or order a simple site with a page for each main service, your contact details and your opening hours.
  3. Sign in to your Google Business Profile, choose Edit profile, then Business information.
  4. Paste your website address into the website field and save.
  5. Make sure the name, address and phone number on the site match your profile exactly.
  6. Connect Google Search Console to the site so you can see which searches bring visitors.

Frequently asked questions

Can I create a Google Business Profile without a website?

Yes. The website field is optional, and you can verify and use your profile without one. Many Singapore shops and home-based sellers start this way, then add a website later when they want to be found in normal Google Search.

Will adding a website make my profile rank higher on Google Maps?

Nobody can promise a ranking. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and a clear website gives Google more information about what you offer. Treat it as a way to give Google more to work with, not a guarantee.

Is a Facebook or Instagram page good enough for the website field?

You can enter a social page address, but it is a weaker choice. Social pages are built for scrolling, are hard for new customers to find through Google, and can be locked or banned. A website you own is more stable and easier for search engines to read.

Do I need a website if I already have many Google reviews?

Reviews build trust once a customer finds you, but they do not create pages for Google Search or AI tools to read. A website helps new customers find you in the first place, and it can show your reviews to people who land on it.
