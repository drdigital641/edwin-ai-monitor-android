Should a Carousell Seller in Singapore Get a Website?

Already selling on Carousell with good reviews? Here's what a website adds and how to get one without a big budget.

Published 9 November 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

You have good reviews on Carousell, steady sales and repeat buyers who message you on the app. Business is moving. So why are people asking about a website for a Carousell seller in Singapore?

Because Carousell is a closed garden. Buyers who already have the app can find you. Buyers on Google cannot.

Should a Carousell Seller in Singapore Get a Website?

Yes, if you want buyers to find you through Google Search. Carousell keeps your listings and reviews inside its own platform. A website puts your business on Google, where people search without knowing about you first. Even a simple five-page site can bring in enquiries you would never see on Carousell alone.

Key Takeaways

  • Carousell reviews and sales history do not appear in Google Search results.
  • A website gives you a permanent address on Google that you own and control.
  • Buyers on Google are actively searching for what you sell, not just browsing a feed.
  • You can display your Carousell reviews and ratings on your own website to build trust.
  • A website protects you if Carousell changes its rules or removes your account.
  • Getting started does not have to cost a large lump sum.

What Does Carousell Not Give You?

Carousell is a marketplace. It owns the platform, the search, and the relationship with the buyer. You rent space there.

Here is what you do not get on Carousell:

  • A page that appears when someone types your business name on Google.
  • A listing on Google Maps for buyers nearby searching 'preloved kids clothes Tampines' or 'handmade earrings Singapore'.
  • A place to tell your full story, show your process, and explain why buyers should trust you over other sellers.
  • An enquiry channel that works even if Carousell suspends your account.

If your account is flagged or the platform changes its algorithm, your income drops overnight. A website is yours. That risk is real. The article on what happens if your social media account gets banned explains the same problem for Instagram sellers and the logic applies equally here.

What Does a Website Add for a Carousell Seller?

A website is not a replacement for Carousell. It works alongside it. Think of it as a second shop front, one that Google can find and recommend.

Google Search traffic. When someone types 'vintage furniture seller Singapore' or 'wholesale phone accessories Singapore', Google shows websites, not Carousell profiles. A website with the right pages can appear for those searches.

Google Maps. A connected Google Business Profile puts you on the map for buyers searching near you. A website makes that profile stronger and more credible.

Your own gallery and reviews. You can copy your best Carousell reviews onto your website and display photos of your stock, packing quality, or past orders. Buyers from Google see your track record before they even message you.

A WhatsApp button. Most Singapore buyers prefer to message rather than fill in a form. A website can open a WhatsApp chat with one tap, so the enquiry feels familiar.

If you are curious about moving customers from social platforms and marketplaces to Google, this article on getting customers from Google instead of only social media in Singapore is worth reading.

Is It Worth It If I Only Sell Occasionally?

If selling is a side income and you are happy with your current volume, a website may not be urgent. But if you want to grow, charge more, or build a recognisable brand, a website is the foundation.

Sellers who move from occasional to full-time often hit a ceiling on Carousell. The platform is competitive and visibility depends on how recently you listed. Google Search works differently: a well-written page about what you sell can bring in enquiries weeks or months after you first published it.

Frequently asked questions

Can my Carousell reviews appear on Google Search?

Not directly. Carousell keeps its review data inside its own platform. If you want buyers on Google to see your track record, you need a website where you can display those reviews yourself, alongside photos and details about what you sell.

Will a website replace my Carousell shop?

No, and it does not need to. Most sellers run both side by side. Carousell reaches buyers already on the app. A website reaches buyers on Google Search and Google Maps who may never open Carousell at all.

How long does it take to appear on Google after getting a website?

Google needs time to crawl, index and rank new pages. For most small businesses, early rankings for specific searches can appear within a few weeks, with growth continuing over months as more pages are indexed. No provider can guarantee a timeline.

Do I need a registered business to get a website in Singapore?

No. Many Carousell sellers operate as individuals or sole traders. You can register a domain and build a website without an ACRA business registration, though having one can add credibility and allows you to set up a Google Business Profile.
