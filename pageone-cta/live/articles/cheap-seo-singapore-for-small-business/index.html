What Small Business Owners Are Really Paying For

When a small business owner in Singapore asks what SEO costs, they usually mean: what do I have to pay to show up when someone nearby searches for what I sell?

That is a completely reasonable question. Most small Singapore businesses — a sofa cleaning service in Tampines, a property agent in Jurong, a car workshop in Ang Mo Kio — do not need a 20-page content strategy or a domain authority campaign. They need three things:

  1. A fast, trustworthy website or landing page that Google can read.
  2. A completed and active Google Business Profile so they appear on Maps.
  3. Their business details structured in a way that Google and AI assistants (like ChatGPT and Gemini) can understand.

Whatever your budget, these foundations are what the money should buy. Low-cost SEO done badly means buying links from overseas directories, stuffing keywords into hidden text, or paying a monthly fee for "reports" that show no real work.

What No SEO Budget Can Buy

Being honest here matters. No SEO provider — low-cost or expensive — can guarantee you a specific Google ranking or a mention by ChatGPT. Google's algorithm changes constantly, and AI assistants choose sources based on their own criteria.

What a good plan at any price can do is give you the correct foundations so that Google can rank you, Maps can show you, and AI assistants can reference you. The work is documented so you always know what has been done.

If any provider promises you "Page 1 in 30 days" or "guaranteed AI mentions", treat that as a red flag, however much they charge.
