Do I Need a Website If I Have a Facebook Page in Singapore?

A Facebook page is a great start, but it will not get you found on Google Search. Here is what Singapore small-business owners need to know.

Published 14 October 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

If you have just started a business in Singapore and someone says 'just set up a Facebook page for now', you are not alone. Thousands of home-based bakers, sole proprietors, tuition teachers and heartland shop owners do the same thing. But if a customer searches Google for what you sell — and most customers in Singapore do exactly that — your Facebook page is unlikely to appear. So do you need a website if you already have a Facebook page in Singapore? The short answer is yes, and this guide explains why, what your real options are and how to do it without spending a fortune.

Direct answer

A Facebook page and a website serve different purposes. Facebook is where your existing followers see your posts; Google Search and Google Maps are where new customers who have never heard of you look for a business like yours. Without a website and a Google Business Profile, you are invisible to those searches. You need both.

Key takeaways

  • Facebook reaches people who already follow you; Google reaches people actively searching for what you sell.
  • Google will not rank a Facebook page the same way it ranks a dedicated business website.
  • A website on your own domain gives you an asset you own — Facebook can change its algorithm or restrict your reach at any time.
  • A Google Business Profile (the listing that appears on Google Maps and 'near me' searches) works best when it links to a real website.
  • Getting a website does not have to mean a large upfront bill; affordable monthly options exist in Singapore from as little as S$100 a month.
  • AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity are now answering business searches too — they read websites, not Facebook pages.

What a Facebook page can and cannot do for your business

Facebook is genuinely useful. You can post photos, collect reviews, run paid ads and message customers directly. For a brand-new business with zero budget, a Facebook page is a reasonable first step.

But here is the reality for Singapore small businesses:

  • Facebook's organic reach (how many people see your posts for free) has been declining for years. Without boosting posts with ad spend, most Facebook pages reach only a small slice of their own followers.
  • When someone opens Google and types 'aircon servicing Tampines' or 'home baker Jurong', Facebook pages rarely appear in those results. Google is showing websites and Google Maps listings, not social profiles.
  • You do not own your Facebook page. Meta can restrict it, change the rules or reduce your reach with no notice.
  • AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity, which are now answering shopping and service searches, pull information from websites and indexed pages — not from Facebook.

So Facebook is a channel for engaging people who already know you. A website is how strangers find you for the first time.

Why Google Search and Google Maps matter more than you think

In Singapore, a huge share of purchase decisions start with a Google search. Someone's aircon is leaking — they search 'aircon repair near me'. A parent wants maths tuition — they search 'primary maths tuition Clementi'. A couple needs a sofa cleaned — they search 'sofa cleaning service Singapore'.

Those searches surface websites and Google Maps listings. A business with no website and no Google Business Profile simply does not exist in those results.

If you want to understand how Google Maps rankings work for small businesses in Singapore, this guide on how to appear on Google Maps for small business Singapore walks through the steps clearly.

What your website actually needs to do (it does not have to be complicated)

Many business owners imagine a website means months of work and a big developer bill. It does not. A small Singapore business needs a website that:

  1. Loads fast on a mobile phone (most Singapore customers will visit on their phone)
  2. Shows your services, location and how to contact you (WhatsApp is fine)
  3. Has a proper domain name that you own (e.g. yourbusiness.com.sg)
  4. Is connected to Google Search Console so Google can find and measure it
  5. Has a Google Business Profile linked to it with your address, hours and photos
  6. Has basic structured data so Google and AI assistants understand who you are

That is it for most heartland or home-based businesses. You do not need animations, a booking system or an online shop to rank on Google.

Frequently asked questions

Will my Facebook page show up on Google Search in Singapore?

Occasionally a Facebook page appears in Google results, but it rarely ranks well for the specific local searches your customers use — like 'aircon servicing Yishun' or 'home baker Bukit Timah'. Google prioritises websites and Google Maps listings over social profiles for those searches.

Can I use Facebook as my only online presence for a Singapore home-based business?

You can start there, but you will miss most of the customers who search Google without knowing your name. A website on your own domain, paired with a Google Business Profile, is what gets you found by strangers who are ready to buy.

What is a Google Business Profile and do I need one?

A Google Business Profile is the listing that appears on Google Maps and in the panel on the right side of search results. It shows your address, opening hours, photos and reviews. Yes, you need one — and it works best when it links to a proper website.

Will AI assistants like ChatGPT recommend my business if I only have a Facebook page?

No. AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity read indexed websites, structured data and tools like llms.txt to find businesses. They do not pull listings from Facebook pages, so without a website you are invisible to AI-powered searches.
