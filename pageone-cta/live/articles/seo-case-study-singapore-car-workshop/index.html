What the website has (you can check it yourself)

No secret tricks were involved. Everything below is visible on the live site, and it covers the fundamentals that Google and AI assistants reward:

1. A listing on Google Maps

A Google Business Profile with the workshop's Ang Mo Kio address and opening hours is what puts a workshop in front of drivers searching "car workshop near me". For any heartland business, this is one of the most important steps.

2. A fast, mobile-ready website on the workshop's own domain

Most drivers search on their phones. The workshop site loads over a secure SSL connection and has a WhatsApp chat button, so customers can enquire in one tap — exactly how Singapore customers prefer to communicate.

3. Structured data and an llms.txt file

Structured data tells Google precisely what the business is: a car workshop in Ang Mo Kio, with its services, opening hours and common questions. The llms.txt file gives AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity a plain summary of the business, so they can describe it correctly when drivers ask for recommendations.

4. A page for each service and question

The sitemap lists 130 pages: services such as car aircon repair, plus answers to the questions drivers actually search. Each page is another way to be found, which is how one workshop shows up on page one for thousands of different searches.

5. Measured in Google Search Console

Search Console shows which searches bring drivers to the site, so effort goes where it pays off. It is also where every number in this case study comes from.

Why car workshops in Singapore need SEO in 2025–2026

Most car owners in Singapore search before they call. They type "car aircon service Ang Mo Kio", "tyre change near me" or "car battery replacement Singapore" into Google. If your workshop does not appear on page one — ideally in the Maps pack — those customers call your competitor.

Word-of-mouth referrals still matter, but they are no longer enough on their own. Even a customer referred by a friend will Google your workshop name before showing up. A blank or broken Google Business Profile kills trust immediately.

For heartland workshops, the Google Maps listing and a simple, fast website are the two highest-return investments you can make.
