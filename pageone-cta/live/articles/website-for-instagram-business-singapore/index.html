Website for Instagram Business Singapore: Do You Actually Need One?

You have an active Instagram business in Singapore. But do you need a website too? Here is an honest answer, and what a website actually adds.

Published 15 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

If you run an Instagram business in Singapore, you already know how to get attention. You post, you reply to DMs, you show your work. Customers find you, trust you and buy from you, all without a website. So why would you need one?

This article gives you an honest answer. Not a sales pitch. Just a clear look at what Instagram alone cannot do for your business, and what a website actually adds.

Do You Need a Website for an Instagram Business in Singapore?

Yes, most Instagram businesses in Singapore benefit from having a website, even a simple one. Instagram controls your visibility, can restrict your account without warning, and cannot be found by people searching on Google. A website gives you a permanent address on the internet that you own, that Google can index, and that works even if Instagram changes its rules tomorrow.

Key Takeaways

  • Instagram is a great sales tool, but Google is where most Singaporeans start searching for a product or service.
  • If your Instagram account is restricted or banned, your whole business disappears online overnight.
  • A website lets you appear in Google Search and Google Maps, not just on Instagram feeds.
  • You can display your best reviews, photos and services on a page you fully control.
  • A basic website for an Instagram business in Singapore does not need to cost a lot or take months to build.
  • Page One Singapore builds and manages a website from S$100 a month, with no setup fee and no contract.

What Instagram Cannot Do for Your Business

Instagram is excellent at discovery within the platform. Someone scrolls, sees your reel, taps your profile, sends a DM. That journey works well, until it does not.

Here is what Instagram cannot do:

  1. Show up in Google Search. When someone types 'home baker Tampines' or 'custom cake Singapore' into Google, your Instagram profile rarely appears in the results. Google does not index Instagram posts reliably. The businesses that appear are the ones with websites.
  2. Protect you from account loss. Instagram can restrict, shadowban or permanently disable an account. It happens to legitimate businesses every week. If your only online presence is Instagram, you lose everything: your content, your followers, your enquiry channel. Read more about this risk in our article on what happens if your Instagram business account gets banned.
  3. Show up in Google Maps searches. 'Near me' searches on Google Maps pull from Google Business Profiles, not Instagram. Without a website and a verified Google Business Profile, you are invisible to that entire group of customers.
  4. Build long-term trust with certain buyers. Many Singapore customers, especially those spending more money, check for a website before they commit. No website can feel informal or temporary to them, even if your Instagram looks professional.
  5. Let you own your audience. Instagram owns your followers. If the platform shuts down tomorrow, you have no list, no contact and no way to reach them.

What a Website Adds to an Instagram Business

A website does not replace Instagram. It works alongside it. Here is what it adds:

Google visibility. A properly set up website with a Google Business Profile means you appear when people search for what you sell, even if they have never heard of you. This is customers finding you, not the other way around.

A home base you control. Your website stays up regardless of what Instagram does. Your photos, prices, story and contact details live there permanently.

Google reviews on your own page. You can display your Google reviews directly on your website, so new visitors see social proof the moment they land. We cover exactly how to do that in this guide: how to show Google reviews on your business website.

Better enquiries from Google. Many Instagram business owners find that once they have a website, they start getting enquiries from Google Search as well as Instagram DMs. These tend to be higher intent, because the customer was actively searching for what you sell.

If you are curious how much of a difference this makes, see the real Search Console results from businesses Page One manages.

Is a Website Worth It If I Only Sell Through Instagram DMs?

Yes, especially if you want to grow. Instagram DMs work well at a small scale. But Google Search brings customers who are ready to buy right now and are specifically looking for your type of business. Instagram brings people who are browsing and may or may not be ready.

A website also lets you rank for neighbourhood searches like 'florist Buona Vista' or 'handmade earrings Singapore'. These are searches with real purchase intent, and Instagram profiles almost never appear for them.
