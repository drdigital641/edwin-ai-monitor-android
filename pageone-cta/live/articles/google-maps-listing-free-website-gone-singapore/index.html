Google Maps Listing Free Website Gone? What a Singapore Owner Should Do Next

Your free Google listing website stopped working? Here is what still works, how to replace it with a site you own, and what it costs in Singapore.

Published 17 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

You open your Google Maps listing and tap the website button. Nothing loads, or it shows an error. If you once made a free site from your Google listing, with an address ending in business.site, this is probably why. This guide is for a Singapore owner whose Google Maps listing free business.site website is gone, and who wants to know what to do about it without paying for something complicated.

What do you do when your Google Maps listing free website is gone?

Google retired the free websites that could be made from a Business Profile, so a business.site address no longer works as a real website. Your listing itself is not deleted. What you need now is a website on your own domain, then a link from your listing to it.

Key takeaways

  • Your Google listing and your free website were two different things. The free website was the part that was retired.
  • Your reviews, photos and Maps pin stay on your Business Profile. You do not start from zero.
  • Customers who tap the website button now find nothing, so the gap costs you enquiries every week.
  • A website on your own domain does not depend on Google or on any social platform.
  • You can set up a simple replacement in a few steps, and it does not need many pages.
  • Page One builds and manages a replacement for S$100 a month, with no setup fee and no contract.

Why did your free Google website stop working?

Google decided to stop offering the simple sites that could be created from a Business Profile. These sites often had an address that ended in business.site, and many Singapore owners built one in a few minutes because it was free and fast.

That is the catch with a free website that lives inside someone else's product. You use it, but you do not own it. When the product changes, your page goes with it. A domain registered in your name does not have this problem, because you hold it and you decide where it points.

Does your Google Business Profile still work without the website?

Yes. Your Business Profile is the listing that shows your name, address, opening hours, photos and reviews on Google Search and Google Maps. It works without a website. Many shops and home businesses in Singapore run on a profile alone.

What you lose is the website button leading anywhere useful. Customers who want more than a phone number, such as your services, your area or your prices, have nowhere to go. Our guide on whether a Google Business Profile needs a website explains why a profile and a website work best together.

If you have a lot of good reviews already, that is an asset worth protecting. Our article on having many Google reviews but no website covers how a site makes those reviews work harder.

What are your options for a replacement website?

You have three realistic options: remove the website link, build a new site yourself, or have one built and managed for you. Each suits a different owner.

  1. Leave the link empty. Costs nothing, but customers have nowhere to learn more about you, and Google has fewer pages to show for your business.
  2. Build it yourself. You choose a builder, register a domain, write the pages, set up hosting and security, and keep it updated. It costs your time.
  3. Have it built and managed. A provider does the setup and the upkeep, and you send changes by message.

How do you replace a business.site website step by step?

Replacing it is a short list of steps, and the order matters. Here is the route that works for most one-person and small shop businesses:

  1. Write down your facts. Business name, address, phone number, opening hours, services and the areas you serve. Use them exactly the same everywhere.
  2. Register a domain in your own name. Keep it short and close to your business name. It usually costs S$15 to S$30 a year.
  3. Build a small site. A home page, a services section, your contact details and a short FAQ are enough to start.
  4. Add your WhatsApp number. Most Singapore customers prefer to message a small business first.
  5. Put the new address on your Business Profile. Replace the old website link so the button works again.
  6. Connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. These tell the search engines that your new site exists. Bing feeds ChatGPT search.
  7. Keep adding pages. Answer the questions your customers ask you, one page at a time.

Why does the website link on your Maps listing matter?

The website link tells Google and your customers where to learn more about you. A working link gives people a reason to trust the listing and gives Google a page it can read about your business.

An empty or broken link does the opposite. A customer comparing three shops on Maps may simply pick the one with a working site. This is especially true in Singapore, where people check a listing quickly on their phone, often while walking past an HDB shop row.

What should the new website show?

Keep the first version small and accurate:

  • Your business name, what you do and the areas you serve.
  • A short list of services with a plain description of each.
  • Your WhatsApp number and opening hours.
  • A few real photos of your work or shopfront.
  • Three to six common questions with honest answers.
  • Any reviews you already have on Google, quoted accurately.

Do not publish claims you cannot support. Plain facts build more trust than big promises.

Frequently asked questions

Is my Google Business Profile deleted if my business.site website stops working?

No. Your listing, reviews, photos and Maps pin stay on your Business Profile. Only the free website made from it was retired, so you need a new website address to put in the website field.

Can I keep using my business.site address?

A business.site address no longer works as a real website, so customers who tap it find nothing. It is better to register a domain in your own name and point your listing to a site you control.

How long does a replacement website take?

Doing it yourself depends on how much time you have for the domain, pages, hosting and Google setup. With Page One, a website is usually live within 7 days of sign-up, built free on your own domain.

Do I lose my Google reviews if I get a new website?

No. Reviews belong to your Business Profile, not to the website. A new website can quote them accurately and link back to your listing, so they work harder for you.

What does a domain cost in Singapore?

Do I need a new website if customers already WhatsApp me?

Many customers do message first, and you should keep that. A website adds a second route for people who find your listing but do not know you yet, and it gives the website button a working place to go.
