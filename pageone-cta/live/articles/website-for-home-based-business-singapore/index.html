Do You Need a Website for Your Home Based Business in Singapore?

Setting up a website for your home based business in Singapore does not have to be complicated or expensive. Here is what you actually need to get started.

Published 20 October 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

Starting a home based business in Singapore is exciting. You have sorted your HDB Home Office scheme approval or rented a space, figured out what you are selling, and told your first few customers. Then someone asks: do you have a website? If your answer is 'not yet', this guide is for you. It explains what a website for a home based business in Singapore actually needs, what it costs, and how to avoid paying for things you do not need right now.

Do You Need a Website for Your Home Based Business in Singapore?

Yes, even if you already have an Instagram page or a WhatsApp catalogue. A website gives you an address on Google that you own. Customers searching 'home baking Singapore' or 'handmade gifts Singapore' on Google will not find your Instagram unless it ranks, and Instagram controls that. A website you own is a permanent asset that no platform can take away.

Key Takeaways

  • A website for your home based business in Singapore gives you a searchable address on Google that you own and control.
  • Instagram and Facebook help but they cannot replace a website for Google Search and Google Maps visibility.
  • Your first website does not need to be large: a clear homepage, a services or products page, and a contact page are enough to start.
  • A Google Business Profile linked to your website helps local customers find you on Google Maps.
  • You can get a website built and managed for S$100 a month with no setup fee and no contract, or do it yourself at a higher time cost.
  • Domain registration is usually S$15-30 a year and is always registered in your name.

What Does a First Website for a Home Based Business Actually Need?

Keep it simple. Customers visiting your site want to know what you do, whether you serve their area, and how to contact you. Here is what your first website should have:

  1. A clear homepage that states what you do and who you serve, for example 'Home-baked cakes delivered across Tampines and Pasir Ris'.
  2. A services or products page with descriptions and, if you are comfortable, prices.
  3. A contact page with your WhatsApp number and the areas you serve.
  4. Your business name, address (if you use one) and contact details in a consistent format, so Google can read them.
  5. An SSL padlock (the 'https' that tells browsers your site is secure). This is now expected by both Google and customers.
  6. A mobile-friendly layout. Most Singaporeans browse on their phones.

You do not need a booking system, an online shop, or dozens of pages on day one. Those come later when you know what customers actually ask.

Instagram and Facebook Are Not Enough on Their Own

Many home based business owners in Singapore start on Instagram or Carousell and do well there. So why bother with a website at all?

Social platforms are great for discovery and community. But Google Search and Google Maps are where people go when they have decided they want something and are ready to buy. A customer typing 'custom birthday cake Jurong' into Google is ready to order. If you have no website and no Google Business Profile, you are invisible to that search.

There is also the ownership question. If Instagram changes its algorithm or your account is flagged, your business page can disappear overnight. A website on your own domain is yours. Read more on this in Do I Need a Website If I Have a Facebook Page?

How to Get Your Home Based Business Found on Google

Having a website is the first step. Getting found on Google takes a bit more:

  1. Register a domain name in your own name. Something like yourbusinessname.com.sg or .com. This usually costs S$15-30 a year.
  2. Build and publish your website with clear, honest descriptions of what you do.
  3. Set up a Google Business Profile. This is free and puts your business on Google Maps. Even a home based business can have one.
  4. Connect Google Search Console so you can see which searches bring people to your site.
  5. Make sure your site loads fast and works on mobile. Google checks this before deciding where to rank you.
  6. Add new pages over time that answer questions customers are already searching for.

Do I Need a Fancy Domain Name?

No. A domain name is simply your website address, for example yourbakery.com. It does not need to be clever, just clear and easy to spell. A .com or .com.sg works fine for most Singapore home based businesses. Avoid hyphens if you can, as customers mistype them. Register it in your own name so you own it outright, not your web designer or agency.

Is My Current Instagram Page Enough to Start, While I Think About a Website?

It is a reasonable starting point, but do not wait too long. Every month without a website is a month Google is not indexing your business. Domains and websites take time to build authority. The sooner your site is live and connected to Google Search Console, the sooner that clock starts. You can always improve the site later. Frequently asked questions

Can a home based business in Singapore have a website?

Yes, absolutely. There is no rule against a home based business having a website. Your website is an independent online presence that points to your contact details and services, separate from your physical address. Many successful home based businesses in Singapore use a website as their main way of being found on Google.

What pages does a first website for a home based business need?

You need a homepage, a page describing your services or products, and a contact page. Those three pages are enough to get started and get indexed by Google. You can add more pages, like an FAQ or individual service pages, as your business grows.

Does a home based business in Singapore qualify for a Google Business Profile?

Yes. Google allows home based businesses to create a Business Profile and hide the home address if they prefer. You can list the areas you serve instead. This puts your business on Google Maps for local searches, which is valuable even without a shopfront.

How long does it take for a new website to appear on Google?

Google can index a new site within days if it is submitted through Google Search Console, but ranking competitively usually takes weeks to months. The more pages you have covering real customer searches, and the better the technical setup, the faster it tends to happen. There is no guarantee of a specific timeline.

Do I need to keep updating my website or is once enough?

A static website that never changes is less likely to rank well over time. Google prefers sites that publish fresh, useful content regularly. Adding new pages that answer questions your customers actually ask is one of the most practical ways to grow your visibility without paying for ads.

What is the difference between a domain and hosting?

A domain is your website address, for example yourbakery.com. Hosting is the server where your website files live so people can visit it. You need both: a domain to own your address and hosting to keep the site online. They are usually billed separately and come from different providers unless you use a managed plan.
