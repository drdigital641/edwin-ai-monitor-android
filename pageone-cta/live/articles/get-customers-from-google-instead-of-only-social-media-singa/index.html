How to Get Customers from Google Instead of Only Social Media in Singapore

If all your enquiries come from Instagram or Facebook, you are invisible to anyone who searches Google. Here is how to change that.

Published 7 November 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

If your enquiries come through Instagram DMs, Facebook messages or TikTok comments, your business is doing something right. But every day, thousands of Singaporeans open Google and type 'aircon servicing near me' or 'sofa cleaning Tampines' or 'car workshop Ang Mo Kio'. If you are not on Google, those customers never find you, no matter how many followers you have.

This article explains how to get customers from Google instead of only social media in Singapore, step by step, without needing a big budget.

How to Get Customers from Google Instead of Only Social Media in Singapore

The short answer: you need two things on Google, a Google Business Profile for Maps results, and a website for Search results. Social media keeps your existing followers engaged. Google Search and Google Maps reach people who have never heard of you and are ready to buy right now. Setting up both correctly is the fastest way to open a second channel of enquiries.

Key Takeaways

  • Social media reaches people who already follow you. Google Search reaches people who are actively looking for what you sell.
  • A Google Business Profile gets you into Google Maps results for free.
  • A website gets you into Google Search results beyond Maps, reaching far more searches.
  • Customers who find you on Google are usually ready to buy, not just browsing.
  • You do not need to pick one or the other. Run both. They work together.
  • Getting set up correctly from the start saves time and money compared to fixing mistakes later.

Why Social Media Alone Has a Ceiling

Social media is powerful for building a community and keeping regulars engaged. The problem is reach. When someone in Bedok types 'home cleaning service near me' into Google, they are not scrolling Instagram. They want a result now. If you are not on Google, that customer goes to whoever is.

There is also a risk. If your Instagram or Facebook account is restricted or suspended, your enquiries stop overnight. We have covered what that feels like in what happens if your Instagram business account gets banned in Singapore. It happens more than people expect.

Google is a different kind of channel. It is pull traffic. The customer comes to you because they searched for exactly what you offer.

Step 1: Set Up and Complete Your Google Business Profile

A Google Business Profile is the listing that appears in Google Maps and in the box on the right side of a Google Search result. It is free.

Here is what to do:

  1. Go to google.com/business and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Enter your business name, category and address (or service area if you are home-based).
  3. Verify your listing. Google will send a postcard or offer a video call verification.
  4. Fill in every field: opening hours, phone number, website, services, photos and a business description.
  5. Ask your happy customers to leave a Google review. Businesses with reviews rank higher and get more clicks.

A complete profile is the single fastest thing you can do to appear on Google Maps for local searches. See our full guide on how to appear on Google Maps for small business Singapore for a deeper walkthrough.

Step 2: Get a Website So Google Can Send You Search Traffic

Google Maps gets you visible for 'near me' searches. But a website gets you visible for every other search: 'best sofa cleaning Singapore', 'how much does aircon servicing cost', 'car workshop that does COE renewal'. Those searches happen on Google Search, not Maps.

Without a website, you are only on Maps. With a website, you can appear for hundreds or thousands of different searches, each one a potential customer.

A common question is whether a Google Business Profile needs a website. The honest answer is: a profile alone limits you. A website multiplies what Google can find.

If you do not have a website yet, Page One Singapore builds one for you at no extra cost as part of a managed plan, on your own domain, usually live within seven days.

Step 3: Make Sure Google Can Read Your Website

Having a website is not enough on its own. Google needs to be able to find it, read it and understand what your business does. This is called technical SEO, and most small business websites have problems here without the owner knowing.

The basics Google needs:

  • A fast, mobile-friendly site (most searches in Singapore happen on phones).
  • An SSL padlock (the 'https' in your address bar).
  • A sitemap so Google knows all your pages exist.
  • A page title and description for every page that describes what that page is about.
  • Structured data, which is code that tells Google your business name, address, opening hours and services in a format it can read directly.

Step 4: Create Pages That Match What People Search For

Google ranks pages, not websites. If you want to appear when someone searches 'aircon servicing Jurong', you need a page that is specifically about aircon servicing in Jurong. One general homepage is not enough.

This is why content matters. Each page or article on your website can target a different search. Over time, those pages add up. Frequently asked questions

Can I get on Google without a website in Singapore?

You can appear on Google Maps by setting up a Google Business Profile, which is free. However, without a website you will miss out on Google Search results, which cover far more searches than Maps alone. A website is what lets you appear when someone searches for a specific service rather than just a location.

How long does it take to start getting customers from Google?

Google Maps results can appear within days of verifying your Google Business Profile. Google Search results from a new website typically take a few weeks to a few months to build up, depending on how competitive your industry is. Publishing relevant pages consistently and keeping your site technically sound speeds things up.

Do I have to stop using Instagram or Facebook if I start focusing on Google?

No. Social media and Google Search do different jobs and work well together. Social media keeps your existing followers engaged, while Google brings in new customers who have never heard of you. Running both at the same time is better than choosing one.
