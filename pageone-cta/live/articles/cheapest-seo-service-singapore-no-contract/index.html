Why long SEO contracts put small businesses off

Many agencies quote hundreds of dollars a month and ask for six- to twelve-month contracts. For a small business turning over S$10,000–S$20,000 a month, that commitment feels enormous — especially when you have no proof it will work for your specific trade and neighbourhood.

So most owners skip SEO and rely on word of mouth, Carousell listings or a Facebook page. That works to a point. But when a customer in Tampines searches 'sofa cleaning near me' on Google or asks ChatGPT 'who does good aircon servicing in Yishun', your business simply does not appear if you have no SEO presence.

The good news: you do not have to choose between a long contract and doing nothing.

How month-to-month SEO works

No minimum contract means:

  • You pay month to month.
  • You can cancel anytime over WhatsApp.
  • The service runs to the end of the month you have paid for.
  • Your domain stays in your name; you keep it if you cancel.
  • If Page One built your website, you can buy the site files when you leave.

This matters because SEO takes time to show results. Most legitimate SEO will take three to six months before you see meaningful traffic growth (see How Long Does SEO Take for a New Small Business in Singapore? for a realistic breakdown). A no-contract arrangement means you are choosing to stay because the results justify it — not because you are locked in.

What to check before you sign up

Ask these questions of any provider, including us, before you pay for the first month:

  1. Is there a setup fee? Some monthly plans add a one-off charge at the start. Page One has none.
  2. How do you cancel, and when does the service stop? It should be a simple message, with the service running to the end of the month you paid for.
  3. Who owns the domain? It should be registered in your name and paid by you, so you keep it whatever happens.
  4. What happens to the website if you leave? Find out whether you can keep or buy the site files.
  5. Are Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools in your name? Then you can check your own search data at any time, with or without the provider.
  6. What exactly is done every month? Ask for specifics: how many articles, how often the technical audit runs, and how your Google Business Profile is managed.
  7. Does anyone promise a ranking? Google's own guide, Do you need an SEO?, says plainly: "No one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google." Treat a guarantee as a warning sign.

Short answer (checked 20 October 2026): a month-to-month plan is worth paying for only if it covers the same four basics as a long-term plan: a Google Business Profile in your name, fresh content that answers real searches, regular technical checks with fixes, and Google Search Console plus Bing Webmaster Tools set up in your name.
