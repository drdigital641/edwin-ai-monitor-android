How to Get a New Business on Google Singapore (Complete Beginner's Guide)

Starting a new business in Singapore? Here is exactly how to get found on Google Search, Google Maps and AI assistants from day one.

Published 18 October 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026 · By Page One Singapore

Starting a new business in Singapore is exciting — and one of the first questions most owners ask is: how do I get my business on Google? Whether you run a home-based baking business, a new renovation firm or a heartland tuition centre, showing up on Google Search, Google Maps and AI assistants like ChatGPT from day one gives you a real head start over competitors who leave it until later.

How Do I Get My New Business on Google in Singapore?

To get a new business on Google in Singapore, you need three things in place: a website (or at minimum a web page) that Google can read, a verified Google Business Profile so you appear on Maps, and regular fresh content so Google and AI assistants keep finding you. Done right, most new businesses can appear in Google results within days of going live.

Key Takeaways

  • A Google Business Profile (free) is the fastest way to appear on Google Maps and local 'near me' searches.
  • You need at least one web page that Google can crawl — a social media page alone is not enough.
  • Structured data, a sitemap and Google Search Console tell Google exactly what your business does and where.
  • Fresh content (articles answering what your customers search for) helps Google and AI assistants recommend you over time.
  • New businesses in Singapore can get everything set up — website, Google Business Profile, Search Console — for as little as S$100 a month with no setup fee and no contract.
  • Nobody can guarantee rankings; the goal is to do every technical step correctly and publish content consistently.

What Is a Google Business Profile and Why Does Your New Business Need One?

A Google Business Profile (GBP) is the free listing that appears on Google Maps and in the panel on the right side of Google Search when someone looks up your business name or searches for a service 'near me'. Without one, your business will not appear on Google Maps at all.

For a new Singapore business, setting up and fully completing your GBP — business name, address or service area, category, opening hours, phone number and photos — is the single fastest win you can make on Google. It is free and takes a few hours to verify.

For a detailed walkthrough of the Maps side of things, read how to appear on Google Maps for small business Singapore.

Step-by-Step: How to Get Your New Business Found on Google Singapore

  1. Register a domain name in your own name (usually S$15–30 a year). Use a .com.sg or .sg for local trust, or a .com if you prefer.
  2. Build a website — at minimum, a single page that clearly states what you do, where you are, and how customers can contact you. Google cannot rank a business that has no web presence. If you are not sure where to start, how to start a website for a new business in Singapore covers the options plainly.
  3. Set up your Google Business Profile at business.google.com. Choose your primary category carefully — it is one of the strongest signals Google uses for Maps rankings.
  4. Connect Google Search Console (free). This tells Google your site exists, submits your sitemap, and lets you measure clicks and positions from day one.
  5. Add structured data to your web page. Structured data is code that tells Google (and AI assistants) your business name, address, phone number, opening hours and services in a format machines can read reliably.
  6. Add an llms.txt file and sitemap so AI assistants like ChatGPT and Perplexity can discover and understand your business. Read more about this in how to get my business recommended by ChatGPT Singapore.
  7. Publish content regularly — articles that answer the questions your customers actually type into Google. Each article is a new door into your site.

What Is On-Page SEO, and What Should a New Business Check?

On-page SEO is the work done on your own web pages so Google and AI assistants can understand them: a clear page title and description, one main heading, a plain answer near the top, descriptive image alt text, links between your own pages, and structured data that matches what visitors see. You control all of it, and it costs nothing but care.

Use this on-page checklist for every page. It follows Google's SEO Starter Guide (checked 19 October 2026) and the checks we run on client sites every day:

  1. A unique page title that names the service and the area, for example 'Aircon Servicing in Tampines | Your Business Name'. Keep it short enough to read in full.
  2. A meta description of one or two sentences that sums up the page. Google may show it under your title, or write its own from the page.
  3. One main heading (H1) that says what the page is about.
  4. The answer first: say what you do, where and for whom in the first lines.
  5. Descriptive image alt text, such as 'technician cleaning an aircon unit in an HDB flat'.
  6. Links between your own pages with words that describe where they lead, not 'click here'.
  7. Structured data that matches the page: your business name, address, phone number and hours exactly as visitors see them.
  8. A fast, mobile-friendly page, because most Singapore customers search on their phones.

Do I Need a Website, or Can I Just Use Instagram or Facebook?

Social media pages do appear in Google Search, but they rank poorly for service searches compared to a real website. If someone searches 'sofa cleaning Tampines' or 'aircon servicing Yishun', Google almost always shows websites — not Facebook pages — in the top results.

More importantly, you do not own your Facebook or Instagram page. If Meta changes its algorithm or your account is restricted, your entire online presence disappears overnight. A website on your own domain is an asset you control.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay Google to appear in Google Search results?

No. Google Search and Google Maps results are free and unpaid — they are called organic results. You only pay if you run Google Ads, which is separate. Getting your business into the free results is what SEO is about.

How do I verify my Google Business Profile for a new Singapore business?

After you create your profile at business.google.com, Google will ask you to verify your business, usually by postcard sent to your address, a phone call or video verification. The process typically takes a few days to two weeks. Once verified, your listing goes live on Google Maps.

Can a brand-new domain rank on Google quickly?

Yes, for specific long-tail searches with less competition, a well-set-up new site can appear in results within weeks. Broader, more competitive searches take longer. The key is correct technical setup from day one — fast hosting, SSL, structured data, a sitemap and Google Search Console connected.

What is structured data and why does a new business need it?

Structured data is code added to your web page that tells Google and AI assistants your business name, address, phone, opening hours and services in a format machines read reliably. Without it, Google has to guess these details. For a new business, getting this right from the start avoids months of confusion in search results.

What is llms.txt and does my new business website need one?

llms.txt is a file you place on your website that tells AI assistants — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity — what your business does and how to describe it accurately. As AI-powered search grows, having this file in place from day one means AI assistants can recommend your business sooner rather than later.
