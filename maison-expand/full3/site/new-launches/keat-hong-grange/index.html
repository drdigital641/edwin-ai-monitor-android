Keat Hong Grange flat types and renovation ideas

2-room Flexi

Type 1: 38 flats, approx. 40 sq m. Type 2: 156 flats, approx. 49 sq m

Keep the flat open and light. A full-height wardrobe wall, a bench seat with drawers and a sliding or fluted-glass partition give a bedroom feel without closing the space. For senior owners, plan step-free floors, slip-resistant tiles and blocking in the walls for grab bars.

The 4-room plan, room by room

  1. Living and dining

    The living and dining area is long and runs along the window wall, with the household shelter on the plan. Zone it into three: an entry cabinet at the door, a dining bench near the kitchen, and a TV and storage wall at the far living end, with the walkway kept along the inner side.

  2. Kitchen and service yard

    The kitchen is an enclosed galley with two facing runs: the hob on the household-shelter side and the sink on the service-yard side. Keep tall units at the ends so the aisle stays open, and put laundry in the yard.

  3. Main bedroom and bath

    The main bedroom sits at the corner and has its own bath. A recessed headboard with lit niches and a full-height wardrobe along the wall toward the bath keep the window wall free.

  4. Common bedrooms

    The two common bedrooms sit along the bedroom window wall. A platform bed with drawers and a wall-to-wall desk along the window suit a child's room and a study.

  5. Common bath

    The flats were built with the PPVC method and, per the brochure, come with floor finishes, internal doors and sanitary fittings. For the first three years, toilet finishes cannot be removed, so plan overlays, vanity cabinets and niches rather than hacking.

Zoning and traffic flow

  • Public zone: entry, living and dining. Keep the line from the door to the kitchen clear.
  • Service zone: kitchen and service yard, close to the dining table and the household shelter.
  • Private zone: bedrooms and baths, away from the entry so the living area works as a buffer.

Keep or hack?

  • Keep: the household shelter walls. HDB does not allow them to be hacked or drilled.
  • Check first: any other wall. We confirm with your approved plan and the permit rules before we quote.
  • Prefer joinery over hacking: a fluted wall, bench or niche changes the room without touching the structure.

The design thinking behind the four concepts

Living room: Russet Lattice Teak Living

A full-height russet-stained lattice teak TV wall with cove lighting and a floating console, a built-in dining bench along the kitchen-side wall, and handleless storage. Window view illustrative.

Why it suits this flat. It is drawn for the long living and dining along the window wall, with the household shelter. Think of the living room as one long elevation. A tall, fluted or reeded wall breaks the glare of a flat surface, hides cabling and shelving behind doors, and lets cove light wash the wall. Warm timber and stone keep it calm, and handleless doors stop the wall from looking busy.

Kitchen: Buttercream Beadboard Galley

A compact enclosed galley: hob on the household-shelter side, sink on the service-yard side, buttercream beadboard cabinets, champagne-brass hardware, pale stone counters and a frosted service-yard door at the end of the aisle.

Why it suits this flat. It is drawn for the enclosed two-run galley that opens to a service yard. A compact HDB kitchen works when every surface earns its place. One continuous run keeps the working triangle short, a tall pantry column takes the bulk storage off the counter, and lit niches or a lit shelf give light where the work happens. A single matte colour on the doors keeps a small room from feeling cut up.

Main bedroom: Cognac Hide Cove Suite

A recessed cognac-hide headboard with lit bedside niches, a built-in platform bed, and a full-height wardrobe in dark cognac-stained veneer with bronze-tinted glass doors, along the wall toward the ensuite. Window view illustrative.

Why it suits this flat. It is drawn for the corner main bedroom with its own bath. A bedroom should feel lower, darker and quieter than the living room. A full-width bed-head wall with lit side niches replaces bedside tables, a built-in platform bed hides drawers, and a full-height wardrobe in one joinery set keeps lines continuous. Dim, warm light does the rest.

Common bedroom: Pistachio Sycamore Day Desk

A built-in platform bed with drawers, a full-height sycamore wardrobe with pistachio lacquer doors, and a wall-to-wall study desk along the window with open knee space and a lit floating shelf. Window view illustrative.

Why it suits this flat. It is drawn for a common bedroom on the bedroom window wall. A shared or child's room has to change with age. Put the bed on a platform with drawers, a wall-to-wall desk under the window with open knee space, and a tall wardrobe. A single accent colour on a few doors gives the room character that can be repainted later.

Practical design rules we use

  • Light

    Use the window wall for the desk or the dining bench so natural light does the work, then add cove, niche and shelf lighting for the evening. Mixing warm light in the living room and bedrooms keeps the home restful.

  • Flow

    Keep a clear walking line from the door to the living area and from the kitchen to the dining table. Furniture that sits inside the joinery, not in front of it, keeps that line open.

  • Storage

    Plan storage along whole walls, floor to ceiling, rather than in small pieces. Shoe and bag storage at the door, a pantry column in the kitchen and a wardrobe wall in every bedroom carry most households.

  • Small-space tricks

    One material family across rooms, handleless doors, mirrors or reeded glass to bounce light, and built-in benches or platform beds with drawers all make a flat feel larger.

Checklist before key collection

  1. Confirm your unit type and keep a copy of the approved plan from HDB.
  2. List what you need built in: kitchen run, wardrobes, shoe cabinet, living-room wall, study desk.
  3. Ask for an itemised quotation and 3D drawings so you can compare scopes line by line.
  4. Choose a contractor from HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors so the permit can be applied for properly.
  5. After keys, report defects within 30 days, then book the start of works.

Planning your Keat Hong Grange renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Report defects within 30 days of key collection, before renovation starts (HDB move-in guide). The Defects Liability Period runs 1 year from key collection.
  • Check floor and wall tiles for hollow or chipped pieces, plaster for cracks, and paint for stains.
  • Test every door, window, lock, tap, floor trap, light point and power socket.
  • Report through the QR code in your Welcome Kit or your project's Building Service Centre, with photos.

What the rules allow

  • Use a contractor listed in HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors; they can apply for the permit for you.
  • A permit is needed for works such as removing floor finishes, raising floor levels in wet areas, or hacking or altering walls.
  • For the first 3 years, toilet wall and floor finishes in a new BTO flat cannot be removed. New tiles can be overlaid.
  • General works: Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Noisy works: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. No works on Sundays or public holidays.
  • Household shelter walls must not be hacked or drilled. Windows need a BCA-approved window contractor; air-conditioners need a BCA-trained installer.

About Keat Hong Grange

Keat Hong Grange is an HDB BTO project in Choa Chu Kang, bounded by Choa Chu Kang Avenue 7, Choa Chu Kang Grove and Keat Hong Link. HDB's sales brochure and project data list 987 flats in five residential blocks of 19 to 23 storeys: 38 2-room Flexi Type 1, 156 2-room Flexi Type 2, 96 3-room, 374 4-room and 323 5-room flats. Your HDB notification gives your date. As of 16 November 2026, HDB's project data lists an estimated completion of 4Q 2026 and an estimated delivery possession date from 30 June 2028. These are HDB estimates, not a confirmed key appointment.

What should I prioritise in a 2-room Flexi flat?

Safety and storage: slip-resistant floors, good lighting, easy-to-reach cabinets and a layout that leaves clear walking space. HDB lists 38 Type 1 (approx. 40 sq m) and 156 Type 2 (approx. 49 sq m) 2-room Flexi flats here.

How do I beat the renovation queue?

Finalise your layout and quotation before keys, report defects in the first days, and let your contractor apply for the HDB permit straight away. With 987 flats in five blocks, contractors book up quickly.
