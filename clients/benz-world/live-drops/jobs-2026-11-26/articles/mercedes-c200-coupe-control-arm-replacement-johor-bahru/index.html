Mercedes C 200 coupe control arm replacement in Johor Bahru: a job from our workshop

A black Mercedes-Benz C 200 coupe came into our Permas Jaya workshop for front suspension and brake work. Here is what the job looked like, the signs that control arms are wearing out, and what the work involves.

26 November 2026

A Mercedes C 200 control arm replacement in Johor Bahru starts with checking the front suspension properly, because a worn arm or bushing can feel like a tyre, steering or brake problem. At Benz World in Permas Jaya, Masai, this black C 200 coupe had its front control arms and brake pads done in the same visit.

This black C 200 coupe came in with the bonnet up and the front wheels off. In the photos four front control arms are laid out on the red floor in front of the bumper. Two are clean and two are visibly dusty and worn, and a set of brake pads sits in its box beside them.

We share jobs like this so Mercedes owners in Johor Bahru can see what is actually replaced on their car, not just the bill at the end.

What a control arm does

Control arms link the wheel hub to the body of the car. Rubber bushings at one end and a ball joint at the other let the wheel move up and down while keeping it in line. When the bushings crack or the ball joint wears, the wheel can move where it should not, and you feel it through the steering and the tyres.

Signs your Mercedes control arms are wearing out

  • Knocking or clunking over bumps, speed breakers or rough roads, usually from the front.
  • Loose or vague steering. The car wanders or needs small corrections to stay straight.
  • Vibration through the steering wheel, especially when braking.
  • Uneven tyre wear on the inner or outer edge of the front tyres.
  • The car pulls to one side even after a wheel alignment.

These signs can also come from tyres, brakes or other suspension parts. A check on the lift shows which part is worn before anything is replaced.

What the job involves

  1. Check on the lift. We look at the bushings, ball joints and other front suspension parts for play and damage.
  2. Replace the worn arms. The worn control arms come off and the replacements go on, tightened to the right setting.
  3. Check the brakes while the wheels are off. With the wheels already off, it is a good time to inspect the brake pads. On this car the brake pads were done in the same visit.
  4. Wheel alignment. New suspension parts change the wheel angles, so an alignment afterwards is usually needed. See our article on wheel alignment vs balancing.

FAQ

How do I know if my Mercedes control arms are worn?

A knock over bumps, loose or wandering steering, vibration when braking and uneven wear on the front tyres are the usual signs. A check on the lift confirms which part is worn.

Do I need a wheel alignment after replacing control arms?

Usually yes. New suspension parts change the wheel angles, so an alignment afterwards keeps the car driving straight and protects the tyres.

Can I drive with a worn control arm?

It is not a good idea to leave it. A worn arm or ball joint affects steering and braking and gets worse over time. Get the car checked soon.
