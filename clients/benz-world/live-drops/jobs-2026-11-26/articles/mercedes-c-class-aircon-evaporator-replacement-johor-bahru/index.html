Mercedes C-Class aircon evaporator replacement in Johor Bahru: a job from our workshop

A black Mercedes-Benz C-Class came to Benz World with an aircon problem. The job covered the evaporator, the expansion valve and the cabin air filters. Here is what the parts look like and when your Mercedes aircon may need the same work.

26 November 2026

A Mercedes C-Class aircon evaporator replacement in Johor Bahru is a bigger job than a gas top-up, because the evaporator sits deep inside the car. At Benz World in Permas Jaya, Masai, this black C-Class had its evaporator, expansion valve and cabin air filters done in one visit.

The job: a black Mercedes-Benz C-Class

In the photo two aircon evaporators stand in front of the bumper, with a set of cabin air filters and an expansion valve still in its box. These are the parts inside the car that cool the air and keep it clean before it reaches the cabin.

What the evaporator, expansion valve and cabin filter do

  • Evaporator. A small radiator inside the car, usually behind the dashboard. Cold refrigerant runs through it and the blower pushes cabin air across it, which is where the air gets cold.
  • Expansion valve. Controls how much refrigerant goes into the evaporator. If it sticks, the aircon can blow warm or freeze up.
  • Cabin air filter. Cleans the air before it passes the evaporator. A clogged filter cuts airflow and lets dirt build up on the evaporator.

Signs your Mercedes evaporator needs a check

  • The aircon will not stay cold, even soon after a regas, which can point to a leak at the evaporator.
  • A musty or sour smell from the vents when the aircon is switched on.
  • Weak airflow even with the fan on high.
  • Water on the front footwell carpet, which can come from a blocked evaporator drain.

For more causes of weak cooling, see our article on why car aircon is not cold, or our Mercedes GLC 200 aircon repair job.

What the job involves

  1. Find the cause. We check the system pressures and look for leaks before deciding the evaporator is the problem.
  2. Reach the evaporator. On many cars the evaporator sits inside the heater box behind the dashboard, so the job takes more time than parts under the bonnet.
  3. Replace the parts that need it. On this C-Class the work covered the evaporator, the expansion valve and the cabin air filters.
  4. Recharge and test. The system is vacuumed and refilled, then we check that the air comes out cold and stays cold.

FAQ

How do I know if my Mercedes aircon evaporator is leaking?

The aircon loses its cold again soon after a regas, and a pressure and leak check finds no leak under the bonnet. A workshop check confirms whether the leak is at the evaporator.

Why does my Mercedes aircon smell musty?

Dirt and moisture can build up on the evaporator and in a clogged cabin air filter. Replacing the filter and cleaning or replacing the evaporator usually deals with it.

How often should I change the cabin air filter?

Check your Mercedes service schedule. In dusty or humid conditions it is worth checking the filter at every service.
