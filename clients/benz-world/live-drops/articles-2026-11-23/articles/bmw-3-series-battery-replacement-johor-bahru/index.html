BMW 3 Series battery replacement in Johor Bahru: a job from our workshop

A white BMW 3 Series (F30) came into our Permas Jaya workshop for a battery replacement. Here is what the job looked like, the signs that a BMW battery is getting weak, and what a proper battery job on a modern BMW involves.

23 November 2026

We share jobs like this so BMW owners in Johor Bahru can see what happens to their car in the bay, not just the bill at the end.

Signs your BMW battery is getting weak

  • Slow cranking. The engine turns over more slowly than usual, especially in the morning.
  • Battery or charging warnings. A message on the dash about the battery or the charging system.
  • Start-stop stops working. On many BMW models the engine start-stop function is switched off when the battery is low.
  • Electrical features acting up. Windows, the screen or the central locking behave oddly after the car has been parked.
  • The car will not start. Clicking, or no response at all, when you press start.

None of these proves the battery is the problem on its own. A weak battery and a charging fault can look the same, which is why we check before we replace.

What a BMW battery job involves

  1. Check first. We test the battery and look at the charging, so a new battery is not fitted to hide a different fault. Our computer diagnostics scan reads any stored fault codes.
  2. Fit the right battery. Modern BMWs are particular about the battery type and size. We fit a battery that matches what the car needs.
  3. Register the new battery where the car needs it. On many BMW models the new battery is registered with the car's computer so the charging system treats it as new. Whether this step is needed depends on the model and the battery.
  4. Final checks. We start the car, check the charging and clear any old warnings before handing it back.

FAQ

How do I know if my BMW needs a new battery?

Slow starting, a battery or charging warning on the dash, start-stop that no longer works, or a car that will not start are the usual signs. A test at the workshop confirms whether it is the battery or the charging system.

Does a new BMW battery need to be registered?

On many BMW models, yes. The new battery is registered with the car's computer so the charging system treats it as new. Whether it is needed depends on the model and the battery fitted.

Can I fit any battery to my BMW 3 Series?

It is best to fit a battery that matches the type and size the car was designed for. The wrong battery can cause charging problems and warnings later.
