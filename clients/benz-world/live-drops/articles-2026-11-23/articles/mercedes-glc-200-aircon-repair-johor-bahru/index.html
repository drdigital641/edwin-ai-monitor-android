Mercedes GLC 200 aircon repair in Johor Bahru: a job from our workshop

A silver Mercedes-Benz GLC 200 (X253) came into our Permas Jaya workshop with an aircon problem. The repair covered the condenser and the compressor, followed by a refrigerant recharge. Here is what the job looked like and when your own Mercedes aircon needs a check.

A Mercedes GLC 200 aircon repair in Johor Bahru starts with finding why the air is not cold, not with simply topping up gas. At Benz World in Permas Jaya, Masai, this GLC 200 needed work on the condenser and the compressor, then a refrigerant recharge, so the system was fixed at the cause.

The job: a silver Mercedes-Benz GLC 200 (X253)

This silver GLC 200, the X253 generation, came in for an aircon repair. The job covered the condenser and the compressor, followed by a refrigerant recharge. In the photos two condensers stand in front of the bumper, two compressors sit on cardboard on the floor, and the aircon recharge machine with its gauges is ready beside the car.

Signs your Mercedes aircon needs a check

  • The air is not cold. The fan blows, but the air stays warm or only cools a little.
  • It cools, then stops. The air is cold at first and turns warm after a while, often in traffic.
  • New noises. A rattle, grind or whine when the aircon is switched on.
  • Gas that does not last. The aircon was recharged recently but is weak again, which usually points to a leak.

For a wider list of causes, see our article on why car aircon is not cold.

What a Mercedes aircon repair involves

  1. Find the cause. We check the pressures and look for leaks and faulty parts before anything is replaced.
  2. Replace what has failed. On this GLC 200 the work covered the condenser and the compressor, the two main parts that cool and pump the refrigerant.
  3. Recharge the system. The aircon recharge machine recovers, vacuums and refills the system with the right amount of refrigerant.
  4. Test before handover. We run the aircon and check that the air comes out cold and stays cold.

FAQ

Why is my Mercedes GLC aircon not cold?

Common causes include a refrigerant leak, a blocked or damaged condenser, a failing compressor, or an electrical fault in the system. A check finds which one before any part is replaced.

Is topping up the aircon gas enough?

Only if nothing is leaking or broken. If the gas runs low again soon, there is a leak or a faulty part, and topping up alone will not last.

What do the condenser and the compressor do?

The compressor pumps the refrigerant around the system. The condenser, at the front of the car, releases the heat taken out of the cabin. If either fails, the aircon cannot cool properly.
