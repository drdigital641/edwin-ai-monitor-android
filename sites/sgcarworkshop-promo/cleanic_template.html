How Cleanic Detailing Gets Found on Google: Our Real Numbers

We are a mobile car grooming and fumigation service with no shopfront. Here is how people find us on Google, with our own Search Console figures, and who builds and runs our website.

Oct 29, 2026 · Cleanic Detailing Team · Singapore
Short answer: In the 28 days to 21 October 2026, Cleanic Detailing got 480 clicks from Google and appeared on page one of Google for 1,210 different searches, 582 of them in the top 3. The figures come from Google Search Console. Our website and Google listings are built and run by Page One Singapore.

Why Being Found on Google Matters for a Mobile Business

Cleanic comes to you. We groom, steam clean and fumigate cars where they are parked, so nobody walks past a Cleanic shop and decides to pop in. For a business like ours, being found online is how new customers reach us.

Many visitors to our site arrive from a problem they typed into Google, sometimes late at night: a cockroach in the car, a child who was sick on the back seat, flies that keep coming back. If our page is not in front of them at that moment, someone else's is.

Who Runs Our Website

To be clear about it: Page One Singapore builds and runs the Cleanic website and our Google listings. We are a Page One client. That is why we can share these numbers, and it is also why you should read them as one business's results, not a promise.

The work is not magic. It is a set of ordinary things done completely and then kept going every day:

  • One page for each service, so car grooming, steam cleaning, fumigation and odour removal can each be found on their own search
  • Guides on the problems car owners actually search for, such as houseflies in the car or vomit in the car
  • Google Business Profile, Search Console and Bing set up, so Google Maps, Google Search and Bing all show the same correct details

A person still checks the facts. Details such as our treatment times are confirmed with Colin before they go on the site.

What the Numbers Don't Show

A click is a visit, not a booking. It means someone searched, saw Cleanic and opened the site. What happens next depends on the page: is the answer clear, is the price shown, is it easy to ask a question?

That is why our pages state prices plainly. For example, the MIRAKN Express is $118 and takes about 1 hour, and the full MIRAKN dry gas fumigation starts from $180 and takes about 1.5 hours. Every page also has a WhatsApp button straight to Colin. We don't publish our enquiry or booking numbers, so we won't claim more than the figures above show.
