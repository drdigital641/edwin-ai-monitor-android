Signs your car aircon compressor is failing

The compressor pumps refrigerant around the aircon system. When it weakens, cooling usually fades first in Singapore's stop-go traffic and midday heat.

Warm air from the vents

Cooling is weak or gone, even with the fan on high.

Cooling cuts in and out

Cold for a few minutes, then warm, then cold again.

Noise when the aircon is on

Grinding, rattling or squealing that stops when you switch the aircon off.

Clutch not engaging

On clutch-type compressors, the front plate does not spin when the aircon is switched on.

Oil or leak marks

Oily residue around the compressor body or its seals can point to a leak.

Belt trouble

A squealing or damaged drive belt can come from a compressor or pulley that is starting to seize.

How we replace a car aircon compressor

  1. Confirm the fault: pressure readings, clutch and electrical checks, and a leak trace, so the compressor is proven to be the cause.
  2. Agree the quote: we explain the options (new or remanufactured, where suitable) and the price. Nothing starts until you approve.
  3. Recover the refrigerant and remove the old compressor.
  4. Check for debris: a compressor that failed inside can send metal particles through the system, so we check whether other parts need cleaning or replacing and tell you before doing extra work.
  5. Fit the compressor with the correct oil, then vacuum the system and charge it with the correct refrigerant for your car (R134a or R1234yf).
  6. Test before handover: pressures, vent temperature and leak check, so you collect a car that cools properly.

New or remanufactured compressor?

Both can be the right choice. A new compressor suits cars that need a specific updated unit or owners who plan to keep the car for many years. A remanufactured compressor is a used unit that has been stripped, rebuilt with worn parts replaced and tested, and is usually a lower-cost option. What changes the price of a compressor replacement?

  • The car and compressor type: clutch, variable-displacement and electric compressors differ in part cost and fitting time.
  • New or remanufactured: a remanufactured unit, where suitable, usually costs less than new.
  • Access: some engine bays need more parts removed to reach the compressor.
  • Damage elsewhere: if the old compressor sent debris through the system, other parts may need cleaning or replacing. We show you before quoting it.
  • Refrigerant type: newer cars use R1234yf, older cars R134a.

Frequently asked questions

How much does car aircon compressor replacement cost in Singapore?

At SG Garage, compressor replacement starts from $800, including parts, labour and GST. The final price depends on the car, the compressor type and what else the inspection finds, and is confirmed before any work starts.

How do I know if my aircon compressor is failing?

Common signs are warm air from the vents, cooling that cuts in and out, a grinding or rattling noise when the aircon is on, and a compressor clutch that does not engage. A leak or an electrical fault can cause the same symptoms, so the system should be tested first.

Can a gas top-up fix a bad compressor?

No. A top-up only replaces lost refrigerant. It does not repair a worn compressor, a faulty clutch or a wiring fault, and if the system leaks the gas escapes again.

Is a remanufactured compressor a good option?

It can be, when the unit is the correct specification, properly rebuilt and tested. We remanufacture compressors in-house as a lower-cost option to new where it suits the car, and explain the choice before you decide. Read remanufactured vs new compressors.

Can I keep driving with a noisy aircon compressor?

Switch the aircon off and book an inspection soon. On many cars the compressor is driven by the engine belt, so a seizing compressor or pulley can damage the belt.
