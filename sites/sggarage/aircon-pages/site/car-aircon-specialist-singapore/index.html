How do we diagnose a car aircon problem?

We test before we quote. Most aircon complaints in Singapore (not cold, cold only when driving, warm at idle, a smell or a noise) can come from more than one part, so each check rules causes in or out.

  1. Vent temperature and pressure check: gauges on the high and low sides show whether the system is low on gas, overcharged, blocked or not compressing.
  2. Leak tracing: if the pressure is low, we find where the gas escapes. Common points are hose joints, the compressor seal, the condenser and the cooling coil.
  3. Compressor and electrical check: clutch engagement, fuses, relays and pressure switches. An electrical fault can stop the compressor and look like a gas problem.
  4. Airflow check: blower, cabin filter, condenser fan and cooling coil, for weak airflow, poor cooling at idle or smells.
  5. Clear quote: we explain what we found and what to fix first. Nothing is done until you approve the price.

How to choose a car aircon specialist in Singapore

Whichever workshop you use, these questions help you avoid paying twice for the same fault.

Do they check for leaks first?

A top-up without a leak check often means the cooling fades again within weeks.

Do they show you the cause?

Ask to see the gauge readings or the leak point before you approve a part.

Are prices clear?

Look for starting prices that include parts, labour and GST, with the final price agreed before work starts.

New or remanufactured?

For compressors, ask which option suits your car and why. Remanufactured vs new

Is the repair tested?

The system should be pressure and vent-temperature tested again before you collect the car.

Can you book a slot?

An appointment means your car gets proper time for the checks.
