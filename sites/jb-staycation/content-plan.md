## Rules for the article writer (every run)
- **Facts only.** Use the current fact set: Prima RM450/650/750; Landak RM550/750/1,000; cleaning included; no deposit for a normal gathering without food catering; catered gatherings (food catering, e.g. Lok Lok, or birthday or shower gatherings with catering) RM1,000 deposit, refunded within 48h after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents; no BBQ or grilling; no sticking decorations on walls; damage or stains to walls, fittings, fixtures or contents affect the refund; check-in 3pm, check-out 11am; minimum 1 night, or 2 nights over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day; no cancellation, one date change; Prima up to 12 guests in 3 rooms, each with 1 queen bed and 2 single floor mattresses, with a ground-floor bedroom; Landak up to 10 guests, a 5-minute walk to KSL; both houses have 3 bathrooms (1 en-suite, 2 shared) with water heaters; no BBQ, no karaoke, no swimming pool, no pets; quiet hours 11pm to 7am; RM500 penalty for smoking, vaping or durian indoors; payment by PayNow or PayLah (SG), RM bank transfer or TNG (MY), or Airbnb.
- **Banned.** Never write: parties, events, karaoke, BBQ (except "No BBQ"), swimming pool, smoke alarm, Woon, HitPay, 20 guests, "best", "No.1", or guarantees. Say "gatherings".
- **Proof gate.** Every page needs at least 2 real items that Page 1 lacks, such as our price grid in RM and SGD, real house photos, a host travel-time estimate, house rules, or Airbnb quotes word for word. With fewer than 2, skip the slot and improve an existing page instead.
- **Before writing,** re-check Google SG Page 1 for the article's keyword on mobile and desktop.
- **Page structure:**
  - One H1 containing the exact keyword.
  - A title under 60 characters that starts with the keyword.
  - A 40-60 word answer block under the H1.
  - A visible Published/Updated date.
  - WhatsApp CTAs after the intro and at the end.
  - Links to the hub /articles/, the matching house page, /prices/ and the next spoke.
  - Article and BreadcrumbList schema. Add FAQPage only for questions that are visible on the page.
  - Add the page to the sitemap with its lastmod, ping IndexNow, and request indexing in GSC once.
- **Workflow.** Dev Desk builds the page through Claude and checks it at 390px and 1280px. Report the URL, title, keyword and the weaknesses it exploits.
- **Never two pages for one keyword.** If a keyword is already owned (see the map), improve that page instead.
