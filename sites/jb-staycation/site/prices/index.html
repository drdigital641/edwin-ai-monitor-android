About the SGD estimate

SGD estimate only; exchange rates vary. Based on RM1 = S$0.3158 (6 Nov 2026; checked 9 Nov 2026 SGT). The final total depends on your dates and group size. Source: exchangerates.org.uk MYR/SGD history. RM is the price we quote; the SGD figure is not an SGD payment offer.

What is included

Prima (landed house): 7ft pool table, mahjong table, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, kitchen, parking for 2 cars inside and 6 outside, 2 TVs with Netflix, washer, covered balcony and a study desk. 3 rooms (each 1 queen bed and 2 single floor mattresses), 3 bathrooms, up to 12 guests.

Landak Kawan Fun House (KSL landed fun house): 8ft pool table, foosball, 2 dart boards, mahjong table, 55-inch TV arcade, board games, children's playroom, dedicated workstation, 100 Mbps Wi-Fi, air conditioning, kitchen for light cooking, parking for 2 cars inside, washer, hair dryer and iron. 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms with water heaters, 5 beds (1 king, 4 queens), up to 10 guests.

Good to know

  • All prices are in Malaysian ringgit (RM).
  • RM 500 per night is the weekday guideline price. For your group size or a gathering, WhatsApp the host.
  • RM 120 cleaning fee per stay.
  • Per-person figures split the whole-house rate at full occupancy; they are not bookable bed prices.
  • Pay in RM directly through HitPay, through the Airbnb listing, or by internet banking to the host.
  • No cancellation, but you can change your dates once.
  • Gatherings need the host's approval, with a booking deposit of about RM 600 to 1,200 depending on group size.
  • Early check-in or late checkout: RM 100 an hour, subject to availability (you can ask for a waiver).
  • Neither house has a swimming pool (both have a pool table). At both houses, BBQ only with the host's prior consent.
