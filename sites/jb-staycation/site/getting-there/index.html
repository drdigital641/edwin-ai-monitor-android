Getting to our Johor Bahru houses from Singapore

Facts below come from official sources, with the date we checked them.

There are two road crossings from Singapore, at Woodlands and Tuas. The RTS Link train from Woodlands North to Bukit Chagar is expected to start in February 2027. Prima is nearer Tuas; Landak Kawan Fun House is nearer CIQ and KSL City Mall.

By road: Woodlands or Tuas

ICA runs the land checkpoints at Woodlands and Tuas. Traffic can build up on long weekends and holidays, so check ICA's driving in and out of Singapore page before you leave.

Bring your passport. ICA says you must still bring it for travel when using QR code clearance. ICA also offers QR code clearance through the MyICA app at both checkpoints (see its QR code page).

By train: the RTS Link

LTA describes the RTS Link as connecting Woodlands North in Singapore with Bukit Chagar in Johor Bahru, a train journey of about five minutes, with Customs, Immigration and Quarantine facilities co-located so passengers clear both countries at their departure point.

On 2 October 2026 LTA published that passenger service is now expected to start in February 2027, subject to independent safety certification and final approvals. We have not listed fares or opening hours because we have no official figure.

Which house for which route

  • Prima: host states about 10 minutes to Tuas and about 15 minutes to Woodlands.
  • Landak Kawan Fun House: host states about 10 minutes to CIQ and a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall.
