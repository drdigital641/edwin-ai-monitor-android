Frequently asked questions

Common questions

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at +65 9006 2198 with your dates, group size and the house you like. We take bookings through WhatsApp only.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

At Landak Kawan Fun House, self check-in is after 3:00 pm and check-out is before 11:00 am. For Prima, message us to confirm the times.

What is the minimum stay?

Landak Kawan Fun House has a minimum of 1 night. Prima shows a minimum of 2 on its listing; message us to confirm the number of nights.

How many guests can stay?

Prima is listed for up to 20 guests and Landak Kawan Fun House for up to 10.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking is listed at both houses.

Are pets and smoking allowed?

At Landak Kawan Fun House, no smoking, no pets, and no BBQ or charcoal grilling. Events need prior approval and may cost extra. For Prima, ask us for the house rules.

What currency are prices in?

Prices are in Malaysian ringgit (RM). Both houses are listed at RM 500 per night.

What should I bring when crossing the border?

Bring your passport. ICA states that you must still bring your passport for travel by land, even if you use QR code clearance. Check official sources for current entry requirements.

Which house is closer to Tuas?

The host states Prima is about 10 minutes from Tuas and about 15 minutes from Woodlands.

Which house is near a shopping mall?

Landak Kawan Fun House is about a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall, as stated by the host.
