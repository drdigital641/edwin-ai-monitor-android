Ask us for the booking payment, security deposit, refund timing, cancellation and date-change terms in writing before paying. The direct-booking policy is not confirmed on this page. Do not assume an Airbnb cancellation policy applies to a direct WhatsApp booking.

Choosing a house

Which house suits our group?

Prima accommodates up to 20 guests in 3 bedrooms with 3 bathrooms, karaoke, mahjong, a pool table and BBQ. Landak Kawan Fun House accommodates up to 10 guests in 3 bedrooms with 5 beds and 3 bathrooms, with games and a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall. Ask about the exact sleeping arrangement before booking, particularly for a full Prima group.

What activities and facilities are included? Is there a swimming pool?

Neither house has a swimming pool. Prima has a pool table, karaoke, mahjong and BBQ; ask about BBQ equipment and supplies. Landak has a pool table, foosball, darts, TV arcade, board games, kids’ playroom and advertised 100Mbps Wi-Fi; BBQ is not allowed. Ask about cooking equipment, consumables and streaming accounts. No meals, transport or attraction tickets are promised.

Where can we park?

Landak has 2 parking spaces. Ask us about vehicle size, extra cars and the approach to the gate. For Prima, ask us to confirm parking capacity and arrangements before bringing several cars. Parking is not a guarantee against loss or damage.

How clean is the house, and are linen and towels included?

See the real host-supplied room photos and each house’s Airbnb rating, then ask us about cleaning between stays, linen changes, towels, toiletries and any cleaning fee. We have not published an unverified cleaning checklist or hygiene guarantee.

Can we celebrate a birthday or have a company gathering?

Tell us the purpose, overnight guests, total visitors, music and any catering before booking. Gathering and visitor permission must be confirmed in writing; the advertised sleeping capacity is not permission for a large gathering. These are homes with neighbours, not unrestricted venues.

Are the houses suitable for children and elderly guests?

Landak has board games and a kids’ playroom, but is a double-storey house with stairs. Ask about stair gates, cots, ground-floor sleeping, step-free bathrooms and any mobility needs before booking either house. Neither house is advertised as wheelchair-accessible or childproof.

Getting there

How long does it take from Singapore, including CIQ queues?

Prima is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link on the Malaysian side, according to the host. Landak is in Taman Abad, a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall. These are not door-to-door journey times from Singapore. Border queues and traffic add time; check ICA and LTA traffic updates before leaving.

What should I prepare before driving a Singapore car to JB?

Check JPJ’s official VEP portal for your vehicle’s registration and RFID requirements, and have valid road tax and insurance. Singapore Customs requires applicable fuel tanks to be at least three-quarters full when leaving by land. Foreign-registered petrol cars should use RON97, not subsidised RON95. Check current official rules before travel; tolls and road charges are separate from accommodation.

Can we come without a car, and is there food nearby?

Landak is a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall. Prima is in Gelang Patah, Iskandar Puteri. See the nearby-place guides and map links to plan food and outings. Check local Grab availability, fares and your onward transport before travelling; no pickup, shuttle or cross-border ride is included.
