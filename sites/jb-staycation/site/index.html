Plan your weekend in three steps

  1. Pick a house. Choose Prima for a big group of up to 20, or Landak Kawan Fun House for up to 10 with games and a short walk to KSL City Mall.
  2. Message us your dates. Send your group size and dates on WhatsApp and we will confirm the rate and availability.
  3. Cross with your passport. Check ICA's traffic updates before you leave, then drive in and settle in. Landak has self check-in after 3:00 pm.

Each house suits a different kind of trip: birthdays, reunions, family weekends or a games night with friends. Everything is under one roof, from the kitchen to the entertainment, so nobody has to split up for the evening.

Getting there from Singapore

You can cross by the Woodlands or Tuas land checkpoints (the host states Prima is about 10 minutes from Tuas and 15 from Woodlands), and the RTS Link train from Woodlands North to Bukit Chagar is expected to start passenger service in February 2027, subject to safety certification (LTA, 2 October 2026).
