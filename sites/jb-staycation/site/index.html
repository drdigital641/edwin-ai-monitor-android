More questions and answers
How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at +65 9006 2198 with your dates, group size and the house you like. Bookings are made through WhatsApp only.

How much is a night?

Both Prima and Landak Kawan Fun House are RM 500 per night. Prices are in Malaysian ringgit (RM).

How many guests can stay?

Prima sleeps up to 20 guests in 3 bedrooms, and Landak Kawan Fun House up to 10 in 3 bedrooms.

Is there a minimum stay?

Landak Kawan Fun House has a minimum stay of 1 night. For Prima, ask us on WhatsApp for the minimum stay on your dates.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Both houses have self check-in. At Landak Kawan Fun House, check-in is after 3:00 pm and check-out is before 11:00 am. For Prima, ask us on WhatsApp for the times.

Are pets, smoking and BBQ allowed?

No pets and no smoking at either house. Prima has a BBQ pit; at Landak Kawan Fun House there is no BBQ, charcoal grilling or durian. Quiet hours at both houses are 11:00 pm to 7:00 am.

Is there parking, and can we cook?

Parking is free at Prima, and 2 cars park inside the gate at Landak Kawan Fun House. Prima has a kitchen; Landak Kawan Fun House has a kitchen for light cooking.

Can we hold a larger gathering, and what is the cancellation policy?

Larger gatherings need prior approval, so ask us first. The host says the cancellation policy is shown before payment and in your confirmation.
