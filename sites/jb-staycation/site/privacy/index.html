Privacy notice

Last updated 2026-11-08.

What we collect

When you message us on WhatsApp we receive your phone number, name shown on WhatsApp and whatever you write, such as dates and group size.

How we use it

We use it to reply to your enquiry, confirm a booking and manage your stay. We do not sell your personal data.

Who sees it

Messages are handled through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy. We share details only with people who help run your stay, or where the law requires.

Website analytics

This website counts taps on contact links (for example WhatsApp) with an anonymous visitor identifier to understand which pages help visitors. It does not store your message.

Your choices

You can ask us on WhatsApp to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop contacting you, in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act and Singapore's PDPA where they apply.

We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed for the purposes above.
