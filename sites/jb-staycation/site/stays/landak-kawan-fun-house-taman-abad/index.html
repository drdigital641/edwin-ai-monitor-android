First time in JB? 5 tips for Singaporeans
  1. Driving in needs a VEP. Singapore cars need an activated VEP RFID tag linked to Touch 'n Go, applied for on the JPJ portal, plus valid road tax and insurance. There is an RM20 road charge each time you enter. Apply a week or more ahead. JPJ VEP portal
  2. Fill up with RON97 only. Foreign-registered petrol cars must not buy subsidised RON95. Singapore's three-quarter-tank rule also applies when you leave by land. Singapore Customs
  3. Top up Touch 'n Go. It pays the road charge and most tolls. Top it up before you cross, and carry some ringgit cash for night markets and kopitiams.
  4. Time the border. Friday evening into JB and Sunday evening back are the worst. From Prima, the Second Link is usually quicker. From Landak, the Causeway is nearer.
  5. Grab works well for getting around JB if you don't drive. Install the app and add a card before you cross. No cross-border pickup is included.

Questions about Landak Kawan Fun House

When can we check in, and is one night possible?

Both houses: check-in 3pm, check-out 11am. Self check-in details are sent after a firm booking. Early check-in and late checkout cost RM 100 an hour, subject to availability, and you can ask for a waiver. The minimum stay is 1 night, or 2 nights over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

Where can we park?

Prima has parking for 2 cars inside and 6 cars outside. Landak has parking for 2 cars inside. Parking is not a guarantee against loss or damage.

Are the houses suitable for children and elderly guests?

Prima has a ground-floor bedroom, so elderly guests do not need to use the stairs. Landak has board games and a children's playroom, but is a double-storey house with stairs. WhatsApp Daniel to check cots, ground-floor rooms or mobility needs for your group. Neither house is advertised as wheelchair-accessible or childproof.

What activities and facilities are included?

Prima has a 7ft pool table and a mahjong table. Landak has an 8ft pool table, foosball, 2 dart boards, a mahjong table, a TV arcade, board games, a children's playroom, a dedicated workstation and advertised 100Mbps Wi-Fi. No BBQ at either house. No meals, transport or attraction tickets are included.

Can we come without a car, and is there food nearby?

Landak is a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall. Prima is in Gelang Patah, Iskandar Puteri. See the nearby-place guides and map links to plan food and outings. Check local Grab availability, fares and your onward transport before travelling; no pickup, shuttle or cross-border ride is included.

Are pets, smoking or BBQ allowed?

No pets. No smoking or vaping indoors: RM 500 penalty. No eating durian indoors: RM 500 penalty. No BBQ. Quiet hours are 11:00 pm to 7:00 am.
