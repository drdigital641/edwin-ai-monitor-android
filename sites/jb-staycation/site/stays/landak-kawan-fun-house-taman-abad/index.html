Landak Kawan Fun House, Taman Abad

GuestsUp to 10
Bedrooms3
Bathrooms3
RateRM 500 per night

Landak Kawan Fun House is a private double-storey home in Taman Abad, Johor Bahru, for up to 10 guests. It has 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms and 1 king bed plus 4 queen beds (bedrooms 2 and 3 each have two queens).

The fun is built in: an 8-foot pool table in the games courtyard, a foosball table, two dartboards and a 55-inch smart TV with an arcade console.

It is about a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall, and the host states 10 minutes by car to JB City Square, KOMTAR JBCC and CIQ.

What is included

  • 8-ft pool table
  • Foosball table
  • Two dartboards
  • 55-inch smart TV with arcade console
  • 100 Mbps Wi-Fi
  • Washer and detergent
  • Hair dryer

  • Workspace

House rules and times

  • Check-in: self check-in after 3:00 pm.
  • Check-out: before 11:00 am.
  • No smoking.
  • No pets.
  • No BBQ or charcoal grilling.
  • Events need prior approval and may cost extra.

Questions about Landak Kawan Fun House

What time is check-in and check-out at Landak Kawan Fun House?

Self check-in is after 3:00 pm and check-out is before 11:00 am.

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 1 night.

Are pets, smoking or BBQ allowed?

No. Smoking, pets, BBQ and charcoal grilling are not allowed. Events need prior approval and may cost extra.

What is there to do inside the house?

There is an 8-foot pool table, a foosball table, two dartboards and a 55-inch smart TV with an arcade console.
