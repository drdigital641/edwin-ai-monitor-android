About this house

The 5 beds are 1 king plus 4 queens. The fun is built in: an 8-foot pool table and two dartboards on the covered patio, a foosball table, a 55-inch TV with arcade games, board games and a kids' playroom.

Wi-Fi is 100 Mbps, the kitchen is set up for light cooking, and 2 cars park inside the gate. The host states about 10 minutes by car to JB City Square, KOMTAR JBCC and CIQ, and about 12 minutes to Mid Valley Southkey.

House rules and times

  • Check-in: self check-in after 3:00 pm.
  • Check-out: before 11:00 am.
  • No smoking.
  • No pets.
  • No durian.
  • No BBQ or charcoal grilling.
  • Quiet hours 11:00 pm to 7:00 am. Guests must not disturb neighbours.
  • Larger gatherings need prior approval and may cost extra.

Questions about Landak Kawan Fun House

What time is check-in and check-out at Landak Kawan Fun House?

Self check-in is after 3:00 pm and check-out is before 11:00 am.

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 1 night.

Are pets, smoking or BBQ allowed?

No. Smoking, pets, durian, BBQ and charcoal grilling are not allowed. Quiet hours are 11:00 pm to 7:00 am.

Is there parking, and can we cook?

Yes, 2 cars can park inside the gate, and there is a kitchen for light cooking. BBQ and charcoal grilling are not allowed.

What is there to do inside the house?

There is an 8-foot pool table, a foosball table, two dartboards, a 55-inch TV with arcade games, board games and a kids' playroom.
