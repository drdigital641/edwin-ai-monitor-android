JB Overnight Stay for KL and Malaysian Travellers

JB Staycations welcomes guests from KL and other Malaysian states. Our two private houses in Johor Bahru suit a one-night stopover before or after crossing to Singapore, or a short JB getaway. Prima, in Gelang Patah, sleeps up to 12; Landak Kawan Fun House, in Taman Abad, sleeps up to 10. From RM 450 a night, cleaning included.

A one-night stopover before or after the crossing

Driving down from KL or another state and crossing to Singapore the next day? Stay a night in JB, rest, and cross in the morning. The minimum stay is 1 night (2 nights over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day). Check-in is 3pm and check-out 11am, with self check-in details sent after a firm booking. Early check-in or late checkout is RM 100 an hour, subject to availability.

  • Prima, in Gelang Patah, is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link on the Malaysian side (host estimate).
  • Landak Kawan Fun House is in Taman Abad, Johor Bahru, a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall for dinner and supplies.

These are not door-to-door journey times. Border queues and traffic add time. Compare the routes in Causeway, Second Link or RTS: which way to JB.

A short JB getaway

Not crossing at all? Both houses work for a short break in Johor Bahru with family or friends: a pool table at each house, games at Landak, and food and malls nearby. See what is around Prima and Landak.

Groups and families

Prima takes up to 12 guests and Landak Kawan Fun House up to 10. Each is a whole private house, so your group has the place to itself. Gatherings are welcome with the host's approval; there is no BBQ at either house.

Questions from Malaysian guests

Can we stay one night in JB before crossing to Singapore?

Yes. The minimum stay is 1 night, or 2 nights over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. Check-in is 3pm and check-out 11am at both houses. Early check-in and late checkout cost RM 100 an hour, subject to availability.

Which house is closer to the crossing?

Prima, in Gelang Patah, is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link on the Malaysian side (host estimate). Landak Kawan Fun House is in Taman Abad, Johor Bahru, a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall. These are not door-to-door journey times; border queues and traffic add time.

How much is a night?

Per night for the whole house, cleaning included. Prima: RM 450 weekday, RM 650 weekend, RM 750 public holiday. Landak Kawan Fun House: RM 550 weekday, RM 750 weekend, RM 1,000 public holiday.

How do Malaysian guests pay?

In RM by bank transfer or TNG eWallet. Bank details are shared once your booking and dates are confirmed. You can also book through the Airbnb listing.

Is there parking?

Prima has parking for 2 cars inside and 6 cars outside. Landak Kawan Fun House has parking for 2 cars inside.

Can our group hold a gathering?

Yes, with the host's approval. Visitors are allowed, and quiet hours are 11pm to 7am. No deposit for a normal gathering; a RM 1,000 deposit for catered gatherings, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains. No BBQ at either house.
