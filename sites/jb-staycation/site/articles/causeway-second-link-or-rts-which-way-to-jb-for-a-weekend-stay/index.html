Causeway, Second Link or RTS: which way to JB for a weekend stay

By JBStaycation, 2026-11-08

By road, Singapore has two land checkpoints: Woodlands and Tuas. The RTS Link train between Woodlands North and Bukit Chagar is now expected to start passenger service in February 2027, subject to safety certification (LTA, 2 Oct 2026). Prima is closer to Tuas; Landak to JB CIQ.

By road: Woodlands or Tuas

ICA operates the land checkpoints at Woodlands and Tuas and lists traffic updates on its driving in and out of Singapore page. Check it before you leave, especially on long weekends.

You must bring your passport. ICA says so on its QR code clearance page (updated 27 Oct 2026), even when you use QR clearance.

By train: the RTS Link

The RTS Link connects Woodlands North in Singapore with Bukit Chagar in Johor Bahru. LTA describes a train journey of about five minutes between the stations, with Customs, Immigration and Quarantine facilities co-located so you clear both countries before boarding (LTA project page).

On 2 October 2026 LTA published that passenger service is now expected to start in February 2027, subject to independent safety certification and final approvals (LTA news release). Until it opens, plan on road or other existing routes.

Which house for which route

Prima in Iskandar Puteri is stated by the host as about 10 minutes from Tuas and about 15 minutes from Woodlands, so it suits groups driving in via the Second Link.

Landak Kawan Fun House in Taman Abad is stated as about 10 minutes from CIQ and a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall, so it suits groups arriving at the Johor Bahru city side.
