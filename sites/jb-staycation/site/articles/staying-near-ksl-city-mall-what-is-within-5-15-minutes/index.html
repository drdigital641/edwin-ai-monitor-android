Things to do near KSL City Mall, 5 to 15 minutes from Landak

By JB Staycations team · Published 9 Nov 2026 · Updated 9 Nov 2026

Landak Kawan Fun House in Taman Abad is about a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall and about 3 minutes on foot to the Yu Ee hawker stalls (host tip). Mid Valley Southkey is about a 10-minute drive, and JB City Square and the Causeway CIQ about 12 minutes (host estimate, excluding border queues).

Walking distance

Landak Kawan Fun House is about a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall, handy for meals, shopping and anything you forgot to pack.

The Yu Ee hawker centre is about 3 minutes away, as stated by the host.

Food and shopping on foot

Inside KSL City Mall (about a 5-minute walk) there is a supermarket on LG for groceries, You Kee XO for char siew and crispy siew yoke, and Tao Yuan for local snacks to bring home. On Mondays only, the KSL Monday Night Market on Jalan Seladang is about a 5-minute walk.

Nearby on foot (about 5-10 minutes): Restoran Yit Foh for wanton noodles and Holiday Plaza for haircuts, nails and massage. See the Around the house section for map links and sample weekend plans. Opening hours change, so check Google Maps before you go.

Within 10-12 minutes by car

Mid Valley Southkey is about a 10-minute drive. JB City Square and the Causeway CIQ are about 12 minutes away (host estimate).

About these times

All times other than the 5-minute walk are drive times stated by the host. Traffic, especially around the checkpoints on weekends and holidays, can change them.

Why this suits a weekend away

You can walk to a mall, then spend the evening at home with the pool table, foosball, darts and arcade TV. Wi-Fi is 100 Mbps. The house sleeps up to 10, with check-in after 3:00 pm and check-out before 11:00 am.

The whole house is RM 550 weekday, RM 750 weekend, RM 1,000 public holiday (about S$174 / S$237 / S$316) a night, cleaning included (about S$17.37 per person on a weekday at 10 guests; an estimate).
