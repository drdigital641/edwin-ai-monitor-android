Staying near KSL City Mall: what is within 5-15 minutes

By JBStaycation, 2026-11-08

Landak Kawan Fun House in Taman Abad is about a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall and 3 minutes to Yu Ee hawker centre. The host states 10 minutes by car to JB City Square, KOMTAR JBCC and CIQ, and 12 minutes to R&F Mall and Mid Valley Southkey.

Walking distance

Landak Kawan Fun House is about a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall, handy for meals, shopping and anything you forgot to pack.

The Yu Ee hawker centre is about 3 minutes away, as stated by the host.

Within 10-12 minutes by car

JB City Square, KOMTAR JBCC and CIQ are about 10 minutes away. R&F Mall and Mid Valley Southkey are about 12 minutes away.

Within 18 minutes by car

AEON Tebrau, IKEA and Toppen are about 18 minutes away.

About these times

All times other than the 5-minute walk are drive times stated by the host. Traffic, especially around the checkpoints on weekends and holidays, can change them.

Why this suits a weekend away

You can walk to a mall, then spend the evening at home with the pool table, foosball, darts and arcade TV. Wi-Fi is 100 Mbps. The house sleeps up to 10, with check-in after 3:00 pm and check-out before 11:00 am.
