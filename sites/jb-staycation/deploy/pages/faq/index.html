Frequently asked questions

Common questions

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at +65 9006 2198 with your dates, group size and the house you like. We take bookings through WhatsApp only.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Both houses have self check-in. At Landak Kawan Fun House, check-in is after 3:00 pm and check-out is before 11:00 am. For Prima, message us to confirm the times.

What is the minimum stay?

Landak Kawan Fun House has a minimum of 1 night. For Prima, ask us on WhatsApp for the minimum stay on your dates.

How many guests can stay?

Prima sleeps up to 20 guests in 3 bedrooms, and Landak Kawan Fun House up to 10 in 3 bedrooms with 5 beds.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is free at Prima, and 2 cars park inside the gate at Landak Kawan Fun House.

Are pets and smoking allowed?

No pets and no smoking at either house. At Landak Kawan Fun House there is also no BBQ, charcoal grilling or durian. Prima has a BBQ pit.

Are there quiet hours?

Yes. Quiet hours at both houses are 11:00 pm to 7:00 am, and guests must not disturb neighbours.

Is a deposit needed?

We have not listed a deposit amount. Ask us on WhatsApp before you book.

What is the cancellation policy?

The host says the cancellation policy is shown before payment and in your confirmation. You can also ask us on WhatsApp before you decide.

Can we hold a larger gathering?

Larger gatherings need prior approval, so please ask us first. At Landak Kawan Fun House they may cost extra.

Can we cook at the houses?

Prima has a kitchen and a BBQ pit. Landak Kawan Fun House has a kitchen for light cooking, and no BBQ or charcoal grilling is allowed there.

Do you need a copy of my ID?

For Prima, please send a copy of your ID by WhatsApp before check-in.

What currency are prices in?

Prices are in Malaysian ringgit (RM). Both houses are listed at RM 500 per night.

What should I bring when crossing the border?

Bring your passport. ICA states that you must still bring your passport for travel by land, even if you use QR code clearance. Check official sources for current entry requirements.

Which house is closer to Tuas?

The host states Prima is about 10 minutes from Tuas and about 15 minutes from Woodlands.

Which house is near a shopping mall?

Landak Kawan Fun House is about a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall, as stated by the host.
