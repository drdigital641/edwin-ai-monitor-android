JB holiday house FAQ for Singapore guests

Quick answers for Singapore groups booking our two private JB houses. Prima sleeps up to 12 and Landak Kawan Fun House up to 10. Prices start at RM 450 a night, cleaning included. No deposit for a normal gathering; RM 1,000 for catered gatherings. Check-in is 3pm, check-out 11am. Anything else? WhatsApp Daniel on +65 9006 2198.

Price and booking

How much does a whole house cost in SGD?

Prices are per night for the whole house, cleaning included. Prima: RM 450 weekday, RM 650 weekend, RM 750 public holiday (about S$142 / S$205 / S$237). Landak Kawan Fun House: RM 550 weekday, RM 750 weekend, RM 1,000 public holiday (about S$174 / S$237 / S$316). For your group size or a gathering, WhatsApp Daniel. The SGD figures are estimates using RM1 = S$0.3158, checked on 9 November 2026 SGT against the latest displayed market observation dated 6 November. Exchange rates vary.

How do we pay?

Singapore guests pay by PayNow or PayLah to the host's mobile, and a receipt is issued. Malaysian guests pay in RM by bank transfer or TNG eWallet. Bank details are shared once your booking and dates are confirmed. You can also book through the Airbnb listing. An SGD estimate on this site is not an SGD price.

Is there a deposit, and what happens if we cancel?

No deposit for a normal gathering without food catering. A catered gathering (food catering, for example Lok Lok, or a birthday or shower gathering with catering) has a RM 1,000 deposit, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents. No BBQ or grilling, and no sticking decorations on walls. Damage or stains to walls, fittings, fixtures or contents affect the refund. There is no cancellation, but you can change your dates once.

When can we check in, and is one night possible?

Both houses: check-in 3pm, check-out 11am. Self check-in details are sent after a firm booking. Early check-in and late checkout cost RM 100 an hour, subject to availability, and you can ask for a waiver. The minimum stay is 1 night, or 2 nights over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

Choosing a house

Which house suits our group?

Prima, a landed house, takes up to 12 guests in 3 rooms, each with 1 queen bed and 2 single floor mattresses, with 3 bathrooms, a 7ft pool table and a mahjong table. Landak Kawan Fun House takes up to 10 guests in 3 bedrooms with 5 beds (1 king, 4 queens) and 3 bathrooms, with an 8ft pool table, darts, foosball, mahjong and a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall.

What activities and facilities are included?

Prima has a 7ft pool table and a mahjong table. Landak has an 8ft pool table, foosball, 2 dart boards, a mahjong table, a TV arcade, board games, a children's playroom, a dedicated workstation and advertised 100Mbps Wi-Fi. No BBQ at either house. No meals, transport or attraction tickets are included.

Where can we park?

Prima has parking for 2 cars inside and 6 cars outside. Landak has parking for 2 cars inside. Parking is not a guarantee against loss or damage.

How clean is the house, and are linen and towels included?

The house is professionally cleaned after every checkout, with fresh linen, towels and toiletries provided. Cleaning is included in the nightly price.

Can we celebrate a birthday or have a company gathering?

Yes, with the host's approval. Visitors are allowed, and quiet hours are 11pm to 7am. No deposit for a normal gathering without food catering. A catered gathering (food catering, for example Lok Lok, or a birthday or shower gathering with catering) has a RM 1,000 deposit, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents. No BBQ or grilling, and no sticking decorations on walls. Damage or stains to walls, fittings, fixtures or contents affect the refund. WhatsApp Daniel with your group size and plans. These are homes with neighbours, not unrestricted venues.

Are the houses suitable for children and elderly guests?

Prima has a ground-floor bedroom, so elderly guests do not need to use the stairs. Landak has board games and a children's playroom, but is a double-storey house with stairs. WhatsApp Daniel to check cots, ground-floor rooms or mobility needs for your group. Neither house is advertised as wheelchair-accessible or childproof.

Getting there

How long does it take from Singapore, including CIQ queues?

Prima is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link on the Malaysian side, according to the host. Landak is in Taman Abad, a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall. These are not door-to-door journey times from Singapore. Border queues and traffic add time; check ICA and LTA traffic updates before leaving.

What should I prepare before driving a Singapore car to JB?

Check JPJ’s official VEP portal for your vehicle’s registration and RFID requirements, and have valid road tax and insurance. Singapore Customs requires applicable fuel tanks to be at least three-quarters full when leaving by land. Foreign-registered petrol cars should use RON97, not subsidised RON95. Check current official rules before travel; tolls and road charges are separate from accommodation.

Can we come without a car, and is there food nearby?

Landak is a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall. Prima is in Gelang Patah, Iskandar Puteri. See the nearby-place guides and map links to plan food and outings. Check local Grab availability, fares and your onward transport before travelling; no pickup, shuttle or cross-border ride is included.
