What is included

Prima (landed house): ground-floor bedroom (no stairs needed), 7ft pool table, mahjong table, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, kitchen, parking for 2 cars inside and 6 outside, 2 TVs with Netflix, washer, covered balcony and a study desk. 3 rooms, each with 1 queen bed and 2 single floor mattresses, 3 bathrooms (1 en-suite, 2 shared) with water heaters, up to 12 guests.

Landak Kawan Fun House (KSL landed fun house): 8ft pool table, foosball, 2 dart boards, mahjong table, 55-inch TV arcade, board games, children's playroom, dedicated workstation, 100 Mbps Wi-Fi, air conditioning, kitchen for light cooking, parking for 2 cars inside, washer, hair dryer and iron. 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms (1 en-suite, 2 shared) with water heaters, 5 beds (1 king, 4 queens), up to 10 guests.

Good to know

  • All prices are in Malaysian ringgit (RM).
  • Prices are per night for the whole house, with weekday, weekend and public holiday rates. For your group size or a gathering, WhatsApp Daniel.
  • Cleaning is included in the nightly price.
  • Minimum stay: 1 night (2 over Christmas Eve and Christmas Day). 24 and 25 Dec are charged at the public holiday rate (Prima RM 750, Landak RM 1,000).
  • Per-person figures split the weekday whole-house rate at full occupancy; they are not bookable bed prices.
  • Singapore guests pay by PayNow or PayLah to the host's mobile, and a receipt is issued. Malaysian guests pay in RM by bank transfer or TNG eWallet. Bank details are shared once your booking and dates are confirmed. You can also book through the Airbnb listing.
  • No cancellation, but you can change your dates once.
  • Gatherings need the host's approval.
  • Early check-in or late checkout: RM 100 an hour, subject to availability (you can ask for a waiver).
  • No BBQ or grilling at either house.

Deposit

  • Normal gathering with no food catering: no deposit.
  • Catered gathering (food catering, for example Lok Lok, or a birthday or shower gathering with catering): RM 1,000 deposit, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents.
  • No BBQ or grilling.
  • No sticking decorations on walls.
  • Damage or stains to walls, fittings, fixtures or contents affect the refund.
