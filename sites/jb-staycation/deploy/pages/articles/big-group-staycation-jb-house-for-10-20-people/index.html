JB homestay for big groups: planning for 10-20 guests

By JBStaycation, 2026-11-08

For 10 people, Landak Kawan Fun House in Taman Abad sleeps up to 10 with 3 bedrooms and 5 beds. For up to 20, Prima in Taman Nusantara Prima has 3 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms, with karaoke, mahjong and a BBQ pit. Both are RM 500 per night, booked on WhatsApp.

Why a whole house suits a big group

One house keeps everyone together: one kitchen, one living area, one place to park. You split the nightly rate across the group, and nobody is booking six separate hotel rooms.

Both JBStaycation houses are private homes in Johor Bahru, not shared buildings.

Up to 10 guests: Landak Kawan Fun House

Landak Kawan Fun House in Taman Abad has 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, 1 king bed and 4 queen beds (bedrooms 2 and 3 each have two queens). The rate is RM 500 per night (about S$158 for the whole house); ask us for the minimum stay on your dates.

For entertainment it has an 8-foot pool table, a foosball table, two dartboards and a 55-inch smart TV with an arcade console. Wi-Fi is 100 Mbps. House rules: no smoking, pets, durian or BBQ, with quiet hours from 11:00 pm to 7:00 am.

Up to 20 guests: Prima

Prima in Taman Nusantara Prima, Iskandar Puteri, sleeps up to 20 guests in 3 bedrooms with 3 bathrooms. The rate is RM 500 per night; ask us for the minimum stay on your dates.

It has a pool table, a karaoke set, a mahjong table, a BBQ pit, a study desk and a covered balcony, plus a kitchen, 2 TVs with Netflix and free parking, in about 2,500 sq ft.

Getting your group across

Many groups drive. The host estimates Prima is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link and Landak about 12 minutes from the Causeway CIQ, excluding border queues. See our guide to getting to Johor Bahru for the Causeway, Second Link and RTS Link options.

Splitting the cost

Both houses are RM 500 per night for the whole house, about S$158. At full occupancy that is about S$7.90 per person per night at Prima (20 guests) and about S$15.80 at Landak (10 guests). SGD estimate only; exchange rates vary. Based on RM1 = S$0.3158 (6 Nov 2026; checked 9 Nov 2026 SGT). Ask for the final total, fees, deposit and payment method for your dates.

Questions to ask before you pay

Guest limit is not the same as bed count. Ask us to confirm the sleeping arrangement (beds and mattresses) for your group, especially at Prima.

Before paying, ask us on WhatsApp to confirm in writing: availability, the final total in RM, payment options, the deposit, cancellation terms and whether day visitors are allowed. We do not publish these terms until the host confirms them.

How to ask about dates

Message us on WhatsApp with your house choice, dates and the number of adults and children. Read the JB guide for Singaporeans for the crossing and first-time tips.
