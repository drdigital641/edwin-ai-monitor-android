Questions about our houses

How much does a whole house cost in SGD?

Prices are per night for the whole house, cleaning included. Prima: RM 450 weekday, RM 650 weekend, RM 750 public holiday (about S$142 / S$205 / S$237). Landak Kawan Fun House: RM 550 weekday, RM 750 weekend, RM 1,000 public holiday (about S$174 / S$237 / S$316). For your group size or a gathering, WhatsApp Daniel. The SGD figures are estimates using RM1 = S$0.3158, checked on 9 November 2026 SGT against the latest displayed market observation dated 6 November. Exchange rates vary.

Which house suits our group?

Prima, a landed house, takes up to 12 guests in 3 rooms, each with 1 queen bed and 2 single floor mattresses, with 3 bathrooms (1 en-suite, 2 shared), a 7ft pool table and a mahjong table. Landak Kawan Fun House takes up to 10 guests in 3 bedrooms with 5 beds (1 king, 4 queens) and 3 bathrooms (1 en-suite, 2 shared), with an 8ft pool table, darts, foosball, mahjong and a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall.

What activities and facilities are included?

Prima has a 7ft pool table and a mahjong table. Landak has an 8ft pool table, foosball, 2 dart boards, a mahjong table, a TV arcade, board games, a children's playroom, a dedicated workstation and advertised 100Mbps Wi-Fi. No BBQ at either house. No meals, transport or attraction tickets are included.

Is there a deposit, and what happens if we cancel?

No deposit for a normal gathering without food catering. A catered gathering (food catering, for example Lok Lok, or a birthday or shower gathering with catering) has a RM 1,000 deposit, refunded within 48 hours after checkout if there is no damage or stains to the house or its contents. No BBQ or grilling, and no sticking decorations on walls. Damage or stains to walls, fittings, fixtures or contents affect the refund. There is no cancellation, but you can change your dates once.

How long does it take from Singapore, including CIQ queues?

Prima is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link on the Malaysian side, according to the host. Landak is in Taman Abad, a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall. These are not door-to-door journey times from Singapore. Border queues and traffic add time; check ICA and LTA traffic updates before leaving.
