Questions about our houses

How much does a night cost?

Both houses are RM 500 per night. Prices are in Malaysian ringgit (RM). Message us on WhatsApp for the rate on your dates.

Is there a minimum stay?

Landak Kawan Fun House has a minimum of 1 night. For Prima, ask us on WhatsApp for the minimum stay on your dates.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp at +65 9006 2198 with the house, your dates and group size. Bookings are made through WhatsApp only.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is free at Prima, and 2 cars park inside the gate at Landak Kawan Fun House.

What are the house rules?

No smoking and no pets at either house, with quiet hours from 11:00 pm to 7:00 am. Gatherings are welcome; larger gatherings need prior approval. Landak Kawan Fun House also has no BBQ, charcoal grilling or durian.

How far are the houses from Singapore?

The host states Prima is about 10 minutes from Tuas checkpoint and about 15 minutes from Woodlands. Landak Kawan Fun House is about 10 minutes from JB CIQ and a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall.
