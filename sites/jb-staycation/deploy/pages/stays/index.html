Questions about our houses

How much does a whole house cost in SGD?

Both houses are RM 500 per night, about S$158 for the whole house. The SGD figure is an estimate using RM1 = S$0.3158, checked on 9 November 2026 SGT against the latest displayed market observation dated 6 November. Exchange rates vary. Ask us for the final total, any fees and payment currency before paying.

Which house suits our group?

Prima, a landed house, takes up to 12 guests in 3 bedrooms with 3 bathrooms, with a 7ft pool table, karaoke, mahjong and BBQ. Landak Kawan Fun House takes up to 10 guests in 3 bedrooms with 5 beds and 3 bathrooms, with an 8ft pool table, darts, foosball, mahjong and a 5-minute walk to KSL City Mall. Ask about the exact sleeping arrangement at Prima before booking.

What activities and facilities are included? Is there a swimming pool?

Neither house has a swimming pool. Prima has a pool table, karaoke, mahjong and BBQ; ask about BBQ equipment and supplies. Landak has a pool table, foosball, darts, TV arcade, board games, kids’ playroom and advertised 100Mbps Wi-Fi; BBQ is not allowed. Ask about cooking equipment, consumables and streaming accounts. No meals, transport or attraction tickets are promised.

Is there a deposit, and what happens if we cancel?

Ask us for the booking payment, security deposit, refund timing, cancellation and date-change terms in writing before paying. The direct-booking policy is not confirmed on this page. Do not assume an Airbnb cancellation policy applies to a direct WhatsApp booking.

How long does it take from Singapore, including CIQ queues?

Prima is about 10 minutes from the Tuas Second Link on the Malaysian side, according to the host. Landak is in Taman Abad, a 5-minute walk from KSL City Mall. These are not door-to-door journey times from Singapore. Border queues and traffic add time; check ICA and LTA traffic updates before leaving.
