Quick answers

Quick answers

What scanner do you use?

Autel, including MaxiSys models, as seen in our photos and videos.

Can you repair an ECU instead of replacing it?

Often yes. We have done both ECU repair and ECM replacement.

Do you do ECU clone, remap or fault-code coding?

Yes. We do ECU clone, ECU remap or tuning, and DTC or fault-code coding when the diagnosis calls for it. We describe the work plainly and only as legal, safe repairs — with no promise of more power.

Will you tell me before you start?

Yes. We explain the cause and the cost before repairs.

Car warning light diagnosis in Kuantan

Modern cars run on many control units. When a warning appears, guessing parts costs money. We read every module, trace the cause and only then repair it, from BMW ABS warnings to Mercedes ECM faults and Honda hybrid systems.
