Quick answers

Hybrid battery and hybrid car aircond in Kuantan

Hybrids use high-voltage batteries and often an electric A/C compressor, so a "not cold" fault can be a hybrid fault too. We combine our aircond work with hybrid diagnosis on Honda, Toyota and BMW hybrids, and we see EVs such as the Porsche Taycan and BMW iX.
