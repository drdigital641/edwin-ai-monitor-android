Quick answers

Quick answers

Do you just top up gas?

No. If gas keeps running low, there is a leak. We find it first.

Can you fix hybrid and EV aircond?

Yes. We have replaced electric A/C compressors on hybrids, such as a BMW X5 Hybrid.

How long does it take?

It depends on the fault. One customer had a dual-zone fault fixed the same morning.

Aircond kereta tak sejuk? Car aircond service in Kuantan

We work on compressors, condensers, evaporators (cooling coils), expansion valves, cabin filters and A/C oil, on local and continental cars. Recent jobs include a BMW G20 cooling-coil leak, a Range Rover compressor, a Camry Hybrid evaporator and Mercedes W211 A/C coding.
