How to read it

Crop choice decides the value.

  • Kale is the strongest crop: 3.46 kg per cycle at about S$40.70/kg for pesticide-free kale.
  • F1 Hybrid Special Pak Choy gives the biggest weight, 7.77 kg in 5 weeks.
  • Lettuce compared with cheap regular lettuce only about breaks even.
  • Produce alone pays back the system slowly. The bigger value is fresh food, learning and engagement.

Comparison prices per kg (FairPrice, 11 Nov 2026): lettuce, regular about S$6.00, local pesticide-free about S$24.80–28.00 · pak choy, regular about S$6.50, pesticide-free about S$13.10 · nai bai, pesticide-free about S$13.10 · choy sum, pesticide-free about S$16.90–19.40 · kale, pesticide-free about S$40.70.
