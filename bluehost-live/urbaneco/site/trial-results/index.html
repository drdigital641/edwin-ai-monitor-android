Highlights

  • Top yielder: F1 Hybrid Special Pak Choy, 86.36 g per cup and 7.77 kg per trial system in 5 weeks.
  • Fastest cycle: pak choy and bayam, ready in 4 weeks.
  • Pick again: loose-leaf lettuce, basil and kale can be picked cut-and-come-again over several weeks. Head crops are harvested once, then replanted.

How we tested

Trials were run indoors under the system's full-spectrum LED lighting with soil-free hydroponics. Results vary with variety, nutrient management and environment. Herbs (7-week cycle) keep producing after the first harvest.
