How to Prepare Your Car for Fumigation in Singapore

What to do before Cleanic arrives: five simple steps, the times to plan around, and what to ask Colin first.

Nov 2, 2026 · Cleanic Detailing Team · Singapore
Short answer: To prepare your car for fumigation in Singapore, tell Colin where it is parked, describe what you have seen, choose the treatment, take out rubbish and personal items, and set aside the time. With Cleanic that is about 1 hour for the $118 MIRAKN Express and about 1.5 hours for the full fumigation, from $180.

How Do I Prepare My Car for Fumigation?

Good news first: there is very little to do. Cleanic is a mobile service, so you do not drive to a workshop and leave the car for a day. The visit happens where the car is parked. What helps most is giving Colin the right information before the day, so the right treatment and time slot are booked.

Here are the five steps.

  1. Tell Colin where the car is parked. Cleanic is a mobile service, so the visit happens where the car is. Say whether it is at home, the office, an MSCP, a condo or a landed property, because an onsite surcharge may apply for MSCP, condo and landed property locations.
  2. Describe what you have seen. Mention cockroaches, small insects, droppings or food residue, and where in the car you noticed them. This helps Colin advise which treatment suits the car.
  3. Choose the treatment. The $118 MIRAKN Express is the shorter, preventive option with no vacuum. The full MIRAKN dry gas fumigation, from $180, keeps the car sealed longer and includes a vacuum. Steam cleaning can be added to the full fumigation in one visit.
  4. Clear out rubbish and personal items. Take out food wrappers, rubbish and your own belongings before the visit. This is a practical tip, not a rule: it makes the tidy-up quicker, and Colin can tell you if your car needs anything more.
  5. Plan the time and ask what you are unsure about. Set aside about 1 hour for the Express, about 1.5 hours for the full fumigation, or about 3 hours with steam cleaning. If you are not sure whether you need to be present, or about anything else, ask Colin on WhatsApp before the day.

What Does Each Treatment Involve?

Knowing what will happen helps you plan. The MIRAKN Express seals the car with the dry gas for about 30 minutes, followed by about 30 minutes of tidy-up that clears dead pests and does a basic tidy, with no vacuum. The full treatment seals the car for about 1 hour, followed by about 30 minutes of tidy-up, and includes a vacuum. For the full timing breakdown, read how long car fumigation takes.

  • MIRAKN Express: $118, about 1 hour, no vacuum, no steam cleaning
  • Full MIRAKN dry gas fumigation: from $180, about 1.5 hours, includes a vacuum
  • Full fumigation plus steam cleaning: about 3 hours in one visit

What Should I Ask Colin Before the Visit?

Some details are best confirmed by message rather than assumed. A short WhatsApp to Colin with your car model, parking location and what you have noticed covers most of it. Questions worth asking include:

  • Which treatment suits what you have seen
  • Whether an onsite surcharge applies at your parking location
  • Whether you need to be present during the visit
  • Whether anything in the car should be removed or left in place

Is the Treatment Safe?

Cleanic confirms that its non-oily MIRAKN treatment is safe for your vehicle and family. MIRAKN is a pest-control treatment only. Cleaning, steam cleaning and odour removal are separate processes, and Cleanic will tell you if your car needs them as well. If you have a specific concern, such as a baby or a pregnancy, ask Colin when you book, and see car fumigation and pregnancy.
