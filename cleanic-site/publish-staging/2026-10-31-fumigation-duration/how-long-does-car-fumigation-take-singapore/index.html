Car Fumigation Guide

How Long Does Car Fumigation Take in Singapore? Express, Full and Steam Times

How long you should plan for each Cleanic treatment, what happens in that time, and what changes the length of the visit.

Oct 31, 2026 · Cleanic Detailing Team · Singapore
Short answer: With Cleanic in Singapore, the $118 MIRAKN Express takes about 1 hour, the full MIRAKN dry gas fumigation (from $180) takes about 1.5 hours, and full fumigation plus steam cleaning in one visit takes about 3 hours. Cleanic comes to your car, so the time is the visit itself, not a day without your car.

How Long Does Car Fumigation Take?

The honest answer is that it depends on which treatment your car needs. People often assume fumigation means leaving the car at a workshop for the day. Cleanic is a mobile service, so the work happens where the car is parked, and the visit is measured in hours, not days.

Here are the three times to plan around.

  • MIRAKN Express: $118, about 1 hour, no steam cleaning, no vacuum
  • Full MIRAKN dry gas fumigation: from $180, about 1.5 hours, includes a vacuum
  • Full fumigation plus steam cleaning: about 3 hours in one visit

What Happens During the $118 MIRAKN Express?

The MIRAKN Express is the shorter, preventive option. The car is sealed with the dry gas for about 30 minutes, followed by about 30 minutes of tidy-up, which includes clearing dead pests and a basic tidy. There is no vacuum and no steam cleaning, which is why it fits into about 1 hour.

What Happens During the Full MIRAKN Fumigation?

The full treatment, from $180, keeps the car sealed with the gas for about 1 hour, followed by about 30 minutes of tidy-up, and it includes a vacuum. That makes about 1.5 hours in total. If you want to understand how the gas works, our page on MIRAKN dry gas fumigation explains it, and the car fumigation service page covers the service itself.

How Long With Steam Cleaning Added?

Steam cleaning is a separate process from the pest treatment. When it is added to the full fumigation in one visit, the whole session takes about 3 hours. Plan for that if the car also has spills, stains or food residue that need physical cleaning. You can read about car steam cleaning separately.

Why Is the Express So Much Shorter?

It comes down to two differences. The sealed time is about 30 minutes for the Express and about 1 hour for the full treatment, and the full treatment adds a vacuum while the Express does not. Neither is better in every case. The Express suits a preventive visit, and the full treatment suits a car where you have already seen pests or want a deeper session. Colin will advise which one suits your car.

How to Plan Around the Visit

Because Cleanic travels to you, you can book a slot when the car is parked and you are nearby, such as at home or at the office. A few things help the visit run to plan.

  • Tell Colin where the car is parked, since an onsite surcharge may apply for MSCP, condo and landed property locations
  • Describe what you have seen, such as cockroaches, small insects or food residue, so the right treatment is chosen
  • Say if you also want steam cleaning, so the slot is about 3 hours instead of 1.5
  • Take out personal items and rubbish before the visit so the tidy-up is quicker

If you are still deciding between the options, our guide to car fumigation cost in Singapore sets out what is included at each price.

Is the Treatment Safe?

Cleanic confirms that its non-oily MIRAKN treatment is safe for your vehicle and family. MIRAKN is a pest-control treatment only. Cleaning, steam cleaning and odour removal are separate processes, and Cleanic will tell you if your car needs them as well.
