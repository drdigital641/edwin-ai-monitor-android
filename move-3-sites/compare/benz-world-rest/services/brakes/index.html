Brakes

Brake inspection and repair in Johor Bahru

Brake inspection and repair at Benz World in Permas Jaya, Johor Bahru, for Mercedes-Benz, BMW, British and American makes.

WhatsApp us

Brake inspection and repair is part of our chassis and suspension work in Permas Jaya, Johor Bahru. Book a check if you hear a squeal or a grind, the steering shudders under braking, the pedal feels soft, or a brake warning shows on the dash.

If the brakes feel unsafe, do not keep driving the car. Ask us about vehicle pick-up instead.
