District 15 questions

Which areas are in District 15?

District 15 covers Katong, Joo Chiat, Amber Road, Marine Parade, Meyer Road, Tanjong Rhu and Siglap (postal sectors 42 to 45).

Which MRT stations are in District 15?

Six Thomson-East Coast Line stations: Tanjong Rhu (TE23), Katong Park (TE24), Tanjong Katong (TE25), Marine Parade (TE26), Marine Terrace (TE27) and Siglap (TE28). Eunos, Kembangan and Paya Lebar on the East-West Line sit just north in D14.

Which schools are in District 15?

Primary schools include Tao Nan, Haig Girls', CHIJ (Katong) Primary, Tanjong Katong Primary, Ngee Ann Primary, and Opera Estate Primary. Secondary and JC options include St. Patrick's School, Dunman High, Victoria School, Chung Cheng High (Main), Tanjong Katong Girls', CHIJ Katong Convent and Victoria JC.
