What we do

  1. S11.1

    After an auto scan, the Topology tab draws every control module the scanner found, connected to the OBD port through the gateway, bus by bus.

  2. S11.2

    Modules are colour-coded. Green modules answered with no fault. On this BMW, two modules were flagged in orange for faults.

  3. S11.3

    You can switch between the Topology map and the List view, then go straight to Fault Scan, Quick Erase or a Report from the same screen.

Good to know

  • A map helps when several modules show faults: modules on the same bus with faults often point to one wiring or network problem, not many broken parts.
  • K Tech’s note calls this topology mapping version 2. Newer Autel tablets such as the MaxiSys Ultra S2 list Topology 3.0 in their brochure.
  • Coverage depends on the car and on the Autel model and software. WhatsApp us the make, model and year.

Questions

What is topology mapping on an Autel scanner?

A picture of the car’s network: every control module the scanner found, linked to the gateway and bus it sits on, with its status shown in colour.

What do the colours mean?

Green means the module answered with no fault. Orange marks a module with fault codes. The legend at the top of the screen explains each colour.

Which Autel tools have topology mapping?

Many MaxiSys tablets do. WhatsApp us the jobs you do and we suggest the model that fits.
