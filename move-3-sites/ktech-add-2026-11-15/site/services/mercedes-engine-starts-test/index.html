Mercedes-Benz “Number of engine starts” test with an Autel scanner

Read the starter counters before you replace a starter.

What we do

  1. S10.1

    The test runs from the engine control unit: Mercedes-Benz › Automatic selection › Auto scan › ECM (Engine Control Unit) › Special function › Perform test “Number of engine starts”.

  2. S10.2

    The screen lists the odometer reading from the CAN bus, the travel distance since the last service due, the number of engine starts, and the travel distance since the last starter replacement.

  3. S10.3

    A note at the top gives the result. On the car in our photo it read “Do not replace starter”.

How it works

  1. 01

    Connect

    Autel scanner on the OBD port, auto scan.

  2. 02

    Open ECM

    Special function, engine starts test.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Read the counters before changing parts.

Good to know

  • Cars with ECO start/stop crank far more often than older cars, so the starter counter is a useful number before a starter job.
  • The counters back up your own checks. Still test the battery and charging first: a weak battery is a common reason for slow cranking.
  • Which special functions show depends on the car, its engine ECU and the Autel model and software. WhatsApp us the model and year to check.

Questions

What is the “Number of engine starts” test?

A special function in the engine control unit of many Mercedes-Benz cars. It shows counters linked to the starter, such as the number of engine starts and the distance since the starter was last replaced.

Can an Autel scanner run it?

Yes. The photo on this page shows an Autel intelligent diagnostic scanner running the test on a Mercedes-Benz, from the ECM special functions.

Does it tell me to replace the starter?

The screen gives a status note, for example “Do not replace starter”. Use it together with your battery, charging and cranking checks.
