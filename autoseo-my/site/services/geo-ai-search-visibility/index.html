Questions, answered

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation): common questions

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimisation is the work of making your business easy for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Perplexity to find, understand and mention when people ask them for recommendations or advice.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. AI tools decide their own answers and they change over time. What we can do is make sure accurate, consistent information about your business is out there for them to use.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. AI tools often rely on the same pages and signals that search engines use, so good SEO is the base that GEO builds on.
