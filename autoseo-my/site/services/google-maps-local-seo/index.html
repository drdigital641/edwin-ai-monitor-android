Why it matters

Google Maps & Local SEO

When a motorist searches “tyre shop near me”, Google usually shows a map with a handful of businesses before any website. Your Google Business Profile decides whether you appear there and whether people trust you enough to tap. Local SEO keeps that profile complete, accurate and active.

What it covers

What we work on

  • A complete Google Business Profile: the right categories, services, hours and service areas
  • Your business name and details kept the same on your profile, website and directories
  • Regular profile posts and photos that show your real work
  • A simple routine for asking happy customers for Google reviews, and replying to reviews

Questions, answered

Google Maps & Local SEO: common questions

Why doesn’t my business show on Google Maps?

Common reasons are an unverified or incomplete profile, the wrong main category, or details that don’t match your website. The free audit checks these first.

Can you get us more Google reviews?

We set up an easy, honest way to ask your real customers for reviews. We never buy reviews or write fake ones, as that breaks Google’s rules and can get a profile suspended.

Do I need a shop address to appear on Maps?

No. Mobile services can use a service-area profile that lists the areas they cover instead of a public address.
