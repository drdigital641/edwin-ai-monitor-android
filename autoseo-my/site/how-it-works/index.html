The steps

What happens at each step

Step 1

Free automotive SEO audit

Send us a WhatsApp message with your business name and location. We check how you appear on Google Search, Google Maps and AI search today, and what is holding you back.

  • Your Google Business Profile and Maps listing
  • Your website, if you have one: speed, mobile layout and pages
  • How you show up for the searches your customers use
  • What AI tools can find about your business

Step 2

A plan for your business

We explain what we found in plain language and suggest what to fix first. The plan is built around your services, your areas and the customers you want more of.

  • Priorities, most important first
  • Which services and areas to focus on
  • A quote on WhatsApp, with no obligation

Step 3

We do the work

We carry out the agreed work across SEO, AEO and GEO, from your website and Google profile to content that answers your customers’ questions.

  • Website and page improvements
  • Google Maps and local SEO
  • Content, FAQs and structured data

Step 4

Review and improve

We look at what is bringing in enquiries and keep improving it. The focus stays on qualified enquiries, WhatsApp leads, calls and customers, not just traffic.

  • What people search to find you
  • Which pages bring enquiries
  • Next improvements, agreed with you

Your part

What we need from you

A short WhatsApp chat about your business: what you do, the areas you serve and the customers you want more of. Real photos of your premises and work help a lot. Before anything that describes your business goes live, you check it for accuracy.
