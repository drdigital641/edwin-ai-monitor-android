Google Business Profile for a car workshop: what to fill in first

A complete Google Business Profile for a car workshop is often the first thing a motorist sees: on Google Maps, in the local results and when they search your name. Most workshops have a profile, but many leave half of it empty. Here is what to fill in first, in order.

  • Published 13 Nov 2026
  • Read time 5 min

Short answer

Start with the basics Google uses to match you to searches: your real business name, the most accurate primary category, a correct pin or service area, your phone and WhatsApp number, and opening hours, including holidays. Then add your services, real photos of the workshop, and reply to every review.

1. Name, category and location

Use the name on your signboard and business registration, exactly as customers know it. Google's own guidelines say not to add keywords or a city to the name, and listings that do can be suspended.

Pick the primary category that best describes the main job you do, for example "Auto repair shop", "Car battery store" or "Tire shop". You can add a few secondary categories for other work you really do. Then check the map pin sits on your entrance, not the middle of the road.

2. Hours, phone and website

  1. Opening hoursSet your normal hours, and add special hours for public holidays and festive closures before they happen.
  2. Phone numberUse the number that is actually answered during opening hours. If customers mostly WhatsApp you, say so in the description.
  3. WebsiteLink to your own website, ideally a page that matches what the profile says about your services and area.

3. Services and description

Add the services you really offer, using the words motorists search for, such as servicing, brake repair, car aircond repair or battery replacement. Keep the description factual: what you do, which cars you work on and the area you serve.

  • Only list services you actually do
  • Use the same business name, phone number and address as on your website and social pages
  • Avoid prices or promises you cannot keep up to date

4. Photos and reviews

Upload real photos: the shopfront so people can find you, the service bays, and finished work. Blur number plates before posting.

Ask happy customers for a review after the job, and reply to every review, good or bad, politely and briefly. Never offer gifts or discounts in return for reviews; Google's rules do not allow it.

Keep it current. A profile with old hours or a closed phone line loses calls, even if everything else is right.

Questions, answered

Questions about this topic

Is a Google Business Profile free for a workshop?

Yes. Creating and managing a Google Business Profile is free. You need a Google account and to verify that you run the business, usually by video, phone or postcard, depending on what Google offers you.

Can I add my area or "best" to my business name?

No. Google's guidelines say the name should match your real-world signage and branding. Extra keywords, locations or claims in the name break the rules and can get the listing suspended.

How do I get more reviews without breaking the rules?

Ask every customer after the job, send them your review link on WhatsApp, and reply to every review. Do not pay for reviews, offer rewards for them or post reviews for yourself.
