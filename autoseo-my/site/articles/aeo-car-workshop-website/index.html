AEO for a car workshop: write the answers motorists ask

AEO for a car workshop means shaping your website so that search engines and AI assistants can lift a clear, correct answer straight from your pages. AEO stands for answer engine optimisation. Motorists now ask full questions, such as why their aircond is not cold, and the page that answers first and plainly is the one that gets quoted.

  • Published 15 Nov 2026
  • Read time 5 min

Short answer

AEO means answering the real question a motorist types or speaks in the first two or three sentences of a page, then adding detail below. For a workshop that means one page per question or service, a short direct answer, clear headings, FAQ markup that matches the text on the page, and the same name, phone and area everywhere.

1. Start from the questions motorists really ask

List the questions customers ask you on the phone or on WhatsApp: why is my battery flat in the morning, how often should I service, what does this warning light mean. Those are the questions to write for. Do not invent questions nobody asks.

2. Put the answer first

  1. Answer in the first linesGive the direct answer in two or three sentences at the top, in plain words, then explain below.
  2. Use question headingsUse headings that are the question itself, so each section can stand on its own as an answer.
  3. One question per pageKeep one main question or service per page so it is clear what the page answers.

3. Make the page easy for machines to read

Search engines and AI assistants read the text that is actually on the page. Keep the answer in normal visible text, not only in an image or a pop-up.

  • Add FAQ markup (FAQPage structured data) only for questions and answers that are visible on the page
  • Use the same business name, phone number and area as on your Google Business Profile
  • Keep opening hours, services and contact details current
  • Only write facts you can stand behind: no prices or promises you cannot keep up to date

4. Keep it honest and up to date

No one can promise that a page will be quoted by an AI assistant. What you control is the quality: correct, plain answers about the work you really do, kept current. That is also what helps a motorist who finds you through normal search.

Start small: write answers to your five most common questions, then add one more each week.

Questions, answered

Questions about this topic

What does AEO mean?

AEO stands for answer engine optimisation. It means writing and structuring your pages so that search engines and AI assistants can find a clear answer to a question and show it to the person asking.

Is AEO different from normal SEO?

It builds on it. You still need a fast, crawlable website and a good Google Business Profile. AEO adds a focus on direct answers, question headings and matching FAQ markup.

Does AEO guarantee my workshop will be quoted by an AI assistant?

No. Nobody can promise that. Clear, correct and current answers on your pages make it possible for search and AI assistants to use them.
