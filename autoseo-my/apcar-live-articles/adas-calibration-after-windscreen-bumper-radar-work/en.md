# ADAS calibration in Kuantan after windscreen, bumper or radar work

Need ADAS calibration in Kuantan after windscreen, bumper or radar work? If ACC or lane keep stopped working afterwards, the radar or camera may no longer point the right way. Here is how to tell and what we do.

> After windscreen, bumper or radar work, the front radar or camera may need aiming again. If ACC or lane keep has switched off, we read the code, aim it in our bay with Autel / MaxiSys equipment, then road test.

## What to look for after the work

- ACC or lane keep switched off
- "Check System" on the dash
- Pre-crash system malfunction
- Lane keep pulls the wrong way

![ACC warning on a Japan-spec Civic](/assets/img/cluster-acc-960.webp "960x1280")

## Why the work can matter

ACC, lane keep (LKAS), lane departure (LDW) and pre-crash use a front radar and camera that must point exactly right.

The radar sits behind the bumper and the camera looks through the windscreen, so work in those areas can leave them out of line. When a code such as "misalignment millimeter wave radar" appears, the system switches off until it is aimed again.

![Front radar module behind the bumper (Honda)](/assets/img/radar-module-960.webp "960x1280")

## Our 4 steps

1. **Read**: Scan for radar / camera codes
2. **Set up**: Target stand placed for the car
3. **Aim**: Radar or camera aiming on the scanner
4. **Road test**: ACC and lane keep checked on the road
