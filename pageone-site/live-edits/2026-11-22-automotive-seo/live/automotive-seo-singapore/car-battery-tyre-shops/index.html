Battery & tyre shop SEO: your questions

How do car battery shops get customers from Google?

Most battery searches are urgent: "car battery flat", "car battery replacement near me", "24 hour car battery". The driver is stuck and taps the first business that shows a price and a call button. A battery shop needs a fast phone page with prices by battery size or car model, its service area and a big call and WhatsApp button.

Should a battery or tyre shop show prices online?

Yes. Drivers compare prices before they call. A price table by battery size, brand or tyre size, with what is included (installation, old battery disposal, warranty), wins the click and the call.

Does Page One have results for car battery searches?

Are call taps or WhatsApp taps better for a battery shop?

Both. Stuck drivers often call, while drivers planning ahead message on WhatsApp. We put both buttons on every page and count each one separately.
