What Google shows today

Insurers hold the claim searches. The long searches are open.

On 22 Nov 2026 we searched Google Singapore for accident and spray painting terms. For "accident claim workshop singapore", the top three were insurance companies. For "car spray painting singapore", it was a car services marketplace and a "best workshops" list. For "spray painting workshop SEO", page one was list articles and overseas web designers.

That tells you two things. The broad terms are hard to win head-on. But the searches drivers type when they're standing next to a dented car (the area, the car, the panel, the damage) are wide open, and so is the Google Maps pack. That's where we put your pages.

Accident repair & spray painting SEO: your questions

How do accident repair workshops get customers from Google?

Drivers search right after an accident or when they see a scratch or dent. They type things like "accident claim workshop", "car scratch repair price" or "spray painting near me". A workshop needs a page for each of those, with clear steps, price ranges where it can give them, real before-and-after photos and a WhatsApp button for photos of the damage.

Who ranks on Google for accident claim searches in Singapore?

When we checked "accident claim workshop singapore" on 22 Nov 2026, the top results were insurance companies' own pages. Independent workshops can still win the longer searches, such as the area, the car brand or the type of damage, and the Google Maps pack.

Can I show spray painting prices on my website?

Yes, and you should, as ranges by panel or job type ("from S$… per panel") with a note that the final quote depends on the damage. Pages with prices get far more WhatsApp messages than pages that say "call for price".

Do before-and-after photos help SEO?

Yes. Real before-and-after photos of your own jobs, with the car model and the job in the caption, help Google and drivers trust the page. We never use stock photos as proof.
