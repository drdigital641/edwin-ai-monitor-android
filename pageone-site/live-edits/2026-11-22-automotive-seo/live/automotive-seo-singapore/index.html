The automotive SEO system, step by step

  1. 1

    Free baseline check

    We search Google Singapore for your top jobs and areas on phone and desktop, and show you where you rank today and who sits above you.

  2. 2

    One page per job, car and area

    Servicing, aircon, brakes, batteries, accident claims: each service gets a full page with your real prices and photos. Area pages cover places within about 10 km of your workshop.

  3. 3

    Google Maps fixed

    Your Google Business Profile gets the right main category, phone, website link, hours and photos, because the map pack often sits above every website.

  4. 4

    Answers drivers search for

    Articles that answer real questions ("why is my car aircon not cold", "how long do brake pads last"), each with your price box and WhatsApp button.

  5. 5

    Every WhatsApp tap counted

    We count every WhatsApp and call tap on your website, and recheck your rankings, so you see what is working.
