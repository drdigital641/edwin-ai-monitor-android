Questions

SEO pricing: your questions, answered

How much does SEO cost in Singapore with Page One?

S$20 a month on the Get Found plan (introductory price), with no setup fee and no minimum contract. Optional SEO Boosts are S$10 each. The only other cost is your domain name, usually S$15–30 a year, which is registered in your name and paid by you directly.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. You pay monthly in advance and can cancel anytime by WhatsApp. If you cancel, your site stays live until the end of the month you paid for, and you keep your domain.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Building your website (or SEO landing page), Google Business Profile, Search Console and Bing setup are all part of the S$20 a month.

What happens when the introductory price ends?

S$20 is an introductory price until we reach our early-client target. Any new price will stay close to S$20.

What is not included?

Paid ads, running your social media accounts and buying links. Your domain name is paid by you directly. We also don't promise rankings, because Google decides them and no honest provider can guarantee them.

Do I need a website before I sign up?

No. If you don't have one, we build it on your own domain as part of the S$20 plan. If you already have one, we add an SEO landing page on a subdomain and leave your current site as it is.
