SEO pricing: your questions, answered

How much does SEO cost in Singapore with Page One?

S$50 a month on the Get Found plan, for anyone who registers by 31 Dec 2026, with no setup fee and no minimum contract. From 2027 the price may rise, depending on demand, and we'll tell you before it changes. Optional SEO Boosts are S$10 per run. The only other cost is your domain name, usually S$15–30 a year, which is registered in your name and paid by you directly.

Is there a minimum contract?

No. You pay monthly in advance and can cancel anytime by WhatsApp. If you cancel, your site stays live until the end of the month you paid for, and you keep your domain.

Is there a setup fee?

No. Building your website (or fixing your current one), Google Business Profile, Search Console and Bing setup are all part of the S$50 a month.

Will the price go up?

It may. S$50 a month is our price for anyone who registers by 31 Dec 2026. From 2027 the price may rise, depending on demand, and we'll tell you before it changes. There's no minimum contract, so you can cancel anytime.

What is not included?

Paid ads, running your social media accounts and buying links. Your domain name is paid by you directly. We also don't promise rankings, because Google decides them and no honest provider can guarantee them.

Do I need a website before I sign up?

No. If you don't have one, we build it on your own domain, free, as part of the S$50 plan. If you already have one, we fix its on-page technical SEO on your current website, or redesign it free if you ask. If you'd rather keep your site untouched, we can add an SEO landing page on a subdomain next to it instead.
