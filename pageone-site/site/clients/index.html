Questions about our clients

Are these real businesses?

Yes. Every website on this page is live on the business's own domain, so you can open it and check. Edwin Garage is our founder's own car workshop, where the system was first built. The others are businesses we build and run websites for.

Where do the Google numbers come from?

From Google Search Console, Google's own report of how a website performs in Google Search. We collect the final data automatically and don't edit it. The figures on this page cover the 28 days to 11 October 2026 (14 Sep to 11 Oct 2026), compared with the 28 days before (17 Aug to 13 Sep 2026).

Why do some websites have no numbers?

Google needs time to find and test a new website. A site launched this month has no Search Console data yet, so we show what we built instead of guessing. The numbers will appear here once Google has them.

How can it be good if it only costs S$20 a month?

The price is low because AI now does the daily SEO work that used to be billed by the hour, not because steps are skipped. The websites and numbers on this page are the proof, not a promise: results vary by business, and newer sites show smaller numbers.

How often is this page updated?

We refresh it from Search Console regularly, and add new client websites once they are live on their own domain. The date of the data is always shown.
