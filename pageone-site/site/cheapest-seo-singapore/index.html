Why SEO used to cost so much

You were paying for hours, not results

Doing SEO properly is a lot of small jobs, repeated every week: checking pages for problems, fixing them, writing new content, updating business listings, telling Google about changes and reporting what happened. Done by hand, every one of those jobs is someone's billable time.

Why SEO is never one-and-done

Search engines and customers both want what's current

Think about it as a customer: when you search, you want the latest information, not a page nobody has touched in three years. Search engines think the same way. Google keeps re-visiting websites to look for changes, and for many searches it prefers pages that are up to date.

A website that's built once and left alone slowly falls behind sites that keep adding useful content and fixing problems. That's why SEO works best as a small, continuous effort, and why doing it by hand gets expensive. Automating it is what makes continuous SEO affordable.
