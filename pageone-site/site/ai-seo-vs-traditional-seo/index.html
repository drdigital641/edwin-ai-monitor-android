The short answer

Price follows labour. AI made the labour cheap.

Most of what you pay for in SEO is people's time: checking pages, fixing problems, writing articles, updating listings and writing reports, every week. When AI does that routine work, far fewer hours are needed, so the price can fall a long way while the work still gets done, and done every day.

Cheap doesn't have to mean bad, and good doesn't have to be expensive. A price tells you how much labour went in. It doesn't tell you how good the result is. That's why we publish our results, with dates, straight from Google Search Console.

How it works

Questions

AI SEO vs traditional SEO: your questions, answered

What is the difference between AI SEO and traditional SEO?

The goal is the same: help a business show up when customers search. The difference is how the work gets done. Traditional SEO is mostly done by hand, so it's priced by the hours people spend on it. Page One uses AI and automation for the repetitive daily work, such as checking pages, reading Search Console data, writing articles and telling Google and Bing about changes. Edwin designs the strategy and the system and reviews the results.

Why is Page One so cheap?

Because most of the cost of SEO has always been people's time, and AI now does most of the routine work. The Get Found plan is S$20 a month (introductory price) because fewer hours are needed, not because steps are skipped. The same system runs Edwin Garage, Edwin's own workshop, which got 2,280 clicks from Google in the 28 days to 5 October 2026, according to Google Search Console.

Is automated SEO low quality?

Quality comes from the strategy, the site structure and what gets published, not from how many hours were billed. The results are measurable: in the 28 days to 5 October 2026, Edwin Garage appeared on page one of Google for more than 4,290 different searches and Cleanic Detailing for 1,181, according to Google Search Console. We follow Google's guidelines: no bought links, no fake reviews and no keyword stuffing.

What is GEO (generative engine optimisation)?

GEO means making a business easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to read, understand and mention. Page One does this with structured data (Schema.org), an llms.txt summary, Bing Webmaster Tools (Bing powers ChatGPT search) and content that answers customers' questions clearly. No one can guarantee an AI assistant will recommend a business, but these steps make it possible.

Does the system really work around the clock?

It runs on a schedule, every day, including weekends and public holidays. Google Search Console data is pulled in automatically, and new articles are picked from the searches Google already shows a site for. When something needs a person, it is flagged to Edwin.

Does a person still look after my site?

Yes. Edwin designs the strategy and the system, reviews the results and replies on WhatsApp personally. Automation does the legwork. It doesn't replace judgement.

Can you guarantee page one rankings?

No, and no honest provider can, because Google decides rankings. What we can show you is the same system working on real Singapore businesses, with dated Google Search Console data, and a report for every SEO Boost.

When is traditional SEO the better choice?

When you need more than search groundwork: paid ads managed for you, a large online shop with thousands of products, several countries, or a big link-building and PR campaign. An agency or an in-house team is the better fit there.
