Small business website checklist

What every small business website in Singapore needs

Whoever builds it, check your website has these. Most website price guides talk about design and page count. These are the things that decide whether customers can find you and contact you.

  • Says what you do and where, in the first screen. "Aircon servicing in Tampines", not "Welcome to our website".
  • One page per main service. Google ranks pages, not businesses. A service with no page is hard to find.
  • Fast and easy on a phone. Most people searching for a local business are on their phone.
  • One-tap WhatsApp or call button on every page, with the number you actually answer.
  • The padlock (HTTPS). Browsers warn visitors about sites without it.
  • Linked to your Google Business Profile, with the same name, phone and hours on both.
  • Business details labelled for Google and AI assistants (structured data), so ChatGPT and Google read your name, area and services correctly.
  • Search Console connected, so you can see which searches find you.
  • A privacy notice if you have a contact form. Under the PDPA, tell people what you'll use their details for.
  • Someone looking after it. Hosting renewals, security updates and new content don't stop after launch.
