Two Singapore rules to know before you launch

  • A .com.sg address needs a registered business. SGNIC, which runs .sg domains, only gives .com.sg to businesses registered (or registering) with ACRA or another recognised Singapore body. A plain .sg or .com has no such rule. If you're still registering, you can start with .com and add .com.sg later.
  • Your contact form collects personal data. Under the PDPA, if your website asks for names, phone numbers or emails, tell people what you'll use them for. A short privacy notice and a line next to the form usually does it. See the PDPC guide to notification.
