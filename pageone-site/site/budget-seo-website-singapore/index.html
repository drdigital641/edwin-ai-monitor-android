  • Who owns the domain? It should be you.
  • What renews after year one? Hosting, plugins and changes can cost more than the launch price.

  • Avoid anyone promising guaranteed first-page rankings or hundreds of backlinks.

Is a one-off SEO task enough?

A one-off task, such as optimising one page's title and headings, can help that page. But SEO never finishes: Google re-checks sites, competitors change theirs and customers search new questions. Ongoing work, checked every day, usually does more than one fix, which is why the S$50 plan is a monthly subscription.
