Short answer

How does ChatGPT pick which businesses to name?

When it searches the web, ChatGPT reads pages from its own crawler and from search partners such as Bing, then writes an answer and links the sources it used. It favours pages that answer the question directly, state facts clearly, and are backed up by other websites. So ChatGPT SEO is mostly good SEO done carefully, plus making sure AI crawlers are let in and Bing knows your site.

No one controls what ChatGPT says, so we don't promise mentions. We make sure nothing stops it from finding and understanding you.

Questions

ChatGPT SEO: your questions, answered

What is ChatGPT SEO?

ChatGPT SEO means making your business easy for ChatGPT to find, read and describe correctly when someone asks it a question with web search on. It is part of GEO, generative engine optimisation. In practice it is the same groundwork as good SEO, plus a few AI-specific steps: allowing OpenAI's search crawler, connecting Bing, and publishing clear facts AI tools can quote.

How does ChatGPT decide which businesses to mention?

When ChatGPT searches the web, it reads pages from its own crawler (OAI-SearchBot) and from search partners such as Bing, then writes an answer and links its sources. Pages that answer the question directly, state facts like price and area clearly, and are mentioned by other websites are easier for it to use. OpenAI does not publish a ranking formula, and no one can control what it says.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and no honest provider can. ChatGPT's answers change with the question, the user and the day. What we can do is make sure nothing stops ChatGPT from finding and understanding your site, and keep adding pages that answer what your customers ask.

How do I check if ChatGPT knows my business?

Open ChatGPT, turn on search, and ask the way a customer would, for example "best aircon servicing in Tampines" or "what does [your business name] do?". See whether you are named, and whether the details are right. Try a few different wordings, because answers vary.

Does this help with Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot too?

Yes. The same steps (clear facts, structured data, crawlers allowed, Bing and Google connected, consistent details everywhere) help every AI assistant that searches the web. Copilot uses Bing directly, and Gemini draws on Google.
