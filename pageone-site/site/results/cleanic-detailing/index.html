The business

Mobile car grooming and fumigation

Cleanic Detailing goes to the customer's car to groom, steam-clean and fumigate it. Customers find this kind of service by searching for the job and the area, so every service needed its own page that answers what people type.

Its Search Console data starts on 20 June 2026. The average position dipped to 13.5 in August, while Google began showing the site for many more searches, which usually pulls the average down for a while. It improved every month after that: 10.4 in September, 7.8 in October.

What you can take from it

Lessons for your own business

One page per service

Grooming, steam cleaning, fumigation and odour removal each have their own page, so each can rank for its own searches instead of one page trying to cover everything.

Write about the customer's problem

Many searches are problems, not service names: a smell, cockroaches, a spill. Guides that answer those bring in people who need the service now.

Let the data pick the topics

New articles target searches Search Console already shows the site for, so effort goes where Google is already paying attention.
