Case study 1 · Car workshop, Ang Mo Kio

Edwin Garage: from first Google data to 2,508 clicks in 28 days

Edwin Garage is our founder's own car workshop, and the first business to run on the Page One system. Its Search Console data starts on 26 June 2026. Within three months, Google was sending it more than 2,000 visitors a month.

  • 2,401 clicks from Google in September 2026, up from 1,369 in July
  • 94,658 times shown on Google in September, up from 38,611 in July
  • Average position between 6.2 and 6.6 every full month since launch, which is page one
  • On page one for 4,200+ different searches in the 28 days to 28 Oct 2026

What we did

  • A fast website with a page for each service customers search for
  • Google Business Profile, Search Console and Bing set up
  • Business details marked up for Google and AI assistants, plus llms.txt
  • Helpful articles published regularly, picked from real Search Console data

Case study 2 · Mobile car detailing

Cleanic Detailing: page one for 1,327 searches within four months

Cleanic Detailing is a mobile car grooming and fumigation service run by Colin Loo. Its Search Console data starts on 20 June 2026. It now ranks in the top 3 for searches like “car grooming singapore” and “steam cleaning”.

  • 505 clicks from Google in September 2026, up from 188 in July
  • 39,551 times shown on Google in September, up from 16,872 in July
  • Average position improved to 7.9 in the 28 days to 28 Oct 2026, from 10.4 the 28 days before
  • On page one for 1,327 different searches, 669 of them in the top 3

What we did

  • Service pages for car grooming, steam cleaning, fumigation and odour removal
  • Guides on the problems customers search for, like cockroaches or vomit in the car

  • New articles chosen from the searches Google already shows the site for

Reading the numbers

Questions about these results

Where do these numbers come from?

Every figure on this page comes from Google Search Console, Google's own report of how a website performs in Google Search. We collect the final data automatically and don't edit it. Data runs to 28 October 2026.

What does average position mean?

Where the website appears in Google results, on average, when someone searches. Positions 1 to 10 are page one. A lower number is better.

Will my business get the same results?

We can't promise that, and no honest provider can. Results depend on your industry, competition and how long your site has been running. Edwin Garage and Cleanic Detailing took about three months to get here. What we can promise is the same system and a report showing the work.
