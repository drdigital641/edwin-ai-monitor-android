The business

Financial consultant

Danny Chua is a financial consultant. Financial planning is a crowded field on Google, and a regulated one, so the site sticks to plain explanations and shows representative details that visitors can check for themselves.

Search Console data starts on 13 August 2026. In August (19 days) Google showed the site 641 times at an average position of 62.6. In the first 17 days of October it was shown 4,546 times at 36.5. Most of its searches are not on page one yet; this is what the climb looks like before they are.

What you can take from it

Lessons for your own business

Impressions come before clicks

In the first months, Google tests a new site by showing it lower down for more searches. Clicks follow once pages move up, so a rising 'times shown' figure is the early sign to watch.

Average position moves slowly

It went from 62.6 to 36.5 here. A big average hides the searches already on page one: 106 of them in the 28 days to 17 Oct 2026.

Regulated fields need care

Plain facts, no promises, and details visitors can verify. That builds trust with people and gives Google and AI assistants something reliable to show.

Questions

About this case study

Why show a case study that is still early?

Because most new websites look like this for the first few months, and anyone selling SEO should say so. Our newer client sites are shown the same way on the results page.

Where do these numbers come from?

Google Search Console, Google's own report on how a website performs in Google Search. We collect the final data automatically and don't edit it. Data runs to 17 October 2026.

How long does SEO take for a new website?

Usually months, not weeks. Google first needs to find and test the pages, then rankings build as articles add up. We can't promise timing or rankings for any business.
