Directory listing plan

No website of your own? Get a listing page instead.

A directory listing is one page about your business: your photos, services, area and opening hours, with WhatsApp and call buttons. You don't buy a domain, and we look after the page.

Who it is for

Businesses that want to be found, without a website of their own

It suits owners who want customers to find and contact them, but don't want to pay for a domain or run a full website. Listings are for car workshops first, shown on our car workshop directory site, and the directory is not live yet.

What you get

One page that does the basics

  • One listing page with photos of your work
  • Your services, the area you serve and your opening hours
  • WhatsApp and call buttons, so enquiring takes one tap
  • A link to your Google Business Profile, if you have one
  • WhatsApp taps counted and reported to you every month

Everything on the page comes from you, and we check it with you before it goes up.

Questions

Directory listing: your questions, answered

How much does a directory listing cost?

A basic listing card is free. The Verified listing page is S$20 a month. This is the Verified listing, not the earlier S$20 early-bird SEO plan. The own-website plan is S$50 a month.

Do I need to buy a domain?

No. We set up your listing page and keep it running. You don't buy, register or renew a domain for it.

Will a listing bring me enquiries?

We can't promise that. A listing gives customers a page to check you out and a one-tap way to message you. We count WhatsApp taps on it and send you the numbers every month, so you can see what it brings.
