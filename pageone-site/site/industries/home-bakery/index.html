Before you open

Opening a home bakery: what to set up first

  • Check the home-based business rules. Read the current HDB or URA home-based business rules and SFA's guidance on home-based food businesses, and follow them. We don't give legal advice, but we can show the details customers should know, such as halal status, on your site.
  • Pick your name and domain. Choose a name you can keep, then register the matching domain in your name (usually S$15–30 a year). Use the same name on Instagram, Google and your website.
  • Decide what you'll list first. Start with your best-selling bakes and your order process. You can add more items as you go.
  • Get on Google Maps. A Google Business Profile can show your service area without showing your home address. We set it up with you.

Questions

Home bakeries: your questions, answered

I bake from home. Can I be on Google Maps without showing my address?

Yes. Google lets you list the area you serve and hide your full address. We set it up that way for home-based businesses.

Is Instagram not enough for a home bakery?

Instagram is great for showing your bakes, but Google and ChatGPT can't rank it like a website. A website catches the customers who search instead of scroll, and it's yours.

I haven't started selling yet. Is it too early?

No. Setting up your website and Google listing before you launch means you're findable from your first order. It's usually live within 7 days.
