About the free check

Is the website check really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email needed. Enter your website address and you get your score and the list of fixes on screen in under a minute.

What does the check look at?

Google basics (title, description, headings, indexing, sitemap), mobile setup, security (HTTPS), your business details for Google Maps, structured data, whether AI assistants like ChatGPT can read your site, and page speed.

What should I do with the results?

Each item is explained in plain English with why it matters. You can fix them yourself, or ask Edwin on WhatsApp and he'll tell you honestly what's worth fixing first.

Can I check a competitor's website?

Yes. The check only reads what's publicly visible, so you can compare your site with anyone's.
