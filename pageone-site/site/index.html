SEO, explained simply

What we do for you, in plain English

SEO (search engine optimisation) means making it easy for Google and AI assistants to find your business and recommend you. In practice:

📍

Show up on Google Maps

When someone nearby searches for what you do, your business appears on the map with your hours, photos, reviews and a call button.

Technical nameGoogle Business ProfileLocal SEO

🔍

Appear when customers search

Your pages use the exact words customers type into Google, like "aircon repair Tampines", so Google knows to show you for them.

Technical nameKeyword researchOn-page SEOTitle tags

🤖

Get recommended by ChatGPT and AI

More people now ask ChatGPT or Gemini "who's good for this near me?". We label your business details so AI assistants can read them and describe you correctly.

Technical nameStructured data (Schema.org)llms.txtGEO / AEO

📱

Fast and easy on a phone

Most customers search on their phone. A slow or fiddly website makes them press back and call someone else.

Technical nameMobile-first designCore Web VitalsHTTPS / SSL

💬

Enquiries straight to your WhatsApp

Every page has a WhatsApp button, so a customer who's ready to buy can message you in one tap.

Technical nameConversion optimisationClick-to-chat

📊

See that it's working

We connect Google's own free report, so you can see how many people found you, what they searched and how many clicked.

Technical nameGoogle Search ConsoleBing Webmaster Tools
