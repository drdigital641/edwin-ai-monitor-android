Affordable, not risky

How do you tell affordable SEO from cheap, risky SEO?

Affordable SEO keeps the price low by doing the routine work efficiently. Risky SEO keeps it low by skipping steps or breaking Google's rules. These six warning signs tell them apart.

Warning signWhy it's a problem
"Guaranteed #1 on Google"Google decides rankings. Google's own guidance warns against anyone who guarantees them.
Hundreds of backlinks for a few dollarsBought links break Google's spam policies and can hurt your site.
No access to your own dataIf you can't see your own Search Console, you can't check what you're paying for.
A long minimum term for a small packageKnow the minimum term before the first invoice. Good work doesn't need a lock-in.
Vague monthly reportsAsk for a list of what was done, not "ongoing optimisation".
The domain registered in their nameYour domain should be yours, so you can leave with your website and your rankings.

Affordable SEO services: your questions, answered

What are affordable SEO services?

How much do affordable SEO services cost in Singapore?

As a general guide, small-business SEO retainers from agencies are often around S$500 to S$3,000 or more a month, sometimes with a setup or audit fee. These are typical ranges, not quotes. Are affordable SEO packages worth it for a small business?

They are when the package covers the essentials and keeps working every month: a site Google can read, a Google Business Profile, Search Console access so you can check results, new pages for real customer searches and regular fixes. What is the difference between affordable SEO and cheap SEO?

Affordable SEO is low-cost because the routine work is done efficiently, and you can see the results in your own Search Console. Cheap-and-risky SEO is low-cost because steps are skipped or rules are broken: bought links, copied content, guaranteed rankings, or no access to your own data.
