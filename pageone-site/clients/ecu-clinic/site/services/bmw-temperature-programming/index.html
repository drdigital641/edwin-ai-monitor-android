BMW Temperature Programming

BMW running hot at 120°C or more? We reprogram it to bring a high coolant temperature down to a stable 95°C or so.

WhatsApp 9893 1923WhatsApp usCall Ching

About this service

Many BMWs from this period run at a high coolant temperature, often 120°C or more. We reprogram the engine’s temperature setting so it runs at about 95°C instead, for steady performance and long-term engine protection.

Daily driving and the car’s other functions are not changed. Check the list below for your model, or WhatsApp us your model and year to confirm.

What we do

  • Reduces high coolant temperature to about 95°C
  • Keeps the engine at a stable operating temperature
  • No change to daily driving or other functions
  • Most BMW models from 2010 to 2018

How it works

  1. WhatsApp us the details. Send your car model, chassis number and the fault code to 9893 1923. A photo of the scan tool screen or warning light helps.
  2. We check and advise. We tell you what we can do: repair your unit, or supply a recon, used or new one.
  3. Drop off or pick-up. Bring the car or part to us at Premier @ Kaki Bukit, or use our pick-up and delivery service.
  4. Fixed, with warranty. Your car is back on the road, and the repair comes with a 6-month warranty.
