About this service

A hybrid car has a high-voltage battery pack that stores energy for the electric motor. As the pack ages, it holds less charge, and the car relies more on the engine. In time the hybrid system warning light can come on.

We replace the pack with a brand-new OEM hybrid battery, and we do the removal and fitting. WhatsApp us your car model and year, and we will check availability and price.

What we do

Signs you may need this

Not sure? WhatsApp us the fault code and we’ll tell you.

Hybrid warning light

A hybrid system warning appears on the dashboard.

Fuel use goes up

The car uses more petrol than it used to.

Engine runs more often

The engine starts more often, even at low speed.

Battery gauge jumps

The battery level drops or rises quickly.
