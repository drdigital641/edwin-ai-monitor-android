Mercedes 7-Speed & 9-Speed TCU Repair

Gearbox warning, harsh shifts or stuck in one gear? We diagnose and repair the transmission control unit (TCU) on Mercedes 7G-Tronic and 9G-Tronic automatics.

About this service

On the Mercedes 7-speed automatic (7G-Tronic, gearbox code 722.9), the transmission control unit sits inside the gearbox, on the conductor plate of the valve body. Because it lives in hot gearbox oil, faults in this unit can cause gear changes to go wrong even when the mechanical parts of the gearbox are fine.

We read the fault codes first, then work on the TCU and conductor plate. When the gearbox pan is off, we fit a new filter, pan gasket and gearbox oil on the same job. We also work on the TCU of the 9-speed 9G-Tronic.

What the conductor plate does

On the 7G-Tronic, the transmission control unit is built onto the conductor plate, which sits on the valve body inside the gearbox and also carries the gearbox speed sensors. Because it lives in hot gearbox oil, its electronics and sensors can develop faults over the years. A speed sensor or TCU fault can put the gearbox into limp mode even when the gears and clutches are fine, which is why we read the codes before anything is opened.

Questions

Which Mercedes gearboxes do you work on?
The 7-speed 7G-Tronic (722.9) and the 9-speed 9G-Tronic. Send us the car model, chassis number and fault code to check.
Do you change the gearbox oil too?
Yes. When we work on the conductor plate we fit a new filter, pan gasket and gearbox oil on the same job.
Do I have to remove and post the unit myself?
No. We read the fault codes and do the TCU and conductor plate work at our workshop in Kaki Bukit. If the car is stuck in limp mode, pick-up and delivery service is available.
Can I keep driving in limp mode?
Limp mode is meant to get the car home or to a workshop, not for daily driving. The longer the gearbox runs with a fault, the more can go wrong, so get it checked soon.
Is there a warranty?
Yes. Our repairs come with a 6-month warranty.
