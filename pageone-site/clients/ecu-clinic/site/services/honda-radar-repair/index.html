Honda Sensing warning, or a “Millimeter Wave Radar Function Error” on the scan tool? We repair your existing front radar unit instead of replacing it with a new one.

About this service

Honda Sensing driver assistance features, such as collision mitigation braking and adaptive cruise control, rely on a millimeter wave radar unit at the front of the car. When that radar unit develops a fault, Honda Sensing warnings come on and the driver assistance features can switch off.

On the scan tool this often shows as P2583, “Millimeter Wave Radar Function Error”, in the driver assistance system. We read the codes first, then repair the radar unit you already have, instead of fitting a new one.

A second code, “Millimeter Wave Radar Aiming Incomplete”, means the radar also needs to be aimed (calibrated) before the system works normally. We tell you what your car needs before any work starts.

Questions

Do I need a new radar unit?
Not always. We repair the existing front radar unit instead of replacing it.
My Honda has both ABS and radar faults. Can you fix both?
Yes. We see cars with both the VSA (ABS) unit and the front radar faulty, and repair both on the same visit. WhatsApp us all the fault codes.
Which Honda models do you work on?
Honda models with Honda Sensing and a front millimeter wave radar, such as the Vezel. WhatsApp us your model and year to check.
Is there a warranty?
Yes. Our repairs come with a 6-month warranty.
What should I send you?
