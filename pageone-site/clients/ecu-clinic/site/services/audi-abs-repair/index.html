About this service

On an Audi, the ABS unit in the engine bay combines the hydraulic pump with the brake electronics control module (address 03 on the scan tool). When the module fails, it often stores a long list of codes at once, including wheel speed sensor codes, even when the sensors themselves may be fine.

We read every code first and rule out the simple causes, such as sensors, wiring and a weak battery. Then we repair your own ABS module. ABS pumps and modules are our main work, and many car workshops send their ABS repairs to us.

What we do

  • Audi ABS module (brake electronics) repair
  • ABS hydraulic pump faults
  • Codes such as 01276 “ABS Hydraulic Pump” and 16352 “Control Module” defective
  • Volkswagen and Skoda ABS units too

Recent job: Audi A3 with 10 ABS codes

The brake electronics (03) had stored 10 codes, including:

  • 01276 ABS Hydraulic Pump: defective
  • 16352 Control Module: defective
  • 00290 and 00287 Left and right rear ABS wheel speed sensors: electrical fault in circuit
  • 01542 Rotation Rate Sensor: electrical fault in circuit
  • 00538 Reference Voltage: electrical fault in circuit
  • 01130 ABS Operation: implausible signal

With the pump, the module and several sensor circuits all faulty at once, the codes pointed to the ABS control module. We repaired the car’s own module, refitted it and cleared the faults.

This scan also had a low voltage code (terminal 30), which a weak battery can cause, so we check the battery as well. More on the lights in our guide: VW or Audi ABS and ESP lights on.

Questions

Do you repair Audi ABS modules?
Yes. We repair the ABS module on Audi cars, and on Volkswagen and Skoda too. WhatsApp us the model, year and fault codes.
Is it safe to drive with the ABS light on?
In most cases your normal brakes still work, but the anti-lock function may be off until the fault is fixed, so the wheels can lock under hard braking, especially on wet roads. If the red brake warning light is on too, or the pedal feels different, stop driving and get the car checked.
