Mercedes Steering Lock (ELV) Failure: Signs and Repair

When a Mercedes will not start and the key will not turn, the steering lock is one of the first things to check.

By The ECU Clinic · 13 Oct 2026

Many Mercedes-Benz cars have an electronic steering lock, usually called the ELV or ESL. It is a small motorised lock on the steering column. When you switch off, it locks the steering. When you insert the key or press start, the car checks the key and the ELV unlocks.

Signs of an ELV fault

  • The key will not turn in the ignition, even though it is the right key.
  • No start, no crank. Nothing happens when you try to start.
  • Steering stays locked after switching on.
  • It works sometimes. The car starts on one try and not the next, which often gets worse over time.

A flat key battery, a weak car battery or an ignition switch fault can cause similar symptoms, so these are worth ruling out.
