Privacy Policy

What we collect when you contact The ECU Clinic, and what we do with it.

Last updated 5 November 2026

Who runs this site

This website is run by The ECU Clinic, 8 Kaki Bukit Avenue 4, #06-43, Premier @ Kaki Bukit, Singapore 415875.

When you contact us

You can reach us by WhatsApp or by phone call on +65 9893 1923. This site has no contact form. What you send us, such as your name, number, car model and fault code, is used only to reply to you and to give you the service you asked for.

Counting taps on our buttons

We count how often visitors tap the WhatsApp and Call buttons, so we can see which pages help people get in touch. Each tap sends one small record to our dashboard: which button type was tapped, the page it was tapped on, whether the device is a phone or a computer, the page you came from, and a random code that lasts only while your browser tab is open. The record is stored with the time of the tap.

The tap counter does not send or store your name, phone number or message. The WhatsApp message and phone call themselves go through WhatsApp and your phone network, not through this counter. The counter does not use cookies.

Server logs

Our website host keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

No selling of data

We do not sell your details or give them to advertisers.

Your rights under the PDPA

We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012. To ask for a copy of the details we hold about you, or to have them removed, WhatsApp us on +65 9893 1923 or call the same number, and ask for Ching.
