How stamp duty works

What is Buyer's Stamp Duty (BSD)?

A tax paid on every property purchase in Singapore. For residential property it is charged in tiers: 1% on the first S$180,000, 2% on the next S$180,000, 3% on the next S$640,000, 4% on the next S$500,000, 5% on the next S$1,500,000 and 6% on the rest.

What is Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD)?

An extra tax on top of BSD for some buyers. For residential property bought from 27 April 2023: Singapore Citizens pay 0% on a first home, 20% on a second and 30% on a third or more. Singapore Permanent Residents pay 5%, 30% and 35%. Foreigners pay 60%, and most entities pay 65%.

When is stamp duty paid?

Within 14 days of exercising the Option to Purchase or signing the sale and purchase agreement. It is paid in cash or, for BSD and in some cases ABSD, partly from CPF depending on your purchase.

What if I buy jointly with someone else?

For joint purchases, ABSD is based on the buyer with the highest applicable rate. Married couples have specific remission rules. Evan can walk you through your case.
