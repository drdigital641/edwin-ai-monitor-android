Home / Selling your home

Sell your home with a clear plan and a fair price.

A good sale starts with an honest price. Evan works out what your home is likely to fetch, prepares it for the market and handles viewings, offers and paperwork.

An honest asking price

Evan looks at recent transactions for similar homes nearby, the floor, facing, condition and remaining lease, then talks you through a realistic price range and why. Pricing too high can leave a home sitting on the market; pricing too low leaves money on the table.

Know your sale proceeds before you list

Before listing, Evan helps you work out what you will receive after the sale: outstanding loan, the CPF you need to refund with accrued interest, any HDB resale levy, and fees. This matters even more if the proceeds fund your next home.

Marketing your home

  • Preparing the home for photos and viewings. See how to prepare your home for sale.
  • Listings on the major property portals and Evan's own social media.
  • Video walkthroughs of your home, like the home tours on Evan's TikTok.
  • Viewings arranged around your schedule, with feedback after each one.

HDB flat owners

Most HDB owners must meet their Minimum Occupation Period before selling, and you need to register an Intent to Sell on the HDB Flat Portal before granting an Option to Purchase. Evan helps you get the timing right, especially if you are also buying your next home.

From offer to completion

  1. Offers negotiated with you, in writing.
  2. Option to Purchase granted and exercised.
  3. HDB resale application or lawyers' conveyancing.
  4. Completion, handover of keys, and sale proceeds.

Common questions

How much commission does a seller pay?

Commission is not fixed by law. It is agreed between you and Evan in writing, using the CEA prescribed estate agency agreement, before your home is marketed.

How long does it take to sell an HDB flat?

It depends on the price, the flat and the market. Once an Option to Purchase is exercised, the HDB resale process to completion usually takes a few more weeks. Evan will give you a timeline for your case.

Can I sell and buy at the same time?

Yes, and timing matters. See the HDB upgrading page for how Evan lines up the sale of one home with the purchase of the next.

Do I need to renovate before selling?

Usually not. Simple repairs, decluttering and good photos often matter more. Evan will tell you honestly what is worth doing.

Thinking of selling?

Tell Evan the property type, area and when you want to move. He will give you a realistic price range.
