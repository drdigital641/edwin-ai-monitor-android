Home / HDB upgrading

Upgrade from HDB without being caught between two homes.

Selling one home and buying the next is the move where timing matters most. Evan plans both sides together, so the numbers and the dates line up.

Why upgrading needs a plan

When you upgrade, the money from your HDB sale usually funds the next purchase, and the dates have to work: when you must move out, when you can move in, and when each payment is due. Getting the order wrong can mean paying Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty you did not expect, or needing somewhere to stay in between.

What Evan works out with you

  • Your sale proceeds. Expected price, minus outstanding loan, CPF refund with accrued interest and fees.
  • Your new budget. Sale proceeds, savings, CPF and the loan you can take on the next home.
  • Stamp duty. Whether ABSD applies if you buy before selling, and the rules on when it can be refunded for married couples. Try the stamp duty calculator.
  • Timeline. Minimum Occupation Period, the HDB resale timeline, and the completion or TOP date of the next home.

Sell first or buy first?

Selling first gives you certainty on your cash, but you may need to rent or ask the buyer for an extension of stay. Buying first secures the home you want, but you need to plan for ABSD and two loans for a period. Evan lays out both options with your real numbers, so you can choose.

A typical upgrade timeline

  1. Budget and plan: proceeds, new loan, CPF and stamp duty.
  2. Shortlist the next home while preparing your flat for sale.
  3. List your HDB flat and negotiate an offer.
  4. Secure the next home, timing the Option to Purchase with your sale.
  5. Complete the HDB sale, then the purchase, and move.

Common questions

When can I sell my HDB flat?

Most HDB flats can be sold once the Minimum Occupation Period is met, which is usually five years from when you collected the keys. Some newer flats have longer periods. Evan will check the rules for your flat.

Will I have to pay ABSD if I buy a condo before selling my HDB?

It depends on your profile. Singapore Citizen married couples buying a second home may need to pay ABSD first and can apply for a refund if they sell the first home within the allowed period. Evan will explain what applies to you.

Where do I stay if I sell before my new home is ready?

Options include negotiating an extension of stay with your buyer, renting short term, or timing the sale later. Evan helps you choose.

How much can I afford for my next home?

Evan works this out with you from your sale proceeds, savings, CPF and the loan you qualify for, before you start viewing.
