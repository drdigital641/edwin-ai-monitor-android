Home / Renting and leasing

Renting out your home, or finding one to rent.

For landlords, Evan helps find suitable tenants and puts the tenancy on a clear footing. For tenants, he helps find a place that fits your budget and commute.

For landlords

  • Rental pricing from recent rental transactions for similar homes nearby.
  • Marketing and viewings on the major portals, with viewings arranged around your tenants and your schedule.
  • Tenant checks. Work pass or identity checks, and confirming the tenant is eligible to rent your type of property.
  • Tenancy agreement with clear terms on deposit, repairs, diplomatic clause and handover, plus a checklist of the home's condition.

HDB flat owners

Renting out a whole HDB flat or rooms has its own rules, including the Minimum Occupation Period, approval from HDB, occupancy caps and quotas for non-Malaysian non-citizen tenants. Evan will check what applies before you list.

For tenants

Tell Evan your budget, the areas you are considering and your move-in date. He shortlists suitable homes, arranges viewings and helps you understand the tenancy agreement before you sign.

Common questions

How much commission does a landlord pay?

Commission for a tenancy is agreed with you in writing before Evan starts work. It commonly depends on the length of the lease.

Can I rent out my HDB flat?

You can usually rent out a whole flat once the Minimum Occupation Period is met and HDB has approved it. Rooms may be rented out earlier in some cases. Evan will check the rules for your flat.

What is a diplomatic clause?

A clause that lets a tenant end the lease early, with notice, if they have to leave Singapore for work. It is common in leases to foreign professionals.

Who pays for repairs during a tenancy?

The tenancy agreement sets this out. Commonly, tenants pay for minor repairs up to an agreed amount, and landlords pay for larger repairs and structural issues.
