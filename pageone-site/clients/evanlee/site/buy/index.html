Buying a home in Singapore, step by step.

HDB resale, condominium or landed. Evan helps you set a realistic budget, find homes that fit how you live, and negotiate and complete the purchase.

Start with what you can really afford

The asking price is only part of the cost. Before viewing, Evan helps you put together the full picture:

  • Loan limit. Your bank or HDB loan amount depends on your income, age, existing debts and the loan-to-value limit. Try the home loan calculator.
  • Cash and CPF. How much of the down payment must be cash and how much can come from your CPF Ordinary Account.
  • Stamp duty. Buyer's Stamp Duty on every purchase, and Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty if it applies to you. Try the stamp duty calculator.
  • Other costs. Legal fees, valuation, renovation and, for HDB resale, any cash over valuation you agree to.

HDB resale flats

To buy an HDB resale flat you need a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter before the seller grants you an Option to Purchase. Evan checks that the flat suits your eligibility (citizenship, household, income ceiling for grants and the remaining lease) and walks you through the HDB resale portal from the Option to the completion appointment.

Condominiums and new launches

For private homes, Evan compares the projects you are considering on the things that matter day to day: location and transport, the size and layout you actually need, maintenance fees, remaining lease and recent transaction prices in the same development.

Landed homes

Landed purchases need extra checks: tenure, land size and any plans to rebuild, and the eligibility rules for buying landed residential property.

From offer to keys

  1. Shortlist and viewings, planned around your schedule.
  2. Offer and negotiation based on recent transactions.
  3. Option to Purchase, option fee and exercise within the stated period.
  4. Loan, lawyer or HDB paperwork, and stamp duty payment.
  5. Completion and handover.

Common questions

Do I pay commission when I buy?

For HDB resale and many private purchases, buyers may pay their own agent a commission. Whether a fee applies and how much is agreed with you in writing before Evan starts work, using the CEA prescribed agreement.

Can Evan help with a new launch condo?

Yes. Evan can help you compare projects and units and guide you through the booking and the Option to Purchase.

What do I need before making an offer on an HDB resale flat?

A valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter, and if you are taking a bank loan, an in-principle approval from the bank. Evan will check what applies to you.

How much stamp duty will I pay?

Buyer's Stamp Duty applies to every purchase, and Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty depends on your residency status and how many homes you own. The calculator on this site gives an estimate; IRAS has the final figure.
