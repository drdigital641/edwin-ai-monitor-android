How Evan works

Clear numbers first. Decisions after.

Property is usually the largest purchase a family makes. Before you view, list or sign anything, Evan sits down with you to work out what is realistic: your budget, your loan, your CPF, the stamp duty and the timeline.

Plan my numbers with Evan
  • Plain explanationsThe Option to Purchase, HDB resale steps, ABSD and loan limits, explained in everyday language before you commit.
  • The right order of stepsSelling and buying in sequence, with dates that line up, so you are not paying for two homes or left without one.
  • Honest pricingAsking prices and offers are based on recent transactions nearby, not on wishful numbers.
  • One person to messageYou WhatsApp Evan directly. He keeps the buyer, seller, banker and lawyer conversations moving.
Working together

Four steps from first message to keys.

Every move is different, but most follow the same shape.

  1. Tell Evan your plan

    A WhatsApp message is enough to start: what you want to do, where, and roughly when.

  2. Get the numbers right

    Budget, loan, CPF, stamp duty and timing, worked out before any viewing or listing.

  3. View, list and negotiate

    A focused shortlist for buyers. Pricing, photos and viewings for sellers. Evan handles the negotiation.

  4. Sign and complete

    Option to Purchase, HDB or lawyer paperwork, and completion dates, followed through to handover.
