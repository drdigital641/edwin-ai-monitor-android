How banks work out your loan

What is TDSR?

The Total Debt Servicing Ratio limits the share of your gross monthly income that can go to all your debt repayments, including the new home loan, car loans and credit facilities. The limit set by MAS is 55%.

What is MSR?

The Mortgage Servicing Ratio applies to loans for HDB flats and for executive condominiums bought from the developer. It caps the home loan instalment at 30% of gross monthly income, on top of the TDSR.

Why does the calculator use a higher interest rate?

Banks do not assess you at the current rate. They use a higher medium-term rate, so you can still afford the loan if rates rise. The default here is 4% a year; change it to match what your bank tells you.

What is the loan-to-value limit?

The most a bank can lend as a share of the price or valuation, whichever is lower. For a first housing loan it is usually 75%, with at least 5% of the price paid in cash. It is lower if you already have a housing loan, or if the loan tenure or your age at the end of the loan is above the set limits.
