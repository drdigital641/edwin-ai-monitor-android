Hello, I am Evan.

I am a property agent and Associate with ERA Realty Network, in the Anthony Sentosa Siantar Division, working from ERA APAC Centre in Toa Payoh. I help families and individuals across Singapore buy, sell, rent and invest in property.

Real estate is a new chapter for me, with the same purpose I have always had: helping people find a place they will love coming home to. Whether you are buying, selling, investing or still exploring your options, you can expect honest advice, genuine service and guidance at every step.

My approach is simple: work out the real numbers first, explain each step in plain language, and keep the process moving so you always know what happens next. You message me directly on WhatsApp, and I keep the conversations with the other agent, the bank, HDB and the lawyers going for you.

What I help with

  • Buying an HDB resale flat, condominium or landed home
  • Selling your home at a realistic price
  • Upgrading from HDB to private property, in the right order
  • Renting out your home, or finding one to rent
