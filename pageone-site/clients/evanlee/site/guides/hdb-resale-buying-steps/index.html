HDB resale: the buying steps in order

From the HDB Flat Eligibility letter to collecting your keys, the steps of buying an HDB resale flat in Singapore, explained simply.

Buying an HDB resale flat follows a set order. Knowing it in advance helps you avoid delays and surprises.

1. Get your HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter

Apply on the HDB Flat Portal. The HFE letter tells you whether you can buy a resale flat, which grants you may qualify for, and how much HDB loan you could take if you choose an HDB loan. You need a valid HFE letter before a seller can grant you an Option to Purchase.

2. Work out your budget

Add up your loan, CPF Ordinary Account savings, cash, any grants and the stamp duty. Leave room for legal fees, renovation, and any cash over valuation if the agreed price is above the valuation.

3. View flats and agree a price

Look beyond the listing photos: remaining lease, floor, facing, condition, and the walk to the MRT, schools or your parents' home. Compare against recent transactions in the same block or street.

4. Option to Purchase

The seller grants you an Option to Purchase in exchange for an option fee. You then have a set period to exercise it. Before exercising, request the HDB valuation if you need it.

5. Submit the resale application

After exercising the Option, buyer and seller each submit their part of the resale application on the HDB portal. HDB processes the application and sets a completion appointment.

6. Completion

At completion, the payments are made and you collect your keys.

Rules and timelines are set by HDB and change from time to time. Check the latest on the HDB website, or ask Evan to walk through your case.
