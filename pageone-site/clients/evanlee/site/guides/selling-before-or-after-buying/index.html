Upgrading: sell first or buy first?

Upgrading from HDB? Compare selling first and buying first: cash certainty, ABSD, bridging, and where you will live in between.

For most upgraders the biggest question is the order: sell the current home first, or secure the next one first. Both can work. The right choice depends on your numbers and your appetite for risk.

Selling first

  • Pros. You know exactly how much cash and CPF you will have for the next home. No second loan to carry.
  • Cons. You may need somewhere to stay between homes. You may feel rushed to buy.
  • Ways to manage it. Ask your buyer for an extension of stay, rent short term, or time your sale for when your next home is nearly ready.

Buying first

  • Pros. You secure the home you want, and move only once.
  • Cons. ABSD may be payable upfront, and you may need to service two loans for a while. If the sale takes longer than planned, cash flow can get tight.
  • Ways to manage it. Check whether you qualify for an ABSD refund after selling, plan a bridging loan if needed, and price the current home realistically from the start.

The numbers to work out first

  1. Expected sale price of your current home.
  2. Outstanding loan and CPF refund with accrued interest.
  3. Your loan limit on the next home.
  4. Stamp duty, including any ABSD paid upfront.
  5. Monthly payments if you hold two homes for a few months.
