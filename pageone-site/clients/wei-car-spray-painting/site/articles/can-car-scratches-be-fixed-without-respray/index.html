Can a car scratch be fixed without spray painting?

Often, yes. It depends on how deep the scratch goes. The fingernail test tells you in a few seconds.

By Wei Car Spray Painting · Updated 1 November 2026

The short answer

A scratch that is only in the clear coat can usually be polished out without paint. A scratch that shows white primer or grey metal needs the panel painted, both to look right and to stop rust.

The three layers of car paint

  1. Clear coat: the glossy top layer that protects the colour.
  2. Base coat: the colour layer.
  3. Primer: the layer that bonds paint to the metal or plastic underneath.

The deeper the scratch, the more layers need to be rebuilt.

The fingernail test

Run your fingernail gently across the scratch. If it does not catch, the scratch is probably in the clear coat and may polish out. If it catches, the scratch has likely gone through the clear coat. If you can see white or grey at the bottom, it has reached primer or metal.

What about touch-up pens?

Touch-up paint is fine for tiny stone chips to keep rust away. On a longer key scratch it usually leaves a raised, uneven line that is easy to see. For those, a proper panel repair gives an invisible result.
