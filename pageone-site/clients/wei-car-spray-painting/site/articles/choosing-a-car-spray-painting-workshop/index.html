How to choose a car spray painting workshop

Paint jobs look similar on the first day. The difference shows months later. Here is what to ask before you book.

By Wei Car Spray Painting · Updated 1 November 2026

1. Do they spray in a booth?

A spray booth keeps dust and insects out of wet paint and controls how it dries. Paint sprayed in the open is more likely to show specks and uneven gloss.

2. What paint do they use?

Ask which paint brand and system the workshop uses. Established refinish brands have tested colour formulas for most car models, which helps the match.

3. How do they match colour?

A good painter will ask for or find your paint code, check the colour against your car and blend into the next panel where needed. Be wary of a quote that skips this.

4. Is the quote clear?

The quote should say which panels are included, whether dents or rust are repaired first and whether trims or lights are removed. Final prices often depend on what is found once sanding starts, so ask how changes are handled.

5. What do other customers say?

Read recent reviews and look at photos of finished jobs, ideally of cars in colours like yours. Wei Car Spray Painting is 100% recommend on Facebook from 238 reviews (checked 1 November 2026): see our Facebook page.
