Full car respray and colour change

A full respray repaints the whole body so the colour and shine are even from front to back. It suits older cars with faded or peeling paint, or owners who want a new colour.

A full car respray repaints every exterior panel of the car. Old paint is sanded, dents and rust spots are fixed, the body is primed and then sprayed in colour and clear coat. It costs more than panel work, so we only recommend it when most panels need paint anyway.

When you need full respray & colour change

  • Clear coat peeling on the roof, bonnet and boot at the same time
  • Paint faded all over from years under the Singapore sun
  • Many small dents, chips and scratches around the car
  • You want a new colour (check the LTA rules first, see below)
  • Preparing an older car you plan to keep for years

How we do it

  1. InspectWe go round the whole car with you and agree which panels, trims and areas are included.
  2. Strip and prepareTrims and lights are removed where needed, the body is sanded, and dents and rust spots are repaired.
  3. PrimeThe bare and repaired areas are primed and sanded smooth.
  4. SprayColour and clear coat are sprayed in the booth, panel by panel.
  5. Refit and polishTrims go back on, the paint is polished and checked in daylight before you collect.

Questions

Is a full respray better than panel spray?

Not always. If the damage is on a few panels, panel spray costs less and matches well. A full respray is worth it when the paint is tired all over.

Can I change my car colour?

Yes. We have changed cars to new colours, such as a Porsche Macan to Fountain Blue Metallic and a Mini Cooper from orange to pink. A colour change is a vehicle modification, so check the current LTA rules on OneMotoring and tell us when you book.

Will you remove my lights and trims?

Where needed for a clean finish, yes. We agree what is removed and refitted when we inspect the car.
