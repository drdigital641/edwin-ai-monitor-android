Car servicing and repairs

Besides spray painting, Wei Car Spray Painting is listed for car repair and servicing. Tell us what your car needs and we will advise.

Wei Car Spray Painting is registered for repair and maintenance of motor vehicles and is listed for repair and servicing as well as spray painting. Call or WhatsApp with your car model and what you need, and we will tell you if we can do it and when.

When you need servicing & repairs

  • Routine servicing
  • Repairs found during a bodywork job
  • Combining a paint job with other work on the car
  • Checks before or after a long trip

How we do it

  1. Tell us the jobWhatsApp or call with your car model, mileage and what you need.
  2. Book a slotWe agree a day to bring the car in.
  3. Inspect and quoteWe check the car and confirm the work and price before starting.
  4. CollectWe let you know when the car is ready.

Questions
