How long does it take to sell a car through consignment?

There is no fixed timeline for selling a car on consignment, because the car sells when a buyer agrees to your price. How fast that happens depends on the asking price, the car's condition and mileage, how much COE it has left, and how ready the paperwork is. Six cars we posted in March 2022 sold in one to three viewings, but that is an example, not a promise.

Is there a normal timeframe for consignment?

No honest dealer can give one before seeing the car. Six cars we posted in March 2022 sold in one to three viewings, with handover three to eight days after the sale. Those were different cars in a different market, so treat them as an illustration of how it can go, not as a timeframe for yours.

What decides how fast a consigned car sells?

Mainly the price compared with similar cars on the market. After that come the condition, the mileage and service records, how much COE is left, and how well the car is photographed and presented. A fair price on a clean, well-documented car draws viewings sooner than a high price on a car with gaps in its history.

What happens once a buyer says yes?

When a buyer commits we handle the deposit, the loan settlement if you still owe the bank, and the ownership transfer, and you receive the agreed amount when the sale completes. The paperwork side is explained in what you need to sell your car in Singapore.

How can you help your car sell sooner?

Agree a realistic target price with us at the start, and ask how any later change to the price would be handled when you agree the terms. Have your service records and documents ready, keep the car clean, and tell us when you are free for viewings, because serious buyers often want to see it at short notice.

When is another way of selling better?

If you need the money by a certain date, selling the car to a dealer outright is the quickest route, while consignment suits owners who can wait for a better price. Our comparison of trade-in, consignment and direct sale sets out the differences, and the full process is on our car consignment page.
