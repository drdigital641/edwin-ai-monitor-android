Sell my car in Singapore: sell it to us or trade it in

Send us your car details on WhatsApp for an offer. Sell it to us outright, or trade it in against a car from our showroom.

To sell your car to Star Performance Motor, WhatsApp us the model, registration year, mileage and a few photos. We make you an offer, inspect the car, and handle the ownership transfer. You can take the money or use it as a trade-in on a car from our showroom.

How selling works

  1. Send details. Model, registration date, mileage, COE expiry and a few photos on WhatsApp.
  2. Get an offer. We give you a figure and explain how we arrived at it.
  3. Inspection. We check the car in person to confirm its condition.
  4. Paperwork and payment. We handle the transfer and settle any outstanding loan with the bank as part of the deal.
  5. Handover. You hand over the car and keys, or drive away in your next car if you are trading in.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an offer for my car?

WhatsApp 9742 7491 with your car's model, registration year, mileage, COE expiry and a few photos. We reply with an offer and arrange to see the car.

Can you take over my outstanding car loan?

An outstanding loan is settled with the bank as part of the sale, and you receive the balance. Tell us your loan balance when you ask for an offer.

Should I sell outright, trade in or consign?

Selling outright is fastest. Trading in saves a step if you are buying from us. Consignment can fetch more if you can wait. Our guide compares all three.

Do you buy cars near the end of their COE?

Yes, and an older car may be worth more as an export. See our export service.
