Buy a used car from a Yishun used car dealer: Star Performance Motor

Pick a car from our showroom or our sgcarmart list, drive it, and have it checked at your own workshop before you commit.

Buying a used car from Star Performance Motor means you see the car at our showroom at North Spring Bizhub in Yishun, take it for a test drive, and can have it inspected at a workshop of your choice before you pay in full. We explain the paperwork and hand the car over when the transfer is done.

How buying from us works

  1. Choose. Browse our live stock on sgcarmart or tell us on WhatsApp what you need: model, budget, how long you plan to keep it.
  2. View and test drive. Come to the showroom in Yishun. We walk you round the car and you take it for a drive.
  3. Inspect. Book a pre-purchase inspection (PPI) at any workshop you trust. Customers often ask us to come along, and we do.
  4. Deposit and paperwork. Once you are happy, a deposit holds the car while we prepare the transfer and any trade-in.
  5. Handover. You collect the car and the keys. Questions after pickup go to the same person you dealt with.

What customers mention most

  • Transparency. Reviewers describe "no hidden fees" and a car "delivered in the exact condition promised".
  • Inspection without pressure. Several buyers say our team went with them to the PPI and explained the results.
  • First-time buyers. Many reviews come from first-time owners who were guided step by step.
  • Help after pickup. One buyer found a minor issue two days after pickup and says it was repaired free of charge.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get the car inspected before I buy?

Yes. You can book a pre-purchase inspection at any workshop you trust before you commit. In one August 2026 handover we drove the car to the buyer's workshop ourselves the day after he paid the deposit.

Where can I see your cars?

At our showroom at 9 Yishun Industrial Street 1, #01-82 North Spring Bizhub, Singapore 768163. Our current cars are listed on our sgcarmart dealer page. Walk-ins are welcome, or WhatsApp 9742 7491 to book a time.

Can I trade in my current car?

Yes. Tell us your car's model, registration year and mileage and we will make you an offer, which can go towards the car you buy.

Do you help first-time buyers?

Yes. Many of our Facebook reviews are from first-time owners. We explain depreciation, COE, inspection and the transfer step by step.
