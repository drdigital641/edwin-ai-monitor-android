Trade-in, then a test drive

BMW 520i M Sport

He came only to sell his car. While handing it over he spotted a modified BMW 520i M Sport in the showroom, test drove it and placed a deposit the same day.

The next day we drove the BMW to his preferred workshop for a full inspection: mechanically sound and in excellent condition. He collected the keys three days later.

Export, then an upgrade

Toyota Vellfire

His old car was near the end of its COE, so we found him an export offer for it. He then bought a well-priced Toyota Vellfire from us.

His pre-purchase inspection happened at the very workshop that had serviced this Vellfire for years, and it passed. We polished and detailed it before handover; his wife said it looked brand new.
