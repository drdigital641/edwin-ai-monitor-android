If you live in the Eunos flats or work in the Kaki Bukit industrial estate, New Zen Werkz is your neighbourhood workshop. We are at Eunos Technolink, Blk 3 Kaki Bukit Road 1, unit #03-04 on the third floor.

Because the drive is this short, it is easy to drop the car off for a service in the morning and collect it later the same day. WhatsApp us first so we can tell you how long the job usually takes.

Getting here from Eunos & Kaki Bukit

2.3 kmby road
about 4 minoutside peak hours

From Eunos MRT Station (EW7) on Eunos Crescent. Route and time from OpenStreetMap routing (free-flowing traffic); peak-hour traffic takes longer.

  1. From Eunos MRT, follow Eunos Crescent and turn left onto Jalan Eunos.
  2. Stay on Jalan Eunos for about 900 m. It carries on as Eunos Link.
  3. Turn right onto Kaki Bukit Avenue 1, then left onto Kaki Bukit Road 1.
  4. Eunos Technolink is Blk 3 on Kaki Bukit Road 1. The workshop is on the third floor, unit #03-04.
