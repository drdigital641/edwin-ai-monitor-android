From the Aljunied and Geylang lorongs, the quickest way to us goes through Geylang East: Sims Avenue, Geylang East Avenue 1 and Geylang East Central bring you out on Paya Lebar Road, and Ubi Avenue 2 does the rest.

It is a short, mostly local-road drive, which makes it easy to bring the car in when something does not feel right rather than waiting for the next service.

Getting here from Geylang & Aljunied

4.4 kmby road
about 7 minoutside peak hours

From Aljunied Road near Sims Avenue. Route and time from OpenStreetMap routing (free-flowing traffic); peak-hour traffic takes longer.

  1. From Aljunied Road, turn left onto Sims Avenue, then left onto Geylang East Avenue 1.
  2. Turn right onto Geylang East Central, then left onto Paya Lebar Road.
  3. Turn right onto Ubi Avenue 2 and follow it about 1.5 km into Kaki Bukit Avenue 1.
  4. Turn left onto Kaki Bukit Road 1. Eunos Technolink is Blk 3; the workshop is on the third floor, unit #03-04.

Questions from Geylang & Aljunied drivers

How far is New Zen Werkz from Aljunied?

About 4.4 km by road from Aljunied Road, roughly 7 minutes outside peak hours, via Geylang East and Ubi Avenue 2.
