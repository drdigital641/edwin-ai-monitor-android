Bedok drivers reach us quickly because Bedok North Road feeds straight onto the Pan-Island Expressway, and the PIE exit at Jalan Eunos is only a few turns from Eunos Technolink.

That makes New Zen Werkz a practical choice when you want a workshop that rebuilds engines and gearboxes in-house, not just one that swaps parts, without crossing the island.

Getting here from Bedok

5.0 kmby road
about 7 minoutside peak hours

From Bedok MRT Station on New Upper Changi Road. Route and time from OpenStreetMap routing (free-flowing traffic); peak-hour traffic takes longer.

  1. From Bedok MRT and Bedok Mall on New Upper Changi Road, turn onto Bedok North Avenue 1, then left onto Bedok North Road.
  2. Join the Pan-Island Expressway (PIE) heading west, about 1 km.
  3. Exit at Jalan Eunos, continue onto Eunos Link, then turn right onto Kaki Bukit Avenue 1.
  4. Turn onto Kaki Bukit Road 1. Eunos Technolink is Blk 3; the workshop is on the third floor, unit #03-04.

Questions from Bedok drivers

How long is the drive from Bedok to New Zen Werkz?
