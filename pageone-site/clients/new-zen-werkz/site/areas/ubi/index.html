Ubi sits right next to Kaki Bukit, so for many Ubi drivers we are the closest independent workshop. Ubi Avenue 2 runs straight into Kaki Bukit Avenue 1, and Eunos Technolink is just around the corner.

If you work in one of the Ubi industrial buildings, you can leave the car with us and be back at your desk within minutes. Tell us on WhatsApp what the car is doing and we will plan the job.

Questions from Ubi drivers

How close is the workshop to Ubi?
