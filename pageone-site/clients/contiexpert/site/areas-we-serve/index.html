Straight-line distance: Novena about 1 km, Balestier about 2 km, Toa Payoh about 3 km, Bishan about 4 km, Thomson about 5 km, Ang Mo Kio about 7 km.

From the west: Tanglin, Bukit Timah, Holland Village, Beauty World, Queenstown, Clementi

The Bukit Timah Road and Dunearn Road corridor runs straight towards Newton Circus, and the PIE is the other common way in. Owners along Bukit Timah often pass the workshop on the way into town.

Straight-line distance: Tanglin about 3 km, Queenstown about 4 km, Holland Village about 5 km, Bukit Timah about 5 km, Beauty World about 8 km, Clementi about 9 km.

From the south: Orchard, River Valley, Bugis, Tiong Bahru, Raffles Place, HarbourFront

From Orchard, Scotts Road leads up to Newton Circus. From the city and River Valley, Clemenceau Avenue and the CTE are the usual ways north. Many owners in this band drop the car before work and collect it after.

What is near the workshop while you wait?

Newton Circus is a busy junction where Bukit Timah Road, Dunearn Road, Scotts Road, Newton Road and Clemenceau Avenue North meet. Newton MRT station (North-South and Downtown lines) and Newton Food Centre are both close by, and Orchard Road and Novena are a short ride away. If the job will take longer, many owners drop the car and head to work or home, and we message them when it is ready.

How do I find the entrance?

Set your navigation to Volt Autocare @Bukit Timah on Google Maps or to 143 Bukit Timah Road, Singapore 229843. The workshop is inside the Shell station. If it is your first visit, WhatsApp us when you are a few minutes away and we will guide you in.

Questions owners ask

Is 10 km the driving distance?

No. The 10 km circle on this page is a straight line from the workshop. Driving distance and time depend on your route and the traffic, so use the Directions button to see your own route in Google Maps.

Do you have other branches?

No. We have one workshop, inside the Shell station at 143 Bukit Timah Road, Newton Circus. The neighbourhoods on this page are where many of our customers drive in from, not separate outlets.

Can I come from outside the 10 km circle?

Yes. The circle is simply where the workshop is most convenient. Owners from anywhere in Singapore are welcome; WhatsApp us first so we can plan the time you arrive.

What is close by while my car is being checked?

Newton MRT station, on the North-South and Downtown lines, and Newton Food Centre are both close to Newton Circus, and Orchard and Novena are a short ride away.
