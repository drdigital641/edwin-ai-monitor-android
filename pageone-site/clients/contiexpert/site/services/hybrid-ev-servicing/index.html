What is a hybrid battery state-of-health check?

State of health, often shortened to SoH, describes how much usable capacity a battery has left compared with when it was new. A battery in good condition holds charge well and its cells stay balanced. As a battery ages, capacity can drop and weaker cells may show up.

A check reads battery data from the car and looks at how the cells and the system are performing. It helps owners and buyers understand the condition of the battery. Results depend on the model and what data the car makes available.

Why does a hybrid battery cooling fan need cleaning?

Many hybrids use a fan to draw cabin air across the high-voltage battery to keep it cool. The air intake and fan can collect dust and fibres over time. If airflow is restricted, the battery may run warmer than it should.

Cleaning the fan and its air path is a basic maintenance task on suitable models. If a fan fault code or a battery temperature warning has appeared, we check that as well.

How does a hybrid or EV pre-purchase inspection work?

It follows the same idea as a standard inspection, with extra checks for the high-voltage side. Before you commit to a used hybrid or EV, you want to know the condition of the battery and the systems around it.

  1. We check the general condition of the car, as for any used car inspection.
  2. We scan for stored fault codes across the systems.
  3. We check the high-voltage battery data and state of health where the car allows it.
  4. We inspect the cooling arrangement and signs of past issues.
  5. We give you a clear summary of findings so you can negotiate or walk away.

Is it safe to work on a hybrid or EV?

High-voltage systems need proper procedures, and owners should never open orange high-voltage cabling or battery covers themselves. If a hybrid or EV warning light comes on, or you notice a burning smell or unusual heat, stop safely, switch the car off and call for advice.

When you bring a car in, we tell you what we can check and what, if anything, would need a specialist.

Will the check tell me exactly how long the battery will last?

No. It shows the battery's present condition, not an exact lifespan. Age, use and climate all affect how a battery ages, so we explain the result in context rather than predicting a number of years.

Should I get a hybrid inspected before buying?

It is a sensible step, especially for a used car, because the battery is a major part of the car's value. An inspection helps you understand its condition before you commit.
