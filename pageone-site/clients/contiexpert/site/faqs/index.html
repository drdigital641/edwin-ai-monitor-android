Questions about servicing your car with us

Answers to what owners ask before they visit Volt Autocare at Newton Circus: where we are, which cars we work on, how quotes work and what to do when a warning light comes on. Anything not covered here, WhatsApp us on +65 8221 5502.

Visiting

Where exactly is the workshop?

Inside the Shell station at 143 Bukit Timah Road, Newton Circus, Singapore 229843. Search "Volt Autocare @Bukit Timah" in Google Maps or tap Directions on any page.

Do I need an appointment?

Please WhatsApp us first. It lets us plan the bay and tell you how long the car is likely to stay, and saves you a wasted trip.

Is ContiExpert the same as Volt Autocare?

Yes. contiexpert.sg is the website of the Volt Autocare workshop at Newton Circus. Google Maps lists us as Volt Autocare @Bukit Timah.

Cars and services

Which makes do you service?

European cars are our speciality, including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi and Volkswagen. We also look after hybrids, EVs and Japanese and Korean cars, for example servicing, batteries, brakes, aircon and pre-purchase inspections. Send us the model and year first.

Can you service my car without voiding the manufacturer warranty?

Warranty terms differ between brands and dealers, so read your warranty booklet or ask the dealer what records they need. Our guide on dealer versus independent workshops explains what to check.

Do you do pre-purchase inspections?

Yes, we offer a standard pre-purchase inspection and a hybrid or EV inspection that also looks at the battery. Book before you pay a deposit on the car.

My warning light is on. Can I still drive in?

A red warning light, overheating, or brakes that feel wrong mean you should stop safely and not drive on. An amber engine light usually allows a careful drive in, but send us a photo first.

Quotes and payment

How much will my repair cost?

It depends on the fault, the model and the parts chosen. We check the car first, explain what we found and agree the cost with you before any work starts.

Will you do work I have not agreed to?

No. If we find something else during the job, we message you with what it is and the cost, and wait for your answer.

Do you publish prices?

Yes for our most popular jobs: servicing packages from $118, aircon cooling-coil wash from $168 and a standard pre-purchase inspection at $60. For anything else, WhatsApp us your model and we give you a clear quote.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes. Customers bring their own parts and we fit them for labour only. Send us the part details first so we can check it suits your car.
