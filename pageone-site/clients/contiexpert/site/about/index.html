About Volt Autocare at Newton Circus

Volt Autocare is a car workshop inside the Shell station at 143 Bukit Timah Road, Newton Circus. We service and repair European cars such as Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi and Volkswagen, and we check hybrids and EVs. contiexpert.sg is our website.

Who we are

The workshop is run by Volt Autocare Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202506592N). Google Maps lists us as Volt Autocare @Bukit Timah, and you can find us on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok as Volt Autocare.

Who you will meet

These photos are from our own TikTok and Instagram videos, filmed at the workshop. Our team explains each job on camera the same way we explain it to you at the counter. In our Google reviews, customers often mention Newt and An by name.

How we work

  1. You tell us what the car is doing, on WhatsApp or at the workshop.
  2. We check the car and show you what we found.
  3. We explain what is urgent and what can wait, and give you the cost.
  4. We start only after you agree, and message you when the car is ready.

Why a workshop at Newton?

Newton Circus is close to the middle of Singapore. Owners from Novena, Orchard, Toa Payoh, Bukit Timah and the city can drop the car on the way to work, and Newton MRT is close by. See drivers within 10 km.
