Quick answers

Where is the workshop?

Inside the Shell station at 143 Bukit Timah Road, Newton Circus, Singapore 229843. Search "Volt Autocare @Bukit Timah" in Google Maps or tap Directions.

Will you replace parts I do not need?

No. We check the car, show you what we found and tell you what is urgent, what can wait and what does not need replacing. Work starts only after you agree to the price.

Which cars do you work on?

Continental cars such as Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi and Volkswagen are our speciality, and we look after hybrids, EVs and everyday Japanese and Korean cars too. WhatsApp us the model and year.

Can I bring my own parts?

Yes, customers bring their own parts and we fit them for labour only. Send us the part details first so we can check it suits your car.

Is ContiExpert the same as Volt Autocare?

Yes. ContiExpert is the continental-car brand of Volt Autocare, and contiexpert.sg is the website of our Newton Circus workshop. Google Maps lists us as Volt Autocare @Bukit Timah.
