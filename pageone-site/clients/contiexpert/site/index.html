Quick answers

Where is the workshop?

We are inside the Shell station at 143 Bukit Timah Road, Newton Circus, Singapore 229843. Use the Directions button for Google Maps, and WhatsApp us if you are unsure where to stop when you arrive.

Which cars do you work on?

European cars are our main work, including Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi and Volkswagen, and we also do hybrid and EV checks. If you drive another make, WhatsApp us the model and year and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

How do I get a quote?

WhatsApp us your make, model, year, mileage and what you have noticed. Most faults need the car to be checked first, so we explain what we find and agree the work and the cost with you before we start.

What are your opening hours?

Please WhatsApp us on +65 8221 5502 to check before you come, especially on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.
