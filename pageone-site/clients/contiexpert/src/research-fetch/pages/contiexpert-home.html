When Should You Service Your European Car

Recognise the Signs Your Vehicle Needs Attention

Following the recommended maintenance schedule is an important part of keeping your European vehicle in good condition. However, certain symptoms may indicate that an inspection is needed sooner.

Look out for:

  • Dashboard warning lights or engine management alerts
  • Unusual engine, brake or suspension noises
  • Difficulty starting the vehicle
  • Reduced engine performance or unusual vibration
  • Transmission hesitation or rough gear changes
  • Air-conditioning that no longer cools effectively
  • Fluid leaks or signs of overheating

If you notice any of these symptoms, contact Volt Autocare to discuss the next steps. If your vehicle is overheating, has a flashing engine warning light or develops a serious braking or handling issue, stop safely and seek professional assistance.
