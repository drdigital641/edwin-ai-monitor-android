How Often Should You Detail Your Car in Singapore?

Published 10 Nov 2026 · Klar Details team

Most cars in Singapore need interior detailing every 6 to 12 months and, if coated, half-yearly coating maintenance. Paint correction is done only when the paint shows damage. The right rhythm depends on how the car is parked, driven and washed, so treat these as guides, not guarantees.

How often should you detail your car in Singapore?

It depends on the service. Interior detailing is suggested every 6 to 12 months, quarterly for private-hire cars. A ceramic or graphene coating is maintained half-yearly. Paint correction is not scheduled: it is done when the paint shows damage. Singapore's sun, rain and humidity are why the gaps are not longer.

These are guides, not rules. A car parked outdoors, driven daily or used for passengers will need more attention than one kept in a covered car park.

How often does the interior need detailing?

Every 6 to 12 months suits most private cars. Humidity, spills, food crumbs and damp carpets build up quietly, and a deep clean resets the cabin. If your car carries passengers for private-hire work, quarterly is a better rhythm. Read our interior detailing guide for what a session includes, or see KlarINTERIOR.

How often does a coated car need maintenance?

Half-yearly. KlarMAINTENANCE inspects the coating, applies KlarSHIELD as a fresh top layer and finishes with a pure-water rinse at about 0 ppm TDS, against 80 to 120 ppm for Singapore tap water. Between visits, wash the car safely: see how to wash a ceramic-coated car.

When does paint need correcting?

Paint correction is a reaction to damage, not a calendar item. Look for swirl marks that show in sunlight, water spots that no longer wash off, dull or chalky patches and light scratches. A free assessment shows how deep the damage goes and whether polishing can remove it before a coating is applied.

What changes the schedule for your car?

  • Parking outdoors or under trees that drop sap and bird droppings.
  • Daily driving, long trips and heavy passenger use.
  • Washing habits, especially automatic brush washes and tap water left to dry.
  • Whether the car has a coating, and how old it is.

Questions

How often should I get my car interior detailed?

We suggest every 6 to 12 months for a private car. Cars used for private-hire driving carry more passengers and spills, so quarterly is suggested. A cabin that smells damp or shows stains needs attention sooner.

How often does a ceramic-coated car need maintenance?

KlarMAINTENANCE is half-yearly. The coating is inspected, KlarSHIELD is applied as a fresh top layer and the car is finished with a pure-water rinse. Safe washing between visits helps the coating reach the longer end of its range.

Do I need paint correction on a schedule?

No. Paint correction is not a routine service. It is done when the paint shows swirl marks, water spots, oxidation or light scratches, and a coating is usually applied afterwards to protect the corrected finish.

Is a new car detailed once or regularly?

A new car is usually detailed once, early, to protect the paint, and then kept up with safe washing and the maintenance its coating needs. See our new car detailing page for how the paint is inspected first.
