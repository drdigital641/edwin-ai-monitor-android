What Should You Do About Cockroaches or Pests in Your Car in Singapore?

Published 14 Nov 2026 · Klar Details team

Remove food and rubbish, then deep clean the cabin, because pests follow crumbs, damp and clutter. Fumigation can help prevent pests and treat bacteria and odour after a deep clean, but it is not a guaranteed way to remove an infestation. If you have seen droppings or live pests, get the cabin assessed.

How do you know a car has pests?

Check for these signs, especially in the boot, under the seats and along the door sills:

  • Droppings or small dark specks in carpets and seat folds.
  • A sour or musty smell that returns, or that is stronger when the aircon starts.
  • Chewed or shed material, or live insects when you move floor mats.

At Klar Details the free assessment checks for odour, mould, droppings or other signs of pests, and recommends whether fumigation is needed.

What should you do first?

  1. Remove food, wrappers, drink cups and rubbish from the cabin and boot.
  2. Take out the floor mats and vacuum seats, carpets and the boot floor.
  3. Wipe hard surfaces and door sills.
  4. Stop eating in the car until it is treated, and keep windows and doors shut when parked so nothing walks back in.

These steps remove what pests live on. They do not reach foam, vents and gaps, which is why a proper deep clean matters.

When does fumigation help?

Klar's interior pest control uses NEA-approved, aviation-grade anti-bacterial fumigation, applied as a fine mist through a closed cabin after a deep clean. It targets bacteria, persistent odour and pest prevention, including in the aircon system. Consider it when there are signs of pests such as droppings, or a persistent musty smell. If the car looks and smells clean, you do not need it.

What are the limits?

Be clear about these before you book:

  • Fumigation is for pest prevention in the cabin. It is not a guaranteed eradication of an infestation, and we would rather say so plainly than overpromise.
  • It does not replace repairs. If water is entering the cabin or the aircon drain is blocked, damp and odour return until that is fixed. See why a car smells musty in Singapore.
  • Severe, long-standing odour in foam may need more than one treatment.

Questions

How do I know if my car has pests?

Look for droppings, chewed or shed material, a sour or musty smell, and insects around the boot, door sills or under the seats. A free assessment checks for odour, mould, droppings and other signs of pests.

Can fumigation get rid of cockroaches in a car?

Fumigation is used for pest prevention in the cabin, not as a guaranteed eradication of an infestation. If you have seen droppings or live pests, tell us when you book and we will advise on the right steps.

What should I clean before pest treatment?

Remove food, wrappers and rubbish, then clean seats, carpets and surfaces. A deep clean comes first, because treating a dirty cabin only masks the problem. Klar deep cleans before fumigation.

Is the fumigation safe?

The product is NEA-approved and the cabin is aired after treatment. We tell you any precautions before you drive. Tell us at the assessment if children or passengers with respiratory sensitivity ride in the car.
