Why Do Tesla and EV Owners Choose Klar Details in Singapore?

Published 18 Nov 2025 · Updated 1 Nov 2026 · Klar Details team

EVs need the same paint care as any premium car, with extra care around high-voltage systems and charging. At Klar Details we use EV-safe processes, in-house EV charging and enclosed, dust-free bays, then protect the paint with KlarCOAT Signature Ceramic or KlarGRAPHENE.

Does EV paint need special treatment?

Many EV makers use water-based paint systems, which can feel softer and mark more easily than older solvent-based finishes. That makes careful washing, controlled polishing and a protective coating especially worthwhile.

Every car is inspected first, so the approach suits the paint in front of us.

Water-based paint is not a flaw. It simply rewards patient preparation and proper protection.

Which coating suits an EV?

Two options, depending on your priorities. KlarCOAT Signature Ceramic offers about 1 to 2 years of protection with minimal upkeep. KlarGRAPHENE is rated up to 3 to 5 years with proper maintenance, and suits owners keeping the car long term.

Both make washing easier and help the paint resist water spots and light contamination.

We recommend the option after seeing the car, and we give a fixed quote before work starts.

How do you work safely around high-voltage systems?

EVs and hybrids carry high-voltage components, so washing and steam cleaning are done with the right precautions: no pressure on seals and charge ports, and care around underbody areas. Our processes are EV and hybrid-safe, and we have worked on Tesla, BYD and Polestar vehicles.

Why do charging and enclosed bays matter?

Our workshop has in-house EV charging, so your battery is not a constraint during longer jobs such as a full exterior treatment. Enclosed, dust-free bays keep dust and debris off fresh coating while it cures.

For new cars, our new car and EV protection packages at Klar Details are built around exactly this approach.

What should I do after delivery?

Wash gently and keep the coating maintained. For routine care, see our guide on when to detail a new car in Singapore.

Because the paint on an EV is often at its best when the car is new, protecting it early is usually easier than correcting it later.

What is the process for an EV?

  1. Free professional assessment of paint and condition.
  2. Hand wash and decontamination using EV-safe methods.
  3. Paint correction where the paint calls for it.
  4. Coating in an enclosed bay, with infrared curing.
  5. Final inspection and aftercare advice.

The steps are the same as for any premium car. The care taken around charging ports and high-voltage areas is what differs.

Questions

Is it safe to wash and steam an EV?

Yes, when done with the correct method. We avoid direct pressure on charge ports, seals and underbody components, and our processes are designed for EVs and hybrids. If your model has particular instructions from the maker, tell us and we will follow them.

Do you have EV charging at the workshop?

Yes, we have in-house EV charging, which is useful when your car is with us for several hours during a coating job. It means you collect the car protected and ready to drive, without a separate charging stop on the way home.

Should I coat a brand-new EV?

Many owners do, because the paint is at its best and protection preserves it. We start with a free assessment and recommend preparation only where the paint needs it. A fixed quote is given before any work begins, with no hard sell.

Which brands have you worked on?

Our work includes Tesla, BYD and Polestar vehicles, among others, as well as hybrid and petrol cars. You can see examples on our work page, and our team can talk through what suits your specific model. If you are unsure, ask us during the free assessment and we will explain the options clearly.
