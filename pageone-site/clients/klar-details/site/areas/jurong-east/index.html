Car Detailing in Jurong East

Klar Details is in Jurong East, at 48 Toh Guan Road East, #02-124, Enterprise Hub, a few minutes' drive from IMM and Jurong East MRT. We are a premium detailing centre: ceramic and graphene coating, paint correction, interior detailing and maintenance. Every job starts with a free assessment, by appointment only.

Why do Jurong East owners come to Toh Guan Road East?

For Jurong East owners the centre is close to home. We do detailing only, in covered, enclosed, dust-free bays, so a coating is not applied beside a repair job. Cars stay under cover from arrival to handover.

We have been operating since 2014 and have serviced over 2,500 cars. Our process is explained on the about page.

Which services suit a Jurong East car?

Four services are the usual starting points: ceramic coating or graphene coating for paint protection, paint correction for swirl marks, or interior detailing every 6 to 12 months.

If you want to see graphene on your own car first, the S$88 bonnet trial is the lowest-commitment start. For coated cars, see coating maintenance.

What should you know before visiting?

Visits are by appointment only. Send photos of the car on WhatsApp first, and we will tell you what we can see and whether an assessment is worthwhile. The free assessment is done in person, and the quote that follows is fixed before work starts.

Cars are worked on in covered, enclosed bays. In-house EV charging is available for electric cars. See contact details to book.

Questions from Jurong East owners

Where is Klar Details in Jurong East?

We are at 48 Toh Guan Road East, #02-124, Enterprise Hub, Singapore 608586. It is a few minutes by car from IMM and Jurong East MRT. We work by appointment only, so please book before you come. Contact us before you set out so that we can confirm the time.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Klar Details is by appointment only. Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or the form, and we will arrange the free assessment. Sending photos of the car by WhatsApp first helps us prepare. Walk-ins are not taken, which lets us give each car the time it needs.

Is the car kept under cover?

Yes. Work is done in covered, enclosed, dust-free bays. Cars are not worked on in an open car park, which matters for coating, because dust and rain affect wet coatings. Covered bays also let us work on a wet day, and the car is protected from weather until handover.

Do you charge for the assessment?

No. The professional car assessment is free. After it you receive a fixed quote, and the quote is fixed before work starts. There is no obligation to proceed, and we do not use hard-sell tactics. Ask any question you like at the assessment; we answer it plainly.
