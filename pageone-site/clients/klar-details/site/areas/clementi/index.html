Car Detailing for Clementi Owners

Clementi owners who want a detailing specialist come to Klar Details at 48 Toh Guan Road East, #02-124, Enterprise Hub, in Jurong East. We offer ceramic and graphene coating, paint correction, interior detailing and maintenance in covered, enclosed bays. Visits are by appointment only, and the assessment is free.

Why do Clementi owners come to Toh Guan Road East?

Because preparation is not rushed. Our Signature exterior treatment takes at least 6 man-hours, in enclosed, dust-free bays, with infrared curing. We do detailing only, which keeps the workshop focused.

The centre has been operating since 2014 and is an MK Group brand. Read more about Klar.

Which services suit a Clementi car?

Start with what the paint needs. If it is clean, ceramic coating or graphene coating protects it. If it shows swirl marks or water-spot etching, paint correction comes first.

For the cabin, interior detailing every 6 to 12 months suits most cars, and fumigation is available for odour and bacteria concerns.

What should you know before visiting?

Visits are by appointment only. Send photos of the car on WhatsApp, and we will advise whether the assessment is worth booking. The assessment is free and done in person in a covered bay.

The quote that follows is fixed before work starts. Book through our contact page.

Questions from Clementi owners

Is Klar Details near Clementi?

We are at Enterprise Hub, 48 Toh Guan Road East, in Jurong East. Clementi owners generally drive to us, and we do not quote journey times because they vary. Appointments let you plan your visit to suit your day. Please contact us to confirm your slot.

Can you detail my car at home?

Coating and paint correction are carried out in our workshop, where bays are enclosed and dust-free and curing equipment is available. Please bring the car to Toh Guan Road East by appointment. Tell us about any special circumstances when you book and we will advise.

How long does a visit take?

It depends on the service. A Signature exterior treatment takes at least 6 man-hours. We confirm the schedule at the free assessment, so you know whether to leave the car for the day or longer. Longer jobs, such as correction with a coating, take more time still.

Do you work on electric cars?

Yes. Our processes are EV and hybrid-safe, and we have in-house EV charging. We have worked on Tesla, BYD and Polestar cars, and we will ask about the model when you book. Charging is available on site, which helps on longer jobs. We will ask which model you drive.
