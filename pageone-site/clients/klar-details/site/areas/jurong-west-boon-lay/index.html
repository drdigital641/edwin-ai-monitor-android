Which services suit a Jurong West or Boon Lay car?

Cars that are driven daily, including private-hire vehicles, benefit from regular interior care. We suggest interior detailing every 6 to 12 months, and quarterly for private-hire use.

For the paint, graphene coating or ceramic coating eases washing and protects against UV and rain. KlarMAINTENANCE keeps a coating in condition every six months.

Questions from Jurong West and Boon Lay owners

Is Klar Details a short drive from Jurong West?

Klar Details is at 48 Toh Guan Road East, Enterprise Hub, in Jurong East. For many owners in Jurong West and Boon Lay it is a short drive, but we do not state journey times as traffic varies. Appointments avoid waiting.

Do you detail private-hire vehicles?

Yes. Private-hire cabins see constant passenger use, so we suggest interior detailing quarterly rather than every 6 to 12 months. We can also advise on fumigation if odour or bacteria are a concern. Quarterly detailing keeps odours and soiling from building up between visits.

Can I get a quote without visiting?

We can advise from photos sent by WhatsApp, but the fixed quote comes after the free in-person assessment. Paint condition and the correction stages needed cannot be judged from images alone. We tell you which factors will affect the figure. Please send clear photos in daylight.

Do you coat older cars?

Yes, if the paint can be brought to a good standard. Older paint often needs correction first. We assess it and tell you plainly whether coating is worth it, or whether correction or maintenance is the better use of your budget.
