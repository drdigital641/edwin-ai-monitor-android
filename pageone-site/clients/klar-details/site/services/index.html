How a visit works

  1. 1
    WhatsApp a few photos

    Tell us the car, how it is used and what bothers you about the paint or cabin.

  2. 2
    Free assessment at Toh Guan Road East

    We inspect the paint under lights, measure what correction it needs and agree the scope.

  3. 3
    Fixed quote before work starts

    You know the price and the hours before we touch the car.

  4. 4
    The work, in an enclosed bay

    Preparation, correction, coating and infrared curing, or the interior programme you chose.

  5. 5
    Handover and aftercare

    We walk you round the car and explain how to wash and maintain it.
