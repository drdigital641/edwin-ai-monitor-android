Frequently asked questions

Why choose Klar Details for professional car detailing in Singapore?

We have operated since 2014 and have serviced over 2,500 cars. The team has 30+ years of combined experience across paint correction, ceramic and graphene coating, and interior detailing. We prepare carefully, apply in enclosed bays, inspect every panel, and give a fixed quote after a free assessment.

How many layers of ceramic or graphene coating does Klar apply?

Typically two layers where the paint suits it, with setting time between them. The number depends on the package and the condition of the paint. We state the layers in your quote, so you know exactly what is being applied before work starts.

How long does KlarCOAT ceramic or graphene coating last in Singapore?

KlarCOAT ceramic lasts about 1 to 2 years with minimal upkeep, and KlarGRAPHENE is rated up to 3 to 5 years with proper maintenance. Actual life depends on the product, usage, weather and how the car is washed. Ask for the cover terms in writing.

Does a brand-new car need paint correction or polishing before ceramic coating?

Not always. A new car can carry transport marks or dealer-polish swirls, but many are clean. We inspect the paint under light at the free assessment and recommend correction only where defects are visible, because a coating seals in whatever lies beneath it.

Does Klar Details offer affordable car detailing and coating packages?

We offer options for different budgets, and we will say plainly if a premium package is more than your car needs. The S$88 graphene bonnet trial is a low-commitment start. Other services are quoted after the free assessment, with the quote fixed before work starts.

What is the difference between mobile car detailing and workshop-based detailing?

Mobile detailing is done at an agreed location and suits simple jobs. Paint correction and coating are better done in a workshop, where bays are enclosed and dust-free and curing equipment is available. Klar works from its centre at Toh Guan Road East, by appointment.

Why does professional ceramic or graphene coating cost more than cheaper alternatives?

Price reflects the product, the preparation, any correction, careful application, curing and aftercare. Cheaper options often reduce one of these. When comparing quotes, compare what each includes and the cover terms, not only the figure. Ask each workshop for a written list of inclusions.

What is the difference between car wax, paint protection film (PPF) and ceramic coating?

Wax is a temporary layer that fades within weeks. PPF is a physical film that protects against stone chips. Ceramic and graphene coatings add gloss and water repellency and make washing easier, but they do not stop chips. No coating replaces PPF.

Can I apply ceramic coating to my car myself?

It is possible, but risky. Uneven application can leave high spots that harden into visible patches, and poor preparation seals swirl marks under the coating. A workshop bay, correct preparation and curing equipment give a more consistent result. We do not recommend it for a car you value.

What is the difference between ceramic coating and graphene coating?

Both add gloss and repel water. KlarCOAT ceramic lasts about 1 to 2 years with minimal upkeep, and KlarGRAPHENE is rated up to 3 to 5 years with proper maintenance. Neither makes paint scratch-proof. The right choice depends on how long you keep the car and how it is maintained.

How long does professional car detailing or coating take?

It depends on the service and the car. A Signature exterior treatment takes at least 6 man-hours, and coatings need setting and curing time. We confirm the schedule at the free assessment, so you know how long to leave the car.

How do I maintain my car after ceramic or graphene coating?

Wash by hand regularly with a pH-neutral shampoo and clean microfibre, avoid abrasive products and brush washes, and follow the aftercare you are given. Our half-yearly KlarMAINTENANCE programme inspects the coating and applies KlarSHIELD each visit. Do not wait for the coating to fail before maintaining it.
