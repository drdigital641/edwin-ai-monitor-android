Questions

Before you book

What is the difference between ceramic coating and graphene coating?

Both add gloss and repel water. KlarCOAT ceramic lasts about 1 to 2 years with minimal upkeep, and KlarGRAPHENE is rated up to 3 to 5 years with proper maintenance. Neither makes paint scratch-proof. The right choice depends on how long you keep the car and how it is maintained.

How long does KlarCOAT ceramic or graphene coating last in Singapore?

KlarCOAT ceramic lasts about 1 to 2 years with minimal upkeep, and KlarGRAPHENE is rated up to 3 to 5 years with proper maintenance. Actual life depends on the product, usage, weather and how the car is washed. Ask for the cover terms in writing.

How much does car detailing or ceramic coating cost in Singapore?

It depends on car size, paint condition, correction needed, the coating line and any interior add-ons. We publish one price, the S$88 graphene bonnet trial. Everything else is a fixed quote given after the free assessment, before work starts. Photos sent by WhatsApp help us advise in advance.
