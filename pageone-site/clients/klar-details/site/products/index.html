Questions about Klar products

Can I buy Klar products for home use?

A retail range built on the KlarCOAT, KlarGRAPHENE and KlarSHIELD lines is being prepared. Register your interest on WhatsApp and we will message you when the first products and prices are confirmed, with the same aftercare guidance we give at handover.

Will the products be the same ones used in the studio?

The retail range is drawn from the same in-house lines our detailers use. Professional coatings still need preparation, correction and curing in the studio, so retail items focus on washing, maintenance and protecting a finish between visits.
