Someone has passed away in Jurong West: first steps and who to call

If this has just happened, we are sorry for your loss. Take a breath. You do not have to do this alone.

Nirvana's own memorial park is in the west, so families in Jurong West are not far from it. If someone has just passed away, the first steps are the same wherever you live, and Benjamin will walk them with you.

The first hour: three things

  1. Get the death certified. A doctor must certify the death first.
  2. Have the ID ready. The NRIC, FIN card or passport of your loved one.
  3. Call Benjamin on +65 8932 9933. Tell him where your loved one is, and whether a wake is wanted.

In a hospital

The hospital doctor certifies the death, and the doctor or hospital staff give you the death certificate number. Then call Benjamin.

At home

The family engages a doctor to come and certify the death. If no doctor can certify the death, the police are called and your loved one is taken to Mortuary@HSA. Then call Benjamin.

In the next few days, the death is registered automatically once it is certified online. Download the digital death certificate from My Legacy (LifeSG) within 30 days, and apply for the Permit to Bury or Cremate on NEA's ePortal (ICA, NEA). Common questions

The family engages a doctor to certify the death. If no doctor can certify, the police are called and she is taken to Mortuary@HSA. Then call Benjamin, who will stay with you as your one contact.

Do I need to live close to Nirvana Memorial Garden to ask Benjamin for help?

No. It is Nirvana's memorial park in the west, but being close to it is not a condition. Benjamin helps families across Jurong West, Boon Lay and Pioneer.
