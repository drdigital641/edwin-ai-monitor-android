What is Nirvana Memorial Garden?

Nirvana Memorial Garden is a memorial park, a landscaped site set aside for remembrance rather than a temple hall. It is run by Nirvana Memorial Garden Pte Ltd, and its official website is nirvana.com.sg.

This website is Benjamin Poon's own. It is not the official site of Nirvana, and Benjamin is a consultant who represents the company, not its owner.

Where is it in Singapore?

The address is 950 Old Choa Chu Kang Road, Singapore 699816. This is in the Choa Chu Kang and Lim Chu Kang direction, in the north-west, and it is a different part of the island from the two temple venues, which are in the east.

It is on Old Choa Chu Kang Road, not Orchard Road. Many families travel by car or taxi, and Benjamin can share directions before your visit.

What do families usually ask about this venue?

Most questions are practical and are best answered with the current information in hand.

  • Which niche types and locations within the garden are being offered at the moment.

  • How to get there, especially for elderly parents.

Getting there: what has been verified

The operator's contact page states that SMRT bus 975 serves the garden, from Bukit Panjang Interchange or from the stop opposite Choa Chu Kang MRT Station. It also mentions a shuttle service from Boon Lay MRT Station.

Routes and shuttle arrangements can change, so please confirm them before you travel. Benjamin can help with this.
