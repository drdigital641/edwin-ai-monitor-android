A clear introduction for families looking at Lin San Temple in Geylang, and how Benjamin can help you prepare.

What is Lin San Temple?

Lin San Temple is a Buddhist temple in the Geylang neighbourhood. Its own website is linsan.sg, which is the place to read about the temple itself.

This site belongs to Benjamin Poon personally and is not the temple's official site. Benjamin is a consultant who discusses options, not the temple's operator.

Where is it in Singapore?

The address is 27 Lorong 12 Geylang, Singapore 399004. Geylang is an established, busy neighbourhood east of the city centre, with narrow side lanes.

Because of the lane layout, it helps to plan your arrival in advance. Benjamin can share directions and meet you when you visit.

What do families usually ask about a temple venue?

These are fair questions, and Benjamin will tell you plainly what he can and cannot answer himself.

  • How a temple setting differs from a memorial park, for example in atmosphere and religious practice.
  • Whether ancestral tablets can be placed here, and how they are looked after.
  • Whether family members of different faiths will feel comfortable visiting.
  • How easy the venue is for elderly relatives to reach and move around.

Getting there

We have not published bus or MRT directions for this venue, because the temple's own contact page does not give them and we only list what we have verified. Please ask Benjamin for directions and he will arrange a viewing with you.

What can Benjamin discuss for this venue?

Benjamin can talk through niche and ancestral tablet questions and help you decide what to ask the temple. Exactly which services he can arrange at Lin San Temple is being confirmed and will be stated once settled.

How does a consultation and viewing work?

  1. Message or call Benjamin and say whether you are asking for yourself or for a family member.
  2. He listens first, then explains the options that suit your situation.
  3. If you wish, he arranges a visit to the temple and meets you there.
  4. You take your time. There is no pressure to decide on the day.
