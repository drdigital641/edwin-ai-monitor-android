Columbarium niches (福位 / 骨灰位) in Singapore

A clear, unhurried explanation of niche options, so your family can choose with confidence.

A columbarium niche is a small space in a columbarium where an urn of ashes is placed after cremation. Families usually compare indoor or outdoor settings, single or family niches, the level and position, the religious setting, and how easily elderly relatives can visit. Benjamin explains these and shares current options from the operator.

What is a columbarium niche?

A niche is a small compartment, usually in a purpose-built hall or tower, where the urn of a loved one is placed. In Chinese it is often called 福位 or 骨灰位, and the building is a 骨灰塔 or columbarium.

A niche holds ashes. An ancestral tablet is a separate matter, because it is a name tablet used for remembrance and rites. Many families think about both together, and our guide on niche versus ancestral tablet explains the difference.

What do families compare when choosing a niche?

There is no single right choice. The best niche is the one that suits your family's customs, your relatives' needs and your comfort. These are the points that come up most often.

  • Indoor or outdoor. Indoor halls are sheltered from sun and rain. Some families prefer the open feel of a garden setting.
  • Single or family niche. A single niche is for one person. Family arrangements let several relatives rest close together.
  • Level and position. Height and location within the hall can affect how easy it is to pay respects, especially for older visitors.
  • Religious setting. Some venues are temples and some are non-temple gardens. Families choose according to their own faith or tradition.
  • Access for elderly visitors. Think about steps, lifts, shelter, seating and the journey from home.

How do I choose a niche step by step?

Take it in small steps and involve the family early. It is a shared decision, and a calm pace helps everyone.

  1. Talk with your family about customs, wishes and who will visit.
  2. Write down your questions. Our guide on questions to ask before choosing can help.
  3. Message Benjamin, who will share current options and prices from the operator.
  4. Visit in person if possible. See arranging a columbarium viewing.
  5. Take time to decide. There is no need to rush.

Common questions

What does 福位 mean?

福位 is a common Chinese term for a niche where an urn is placed. It is often used together with 骨灰位, and both refer to a place for ashes.

What is the difference between a niche and an ancestral tablet?

A niche holds ashes. An ancestral tablet is a name tablet for remembrance and rites. Some families have both, others choose one.

Should I choose indoor or outdoor?

It depends on your family's preference and on how easily relatives can visit. Benjamin can describe what each venue offers so you can compare.

Can I see a niche before deciding?

Yes, a visit is usually helpful. Benjamin can help arrange a viewing; please ask him for the current process.

How much does a niche cost?

Prices differ by venue and option, and they can change. Benjamin will share current options and prices from the operator when you ask.
