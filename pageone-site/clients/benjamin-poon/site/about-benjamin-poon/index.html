About Benjamin Poon

Service Advisor and Life Managing Director, Singapore. English and Chinese.

Benjamin Poon (潘国雄) is a Service Advisor and Life Managing Director in Singapore. He represents Nirvana Singapore (富贵山庄) and helps families look into columbarium niches, ancestral tablets, funeral arrangements and will planning.

He joined in late 2025. He speaks English and Chinese, and he prefers to explain options calmly so that families can decide together, without pressure.

Benjamin works with three venues: Nirvana Memorial Garden on Old Choa Chu Kang Road, Lin San Temple in Geylang and Thian Leng Chong Toh Tong on Changi Road.

For will planning he works with Advance Planners as a certified will planner. He gives general information and helps you prepare; questions that need legal advice go to a qualified lawyer. See Will planning.
