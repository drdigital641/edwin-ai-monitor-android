A memorial advisor who speaks Cantonese and Hokkien

Some conversations are easier in the language you grew up with.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-10-25 · 4 min read
Benjamin Poon (潘国雄) is a Service Advisor and Life Managing Director in Singapore who is fluent in Cantonese and Hokkien, speaks Mandarin and English, and understands some Teochew. Older relatives can call him directly and talk about columbarium niches, ancestral tablets, funeral arrangements and wills in the language they are most comfortable with.

Why language matters here

These are decisions about a parent's own resting place. When the explanation comes through a son or daughter translating, details get lost, and parents may not ask what they really want to ask.

Speaking with Benjamin directly in Cantonese or Hokkien lets your parents hear the options clearly and say what they want in their own words.

What Benjamin can explain in dialect

  • Columbarium niches (福位 / 骨灰位), including family suites.
  • Ancestral tablets (神主牌).
  • Funeral arrangements, for now or for later.
  • Will planning basics (general information, not legal advice).
  • Qing Ming and Zhong Yuan prayers at Nirvana.

How to start

  1. Call or WhatsApp +65 8932 9933. Your parent can call him directly.
  2. Say which language you prefer: Cantonese, Hokkien, Mandarin or English.
  3. If useful, Benjamin arranges a visit with the whole family.
