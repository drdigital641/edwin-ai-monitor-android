Nirvana Memorial Garden and fengshui: what the booklet says about the "Toad's Lair"

For many Chinese families, fengshui is part of choosing where a loved one rests. Here is what Nirvana itself says, and what it does not say.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-11-02 · 3 min read
Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2025 booklet says the garden in Choa Chu Kang faces southeast (sits northwest, faces southeast) and stands on a rare "Toad's Lair" (蟾蜍穴) fengshui formation, which it calls a symbol of wealth and prosperity. It also says each niche is designed following fengshui principles. These are Nirvana's own statements. If fengshui matters to your family, ask your own fengshui master, and Benjamin Poon can arrange a viewing.

What does Nirvana say about its fengshui?

  • The garden is in Choa Chu Kang and faces southeast (坐西北向东南).
  • It describes a rare "Toad's Lair" (蟾蜍穴) formation, a symbol of wealth and prosperity.
  • The booklet adds that in ancient times toads protected crops, kept pests away and brought bountiful harvests.
  • Each niche is designed following fengshui principles, for a harmonious and auspicious resting place.
  • The surroundings are described as peaceful and dignified, with the architecture built around fengshui ideas.

What the booklet does not say

The booklet does not name a fengshui master, show a fengshui chart, or say how the formation was assessed. It does not promise any result for a family. We repeat Nirvana's description as its own claim and do not add fengshui readings of our own. Whether a particular niche position suits your family is a question for your own fengshui master.

Questions to ask if fengshui matters to your family

  1. Can my fengshui master visit the site and see the available niche positions before I decide?
  2. Which niche positions are available now, and which suite or level are they in?
  3. Can families who want to stay together be placed near each other?
  4. Please put the exact unit and terms in writing before I commit.

To see the suites themselves, read the Family, Free Thinker, Modern and Royal suites. For the garden, see Nirvana Memorial Garden.
