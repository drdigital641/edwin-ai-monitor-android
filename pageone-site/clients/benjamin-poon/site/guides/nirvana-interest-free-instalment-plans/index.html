Nirvana Memorial Garden interest-free instalment plans

Many families prefer to spread the cost of a niche over time. Here is what Nirvana says about paying by instalment, and what you still need to confirm.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-11-09 · 3 min read
Nirvana Memorial Garden says it offers interest-free instalment plans, so families can pay in parts without bearing any interest. This comes from the "Why choose Nirvana" page of its 2025 booklet. The booklet gives no instalment length, deposit or eligibility rules, so please ask Benjamin Poon to confirm the current terms in writing before you decide.

What does Nirvana say about instalments?

The booklet lists interest-free instalment plans as the fifth of eight reasons to choose Nirvana. It says the plans let customers pay the fees in instalments without bearing any interest, so families can manage the cost with ease and peace of mind. That is the whole statement. It is Nirvana's own wording.

What does the booklet not say?

It does not say how many instalments there are, whether a deposit is needed, which products qualify, or who can apply. It also gives no prices. Do not assume that every product or every suite is covered, and do not assume the terms stay the same over time.

How does this fit with the one-time maintenance fee?

Nirvana says it charges a one-time, perpetual maintenance fee for niches and ancestral tablets, and that 75% of that fee goes into its trust fund. The booklet does not say whether the maintenance fee can also be paid in instalments. Ask this directly. Our guide to the one-time maintenance fee and trust fund explains what Nirvana says about that fee.

What should you ask before agreeing?

  1. Does the instalment plan cover the niche or tablet I am considering, and the maintenance fee too?
  2. How many instalments are there, how much is due each time, and is a deposit needed?
  3. Is it truly without interest? Are there any administrative or other charges, and where is that written?
  4. What happens if a payment is late, or if the family wants to settle early?
  5. Can I have the full terms and the quotation in writing before I sign?

Take your time, and bring a family member if you can. Our questions to ask before choosing a niche cover the rest of the checklist.

Is it better to plan early?

Planning before a need arises gives a family time to compare, ask questions and decide without pressure. See planning a niche while healthy. Benjamin does not push any plan. He explains the options and leaves the decision to your family.

Where does this information come from?

Details here come from Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2025 booklet (pages 24 and 25) and are Nirvana's own statements. This page is general information, not financial advice. Benjamin will confirm what is current and anything that may have changed.
