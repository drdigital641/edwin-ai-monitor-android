Who is behind Nirvana Memorial Garden in Singapore?

When a family plans ahead, it is natural to ask who runs the place and how long they have been doing this. Here is what Nirvana's own booklet says.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-11-05 · 3 min read
Nirvana Memorial Garden Singapore says it was established in 2009 and is part of Nirvana Asia Group, a Malaysian group that says it was founded in 1990 with 50 acres in Semenyih. The booklet says the group now has 22 branches in five countries (Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand and Vietnam) and calls its Malaysian park the country's largest memorial park. These are Nirvana's own statements; Benjamin can answer your questions about them.

How does Nirvana describe its beginnings?

The booklet says Nirvana Group was founded in 1990. It describes a start with 50 acres in Semenyih, Malaysia, which it says has grown into Malaysia's largest memorial park, now over 4,000 acres. The booklet says the founder was moved to found the group after the passing of his father-in-law, wanting to bring dignity and peace to end-of-life care.

How big is Nirvana Asia Group, and where is Singapore in it?

According to the booklet, Nirvana Asia Group has a business presence in five countries and 22 branches locally and overseas. Singapore is one of those branches. The booklet says Nirvana Memorial Garden in Singapore was established in 2009, and that it is part of the Malaysian group, with more than three decades of experience in the industry.

  • Countries: Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand and Vietnam.
  • Branches: 22 in total, including Kuala Lumpur, Ipoh, Penang, Johor, Sabah, Sarawak, Medan, Jakarta and Singapore.

Which Singapore milestones does the booklet list?

  • August 2012: Nirvana Trust Fund set up, which Nirvana says is to sustain operations and protect customers' interests.
  • June 2016: land lease extended from 2029 to 2098 (99 years).
  • April 2017: began offering funeral service packages.
  • April 2018: Nirvana says it was named the "Largest Bereavement Company in Singapore" by the Singapore Book of Records.
  • October 2020: launched Journey with Love, a care service for the departed led by an all-female team.

For the lease, see Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2098 land lease. For the trust fund, see the one-time maintenance fee and trust fund.

What questions should you still ask?

  1. Ask Benjamin what the lease, trust fund and maintenance arrangements mean for the niche or tablet you are considering.
  2. Ask to see the current written terms before you decide. A booklet is a summary, not a contract.
  3. Visit first. Seeing the garden with your family often answers more than any booklet.

Details here come from Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2025 booklet and are Nirvana's own statements. Benjamin will confirm what is current and anything that may have changed.

Common questions

When was Nirvana Memorial Garden Singapore established?

Its 2025 booklet says 2009.

Is Nirvana part of a larger group?

Yes. The booklet says it is part of Nirvana Asia Group, a Malaysian group founded in 1990 with 22 branches across five countries.

Is Nirvana Memorial Garden the same as a government columbarium?

No. Nirvana is a private memorial park. Government columbaria are run by the NEA. Each has its own terms, so compare them for your family.
