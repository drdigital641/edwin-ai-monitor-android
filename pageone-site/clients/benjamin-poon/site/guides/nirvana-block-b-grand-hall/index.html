What is the Block B Grand Hall (大雄宝殿) at Nirvana Memorial Garden?

Families planning a visit often ask what the Grand Hall is. Here is what Nirvana's own booklet shows, and what it leaves out.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-11-13 · 3 min read
The Block B Grand Hall (大雄宝殿) is a hall at Nirvana Memorial Garden that the 2025 booklet shows across a two-page photo spread. The photos show a high hall with a curved gold balcony, a large lotus-shaped ceiling light, golden statues, drums and walls of tablets. The booklet gives no opening hours, capacity or ceremony schedule for the hall, so please ask Benjamin Poon to confirm what suits your family.

What does the booklet show?

The booklet names the hall "Block B Grand Hall (大雄宝殿)" and gives it a full two-page spread. The photo shows an interior with a curved gold balcony, figures of flying apsaras, a giant pink lotus ceiling light, standing golden statues, drums and tablet walls. These are what the photograph shows, not a description from Nirvana.

How does it relate to the tablet area and the prayer ceremonies?

The booklet shows a separate tablet area with a Ksitigarbha (地藏菩萨) statue and glass-shelf tablet walls, described in Nirvana's ancestral tablets. It also says Nirvana holds prayer ceremonies for festivals such as Qing Ming and Zhong Yuan, covered in Qing Ming and Zhong Yuan prayers. The booklet does not say where each ceremony is held, so do not assume they take place in the Grand Hall.

What does the booklet not say?

  • Opening hours for the hall, or whether it is open for visits at any time.
  • How many people it holds.
  • Which ceremonies or services take place in it.
  • Whether tablets or niches there are available, or at what cost.

Benjamin will check these with Nirvana for you rather than guess.

What should you ask before visiting?

  1. Is the Grand Hall open on the day we plan to visit, and can we go in?
  2. Are there any times we should avoid, such as during a ceremony?
  3. Is there seating, and how much walking is involved for older family members?
  4. Which area is the tablet or niche option we are considering in, and can we see it?

Our guide to arranging a columbarium viewing and the one on visiting with elderly parents help you plan the day.

Where does this information come from?

The hall name and photos come from Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2025 booklet (pages 14 and 15). The descriptions are of what the photos show. Benjamin will confirm what is current.
