How to get to Nirvana Memorial Garden by public transport or by car

Whether you come from the east, the city or nearby Choa Chu Kang, here are the options in one place.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-10-26 · 4 min read
Nirvana Memorial Garden is at 950 Old Choa Chu Kang Road, Singapore 699816. By public transport, take SMRT bus 975 from Bukit Panjang Interchange or from the stop opposite Choa Chu Kang MRT Station, or take Nirvana's daily shuttle from Boon Lay MRT Station. By car or taxi, use the address or Nirvana's own map link. Benjamin Poon can meet you there and show you around.

By MRT and bus 975

According to Nirvana's contact page, SMRT bus 975 serves the garden.

  • From Choa Chu Kang MRT Station: board bus 975 at the stop opposite the station.
  • From Bukit Panjang: board bus 975 at Bukit Panjang Interchange.
  • Coming from the east or the city? Take the MRT to Choa Chu Kang or Bukit Panjang first, then bus 975.

By the Boon Lay shuttle

Nirvana says its shuttle runs daily from Boon Lay MRT Station, with pick-up and drop-off along Boon Lay Way. Seats are first come, first served, so arrive 5 to 10 minutes early.

Nirvana's 2025 booklet lists departures from Boon Lay MRT (Exit C) at 9.45am, 10.45am, 11.45am, 1.45pm, 2.45pm and 3.45pm, plus 12.45pm on weekends and public holidays. Return trips leave Nirvana at 9.15am, 10.15am, 11.15am, 1.15pm, 2.15pm, 3.15pm and 4.45pm, plus 12.15pm on weekends and public holidays.

Times can change, so please check with Benjamin before you travel.

By car or taxi

Key in 950 Old Choa Chu Kang Road, Singapore 699816, or open Nirvana's Google Maps link. It is in the north-west, along Old Choa Chu Kang Road, not in town.

Nirvana's contact page does not publish parking details, so we do not guess them here. Ask Benjamin about parking and the best drop-off point, especially if you are bringing elderly parents.

Visiting with elderly parents

  • A car or taxi to the door is usually easiest for parents who cannot walk far.
  • Avoid the busiest festival days if you can, such as the Qing Ming period.
  • Tell Benjamin in advance, so he can meet you at the entrance.
