Visiting a columbarium with elderly parents

Many parents want to choose their own resting place. A calm, well-planned visit helps them do that.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-10-23 · 4 min read
Plan a short visit at a quiet time, check access for walking and steps beforehand, bring water and somewhere to sit, and let your parents lead the conversation. Benjamin Poon can arrange viewings at Nirvana Memorial Garden and discuss the two temples he works with, in Chinese or English, at your parents' pace.

Before the visit

  • Ask your parents gently whether they want to come; some prefer to decide from photos first.
  • Choose a weekday morning if you can.
  • Ask about lifts, steps and seating in advance.
  • Write down the questions your parents care about.

During the visit

  • Keep it short; you can always come back.
  • Let your parents speak first and set the pace.
  • Do not decide on the day unless they want to.

Talking about it at home

It is normal for this to take more than one conversation. Our guide on questions before choosing a niche can help structure it.
