Planning a niche while you are well: like insurance, it is best done early

Nobody buys insurance once they are already sick. Memorial planning works the same way.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-10-25 · 5 min read
Planning a niche or ancestral tablet while you are healthy means you choose the place yourself, your family knows your wishes, and nobody has to decide in a hurry later. At government columbaria NEA does not allow advance booking, so families who want to plan ahead usually look at private memorial parks, which set their own terms. Benjamin Poon (潘国雄) helps you plan calmly, in Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien or English.

Why plan before it is needed?

  • Your own choice. You pick the place, the setting and the faith section, instead of leaving it to others.
  • No rushed decisions. After a death, families often have only a few days to arrange everything.
  • Fewer disagreements. When wishes are clear, siblings do not need to argue about them.
  • Time to visit and compare. You can see the place in person, more than once if you like.

What to decide

  1. Niche, ancestral tablet, or both. See the difference.
  2. Just for yourself, for a couple, or a family suite.
  3. Faith and customs to follow, including any dialect-group practices your family keeps.
  4. Who in the family should know, and where the papers will be kept.
  5. Whether a will should be done at the same time.

Talking about it with your parents

Many older Singaporeans are more open to this topic than their children expect. It often helps to talk in the language they are most at home in. Benjamin can explain everything in Cantonese or Hokkien, so your parents hear it directly, not through translation.

There is no pressure to decide on the first visit. Most families talk it over at home first.

Common questions

Is it unlucky to plan this early?

Many families see it the other way: it is a caring thing to do for the people you leave behind. Each family decides what feels right.

Can I plan ahead at a government columbarium?

NEA states that no advance booking of niches is allowed at its columbaria. Private operators set their own terms; Benjamin will confirm them for Nirvana Memorial Garden.

Can my parents speak to Benjamin in dialect?

Yes. He is fluent in Cantonese and Hokkien and understands some Teochew.
