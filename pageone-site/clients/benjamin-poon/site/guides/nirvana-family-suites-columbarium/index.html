Nirvana Family Suites and other columbarium suites

Some families want parents, and later the next generation, to rest together. That is what a family suite is for.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-10-23 · 4 min read
Nirvana Memorial Garden's 2025 booklet shows four kinds of columbarium suite: Family Suites (家庭骨灰福殿), Free Thinker Suites (无宗教骨灰福殿), Modern Suites (现代化骨灰福殿) and the Royal Suite (帝王阁). Family Suites are private rooms in white with a crystal chandelier and seating for visits. Options and prices depend on the suite and current availability, so Benjamin Poon confirms them for you.

The four suites in the booklet

  • Family Suites (家庭骨灰福殿): white rooms with a crystal chandelier and seating for visits.
  • Free Thinker Suites (无宗教骨灰福殿): for families who prefer a non-religious setting, with softly lit walls of round niches.
  • Modern Suites (现代化骨灰福殿): gold walls with blue-and-white porcelain-style niches and a seating area.
  • Royal Suite (帝王阁): warm gold niche walls, a timber ceiling and a lounge with sofas.

Why families look at a family suite

A family suite lets relatives be placed in one private space, so the family gathers in one room on Qing Ming and anniversaries. It also suits families who want to plan ahead together rather than decide one niche at a time.
