Nirvana Memorial Garden for families in the west: getting there and visiting

For families in the north-west, Nirvana is the local option. Here is how to get there and what to expect.

By Benjamin Poon · Updated 2026-10-23 · 5 min read
Nirvana Memorial Garden is at 950 Old Choa Chu Kang Road, Singapore 699816. According to Nirvana, SMRT bus 975 runs from Bukit Panjang Interchange and the stop opposite Choa Chu Kang MRT Station, and a shuttle runs from Boon Lay MRT Station (Exit C). Benjamin Poon represents Nirvana and can arrange a viewing in Chinese or English.

Getting there by bus

  • SMRT bus 975 from Bukit Panjang Interchange.
  • SMRT bus 975 from the stop opposite Choa Chu Kang MRT Station.

The Boon Lay shuttle

Nirvana's 2025 booklet lists a shuttle from Boon Lay MRT Station, Exit C, at 9.45am, 10.45am, 11.45am, 1.45pm, 2.45pm and 3.45pm, with an extra 12.45pm trip on weekends and public holidays.

Return trips leave Nirvana (Block B entrance or the bus stop outside) at 9.15am, 10.15am, 11.15am, 1.15pm, 2.15pm, 3.15pm and 4.45pm, plus 12.15pm on weekends and public holidays.

Timings can change, so please check with Benjamin before you travel.

What you can see on a visit

Nirvana says it is open every day, including weekends and public holidays. More on the suites in our Family Suites guide.

  • The columbarium suites: Family Suites, Free Thinker Suites, Modern Suites and the Royal Suite.
  • The ancestral tablet area.
  • The Block B Grand Hall (大雄宝殿).
  • The Level 5 lounge, Level 9 prayer area and cafe, per the booklet.

Visiting with Benjamin

Benjamin can meet you there, show you the suites and tablet halls, and answer questions in Chinese or English. Older parents are welcome to come along and take their time.

Common questions

Which bus goes to Nirvana Memorial Garden?

SMRT bus 975, from Bukit Panjang Interchange or opposite Choa Chu Kang MRT Station, according to Nirvana.

Is there a shuttle?

Yes, from Boon Lay MRT Station Exit C, per Nirvana's 2025 booklet. Check the current times with Benjamin.

Is Nirvana open on public holidays?

Nirvana says it is open every day, including weekends and public holidays.
