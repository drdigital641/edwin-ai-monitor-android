Rest assured · 安心托付 圆满无忧

Memorial care in Singapore since 2009

Nirvana Memorial Garden at 950 Old Choa Chu Kang Road is part of Nirvana Asia Group, founded in Malaysia in 1990 by Tan Sri Kong Hon Kong, which describes itself as Asia's largest integrated bereavement care provider. It brings columbarium niches, ancestral tablets, prayer ceremonies and funeral services together in one place, under the philosophy "Caring for Life".

Below is an overview of each product and service. Benjamin can walk you through any of them in person, in English or Chinese.

Ancestral tablets · 神主牌

A modern way to honour tradition

With families living apart, smaller homes and growing environmental concerns, keeping ancestral tablets at home is becoming less practical.

Nirvana offers a respectful, modern alternative: tablets kept in dedicated halls, preserving tradition while honouring loved ones with dignity and care.

About ancestral tabletsWhatsApp Benjamin
Major prayer ceremonies · 大型法会

Honouring traditions, uniting families

Nirvana marks Chinese festivals such as Qing Ming and Zhong Yuan with prayer ceremonies and blessings, keeping filial piety alive and families connected across generations.

Many ceremonies take place in the Block B Grand Hall (大雄宝殿), with its lotus ceiling light and golden statues.

Ceremonial services · 山庄礼仪服务

Eternal flowers and personal touches

Ceremonial services include an ling (安灵) rites, memorial engravings on tablets and eternal flower dedications.

Eternal flowers are fresh flowers that are carefully preserved so they keep their beauty for a long time. Families use them to decorate a niche: "always remembered, forever loved."

Funeral services · 殡仪服务

Complete funeral services, handled with care

Funeral planning and coordination, embalming, encoffinment and funeral processions, with a choice of packages to suit different needs and traditions.

For departed women, an all-female team of embalmers, beauticians and ceremony specialists handles every step, from cleansing to make-up and dressing.

A signature care service for the departed

Launched in 2020, Journey With Love is a care service for the departed delivered by professionally trained female embalmers and beauticians, using quality spa products.

Trust fund · 信托基金

Your maintenance fee, protected

When you buy a niche or pedestal, 75% of the one-time maintenance fee goes into the Nirvana Trust Fund, and the remaining 25% is kept as current maintenance reserves.

Precepts Trustee Limited is the appointed trustee and checks that the money is used only for its purpose. Certified accountants audit the fund's accounts every year.
