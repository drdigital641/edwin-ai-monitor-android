Parts for China & EV cars

Pick a part type.

Parts are grouped by what you see on the car, so you can find the right group even if you do not know the exact name. The groups are: bumpers, lamps & lights, mirrors, body panels, grilles & trims, glass & fittings, other parts and suspension & wheel hubs. Each group page lists the parts inside it, with the names owners also use, for example headlight for headlamp or wing for fender.

Every part is sourced to order from several EV parts suppliers and shown as one catalogue. Open a group, pick the part, and tap Get a quote. WhatsApp opens with the part filled in. Tell us the car model, the build month and year, and left or right if the part has a side. We check fitment before we quote.

Common questions

Which part types can you source?

We list 8 groups: bumpers, lamps & lights, mirrors, body panels, grilles & trims, glass & fittings, other parts and suspension & wheel hubs. Each group page shows the parts inside it and how to ask for them.

I do not know what the part is called. What do I do?

Send a photo of the damage and your car model on WhatsApp. Our guide on part names also helps, and we tell you the name and check what we can find.

Do you list parts for every brand?

The groups apply to the brands in our catalogue. Suspension and wheel hub parts are listed for Tesla only for now. For other brands, ask us and we check.
