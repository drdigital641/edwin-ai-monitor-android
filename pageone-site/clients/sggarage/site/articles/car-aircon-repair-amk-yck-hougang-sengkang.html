Common Symptoms of a Faulty Car Aircon

If your car aircon isn’t performing as it should, it’s important to address it early. Here are some common warning signs:

  • Air isn’t as cold as it used to be
  • Weak airflow even at maximum fan speed
  • Unusual noises like hissing, rattling, or clicking
  • Bad smells coming from the vents
  • Warm air blowing instead of cold

These problems can worsen over time, so don’t wait to get them checked.

What Causes Car Aircon Problems?

Most aircon issues are caused by wear and tear or Singapore’s extreme weather. Here are the most common causes:

  1. Leaking refrigerant gas – This is one of the top reasons for an aircon that doesn’t cool well.
  2. Faulty cooling coil (evaporator) – Over time, coils can corrode, especially in humid environments.
  3. Compressor failure – The compressor is the heart of your aircon system, and when it fails, the system won’t cool at all.
  4. Clogged condenser – Dirt and debris can block the condenser, reducing efficiency.
  5. Electrical issues – A faulty relay or sensor can disrupt the system.

In Singapore, stop-and-go traffic and constant use of aircon due to our climate mean these issues are more common. Regular maintenance is key to preventing them.

FAQs About Car Aircon Repair

How do I know if my cooling coil needs replacement?

If your aircon isn’t cold and you notice a musty smell, it could be a sign of a leaking or clogged cooling coil. Diagnosis is the key.

How long does a typical aircon repair take?

Most repairs, like cooling coil or compressor replacement, take between 1-6 hours. We offer same-day collection for many jobs.

Can topping up refrigerant fix my aircon?

Only temporarily. If there’s a leak, the problem will return. It’s better to fix the root cause to avoid repeated costs.
