How to Book an Appointment at Edwin Garage

Edwin Garage·Ang Mo Kio·11 July 2026

Edwin Garage works by appointment so that every car gets the time a proper diagnosis needs. Booking takes about two minutes and guarantees your slot. Here is how it works, what to prepare and the few cases where you can come without a booking.

How to book online

  1. Go to sggarageedwin.as.me.
  2. Choose the type of service: servicing, aircon, diagnosis or accident and bodywork.
  3. Pick a date and time that suits you. We are open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 8pm, and closed on Sundays and public holidays.
  4. Enter your name, contact number, car model and plate number.
  5. Describe the problem in a sentence or two, then confirm. You will receive a confirmation email with your slot.

Need to change or cancel? Use the link in your confirmation email, or WhatsApp us so the slot can go to another customer.

When you don't need an appointment

  • Battery replacement: walk-ins are welcome during opening hours, subject to stock.
  • Breakdowns, overheating and accidents: WhatsApp us first at 9785 6612 so we can advise and prepare. If you need a tow, our partner is on 8455 5669.

Everything else, including diagnostics, inspections and checks, needs a booking. Our article on why we are appointment-only explains the reason.

What to tell us when you book

The more detail you give, the faster we can find the fault:

  • What the problem is, and when it happens: cold start, at idle, at speed, after rain.
  • Any warning lights; a photo of the dashboard helps.
  • Recent repairs or servicing, and whether the problem started after them.
  • Your service records, if you have them.

Questions before booking?

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us: message 9785 6612 with photos or a short video of the issue and we will advise on cost and timing. We don't take bookings by phone call.

Finding us and paying

We are at Blk 5032 Ang Mo Kio Industrial Park 2 #01-295, Singapore 569535, a short drive from Hougang, Yio Chu Kang and Serangoon. Payment is settled at the workshop on the day: cash and PayNow are preferred, and credit cards are accepted with a 3% surcharge.

Key Takeaways

  • Book online at sggarageedwin.as.me; it takes about two minutes.
  • Battery replacement is walk-in; breakdowns, overheating and accidents can WhatsApp first.
  • Describe the symptoms and send dashboard photos so we can diagnose faster.
  • Enquiries by WhatsApp 9785 6612 only; cash or PayNow preferred, card +3%.
