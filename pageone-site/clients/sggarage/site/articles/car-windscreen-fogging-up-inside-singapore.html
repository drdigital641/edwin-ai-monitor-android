Why Does My Car Windscreen Fog Up Inside, and How Do I Clear It?

Edwin·Master Automotive Technician·November 13, 2026·Edwin Garage, Ang Mo Kio

A windscreen fogs up inside when warm, damp air in the cabin meets cooler glass. In Singapore the usual causes are damp mats and clothes after rain, the aircon on recirculation, a dirty cabin filter, an aircon that is not drying the air, or a water leak. To clear it, switch the aircon on, choose fresh air, and aim the airflow at the glass. If you cannot see clearly, pull over safely first.

We are Edwin Garage, a car workshop in Ang Mo Kio. This guide covers how to clear a fogged windscreen quickly, why it happens, and when it means something needs checking. If the fog is on the outside of the glass, the wipers clear it; this guide is about fog inside the car.

How do you clear a fogged windscreen quickly?

  1. Pull over if you cannot see. Do not drive with poor visibility. Stop somewhere safe, use hazard lights if needed, and clear the glass first.
  2. Turn the aircon on. The aircon removes moisture from the air as it cools, so it clears fog faster than heat alone.
  3. Use the vehicle’s windscreen demist setting and follow its manual. This commonly combines air conditioning, outside air and a higher fan speed. A working aircon can remove moisture; gently warming the airflow helps warm the glass. Outside air in Singapore can also be humid, so fresh-air mode alone is not a guaranteed cure. Avoid overcooling the glass.
  4. Aim the airflow at the windscreen and raise the fan speed. Many cars have a demist or defrost button that sets this up.
  5. A slight window opening may help only when safely stopped and conditions allow. It is not guaranteed to dry the cabin in humid rain; close it if rain enters or the mist worsens. Adjust controls or wipe the glass only when safely stopped, never while driving with obscured vision. Resume only when the windscreen and other necessary windows are clear and stay clear.

Avoid wiping with your hand or a cloth. It smears the film and can leave streaks that glare at night. If the glass is streaky even when clean, see our guide to car wiper streaks.

Recirculation mode

Recirculation reuses the cabin air. With a few passengers breathing, or after rain, the humidity builds up and settles on the glass. Switching to fresh air is often all that is needed.

Damp mats and carpet

A wet floor mat acts like a slow humidifier. Take the mats out to dry after a rainy day, and check the carpet underneath. If the carpet is wet and nothing was spilled, look for the source of the water.

Cabin filter and the aircon

The cabin filter cleans incoming air. When clogged, it can restrict airflow and slow demisting, but weak flow does not prove the filter is at fault. A working aircon can dehumidify air before the heater warms it, so warm demist airflow does not automatically mean the aircon is faulty. Persistent fog needs the airflow, moisture source and climate-control operation checked together.

When should you see a workshop?

  • The fog keeps coming back after you dry the cabin.
  • The carpet or floor is wet and you cannot find why.
  • There is a damp or musty smell when the aircon starts.
  • The aircon blows weak or warm air.
  • The glass clears slowly even with the aircon on fresh air.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my car windscreen fog up inside?

Fog forms when warm, moist air inside the cabin meets cooler glass. In Singapore the usual causes are damp carpets or mats, breath and wet clothes after rain, the aircon on recirculation, a dirty cabin filter that limits airflow, or an aircon that is not drying the air well. A persistent damp smell or wet carpet can point to a water leak.

What is the fastest way to clear a fogged windscreen?

If visibility is impaired, stop somewhere safe before adjusting controls. Follow the vehicle’s windscreen demist setting and manual: this commonly uses aircon, outside air and higher fan speed, with warmer airflow to warm the glass. Fresh-air mode alone may not work in humid rain; avoid overcooling the glass. Open a window slightly only when safely stopped and conditions allow, and close it if rain enters or mist worsens. Do not wipe or adjust controls while driving with obscured vision. Resume only when the necessary windows are clear and stay clear.

Is it safe to drive with a fogged windscreen?

No. Pull over safely and clear the glass first. Driving with poor visibility, especially at night or in heavy rain, is dangerous.

Why does the fog return when I switch the aircon off?

Moisture is still inside the cabin. The aircon removes humidity as it cools, so without it the damp air meets the cool glass again. Dry the carpets and mats, and look for the source of the moisture.

Could a dirty cabin filter cause a fogged windscreen?

It can contribute. A blocked cabin filter reduces airflow, so the demisting is slower and weaker. Our guide to cabin filter replacement explains when to change it.

When should I see a workshop about a fogging windscreen?

Arrange an appointment if fog keeps returning after drying the cabin, carpets are wet without a spill, there is a damp or musty smell, or airflow or cooling is unexpectedly weak. These can indicate moisture ingress, drainage, filter, blower or climate-control issues, but symptoms alone cannot diagnose the cause. If demisting cannot maintain clear visibility, do not drive to an appointment: arrange safe assistance or recovery.

Key Takeaways

  • Fog inside the glass is moisture: use the aircon on fresh air, aimed at the windscreen.
  • Do not drive until you can see clearly. Pull over safely first.
  • Dry the mats and carpet; damp is the most common source after rain.
  • A fog that keeps returning, a wet floor or a musty smell means a workshop check.
