Car Coolant Leak: Signs, Risks & Proper Diagnosis

Edwin Garage·Ang Mo Kio·4 July 2026

Coolant leaks are a common issue many car owners in Singapore face, especially given our hot and humid climate. Your car's cooling system works extra hard to regulate engine temperature in these conditions, making it even more important to catch and address leaks early. In this FAQ, we'll break down everything you need to know about coolant leaks — from signs and risks to proper diagnostic methods.

What are the common signs of a coolant leak?

Detecting a coolant leak early can save you from expensive repairs. Here are some signs to watch for:

  • Puddle under your car: If you spot a green, orange, or pink liquid under your parked vehicle, it could be coolant.
  • Overheating engine: If your engine temperature gauge frequently hits high levels, a coolant issue might be to blame.
  • Sweet smell: A sweet or syrupy smell around your car could indicate a coolant leak.
  • Reduced coolant levels: Check your coolant reservoir; if it's consistently low despite refills, you likely have a leak.

At Edwin Garage in Ang Mo Kio, we always advise checking for these signs regularly, especially before long drives in Singapore's tropical heat.

What are the risks of ignoring a coolant leak?

Ignoring a coolant leak can lead to severe consequences for your car. Here's why you shouldn't delay repairs:

  • Engine Overheating: Coolant prevents your engine from overheating. A lack of coolant could cause your engine to seize or sustain permanent damage.
  • Damage to other components: Overheating can damage your radiator, hoses, and even head gaskets — all expensive parts to replace.
  • Breakdowns: Overheating can result in unexpected breakdowns, leaving you stranded in Singapore's heat and humidity.

If you suspect a coolant leak, it's safer to address the issue promptly at a trusted car workshop in Singapore like Edwin Garage.

How is a coolant leak properly diagnosed?

Diagnosing a coolant leak requires a combination of expertise and tools. At Edwin Garage, here's how we approach it:

  • Visual Inspection: We inspect the radiator, hoses, and clamps for visible signs of leaks or corrosion.
  • Pressure Test: A cooling system pressure test helps us locate leaks that aren't immediately visible.
  • UV Dye Test: We may add a UV-sensitive dye to the coolant, allowing us to spot leaks under UV light.

By combining these methods, we ensure accurate diagnosis, so your car gets the right fix the first time.

Can Singapore's climate make coolant leaks worse?

Yes, Singapore's weather plays a big role. The constant heat and humidity can cause your car's cooling system to work harder, increasing wear and tear on components like hoses, seals, and radiators. Over time, this can lead to leaks. Additionally, traffic congestion and stop-start driving in Singapore exacerbate engine stress, making regular maintenance essential.

How can I prevent coolant leaks?

Preventive maintenance is the best way to avoid coolant leaks. Here's what you can do:

  • Regularly inspect your coolant levels and top up as needed.
  • Flush and replace your coolant every 2-3 years, or as recommended by your car manufacturer.
  • Have your radiator, hoses, and clamps checked during routine servicing.
