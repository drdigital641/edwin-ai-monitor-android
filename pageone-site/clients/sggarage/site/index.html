How do I book a car service at Edwin Garage?
All bookings are online at sggarageedwin.as.me. We work by appointment only. For enquiries, WhatsApp 9785 6612.
How much does car servicing cost at Edwin Garage?
Servicing (engine oil and filter) is from $128 for Japanese/Korean cars and from $148 for Continental cars, including parts, labour and GST. The final fee is confirmed after inspection, before any work starts. See the full price list.
Where is Edwin Garage and when is it open?
We're at Blk 5032 Ang Mo Kio Industrial Park 2 #01-295, Singapore 569535, about 5–10 minutes from the AMK MRT. Open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 8pm; closed on Sundays.
Which car makes do you repair?
Japanese, Korean, Continental, hybrid and electric cars. We have worked on Tesla since 2019. Everything is done in-house at our Ang Mo Kio workshop.
