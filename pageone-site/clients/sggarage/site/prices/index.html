Why is the final price set after inspection?

Two cars with the same symptom often need different work. An aircon that blows warm air might need only a gas top-up, or it might have a leaking cooling coil or a failing compressor. A car that won't start might need a battery, or the alternator or starter could be the real cause. We test first, show you what we found, then quote. That is why we publish "from" prices instead of ranges we can't stand behind.

What changes the price of a car service in Singapore?

  • Car make: Continental/European cars usually need costlier oil and filters, so servicing starts from $148 instead of $128.
  • Engine oil grade and capacity: larger engines and fully synthetic specifications cost more.
  • Service schedule: a major service adds items such as filters and spark plugs, so it is priced after inspection.
  • What the inspection finds: worn brake pads or a weak battery are quoted separately, and only replaced with your approval.
