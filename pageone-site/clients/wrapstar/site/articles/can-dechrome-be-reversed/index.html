Can dechrome be reversed?

By Wrapstar Autostyle · Last updated 28 October 2026

The short answer

Yes. Dechrome covers chrome trims with black film instead of removing or painting them, so the film can be peeled off later and the chrome underneath is still there. How cleanly it comes off depends on the age of the film and the condition of the surface under it, so have a studio remove it rather than peeling it yourself.

What happens to the chrome under the film?

Dechrome is a covering, not a change to the part. Window surrounds, grille bars, roof rails and badges are covered in gloss or satin black film, and the chrome is still there under it if you want it back. That is the main difference from replacing or painting the parts.

What affects how easily the film comes off?

  • How long the film has been on the car, and how much sun and heat it has had.
  • The condition of the surface underneath, such as chips, scratches or earlier repairs.
  • Whether the part was resprayed or repaired before the film went on.
  • Who removes it and how: a studio that knows the film is more likely to take it off cleanly than a quick peel at home.

Why does it matter if you plan to sell the car?

Because dechrome is reversible, you can choose to keep it or return the car to its chrome look before a sale. Which is better depends on the buyer. Our guide on whether to remove a wrap before selling covers the same question for wraps.

What should you ask a studio before booking dechrome?

  • Exactly which parts will be covered: window trims, grille, roof rails, badges, lettering?
  • Which film and finish (gloss or satin black) will be used?
  • How will the film be removed later, by whom, and what should you expect the surface to look like?
  • What is the price, and what is in writing?

Questions

Will dechrome damage the chrome underneath?

The chrome is still there under the film. How the surface looks after removal depends on its condition when the film went on and how the film is taken off, so ask your studio how they remove it.

Can I dechrome without wrapping the whole car?

Yes. Dechrome is often done on its own, as a quick way to change how a car looks, and it also pairs with a colour change wrap or colour PPF.

Should I remove dechrome film myself?

We recommend asking a studio to do it. Removal is easier to do cleanly when the person knows the film and checks the surface as it comes off.
