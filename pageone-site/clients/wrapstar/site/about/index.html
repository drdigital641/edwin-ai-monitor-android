About Wrapstar Autostyle

A car wrap and film studio in Yishun, for cars that deserve more.

Who is Wrapstar Autostyle?

Wrapstar Autostyle is a car wrap and film studio in Yishun, Singapore. We do colour change wraps, colour PPF, clear paint protection film, window film, dechrome, polishing and ceramic coating, at 9 Yishun Industrial Street 1, #01-82, North Spring Bizhub, in the same unit as our sister business Star Performance Motor.

What do we stand for?

Our tagline is "for cars that deserve more". A wrap should look like it came from the factory: clean edges, tidy seams, no bubbles. We ask owners to come back after a job so we can check the film, because the finish is what people see every day.

What is our sister business?

Star Performance Motor is a used car dealer in the same unit. Buy, sell or trade in your car there, and give it a new colour or protection here, in one visit.
