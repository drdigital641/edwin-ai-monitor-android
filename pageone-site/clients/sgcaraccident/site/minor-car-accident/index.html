I had a minor accident

A small knock or scratch still has a few must-dos. Here they are.

Someone hurt or in danger? Call 995 or 999 first. Never wait to finish anything on this website.

Is anyone hurt, or is this a special case?

Call 995 for an ambulance now. Don't move an injured person unless they are in danger. A Police report is needed when someone goes to hospital by ambulance or gets 3 or more days of medical leave.

Do these at the scene

  • Make everyone safe first. Switch on your hazard lights and stand away from traffic.
  • Exchange particulars with the other driver: name, NRIC, phone number, address, insurer, and both vehicle numbers.
  • If it is safe, take photos before the cars are moved: all vehicles, plates, damage and the whole scene.
  • Save your dashcam footage now, before it records over.
  • Contact your insurer. Use your insurer's hotline for a tow; GIA advises against dealing with unauthorised tow trucks at the scene.
  • Don't admit liability, sign anything promising to pay, or argue about who is at fault. The insurers decide that.

Yes, report it to your insurer, even if it is small

GIA's Motor Claims Framework says all motorists have to report to their insurers any accident, no matter how small.

Report the accident to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day. Take your vehicle, damaged or not, to your insurer's approved reporting centre or authorised workshop. (GIA Motor Claims Framework)

This applies even if you don't want to claim, or you agree to settle privately. GIA says that if you don't report, the insurer can repudiate liability, so you could lose protection under your policy, and delays in reporting can cost you your NCD entitlement.

Both agree to settle privately?

A private settlement does not remove your duty to report the accident to your own insurer. GIA calls such an agreement a private matter between the two parties; it does not replace the reporting process.

For a small knock, both drivers sometimes agree to settle the damage between themselves. You can record that on our private settlement form, with both drivers' particulars, photos and signatures.

Questions people ask

Must I report a minor car accident in Singapore?

To your insurer, yes. Under the GIA Motor Claims Framework, report every accident, however small, within 24 hours or by the next working day, even if you don't claim or you settle privately.

Do I need to call the Police for a minor accident?

Not always. A Traffic Police report is needed only in specified cases: a death, an injury that needs an ambulance or 3 or more days of medical leave, damage to government property, a foreign vehicle, a pedestrian or cyclist, or a hit and run.

My car has no damage. Do I still report?

Yes. GIA says to take your vehicle, damaged or not, to your insurer's approved reporting centre or authorised workshop within 24 hours.

Can we settle a small scratch privately?

Yes, if both drivers agree. You still report the accident to your own insurer. A private agreement is a private matter between the two of you and does not replace the reporting process.
