Questions people ask

Do I have to report every accident to my insurer?

Yes. Under the GIA Motor Claims Framework, report every accident to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day, even a small one, even if you settle privately or don't claim.

When do I need a Police report?

Only in specified cases, such as an injury, a death, damage to government property, a foreign vehicle, a pedestrian or cyclist, or a hit and run. A Police report does not replace your insurer report.

Who decides who is at fault?

The insurers involved assess liability. SGCarAccident does not decide fault. Don't admit liability at the scene; several insurers advise this.
