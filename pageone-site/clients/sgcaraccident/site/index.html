Questions people ask

What should I do first after a car accident in Singapore?

Make sure everyone is safe. Call 995 if anyone is hurt and 999 for the Police when needed. Take photos of the cars, the plates and the scene, swap details with the other driver, then report to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day.

Do I need to report an accident to my insurer if I'm not claiming?

Yes. Under the GIA Motor Claims Framework you report every accident to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day, even when you settle privately or don't claim.

When do I need to make a Police report?

When the accident involves any of the cases listed in our accident report guide, such as injury or death. For a damage-only accident with none of those cases, you report to your insurer only.
