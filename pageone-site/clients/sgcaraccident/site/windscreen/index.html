Windscreen claim: what your cover means

Two quick screens and some photo tips.

What cover do you have?

Comprehensive policies usually include windscreen cover. Check your policy schedule for the windscreen limit, any excess, and whether a windscreen claim affects your NCD.

Third-party-only cover pays for damage you cause to others, not your own windscreen. The repair would be at your own cost.

Your certificate of insurance or policy schedule names your cover type. Your insurer can also tell you.

Photo tips

  • The whole windscreen from outside, with the plate in view
  • A close-up of the chip or crack
  • The damage seen from inside the car

Questions people ask

Does a windscreen claim affect my NCD?

It depends on your policy. Many comprehensive policies list windscreen cover separately. Check your policy schedule or ask your insurer.

Is my windscreen covered under third-party-only insurance?

No. Third-party-only cover pays for damage to other people's property or injuries, not your own car's glass.

What photos do I need for a windscreen claim?

The whole windscreen with the plate showing, a close-up of the damage, and a view from inside the car.
