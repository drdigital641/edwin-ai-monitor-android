Privacy

This guide does not ask for your details, except in the private settlement form.

Settlement form details (names, last 4 characters of NRIC/FIN, mobile numbers, vehicles, insurers, signatures and photos) are stored privately. They are never published on the website or in search, and only someone holding the secure link can open them.

We keep a form for 90 days so both drivers can download the signed PDF, then delete it.

When you tap Call hotline, WhatsApp opens with a ready message. We count the tap (page and button only, no personal details) to improve the guide.
