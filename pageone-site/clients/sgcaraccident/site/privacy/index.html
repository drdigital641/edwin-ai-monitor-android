Privacy

Enquiry forms on this site don't store your details on our website. When you tap Send, WhatsApp opens with your message filled in, and it reaches us only if you tap Send in WhatsApp. We use those details only to reply to your enquiry, and you can ask us to delete them at any time on WhatsApp.

The accident statement helper works on your phone only. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

Your answers in the guided steps are kept on your phone for this visit only, so you can go back without losing them.

Private settlement form details (names, last 4 characters of NRIC/FIN, mobile numbers, vehicles, insurers, signatures and photos) are stored privately. They are never published, only someone holding the secure link can open them, and they are deleted after 90 days.

When you tap a WhatsApp or call button we count the tap (page and button only, no personal details) to improve the guide.
