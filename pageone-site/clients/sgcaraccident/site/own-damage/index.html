At the scene, do these now

  • Make everyone safe first. Switch on your hazard lights and stand away from traffic.
  • Exchange particulars with the other driver: name, NRIC, phone number, address, insurer, and both vehicle numbers.
  • If it is safe, take photos before the cars are moved: all vehicles, plates, damage and the whole scene.
  • Save your dashcam footage now, before it records over.
  • Contact your insurer. Use your insurer's hotline for a tow; GIA advises against dealing with unauthorised tow trucks at the scene.
  • Don't admit liability, sign anything promising to pay, or argue about who is at fault. The insurers decide that.

Questions people ask
What if I caused the accident?
Make everyone safe, exchange particulars, take photos if safe, and report to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day. Don't admit liability at the scene; the insurers assess it.
Will a claim affect my No Claim Discount?
A claim on your own policy can reduce your NCD unless your policy has NCD protection. The exact effect depends on your policy, so check it or ask your insurer.
What happens if I don't report the accident?
GIA says the insurer can repudiate liability, so you could lose protection under the policy, and delays in reporting can cost you your NCD entitlement.
