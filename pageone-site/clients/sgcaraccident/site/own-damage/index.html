Do you have an NCD protector?

A claim on your own policy can change your No Claim Discount (NCD).

An NCD protector is meant to keep your NCD after a claim. Check your policy for how many claims it covers.

Without a protector, a claim usually cuts your NCD by 30% and your premium goes up at renewal.

Look for "NCD protector" on your policy schedule, or ask your insurer before you decide to claim.

Repairs go through your insurer's authorised workshop

For an own damage claim, the car is repaired at a workshop your insurer has authorised. Ask your insurer which ones you can use.

Report to your insurer within 24 hours

The GIA Motor Claims Framework says: report the accident to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day. Take your vehicle, damaged or not, to your insurer's approved reporting centre or authorised workshop.

Report even if you decide not to claim.

What to bring, and which photos

Bring:

  • Your vehicle (if it can be driven)
  • Your driving licence and NRIC
  • Your certificate of insurance or policy
  • The other driver's name, contact and plate number
  • Your photos and any camera footage

Photos to take:

  • All cars, front and back, with plates showing
  • Close-ups of every dent and scratch
  • The whole scene, with road signs or landmarks
  • The road surface, if wet or damaged

Questions people ask

What is an own damage claim?

It's a claim on your own motor policy to repair your car when you caused the accident, or when no one else can be claimed from. It needs comprehensive cover.

Will an own damage claim affect my NCD?

Usually yes. Without an NCD protector, a claim cuts your NCD (often by 30%) and your premium rises at renewal. With a protector, check how many claims it covers.

Can I choose my own workshop for an own damage claim?

Own damage repairs go through your insurer's authorised workshops. Ask your insurer for the list that applies to your policy.

How long do I have to report the accident?

Within 24 hours or by the next working day, under the GIA Motor Claims Framework.
