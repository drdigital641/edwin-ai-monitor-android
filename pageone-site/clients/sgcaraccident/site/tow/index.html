My car can't be driven

Safety first, then the right tow.

Someone hurt or in danger? Call 995 or 999 first. Never wait to finish anything on this website.

Safety first

  • Switch on your hazard lights
  • Get everyone out on the side away from traffic
  • Stand behind the barrier if there is one
  • If anyone is hurt, call 995

Use your insurer's tow hotline

GIA advises calling your insurer's hotline for a tow truck, and not dealing with unauthorised tow-truck operators at the scene. Your insurer's number is on your certificate of insurance.

Questions people ask

Should I accept a tow truck that turns up at the scene?

GIA advises motorists not to deal with unauthorised tow-truck operators at the scene. Call your insurer's hotline for a tow.

Should I move my car after an accident?

Take photos first if it is safe. Don't move an injured person or the vehicles if someone is hurt, unless there is danger.
