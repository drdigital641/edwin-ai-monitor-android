Emergency tow after an accident

Safety first. Then we'll get a tow to you.

Safety first

  • Switch on your hazard lights
  • Get everyone out on the side away from traffic
  • Stand behind the barrier if there is one
  • If anyone is hurt, call 995

Questions people ask

What should I do while waiting for a tow?

Keep your hazard lights on, stay off the road and behind any barrier, and keep your phone with you.

Should I move my car after an accident?

Only if it's safe and the car can be driven. Take photos first if you can do so safely.
