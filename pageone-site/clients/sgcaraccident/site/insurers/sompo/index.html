Sompo: where to report an accident

Report within 24 hours or by the next working day, even if you don't claim.

Source: Sompo ExcelDrive workshops and accident reporting centres, checked on 2026-11-19. Lists change, so check the insurer's page or call your insurer before you go.

Report the accident to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day. Take your vehicle, damaged or not, to your insurer's approved reporting centre or authorised workshop. (GIA Motor Claims Framework)

Questions people ask

Where do I report a car accident to Sompo?

At a place on Sompo's own list shown here. Check the source link or call Sompo before you go, as lists change.

How long do I have to report to Sompo?

Within 24 hours or by the next working day, under the GIA Motor Claims Framework.
