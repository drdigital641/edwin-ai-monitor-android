Budget Direct: where to report an accident

Report within 24 hours or by the next working day, even if you don't claim.

Source: Budget Direct authorised workshops and accident reporting centres, checked on 2026-11-19.

The GIA Motor Claims Framework says: report the accident to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day. Take your vehicle, damaged or not, to your insurer's approved reporting centre or authorised workshop.

Where do I report a car accident to Budget Direct?

How long do I have to report to Budget Direct?

Within 24 hours or by the next working day, under the GIA Motor Claims Framework.
