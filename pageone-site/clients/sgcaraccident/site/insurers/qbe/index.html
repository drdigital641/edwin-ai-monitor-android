QBE: where to report an accident

Report within 24 hours or by the next working day, even if you don't claim.

Questions people ask

Where do I report a car accident to QBE?

At a place on QBE's own list shown here. Check the source link or call QBE before you go, as lists change.

How long do I have to report to QBE?

Within 24 hours or by the next working day, under the GIA Motor Claims Framework.
