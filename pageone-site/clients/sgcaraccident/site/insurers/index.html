Questions people ask

Where do I report a car accident in Singapore?

At your insurer's approved reporting centre or authorised workshop, within 24 hours or by the next working day. Pick your insurer above to see its list.

Is an accident reporting centre the same as a Police station?

No. The reporting centre takes your report for your insurer. A Police report is a separate report, needed only in specified cases.
