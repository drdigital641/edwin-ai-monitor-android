Frequently asked questions

Short answers. Check your own policy for the details that apply to you.

Reporting

Must I report an accident if there is no damage?

Yes. Under the GIA Motor Claims Framework you report any accident to your insurer, no matter how small, and take your vehicle, damaged or not, to the reporting centre within 24 hours or by the next working day.

Must I report to my own insurer?

Yes, every time, even if you are not at fault, don't plan to claim, or settle privately.

What happens if I fail to report an accident?

GIA says the insurer can repudiate liability, so you could lose protection under your policy, and insurers can take away your NCD entitlement for late reporting. Some insurers, such as AIG and Income, state the NCD may be cut by 10%. GIA says you won't be penalised for late reporting with a valid reason.

Do I need a Police report?

Only in specified cases: a death, an injury (hospital by ambulance, or 3 or more days of medical leave), government property damaged, a foreign vehicle, a pedestrian or cyclist, or a hit and run.

Fault and claims

What if I do not know who is at fault?

You don't have to decide. Collect the facts and report to your insurer. The insurers involved assess liability.

What happens if the other driver refuses to cooperate?

Note their vehicle number, take photos and keep your dashcam footage. If they drove off, it is a hit and run and needs a Police report. Report to your insurer as usual.

Can I claim against the other driver's insurance?

Yes, through a third-party claim if the other driver is responsible. Their insurer assesses the claim; acceptance is not guaranteed.

Can I repair my car before the third-party claim is approved?

Ask first. The other insurer may want to survey your car, and some policies have conditions about the order of claims. Repairing early can make a claim harder.

Settlement and injuries

What if both drivers agree to settle privately?

You can, and our private settlement form helps you record it. You must still report the accident to your insurer. GIA says such an agreement is a private matter that does not replace the reporting process.

What happens if someone is injured?

Call 995. Don't move an injured person unless they are in danger. Make a Police report when someone goes to hospital by ambulance or gets 3 or more days of medical leave, and report to your insurer.
