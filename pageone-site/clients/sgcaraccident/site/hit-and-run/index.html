Hit and run: the other driver left

Your car was hit and the other driver drove off. Here is what to do.

Safety first

  • Switch on your hazard lights and stand away from traffic
  • If anyone is hurt, call 995 for an ambulance
  • If the road is blocked or dangerous, call 999
Tow help

Collect what you can, now

  • Write down the other vehicle's number, colour and make if you saw them, and which way it went
  • Save your dashcam footage now, before it records over
  • Take photos of the damage and the whole scene
  • Ask anyone who saw it for their name, phone number and any footage
  • Parked car? Check for a note on the windscreen, and ask the car park operator whether CCTV covers the spot

Make a Traffic Police report

A hit and run is one of the cases that needs a Traffic Police report.

Make the Police report online through the SPF e-services Traffic Accident Report (Singpass needed), or at any police station. A Police report does not replace your report to your insurer.

Report to your insurer too

Report the accident to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day. Take your vehicle, damaged or not, to your insurer's approved reporting centre or authorised workshop. (GIA Motor Claims Framework)

Ask your insurer how a claim works when the other driver can't be traced, and how it may affect your NCD. The answer depends on your policy.

Questions people ask

Someone hit my parked car and left. What should I do?

Take photos, check for a note, ask the car park operator about CCTV and save any footage. Make a Traffic Police report, because a hit and run needs one, and report to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day.

Do I need a Police report for a hit and run?

Yes. The Singapore Police Force lists hit and run as one of the cases that needs a traffic accident report. You can report online through SPF e-services (Singpass) or at any police station.

Do I still report to my insurer?

Yes. The Police report does not replace your insurer report. Report within 24 hours or by the next working day under the GIA Motor Claims Framework.

Can I claim if the other driver is never found?

It depends on your policy. Ask your insurer how it handles a claim when the other driver can't be traced, and whether it affects your No Claim Discount.
