Private settlement

For when both drivers agree to settle the damage privately.

When a private settlement fits

If both parties mutually agree to settle the damage privately, without making a repair claim against each other, they may consider a private settlement.

This form records both parties' particulars, vehicle numbers, the accident date and location, what happened, any agreed amount, supporting photos, and both drivers' confirmation.

You must still report the accident to your own insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day under the Motor Claims Framework. GIA says a private agreement is a private matter between the two parties and is not a substitute for the reporting process.

Completing this form does not guarantee that no future claim can arise. If you're unsure, ask your insurer before you sign.

How it works

  • Both drivers agree what happened and how it's settled
  • You fill it in and sign first

Questions people ask

Is a private settlement allowed in Singapore?

Drivers can agree to settle privately. You must still report the accident to your own insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day.

Does a signed form mean no claim can come later?

No. The form records what you both agreed, but it does not guarantee that no future claim can arise.

Can the other driver sign on their own phone?

Yes. Share the secure link. They check the details and sign on screen, and either of you can download the signed PDF.
