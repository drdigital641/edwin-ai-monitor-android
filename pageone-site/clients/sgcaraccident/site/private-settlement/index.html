Questions people ask

Is a private settlement legal in Singapore?

Yes, drivers can agree to settle privately. You must still report the accident to your own insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day.

What goes into a private settlement form?

Both drivers' particulars, vehicles and insurers, the date, time and place, a short description, how it is settled (no payment, or an agreed amount) and both signatures.

Can the other driver sign on their own phone?

Yes. Share the secure link. They check the details and sign on screen, and either of you can then download the signed PDF.
