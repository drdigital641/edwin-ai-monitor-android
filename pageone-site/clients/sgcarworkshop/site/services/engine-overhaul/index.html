What engine overhaul involves

The engine is taken out or opened up, cleaned and measured, and worn parts such as piston rings, bearings, gaskets, seals and valves are replaced before it is rebuilt and tested. It is a big job, so a diagnosis first is normal.

Signs you may need it

  • Heavy oil burning or blue smoke from the exhaust.
  • Low compression or a knocking sound from inside the engine.
  • Coolant and oil mixing, or repeated overheating.
  • Power has dropped a lot, even after smaller repairs.

What to ask the workshop

  • What the diagnosis found and why an overhaul is needed rather than a smaller repair.
  • Which parts will be replaced, and whether they are new.
  • How long the car will be in the workshop.
  • Whether the work carries a warranty.

More: General servicing and repair

Questions

Is an overhaul always needed for a smoking or knocking engine?

Not always. A workshop should diagnose the cause first; some problems are fixed with a smaller repair.

How is this different from gearbox overhaul?

Gearbox work is on the transmission. See the gearbox repair page for workshops that overhaul gearboxes.
