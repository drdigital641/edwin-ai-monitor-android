Car aircon repair in Singapore

A car aircon that blows warm air often has low gas, a leak, or a worn part such as the compressor, cooling coil or condenser. A workshop usually checks for leaks first, then tops up the gas or repairs the part. Describe the symptom, such as weak cooling or a bad smell, and ask the cost first.

What car aircon repair involves

Aircon work begins with a check of pressures, leaks and the cooling fan. Common jobs are a gas top-up after a leak repair, cleaning the evaporator, and replacing the compressor, condenser or expansion valve.

Signs you may need it

  • The air is not cold or only cools while driving
  • A musty smell from the vents
  • Water dripping inside the cabin
  • A noise when the aircon switches on

What to ask the workshop

  • Whether the system is checked for leaks before gas is added
  • Which part has failed and why
  • Whether the evaporator needs cleaning
  • What warranty is given on parts

Related car problem guides

  • Car aircon not cold

Questions about car aircon repair

Why does my aircon only feel cold when the car is moving?

This often points to the cooling fan or a condenser problem. A check of pressures and the fan shows which.

Is adding gas enough?

If the gas has leaked out, the leak should be found and fixed first, or the system will lose gas again.
