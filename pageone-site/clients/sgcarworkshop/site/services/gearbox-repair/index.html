Gearbox repair in Singapore

Gearbox repair starts with a diagnosis: reading the car's fault codes, checking the fluid and test driving it. From there a specialist can service, repair or overhaul the gearbox. Slipping gears, harsh shifts and warning lights are common reasons to book in early, before a small fault grows.
