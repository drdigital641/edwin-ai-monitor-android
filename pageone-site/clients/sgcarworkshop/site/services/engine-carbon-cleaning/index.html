What engine carbon cleaning involves

In walnut blasting, the intake is opened and crushed walnut shell is blown at the valves to remove carbon without scratching the metal. Other methods clean the intake, throttle body or EGR valve with tools or cleaning agents. Which method suits depends on the engine.

Signs you may need it

  • Rough idle or shaking at a standstill.
  • Hesitation or loss of power when you accelerate.
  • A misfire or engine warning light on a direct-injection engine.
  • Higher fuel use than before.

What to ask the workshop

  • Whether your engine is direct-injection and likely to build up carbon.
  • Which method they use: walnut blasting or another cleaning method.
  • Whether they show you before-and-after photos of the valves.
  • How long the car will be in the workshop.

Questions

What is walnut blasting?

A way to clean carbon off the intake valves by blowing crushed walnut shell at them. The shell is hard enough to remove carbon but softer than the valve metal.

Does every car need carbon cleaning?

No. It mostly matters on direct-injection petrol engines with symptoms such as rough idle. Ask a workshop to check first.
