Spray painting and bodywork in Singapore

Car spray painting covers panel respray, full respray and colour change. A workshop sands the old paint, fixes any dents, primes the panel and sprays colour and clear coat in a booth. Ask for a quote with photos of your car, and check LTA rules before changing your car's colour.
