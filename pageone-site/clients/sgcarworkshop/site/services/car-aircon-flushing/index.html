What car aircon flushing involves

The system is emptied of refrigerant, then a flushing agent is pushed through the pipes, condenser and cooling coil to carry out old oil and debris. Parts that cannot be flushed are usually replaced, then the system is refilled with the right oil and gas.

Signs you may need it

  • The aircon compressor has failed and is being replaced.
  • Metal debris or black oil was found in the system.
  • The aircon stays weak even after a gas top-up.
  • A workshop has advised a flush with a major aircon repair.

What to ask the workshop

  • Why a flush is needed for your car.
  • Which parts are flushed and which are replaced.
  • Which refrigerant and oil your car uses.
  • Whether the work carries a warranty.

Questions

Is a flush the same as a gas top-up?

No. A top-up adds refrigerant. A flush cleans the inside of the system, usually as part of a bigger repair.

Does every aircon repair need a flush?

No. It is mostly advised after a compressor failure or when the system is contaminated. Ask the workshop to explain.
