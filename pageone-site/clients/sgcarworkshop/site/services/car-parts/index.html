What car parts involves

Car parts shops supply replacement parts such as filters, brakes, suspension, lights and body parts. Parts come as original (from the carmaker), OEM (from the same supplier) or aftermarket.

Signs you may need it

  • You have a part number or a workshop has told you which part is needed
  • You are comparing original and aftermarket options

What to ask the workshop

  • Whether the part is original, OEM or aftermarket
  • Whether it fits your exact model and year (have your chassis number ready)
  • What the return policy is

Questions about car parts

What is the difference between OEM and aftermarket parts?

OEM parts come from the same supplier that makes them for the carmaker. Aftermarket parts are made by other companies and vary in fit and quality.
