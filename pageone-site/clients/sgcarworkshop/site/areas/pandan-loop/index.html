Car workshops in Pandan Loop

Pandan Loop is a small industrial area in the West region, near Clementi and Jurong East.

Pandan Loop is a small industrial area in the south-west, near Pandan Reservoir and Clementi. Clementi MRT station on the East West Line is the nearest station, with Clementi Mall and West Coast Park nearby. Pandan Loop and the roads around it hold light industrial units and trade premises. The Ayer Rajah Expressway and West Coast Highway pass close by, and Jurong East and Bukit Batok are the neighbouring areas.
