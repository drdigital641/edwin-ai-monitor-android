Car workshops in Woodlands

Woodlands is the main industrial area in the north-west, around Woodlands Industrial Park and the Woodlands Avenue roads, close to the Causeway.

Woodlands is the largest town in the north-west of Singapore, facing Johor Bahru across the Causeway. Woodlands MRT station, which serves both the North South and Thomson-East Coast lines, is beside Causeway Point and Woodlands Civic Centre, and Woodlands Checkpoint is the main land crossing to Malaysia. Industrial and trade units are found around Woodlands Industrial Park E and Woodlands Avenue 12, away from the town centre. The Bukit Timah Expressway and Seletar Expressway connect the area to the city, and Admiralty and Marsiling are the neighbouring districts. Woodlands Waterfront Park sits on the northern shore.
