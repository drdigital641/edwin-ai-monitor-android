  • Nearest MRT: Kranji MRT (NS7, North South Line), about 1.3 km. Next nearest: Marsiling MRT (NS8, North South Line), about 2.4 km.
  • Where the workshops are: 60 Jalan Lam Huat (17). Many share one building, so check the unit number before you park.
  • Opening days: of the 16 workshops here with Google Maps hours, 16 open on Saturday, none on Sunday and 2 open late on weekdays. Use the Open Sunday and Open late filters above.

  • Payment: on Google Maps, 9 accept credit cards, 9 accept debit cards, 7 accept contactless.
