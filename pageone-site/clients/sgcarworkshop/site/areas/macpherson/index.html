Car workshops in MacPherson

MacPherson is an area in the Central region around MacPherson Road, close to Ubi and Paya Lebar.

MacPherson is in the central region, between Paya Lebar and Toa Payoh. MacPherson MRT station, an interchange between the Circle and Downtown lines, is the nearest station, with Paya Lebar MRT to the east. The area is centred on MacPherson Road, Circuit Road and Pine Close, with a mix of HDB blocks, shophouses and light industrial units. The Pan-Island Expressway and Kallang-Paya Lebar Expressway pass close by, so it is an easy drive from Ubi, Geylang and Toa Payoh.
