Car workshops in Alexandra and Bukit Merah

Alexandra and Bukit Merah cover the south side of the Central region, around Alexandra Road, Bukit Merah and Henderson.

Alexandra and Bukit Merah are in the south-west of the central region. Redhill MRT station and Queenstown MRT station on the East West Line serve the area, with Tiong Bahru MRT close by. Alexandra Road is home to Alexandra Hospital and the IKEA Alexandra store, and Henderson Road and Bukit Merah Central hold the local HDB estates and shops. The Ayer Rajah Expressway and Central Expressway link the area with the city and the west, and Telok Blangah is the next district to the south.
