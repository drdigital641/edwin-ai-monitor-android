About Kranji

Kranji is in the north-west of Singapore, between Woodlands and the Lim Chu Kang farmland. Kranji MRT station on the North South Line is the nearest station, and the Kranji War Memorial and Kranji Reservoir are the well-known landmarks. The industrial pocket lies around Kranji Way, Kranji Road and Jalan Lam Huat, and extends towards Sungei Kadut. The Bukit Timah Expressway and Kranji Expressway pass close by, so it connects quickly to Woodlands, Choa Chu Kang and the Second Link crossing at Tuas for drivers who travel for work.
