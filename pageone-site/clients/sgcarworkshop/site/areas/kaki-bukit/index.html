About Kaki Bukit

Kaki Bukit sits between Bedok and Paya Lebar in the east of Singapore. Kaki Bukit MRT station on the Downtown Line serves the estate, and Bedok Reservoir and Bedok Reservoir MRT lie a short drive to the east. The area is mostly light industrial, laid out around Kaki Bukit Avenue 1 to 6, Kaki Bukit Road and Kaki Bukit Industrial Terrace, with trade units, warehouses and car-related businesses sharing the same blocks. Many drivers come here from the surrounding Bedok, Tampines and Geylang neighbourhoods, and the Pan-Island Expressway and Tampines Expressway are both close, which makes it easy to reach from most of the east.
