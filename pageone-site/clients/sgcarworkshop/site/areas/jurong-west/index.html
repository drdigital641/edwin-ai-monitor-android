In short: 25 workshops are listed in Jurong West, across 2 industrial estates. The most common services here are general servicing and repair, spray painting and bodywork and car detailing and coating.

Jurong West is a large area in the West region with industrial units alongside housing estates.

Jurong West is a large residential and industrial area in the west. Boon Lay MRT station and Pioneer MRT station on the East West Line serve it, and Jurong Point is the main shopping mall, beside the Boon Lay bus interchange. Nanyang Technological University lies to the west, and Jurong Lake and the Jurong Industrial Estate are to the east. The Pan-Island Expressway and Ayer Rajah Expressway link it with the city, and Tuas and Jurong East are the nearest neighbouring areas.
