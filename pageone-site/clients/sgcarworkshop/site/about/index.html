About SG Car Workshop

SG Car Workshop is a directory of car workshops and car services in Singapore, run by Page One Singapore, a web and SEO service for small businesses.

How entries are checked

Every fact on a page comes from the business or from its public records, and the business confirms it before the directory goes live. If a detail cannot be confirmed, such as a price or opening hours, we leave it out. We never copy a business's website text. We do not rank workshops or use rating markup; a rating line appears only with a source link and the date we checked.

Who we list

Car workshops and car services only: repair and servicing, gearbox, ABS and ECU modules, spray painting and bodywork, batteries, detailing and coating, wraps and PPF, and parts. Car dealers and insurance are not listed.

A note on one listing

SG Garage, a workshop in Ang Mo Kio, belongs to the founder of Page One Singapore. We list it like any other entry and say so here.
