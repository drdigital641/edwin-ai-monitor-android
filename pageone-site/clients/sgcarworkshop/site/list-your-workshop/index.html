What we need from you

  • Your agreement in writing (WhatsApp is fine) and permission to use your photos
  • Business name, area, phone or WhatsApp, and the services you really offer
  • For Verified: your address, opening hours, a few photos of real jobs, and a review source we can link to

What we do not do

We do not rank workshops, use rating or review markup, or list car dealers or insurers. A rating line is shown only when it comes from a source people can open, with its link and the date we checked. Anything we cannot confirm with you stays off the page.
