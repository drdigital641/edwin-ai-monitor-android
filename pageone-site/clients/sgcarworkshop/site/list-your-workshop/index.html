Claim your free workshop listing

Claiming your listing on SG Car Workshop is free. Drivers already find your workshop here, with your WhatsApp and Call buttons. Claim it to run it yourself.

What you get, free

  • A verified badge once we have checked that you run the business.
  • Your own photos, services and brands added to your page, so drivers see what you really do.
  • Correct hours and WhatsApp, kept up to date by you.
  • Better matching to the right guide. Your claimed details and specialties help drivers match you to the right car problem or brand guide. There is no order advantage: guides list workshops A to Z.
  • An open count of drivers who tapped WhatsApp and Call through SG Car Workshop. We show your total WhatsApp tap count, all-time and never reset, on your card and in the estate list, like "12 drivers have WhatsApped this workshop via SG Car Workshop", once it reaches 5. Counts are anonymous and hold no personal data. We tell you your Call count too.

No fee, no contract, and you can ask us to remove your listing at any time. We do not promise a number of enquiries.

How listings are ordered
