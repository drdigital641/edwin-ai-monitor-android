How to choose a car workshop or car repair workshop in Singapore

Most drivers look for a car workshop only when something goes wrong, so it helps to know what to look for before you need one. These are simple checks that work for servicing, repairs and accident work.

General guide in plain words. It is not a diagnosis and gives no prices: only a workshop that inspects your car can say what is wrong.

What to look for

  1. Work that matches your problem and your car

    Some workshops do mostly servicing, others focus on gearbox, aircon, bodywork or certain makes. Check the services and car makes on the workshop page, and ask if they often work on your model.

  2. A clear written quotation

    A good habit is to ask for a written quote that lists parts and labour separately before any work starts, and to ask the workshop to call you before doing anything extra.

  3. Explanations you can follow

    You should come away knowing what is wrong, why a part needs changing and what can wait. Ask to see the old parts that were replaced.

  4. Parts choice

    Ask whether they will fit original, OEM or aftermarket parts, and what the difference means for your car. Let them explain the options.

  5. Warranty on the work

    Ask whether the repair or parts come with a warranty, how long it lasts and what it covers. Keep the invoice.

  6. Easy to reach and easy to get to

    A workshop you can WhatsApp for updates, with opening hours that suit you and a location near home or work, makes follow-up visits simpler.

What to ask before you book

  • Your car make, model, year and mileage.
  • What you have noticed and when it started.
  • Ask for a written quote before work starts.
  • Ask them to call you before any extra work.
  • Ask what warranty comes with the parts and labour.
  • Ask how long the job usually takes and whether you need to leave the car.
Walk away or get a second opinion if: the quote keeps changing without a clear reason, you are pushed to decide on the spot, or the workshop will not explain what it found. Getting a second opinion on a big repair is normal.

Questions

Is a car workshop the same as a car repair workshop?

People use both words for the same thing: a place that services and repairs cars. Some focus on certain jobs, such as bodywork or gearbox, so check what each one lists.

Do I have to go back to the dealer for servicing?

Many owners use independent workshops after the warranty period. If your car is still under the maker's warranty, check its terms first, and keep every service record.

How many quotes should I get?

For a big repair, two or three quotes for the same job help you see what is fair. Make sure each quote covers the same parts and work.
