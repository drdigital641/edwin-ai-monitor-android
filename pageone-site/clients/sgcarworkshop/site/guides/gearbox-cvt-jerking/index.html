Gearbox or CVT jerking, shuddering or slipping

Jerking, shuddering or a delay when you pull away can come from the gearbox, but not always. Engine problems and worn mounts can feel the same, so a proper check matters before anyone talks about replacing a gearbox.

General guide in plain words. It is not a diagnosis and gives no prices: only a workshop that inspects your car can say what is wrong.

Likely causes

  1. Old or low transmission fluid

    The fluid cools and lubricates the gearbox. Old, burnt or low fluid is a common cause of rough shifting and shudder. Many makers list a fluid service interval in the manual.

  2. Worn belt, chain or clutches

    A CVT uses a belt or chain and pulleys; a conventional automatic uses clutches. Wear here can cause shudder, slipping or a whining noise.

  3. Faulty solenoids or valve body

    These control the fluid pressure that makes the gearbox shift. A fault can cause harsh or delayed shifts.

  4. Sensor, wiring or software issues

    The gearbox computer relies on sensors. A fault can cause odd shifting, and a warning light may come on.

  5. Engine misfire or worn mounts

    A misfiring engine or a worn engine or gearbox mount can feel like gearbox jerking. This is why the whole picture needs checking.

What to tell the workshop

  • When it happens: cold start, pulling away, a particular speed, or when braking.
  • Whether it jerks in Drive only or also in Reverse.
  • Any warning lights or fluid leaks under the car.
  • When the gearbox fluid was last changed.
  • Ask for a scan for fault codes and a fluid check before any major work.
Do not wait if: If the gearbox slips badly, will not move the car, or you see a fluid leak or smell burning, avoid driving it and have it checked.

Questions

Does every jerk mean a gearbox rebuild?

No. Fluid, a sensor or an engine problem can cause similar symptoms. Ask for the cheaper checks first, such as a scan and a fluid inspection.

Should I change CVT fluid?

Follow the maker's guidance for your model and ask the workshop which fluid is specified. CVTs are often fussy about the fluid used.
