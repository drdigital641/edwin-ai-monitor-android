Honda servicing and repair

Honda cars are widely used in Singapore. These notes cover what to ask the workshop and the maintenance topics that matter for many Honda models.

General guide in plain words. It is not a diagnosis and gives no prices: only a workshop that inspects your car can say what is wrong.

What to know and ask

  1. Service to the maker's schedule

    Check the owner's manual for the oil, filter and fluid intervals for your model. Ask the workshop to quote against that schedule.

  2. CVT fluid

    Many Honda models use a CVT, and these are usually specific about the fluid. Ask which fluid the workshop uses and why.

  3. Engine oil and oil leaks

    Keep the oil level and change interval in check. A slow oil leak from a gasket is worth finding early.

  4. Aircon performance

    If the aircon is weak or noisy, have it checked for leaks and compressor health rather than only topping up.

  5. Suspension and brakes

    Ask for an inspection of bushes, links and brakes at servicing, especially on cars with higher mileage.

  6. Hybrid and newer models

    Newer and hybrid models have more electronics. Ask whether the workshop has the diagnostic tools for your model.

What to tell the workshop

  • Model, year and engine or hybrid type.
  • Mileage and service history.
  • Symptoms: noises, shuddering, warning lights, smells.
  • Whether you want the work done to the maker's schedule or only fix the fault.
Do not wait if: A red warning light, overheating or a drop in braking performance should be checked before you drive further.

Questions

Does my Honda need special fluid?

Many Honda transmissions are specific about fluid. Check the manual and ask the workshop which fluid they use.

Can an independent workshop service my Honda?

Yes, many owners use independent workshops. Ask about their experience with your model.
