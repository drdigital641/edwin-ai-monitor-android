Questions

What is SG Car Workshop?

A directory of car workshops and car services in Singapore. Each entry shows what the business does, where it is and how to contact it, using details confirmed by the business or taken from its public listing.

How do I contact a workshop?

Tap WhatsApp or Call on a card. The WhatsApp message is prefilled with the workshop name and says you found them on SG Car Workshop, so you only need to add your car and what you need.

Do you rank or review workshops?

No. Entries are not ranked and we do not write reviews. Profile pages may show a rating line from a source people can open, with its link and the date we checked.

Which regions can I search?

Singapore has five planning regions: North, North-East, East, West and Central. Pick one or more in the search panel to see workshops that serve them. Mobile services can cover several regions.

Own a workshop? List it here

Car workshops and car services only. Basic listings are free.
