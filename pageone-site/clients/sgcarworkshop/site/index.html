How the listings work

  1. Free card. Every workshop has a free card and short page built from its public Google Business Profile: address, WhatsApp and Call using the mobile number on that profile, Directions, and a link to its Google Maps listing.
  2. Claim it free. Owners can claim their listing for free: correct hours and WhatsApp, add your own photos, services and brands, and see how many drivers tapped WhatsApp and Call.
  3. Featured (paid). Featured workshops at the top of the home page are paid placements and are marked Featured (paid).

Questions

What is SG Car Workshop?

A directory of car workshops and car services in Singapore. Each entry shows what the business does, where it is and how to contact it, using details from the business or its public listing.

How do I contact a workshop?

Tap WhatsApp or Call on a card. The WhatsApp message is prefilled with the workshop name and says you found them on SG Car Workshop, so you only need to add your car and what you need.

Do you rank or review workshops?

We do not sell rankings and we do not write reviews. Where an estate has at least 8 workshops with 20 or more Google ratings, we rank those by Google rating and number of ratings together; the formula is on the About page. Paid Featured workshops are never part of a ranking.

How can I browse by area?

Pick a region (North, North-East, East, West, Central, or Mobile for services that come to you) in the search panel, or open an estate page such as Kaki Bukit or Yishun to see the workshops in that industrial area, a map and nearby areas.
