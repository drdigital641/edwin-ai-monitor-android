Cleanic Detailing

Mobile service, island-wide

Cleanic Detailing is a mobile car fumigation and interior cleaning service run by Colin. The work is done where the car is parked, at an HDB, condo or office carpark across Singapore. Questions

Do you work at night and on weekends?

Yes, every day including public holidays, subject to availability. It is a 24-hour mobile service.

How much does car fumigation cost?

The MIRAKN Express package is $118 and takes about an hour, without steam cleaning. Full MIRAKN dry gas fumigation starts from $180 and takes about an hour and a half.

How much is interior cleaning and how long does it take?

Hot dry steam cleaning and shampoo scrub cleaning each start from $148. Most full interior jobs take one to two hours on site.

Do I need to bring my car to a workshop?

No. The work is done where the car is parked, at an HDB, condo, office or other accessible location in Singapore.
