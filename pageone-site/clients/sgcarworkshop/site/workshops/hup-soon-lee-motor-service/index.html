Hup Soon Lee Motor Service

Yishun · North

HS

Hup Soon Lee Motor Service is a long-running car repair workshop at 1018 Yishun Industrial Park A. According to its Google listing it works on private and commercial vehicles, including Euro 4, 5 and 6 models. Call to ask about your car.

Yishun is in the North planning region of Singapore, alongside Woodlands, Sembawang and Kranji.
