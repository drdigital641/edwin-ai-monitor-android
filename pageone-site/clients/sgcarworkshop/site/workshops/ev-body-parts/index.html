EV Body Parts

Search its catalogue by car and part, or send the car brand, model, year and a photo of the part on WhatsApp. Fitment is checked before you get a quote. It serves workshops and car owners.

Questions

Which cars are covered?

China and EV cars such as Tesla, BYD, Denza, MG, Zeekr and XPeng. If your part is not in the catalogue, ask on WhatsApp anyway.

How do I get a price?

Send the car brand, model, build year, the part (and left or right) and a photo on WhatsApp. Fitment is checked first, then you get a quote.

Do you sell to workshops?

Yes. It serves both workshops and car owners in Singapore.

Are the pictures the actual parts?

No. The catalogue pictures are illustrations. The actual part and its fitment are confirmed before the quote.
