Wei Car Spray Painting

Yishun

Wei Car Spray Painting is a spray painting and bodywork workshop at Blk 1017 Yishun Industrial Park A, Singapore. It resprays single panels and whole cars, changes car colours and fits body kits. WhatsApp or call 9638 3306 with photos of your car to ask about your job.

Services

  • Car spray paintingSingle panels or several panels resprayed in your car's colour.
  • Full respray and colour changeThe whole car repainted in its own colour or a new one. Check the current LTA rules on OneMotoring before you change your car's colour.
  • Body kit installationKits and spoilers fitted to the car and painted to match.

Recent work

About the workshop

The company, Wei Car Spray Painting Pte. Ltd., was incorporated in June 2012 (UEN 201215981N) and is registered for repair and maintenance of motor vehicles. The workshop is in Yishun Industrial Park A, across three adjoining units, and the shopfront photo shows its signboard with a spray booth behind it.

Most of what the workshop posts on Facebook is paint work: full resprays, colour changes and the occasional body kit. One post, from March 2023, thanks a customer who was back for the fourth time.

Jobs the workshop has posted

  • Porsche Macan: full body colour change to Fountain Blue Metallic, May 2025.
  • Porsche Cayenne: new-model body kit, imported from overseas and installed, February 2026.
  • MG: exterior respray in British Racing Green, November 2023.
  • Honda Civic: full respray inside and out, gloss black roof and a new spoiler, November 2023.
  • Mini Cooper: new colour, from orange to pink, May 2022.

How to ask for a quote

Send clear photos of the car in daylight, with the model and year, and say what you want done. Prices are not listed on this page, so ask Wei directly on WhatsApp.

Questions

Where is Wei Car Spray Painting?

At Blk 1017 Yishun Industrial Park A, units #01-242, #01-246 and #01-252, Singapore 768759. The Google Maps link on this page opens the location.

What work does the workshop do?

Panel resprays, full resprays, colour changes and body kit installation with matching paint, done in a spray booth.

How do I get a quote?

WhatsApp or call 9638 3306 with clear daylight photos of the car, its model and year, and what you want done. Prices are not listed on this page.

Can I change my car's colour freely?

Check the current LTA rules on OneMotoring before you change your car's colour, and tell the workshop what you plan.
