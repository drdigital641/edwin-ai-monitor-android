Soon Kim Motor Workshop

Ang Mo Kio

4.8 (44 Google ratings) (checked November 2026) See Google reviews

Soon Kim Motor Workshop is a tyre shop and car workshop at 5033 Ang Mo Kio Industrial Park 2. The signboard reads Soon Kim Motor Repair Workshop, with Kim Ker Tyre Service inside. Tyres come first here: new tyres, tyre changes, puncture repairs and wheel balancing. Car batteries, aircon service and general repairs are done in the same unit. It is open every day from 8am to 6pm, Sunday included. WhatsApp 9228 2188 or call 6481 9656.

Inside the shop

Tyres first

Most of the shop is tyres. Racks of new tyres line the walls and more are stacked outside, and a wheel balancing machine sits in the middle of the bay. Bring the car in for a tyre change, a puncture check or balancing, or WhatsApp your tyre size first to check stock.

Batteries are kept in stock too, so a flat battery and a worn tyre can be sorted in one visit.

A tyre shop open on Sunday

Many workshops in Singapore close on Sunday. Soon Kim's Google Maps hours show it open every day from 8am to 6pm, and several Google reviews mention getting a punctured tyre or a small fault fixed on a Sunday morning. If you need tyres on a Sunday in Ang Mo Kio, it is worth a message before you drive over.

What customers mention on Google

Customers on Google Maps mention quick puncture repairs, help on Sundays when other workshops were closed, fast small repairs such as brake lights, friendly service without hard selling, and coming back for regular servicing. Google shows a rating of 4.8 from 44 reviews (checked 7 November 2026).

Questions

Is Soon Kim open on Sunday?

Yes. Its Google Maps hours show it open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 8am to 6pm (checked 7 November 2026). One review says it closes on the second day of Chinese New Year.

Which tyre brands does it carry?

Brands seen in the shop include Falken, Continental, Yokohama and Sincera. WhatsApp your tyre size to check what is in stock.

Can it balance my wheels after a tyre change?

Yes. The shop has a CEMB wheel balancing machine for balancing wheels after new tyres are fitted.

What else does it do besides tyres?

Car battery replacement (Amaron and Delkor in stock), car aircon service and general motor repair.
