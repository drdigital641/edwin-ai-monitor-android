JSS MotorWorkz

Toh Guan · West

J

JSS MotorWorkz is a workshop on level 2 of Enterprise Hub, 48 Toh Guan Road East. Its Google listing covers servicing, engine and gearbox repair and overhauls, diagnostics, aircon, walnut blasting, tyres and batteries. Message the workshop on WhatsApp to ask about your car.

Toh Guan is in the Jurong East planning area, part of the West region of Singapore.
