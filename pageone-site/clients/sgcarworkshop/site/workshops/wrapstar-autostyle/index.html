Wrapstar Autostyle

Yishun · wrapstarautostyle.com

Wrapstar Autostyle runs its own website.Visit wrapstarautostyle.com

Wrapstar Autostyle is a car wrap studio at North Spring Bizhub in Yishun. It offers colour change wraps, paint protection film in clear or colour, window film and dechrome. The studio's own website has a colour catalogue and a gallery of past jobs grouped by colour, which is the quickest way to see what a finish looks like on a real car. Message the studio on WhatsApp with your car model and the look you want. Check LTA rules before changing your car's colour.

What it does

  • Colour change wraps
  • Paint protection film (PPF), clear and colour
  • Window film
  • Dechrome
