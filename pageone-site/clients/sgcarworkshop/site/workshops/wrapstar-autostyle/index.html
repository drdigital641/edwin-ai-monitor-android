Questions

How much does a car wrap cost?

The studio's posted starting price for a full colour wrap is $1,588. Colour PPF, clear PPF, window film and dechrome are priced by car and film, so send your model on WhatsApp for a quote.

Should I pick a vinyl wrap or colour PPF?

Vinyl is the lower-cost way to change colour. Colour PPF also changes colour and adds protection against stone chips, so it suits owners who want protection as well as looks.

Which colours can I choose?

The studio's website shows colours from past jobs, from satin black and matte green to baby blue and bright yellow, and other colours can be sourced on request.

Where is the studio and when is it open?

9 Yishun Industrial Street 1, #01-82, North Spring Bizhub, Singapore 768163. Open Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm and Saturday 9am to 3pm, closed Sunday.
