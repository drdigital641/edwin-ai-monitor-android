Lim Tan Motor

Sin Ming and Bishan · Central

Lim Tan Motor is a car workshop at 176 Sin Ming Drive, Sin Ming Autocare in Sin Ming and Bishan. Its own website lists diagnostic checks, brake repair and aircon repair and maintenance.

Sin Ming and Bishan is in the Bishan planning area, part of the Central region of Singapore.
