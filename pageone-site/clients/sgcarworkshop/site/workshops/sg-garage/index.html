SG Garage

Ang Mo Kio · sggarage.com

SG Garage runs its own website.Visit sggarage.com

SG Garage is a car workshop in Ang Mo Kio that handles everyday servicing and repairs. Its recent work includes alternator replacements, where the team first checks the battery voltage with a multimeter so that a weak battery is not mistaken for a charging fault. It also works on car aircon. The workshop belongs to the founder of Page One Singapore, who built this directory, so we say so openly. Full service details and ways to book are on the workshop's own website, one tap away.

What it does

  • Battery and charging system checks
  • Alternator replacement
  • Car aircon repair
