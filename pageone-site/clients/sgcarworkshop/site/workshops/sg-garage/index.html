Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes for servicing, repairs and diagnosis: book a slot online or message on WhatsApp. Battery replacement is the exception, it is walk-in from Monday to Saturday, 10am to 8pm, subject to stock.

Are the listed prices the final price?

They are starting prices that cover parts, labour and GST. The final price is confirmed after the car is inspected, and nothing starts until you agree. If you decide against the repair, diagnosis is $30.

Where is SG Garage and when is it open?

Blk 5032 Ang Mo Kio Industrial Park 2, #01-295, Singapore 569535, open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 8pm, closed on Sundays.

Which cars can it work on?

Japanese, Korean and European cars, hybrids and electric cars. Tesla work has been part of the mix since 2019.

How can I pay?

Cash or PayNow are preferred. Credit cards carry a 3% surcharge.
