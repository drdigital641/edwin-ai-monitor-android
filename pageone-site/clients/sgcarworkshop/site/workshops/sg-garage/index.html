SG Garage

Ang Mo Kio

SG Garage, also known as Edwin Garage, is an owner-run workshop in Ang Mo Kio Industrial Park 2. It services and repairs Japanese, Korean, European and electric cars, fixes car aircon and handles accident repairs with the insurance paperwork. WhatsApp 9785 6612 to ask about your car.

Services

  • Car servicingEngine oil and filter service. A major service is priced after the car is inspected.From $128 (Japanese, Korean) / $148 (European)
  • Car aircon repairGas top-up after a leak check, chemical wash, and cooling coil or compressor replacement when the fault goes deeper.Gas top-up from $20, chemical wash from $120, coil from $600, compressor from $800
  • Brake padsPad replacement, with the brakes checked before and after.From $120 (Japanese) / $140 (European)
  • Car battery replacementWalk-in, Monday to Saturday 10am to 8pm, subject to stock. The battery, alternator and starter are tested first so the right part is replaced.From $120, AGM from $250
  • ABS pump repairRepair of the ABS pump, with a new unit as the alternative.Repair from $950, new unit from $1,600
  • Accident claim and spray paintingAccident repair with help on the insurance claim, panel beating and spray painting.Quoted after inspection
  • Tesla, hybrid and EV workElectric and hybrid cars are worked on in the same bays. Tesla jobs since 2019.

Prices are starting prices and include parts, labour and GST. The final price is confirmed after inspection and before work starts. Diagnosis is $30 if you decide not to go ahead.

Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes for servicing, repairs and diagnosis: book a slot online or message on WhatsApp. Battery replacement is the exception, it is walk-in from Monday to Saturday, 10am to 8pm, subject to stock.

Are the listed prices the final price?

They are starting prices that cover parts, labour and GST. The final price is confirmed after the car is inspected, and nothing starts until you agree. If you decide against the repair, diagnosis is $30.

Where is SG Garage and when is it open?

Blk 5032 Ang Mo Kio Industrial Park 2, #01-295, Singapore 569535, open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 8pm, closed on Sundays.

Which cars can it work on?

Japanese, Korean and European cars, hybrids and electric cars. Tesla work has been part of the mix since 2019.

How can I pay?

Cash or PayNow are preferred. Credit cards carry a 3% surcharge.
