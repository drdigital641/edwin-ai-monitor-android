Jacky Gearbox

Woodlands and Kranji

Jacky Gearbox is a gearbox and automatic transmission specialist with a main workshop at Woodlands Wave 9 and a second one in Kranji. Jacky tests the gearbox himself, explains the fault, quotes before any work starts and road-tests the car before handing it back. WhatsApp or call 8430 9665.

Questions

How much does a gearbox repair cost?

It depends on the car and the fault, so there are no blind quotes. The diagnostic (from S$30) finds the cause, then you get a clear price before the repair starts.

Can you collect a car that cannot move?

Yes. Pick-up to the workshop for gearbox repairs is free during office hours, and a tow truck is sent free of charge if the transmission has failed.

Which cars does Jacky work on?

Continental, Japanese and Korean cars, plus makes such as Land Rover, Jaguar, Volvo, Ford, Renault, Peugeot and Chevrolet.

Is there a warranty?

Repairs carry a standard 6-month warranty unless stated otherwise.
