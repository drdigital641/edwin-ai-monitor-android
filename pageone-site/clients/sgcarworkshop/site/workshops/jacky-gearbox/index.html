Gearbox

Jacky Gearbox

Woodlands · jackygearbox.com

Jacky Gearbox runs its own website.Visit jackygearbox.com

Jacky Gearbox is a gearbox and transmission specialist in Woodlands, with a second workshop in Kranji. Jacky diagnoses the gearbox himself and explains the fault before any work starts. The pre-repair diagnostic starts from S$30, repairs carry a standard six-month warranty unless stated otherwise, and free towing is offered. Guides on slipping gears, jerking shifts and warning lights are on the workshop's own website, along with details for both locations. A second opinion on a gearbox quote is welcome too, so bring the quote you already have.

What it does

  • Gearbox diagnostic and repair
  • Gearbox upgrades
  • Gearbox (ATF) servicing
  • Free towing
