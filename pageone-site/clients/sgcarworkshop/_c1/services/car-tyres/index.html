What tyre shops involves

A tyre job starts with checking the damage and the tyre size. A small puncture in the tread can often be plugged or patched; a cut sidewall, a bulge or worn tread means a new tyre. After a new tyre is fitted, the wheel is balanced so it runs smoothly.

Signs you may need it

  • A nail or screw in the tread
  • A tyre that keeps losing pressure
  • A cut or bulge on the sidewall
  • Worn tread or uneven wear
  • Vibration or shaking at speed

What to ask the workshop

  • Whether the tyre can be repaired or must be replaced
  • Which brands and sizes are in stock
  • Whether balancing and new valves are included
  • Whether the other tyre on the same axle should be changed too

Questions about tyre shops

Where do I find my tyre size?

It is printed on the tyre sidewall, for example 195/65 R15. Send it to the shop with your car model.

Is wheel balancing the same as alignment?

No. Balancing stops a wheel from vibrating; alignment sets the angle of the wheels so the car drives straight and the tyres wear evenly.
