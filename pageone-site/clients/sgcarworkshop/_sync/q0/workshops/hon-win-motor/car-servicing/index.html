Every service on the job card

  • Engine oil and oil filter changed
  • OBD diagnostic scan
  • Wiper water, coolant and brake fluid topped up
  • Battery health checked
  • Engine warning lights checked
  • Belts, idling speed and ignition timing checked
  • Radiator hoses and water pump checked
  • Brake pads and engine mounts checked

Spark plugs and automatic gearbox oil are checked and changed only if they need it.

Pick your engine oil

We stock Total Quartz 9000, Petronas Syntium, Castrol Edge and Mobil 1. The price of a service depends on the oil you choose and how much your engine takes, so tell us your car model on WhatsApp and we will give you the price before we start.

Continental cars need brand-specific oil filters, so their servicing is priced differently from Japanese and Korean cars.

How long it takes

An oil and filter change takes about 20 to 40 minutes. A full service takes about 1.5 hours. Walk-ins are welcome, but a WhatsApp first lets us tell you if a lift is free.

Hybrids and EVs

We service petrol and hybrid cars. On electric cars we do not work on the high-voltage battery, but we can handle tyres, body repairs and other non-battery work.

Buying a used car? Check it first

We also do pre-purchase inspections at our workshop. It takes about 1.5 hours and covers engine and gearbox condition, electrical faults, hidden structural problems and known faults for that model, with an OBD scan report.

Questions

How long does a car service take?

An oil and filter change takes about 20 to 40 minutes. A full service takes about 1.5 hours.

Do I need to book a service?

No, walk-ins are welcome. WhatsApp 9171 8466 first to check that a lift is free.

Which engine oils do you use?

Total Quartz 9000, Petronas Syntium, Castrol Edge and Mobil 1. The price depends on the oil you pick.

Do you change parts that do not need changing?

No. Spark plugs and gearbox oil are checked and changed only if needed, and we quote before we replace any part.
