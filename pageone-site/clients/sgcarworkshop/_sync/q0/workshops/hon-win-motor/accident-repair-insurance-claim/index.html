Hon Win Motor / Accident claims

At the scene, if it is safe

  • Stop and switch on your hazard lights
  • Take photos of every car, the damage, the number plates and the road
  • Exchange names, phone numbers, car numbers and insurer names with the other driver
  • Keep your dash cam footage
  • Call for help first if anyone is hurt

Report the accident to your insurer within 24 hours, or by the next working day, even if you do not plan to claim. Your insurer will tell you where to report.

How we help

We do accident repairs and body work at our workshop in Defu Lane, and we help you with the insurance claim for the repair. Send us photos of the damage, your car model and your insurer's name on WhatsApp, and we will tell you what to do next.

If your car cannot be driven, call or WhatsApp 9171 8466. Towing is free when the repair is done at our workshop.

Check your policy first

Some motor policies only cover repairs at the insurer's own authorised workshops. Others let you choose. Check your policy, or send us your insurer's name and policy type and we will tell you if we can take the job before you decide.

When you also need a police report

Most accidents only need a report to your insurer. You also need to make a police report if anyone is injured, if the other driver drives off, or if a government vehicle, a foreign vehicle, or public property is involved.

Questions

Do you handle accident insurance claims?

Yes. We do the accident repair and help you with the insurance claim. WhatsApp us photos of the damage and your insurer's name to start.

Do I need to report the accident if I am not claiming?

Yes. Report it to your insurer within 24 hours, or by the next working day, even if you do not plan to claim.

Can I choose my own workshop?

It depends on your policy. Some only cover repairs at the insurer's authorised workshops. Send us your insurer and policy type and we will check with you.
