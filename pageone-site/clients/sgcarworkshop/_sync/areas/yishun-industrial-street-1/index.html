  • Nearest MRT: Yishun MRT (NS13, North South Line), about 1.2 km. Next nearest: Khatib MRT (NS14, North South Line), about 2.4 km.
  • Where the workshops are: 2 Yishun Industrial Street 1 (6). Many share one building, so check the unit number before you park.
  • Opening days: of the 6 workshops here with Google Maps hours, 6 open on Saturday, 3 on Sunday and 1 open late on weekdays. Use the Open Sunday and Open late filters above.
  • Parking: 2 workshops list parking on Google Maps: free parking lot (2), paid parking lot (1), free street parking (1). Parking rules differ by building, so check the signs at the entrance.
  • Payment: on Google Maps, 6 accept credit cards, 6 accept debit cards, 6 accept contactless.
