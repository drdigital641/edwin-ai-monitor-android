Car workshops in Tuas

In short: 2 workshops are listed in Tuas, across 1 industrial estates. The most common services here are general servicing and repair.

Tuas is the industrial area at the far west of Singapore, around Tuas Avenue and Tuas South.

Tuas is the industrial far west of Singapore. Tuas Link MRT station on the East West Line is the western end of the line, and Tuas West Road and Tuas Avenue run through the factory and warehouse estates. Tuas Mega Port and the Tuas Checkpoint at the Second Link to Malaysia are the well-known landmarks, and the Ayer Rajah Expressway and Pan-Island Expressway are the main routes from the rest of the island. Jurong West is the nearest residential area.
