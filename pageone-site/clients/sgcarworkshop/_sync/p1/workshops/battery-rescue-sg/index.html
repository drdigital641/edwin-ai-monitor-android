Battery Rescue SG

Onsite, north and north-east Singapore

Battery Rescue SG replaces car batteries where the car is stuck, so a flat battery does not mean waiting for a tow. Every battery fitted is a Delkor with a one-year warranty. Call the 24-hour hotline on 8125 9169, or send your location and car model on WhatsApp.

Questions

Can you come to me if my car will not start?

Yes, in Woodlands, Sembawang, Yishun, Ang Mo Kio, Yio Chu Kang, Hougang, Sengkang and Punggol. Call the 24-hour hotline or WhatsApp your location and car model.

Which battery brand do you fit?

Delkor only, made by Clarios Delkor in Korea. Every battery carries a one-year warranty.

How is the onsite price worked out?

It is the battery price plus a $50 onsite fee before 10pm, or plus $80 from 10pm to 9am and on public holidays. Example for an NS40ZL: $169.90 by day, $199.90 at night.

Where can I walk in?

At any of 10 Motoserv outlets, Monday to Saturday 9am to 6pm. The $20 website code applies to walk-ins only.
