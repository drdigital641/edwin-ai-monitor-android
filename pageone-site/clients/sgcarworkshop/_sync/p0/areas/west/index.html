Questions about this region

Which areas does the West page cover?

Car workshops and car services in the West region of Singapore, covering Toh Guan and Jurong East, Jurong West, Pandan Loop, Bukit Batok and Tuas.

How do I contact a workshop in the West region?

Tap WhatsApp or Call on a workshop card or page. WhatsApp messages are prefilled with the workshop name and say you found them on SG Car Workshop, so you only need to add your car and what you need.

Where can I look if there is nothing close to me?

Try another region: North, North-East, East, Central, Mobile (island-wide).
