Car detailing and coating in Singapore

Car detailing cleans and restores the inside and outside of a car beyond a normal wash. It can cover interior steam cleaning, fumigation, odour removal, polishing and protective coatings. Some detailers travel to you. Tell them what bothers you most, such as smell, stains or dull paint, so the job fits.

What car detailing and coating involves

Detailing cleans and restores a car inside and out. Exterior jobs include washing, paint correction (polishing out swirls) and protective coatings. Interior jobs include cleaning seats, carpets and the cabin.

Signs you may need it

  • Swirl marks or dull paint under sunlight
  • Water no longer beads on the paint
  • Stains or smells in the interior
  • Preparing a car for sale or after buying a used car

What to ask the workshop

  • Whether paint correction is done before a coating
  • How the coating should be washed and maintained
  • How long the car needs to stay
  • Which products are used

Questions about car detailing and coating

What is the difference between polishing and coating?

Polishing removes fine marks from the paint. A coating is a protective layer applied on top, usually after polishing.
