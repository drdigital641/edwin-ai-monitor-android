Car workshops in Defu and Hougang

Defu and Hougang cover the Defu Lane industrial area and the Hougang neighbourhood in the North-East region.

Defu and Hougang are in the north-east of Singapore. Defu Industrial Park (Defu Lane and Defu Avenue) is a light industrial pocket near Hougang and Kovan. Hougang MRT station and Kovan MRT station on the North East Line are the nearest stations, with Hougang Mall and Kovan Hub close to each. The Tampines Expressway and Kallang-Paya Lebar Expressway connect the area with Serangoon, Sengkang and Paya Lebar. Hougang Stadium and the Punggol and Sengkang towns lie to the north-east.
