New Zen Werkz

Kaki Bukit

New Zen Werkz is an independent car workshop at Eunos Technolink, Kaki Bukit Road 1. It services and repairs continental, Japanese and American cars, and rebuilds engines and gearboxes in its own workshop. The fault is diagnosed first and explained to you before work starts. WhatsApp 8678 1878 or call 6742 8117 to ask about your car.

Services

  • Car servicing and oil changeScheduled servicing, engine oil and filter changes and fluid checks, following the car maker's schedule.
  • Engine repair and overhaulFrom oil leaks and misfires to a full engine strip-down and rebuild in the workshop.
  • Gearbox and transmission repairDiagnosis and repair of automatic gearboxes that slip, shift harshly, leak or go into limp mode.
  • Brake repairBrake pads, discs, calipers and brake fluid, checked and replaced when due.
  • DiagnosticsWarning lights, odd noises and faults that come and go, traced to the cause before any repair.
  • Battery, aircon, steering and tyresBattery testing and replacement (including start-stop cars that need registering), aircon checks, steering and suspension work, and tyre replacement.

Questions

Which cars does New Zen Werkz work on?

Continental, Japanese and American cars. Mercedes-Benz and Bentley work sits alongside Japanese and American makes.

Can engines and gearboxes be rebuilt there?

Yes. Engines and automatic gearboxes are stripped, inspected and rebuilt in the workshop, not only swapped for replacement units.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. The car is diagnosed first, and you hear what was found and your options before any work starts.

Where is it and when is it open?

Blk 3 Kaki Bukit Road 1, #03-04 Eunos Technolink, Singapore 415935. Open Monday to Friday 9:30am to 7pm and Saturday 9:30am to 4pm, closed on Sunday.
