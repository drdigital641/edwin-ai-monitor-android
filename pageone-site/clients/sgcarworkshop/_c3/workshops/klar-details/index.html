Klar Details

Jurong East

Klar Details is a car detailing studio at 48 Toh Guan Road East, Enterprise Hub, in Jurong East, detailing cars since 2014. It does ceramic and graphene coating, paint correction, interior detailing and coating maintenance in enclosed bays. Every job starts with a free assessment and a fixed quote before work begins. WhatsApp 8917 3950 or call 6635 6703 to book.

Services

  • Ceramic coating (KlarCOAT)The studio's own ceramic coating, applied after the paint is assessed and prepared.
  • Graphene coating (KlarGRAPHENE)A graphene coating for Singapore weather. A bonnet trial lets you see it on your own car first.Bonnet trial S$88
  • Paint correctionSwirl marks, water spots, oxidation and light scratches corrected in enclosed bays.
  • Interior detailing (KlarINTERIOR)Deep cleaning with controlled-pressure steam, seat extraction and leather care, plus anti-bacterial fumigation.
  • Coating maintenance (KlarMAINTENANCE)A half-yearly coating inspection, a top layer and a pure-water final rinse.
  • New car and EV protection, premium hand washProtection packages for new cars and EVs, and a hand wash with exterior decontamination.

Questions

How is the price worked out?

By car size, paint condition and the correction and coating chosen. You get a fixed quote after a free assessment, before any work starts.

Can I try graphene coating first?

Yes. The bonnet trial is S$88 and covers a hand wash, preparation, graphene coating on the bonnet, a water-beading demo and a gloss treatment.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. The studio works by appointment, Monday to Friday 9am to 8pm and Saturday 9am to 2pm, closed on Sunday and public holidays.

Where is the studio?

48 Toh Guan Road East, #02-124, Enterprise Hub, Singapore 608586, a few minutes from IMM and Jurong East MRT.
