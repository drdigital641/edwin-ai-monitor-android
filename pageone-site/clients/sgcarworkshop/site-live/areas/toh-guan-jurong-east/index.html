Toh Guan and Jurong East are in the West region, around Toh Guan Road East and the Enterprise Hub industrial building. SG Car Workshop lists 2 workshops here: JSS Motorworkz and Revol Carz Makeover.

About Toh Guan and Jurong East

Toh Guan and Jurong East are in the west of Singapore. Jurong East MRT station, an interchange for the North South and East West lines, sits beside JEM, Westgate and IMM, and the Chinese Garden and Science Centre Singapore are nearby. Toh Guan Road and the Toh Guan industrial estate hold trade units, warehouses and car-related businesses. The Ayer Rajah Expressway and Pan-Island Expressway pass close by, so the area is easy to reach from Clementi, Bukit Batok and Jurong West.

SG Car Workshop lists the workshops it has for this area on one page, so you can compare services, open each workshop page for its address and contact details, and message a workshop directly on WhatsApp or by phone. Listings show general servicing and repair and spray painting and bodywork.
