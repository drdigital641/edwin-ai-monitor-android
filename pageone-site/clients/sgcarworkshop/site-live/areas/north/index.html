Car workshops in the North region

Car workshops and car services in the North region of Singapore, covering Woodlands, Kranji, Yishun and the Sembawang and Admiralty areas.

The North region covers the towns along the Causeway and the Straits of Johor: Woodlands, Kranji, Yishun, Sembawang and Admiralty. The North South Line runs the length of it, from Yishun to Woodlands, and the Seletar Expressway and Bukit Timah Expressway are the main roads.
