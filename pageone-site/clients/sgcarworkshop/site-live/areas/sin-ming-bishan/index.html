Car workshops in Sin Ming and Bishan

Sin Ming and Bishan take in the Sin Ming Industrial Estate and the Bishan area in the middle of Singapore.

Sin Ming and Bishan lie in the centre-north of Singapore. Sin Ming Industrial Estate, around Sin Ming Road, Sin Ming Lane and is a long-running motor trade area, while Bishan town has Junction 8 and Bishan MRT station, an interchange between the North South and Circle lines. Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park is a large green space nearby, and Thomson Road and the Central Expressway are the main routes. Toa Payoh and Ang Mo Kio are the neighbouring areas for anyone looking a little further out.
