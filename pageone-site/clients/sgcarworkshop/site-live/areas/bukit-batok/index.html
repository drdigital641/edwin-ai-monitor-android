Car workshops in Bukit Batok

Bukit Batok has industrial parks in the West region, including Bukit Batok Industrial Park A.

Bukit Batok is a town in the west of Singapore. Bukit Batok MRT station on the North South Line sits beside West Mall, and Bukit Gombak and Jurong East stations are the next stops either side. Bukit Batok Nature Park and Little Guilin are popular green spaces, while Bukit Batok Industrial Park A holds light industrial units. The Pan-Island Expressway and Bukit Timah Expressway connect it to Choa Chu Kang, Jurong East and the city.
