In short: 11 workshops are listed in Tampines, across 2 industrial estates. The most common services here are general servicing and repair, spray painting and bodywork and car aircon repair.

Tampines is a town in the East region with light industrial parks around Tampines Street 92 and 93.

Tampines is a large town in the east of Singapore. Tampines MRT station on the East West and Downtown Lines, with Tampines West and Tampines East stations on the Downtown Line, serve the town, and Tampines Mall, Century Square and Our Tampines Hub are its main landmarks. Alongside the HDB estates there are light industrial parks around Tampines Street 92 and 93, close to Bedok Reservoir. The Tampines Expressway and Pan-Island Expressway both run nearby, so it is an easy drive from Bedok, Pasir Ris, Simei and Kaki Bukit.
