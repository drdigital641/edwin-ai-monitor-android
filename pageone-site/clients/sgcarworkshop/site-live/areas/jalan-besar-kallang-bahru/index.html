In short: 1 workshops are listed in Jalan Besar and Kallang Bahru, across 1 industrial estates. The most common services here are car detailing and coating, general servicing and repair and spray painting and bodywork.

Jalan Besar and Kallang Bahru are central areas with a long history of motor trade units and workshops.

Jalan Besar and Kallang Bahru are close to the city centre. Jalan Besar MRT station on the Downtown Line and Lavender MRT station on the East West Line serve the area, and Farrer Park is nearby. Jalan Besar is known for its shophouses and the Jalan Besar Stadium, while Kallang Bahru and Kallang Basin are close to the Singapore Sports Hub and the National Stadium. The Pan-Island Expressway and Kallang-Paya Lebar Expressway connect the area to the east and north of the island.
