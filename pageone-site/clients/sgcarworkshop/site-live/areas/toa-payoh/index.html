Car workshops in Toa Payoh

In short: 2 workshops are listed in Toa Payoh, across 1 industrial estates. The most common services here are general servicing and repair and tyre shops.

Toa Payoh has an industrial park and trade units in the Central region, close to Bishan and MacPherson.

Toa Payoh is one of Singapore's oldest HDB towns, in the central region. Toa Payoh MRT station on the North South Line sits beside HDB Hub and Toa Payoh Central, and Braddell MRT lies to the north. Toa Payoh Industrial Park and the Toa Payoh Lorong 8 area hold light industrial units and trade premises. The Central Expressway and Pan-Island Expressway run along the edges of the town, so the area is quick to reach from Bishan, Novena and MacPherson.
