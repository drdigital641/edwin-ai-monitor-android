Car workshops in Yishun

Yishun has two main motor trade clusters, Yishun Industrial Park A and the Yishun Industrial Street 1 area around North Spring Bizhub. SG Car Workshop lists 3 workshops here: Wrapstar Autostyle, Wei Car Spray Painting and Saaban Car Workshop.

About Yishun

Yishun is a large northern town, with Yishun MRT station on the North South Line beside Northpoint City and the Yishun bus interchange. Khoo Teck Puat Hospital and Yishun Pond are on the north side of the town, and Lower Seletar Reservoir lies to the east. Yishun has two motor trade clusters: Yishun Industrial Park A and the area around Yishun Industrial Street 1, near North Spring Bizhub. The Seletar Expressway and Central Expressway link it to Ang Mo Kio and the city, and Sembawang and Woodlands are the nearest neighbouring areas.

SG Car Workshop lists the workshops it has for this area on one page, so you can compare services, open each workshop page for its address and contact details, and message a workshop directly on WhatsApp or by phone. Listings show car wrap and ppf, spray painting and bodywork and general servicing and repair. If you want to compare more options nearby, see Woodlands, Ang Mo Kio.
