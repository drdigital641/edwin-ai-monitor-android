Privacy

This page explains what SG Car Workshop collects and what it shows from other sources.

Listing information

Basic workshop pages show business details from the workshop's public Google Business Profile, such as its name, address, opening hours and mobile number. To correct or remove a listing, use Remove my listing.

Taps on contact buttons

When a live visitor taps WhatsApp, Call, Directions or a Google Maps link, we record the tap with the workshop, the page, a random visit identifier and the device type, so we can report results to the business. We do not record the message you write. Do-not-track browsers are not counted.

Google Maps

Some pages load a Google Maps embed, and the Show opening hours button asks Google Maps for opening hours when you press it. Google handles that data under its Privacy Policy and the Google Maps Terms. We do not store ratings, reviews, photos or phone numbers from Google.
