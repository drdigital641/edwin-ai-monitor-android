How the listings work

  1. Basic listingGet foundA free card and short profile page: name, address, services, WhatsApp and Call using the number the workshop publishes itself, Directions, and a link to its Google Maps listing.
  2. VerifiedLanding pageA full page with real photos, services, address, map, reviews line and FAQs.
  3. PremiumFull Page One website setupEverything in Verified, plus the business's own website and local search work. Premium workshops are featured at the top.

Questions

What is SG Car Workshop?

A directory of car workshops and car services in Singapore. Each entry shows what the business does, where it is and how to contact it, using details from the business or its public listing.

How do I contact a workshop?

Tap WhatsApp or Call on a card. The WhatsApp message is prefilled with the workshop name and says you found them on SG Car Workshop, so you only need to add your car and what you need.

Do you rank or review workshops?

No. Entries are not ranked and we do not write reviews. Profile pages may show a rating line from a source people can open, with its link and the date we checked.

How can I browse by area?

Pick a region (North, North-East, East, West, Central, or Mobile for services that come to you) in the search panel, or open an estate page such as Kaki Bukit or Yishun to see the workshops in that industrial area, a map and nearby areas.
