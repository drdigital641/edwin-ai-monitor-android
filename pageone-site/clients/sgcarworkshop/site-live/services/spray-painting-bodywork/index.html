In short: 62 workshops on this site list spray painting and bodywork, across 20 industrial estates. The shortlist below shows 12 with strong Google ratings; every one is listed by area further down.

What spray painting and bodywork involves

Bodywork repairs dents and damaged panels. Spray painting then sands the area, applies primer, and sprays colour and clear coat in a booth. Jobs range from a single bumper touch-up to a full respray or colour change.

Signs you may need it

  • Scratches that go through to bare metal or primer
  • Dents from a minor knock or a parking incident
  • Paint that is fading, peeling or showing rust bubbles
  • Accident damage that also needs an insurance claim

What to ask the workshop

  • Whether the quote covers one panel or blending into nearby panels for a colour match
  • How the colour is matched to your car
  • How long the car needs to stay for the paint to cure
  • Whether the workshop handles insurance claims if it is an accident repair

Questions about spray painting and bodywork

Do I need approval to change my car's colour?

A colour change must be registered with LTA. Check LTA's current rules before booking the job.

Why does a small scratch repair cover more than the scratch?

Paint is often blended into the panels around the repair so the new colour matches under different light.

How long does a panel respray take?

It depends on the damage and paint drying time. Ask the workshop for an estimate when you send photos of the car.
