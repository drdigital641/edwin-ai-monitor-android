What hybrid and EV servicing involves

Servicing a hybrid covers the usual oil, filters and brakes, plus checks on the hybrid battery, its cooling fan and the inverter cooling. An EV has no engine oil, but tyres, brakes, suspension, aircon and fluids still need checks. High-voltage parts need trained staff and the right tools.

Signs you may need it

  • A hybrid system or battery warning on the dashboard.
  • The engine runs more often than usual, or fuel use rises.
  • The hybrid battery fan is loud, or the battery seems to lose charge fast.
  • Your hybrid or EV is due for its regular service.

What to ask the workshop

  • Which hybrid and EV models they work on.
  • Whether staff are trained for high-voltage work.
  • Whether they can test the hybrid battery's health, and what the report shows.
  • For a battery replacement, whether the battery is new or reconditioned, and the warranty.

Questions

Can a hybrid be serviced at a normal workshop?

Routine servicing is similar to a petrol car, but hybrid system work needs the right training and tools. Ask the workshop before you book.

Does an EV need servicing?

There is no engine oil to change, but tyres, brakes, suspension, the cabin filter and fluids still need checks.
