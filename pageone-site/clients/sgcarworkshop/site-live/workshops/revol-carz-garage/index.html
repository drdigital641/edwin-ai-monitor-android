Ang Mo Kio · North-East

Recent jobs

Summarised in our own words from the workshop's own published job write-ups. Open the original for the full post.

BMW 730Li: front wheel bearing

A 730Li came in with a hum that rose with road speed and a growl when cornering. The garage found a rough front wheel bearing on the driver side and fitted a new BMW-spec bearing. The write-up says the noise was gone after a short road test (job done 18 April 2026).

BMW 116i: EVAP purge valve

A 116i had a check engine light on for weeks, with a rough idle at traffic lights and slow cold starts. The garage traced it to a leaking EVAP purge valve using a smoke test and bench test, fitted a new valve and cleared the codes. The write-up says the idle was steady again (job done 2 May 2026).
