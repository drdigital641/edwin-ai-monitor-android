XADO Singapore

Island-wide

XADO Singapore sells XADO engine oils and revitalizant engine treatments. XADO is a car-care brand founded in 1991, and its products are made under German and Dutch licences. The treatment is added at an oil change, so you can buy it online or have it fitted at one of the workshops listed on xado-shop.sg. WhatsApp 9339 1185 or call 6876 1456 to ask which product suits your car.

Questions

How is XADO used?

The treatment is added to fresh engine oil at an oil change, or you choose an XADO oil that already has revitalizant blended in. No engine strip-down is needed.

Where can I get it fitted?

At one of the workshops listed on the xado-shop.sg workshop finder. WhatsApp the workshop, give your car model and mileage, and ask for XADO.

Can I buy it online?

Yes, from the online shop at shop.xado-shop.sg.

Will it fix a broken engine part?

No. XADO describes the treatment as protecting and restoring worn friction surfaces, not repairing broken parts.
