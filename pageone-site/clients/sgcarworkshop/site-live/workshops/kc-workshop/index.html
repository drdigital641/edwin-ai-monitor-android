Before you go

General guidance, not claims about this workshop.

  • Accident claims: have your insurer and claim details ready, and say which parts of the car were hit.
  • ECU programming: tell them your car model and year, what you want changed, and any fault codes you have.
  • Engine diagnostics: describe the symptom, when it happens and any warning lights, so the check is quicker.
