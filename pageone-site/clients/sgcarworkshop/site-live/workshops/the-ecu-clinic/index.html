The ECU Clinic

Kaki Bukit

The ECU Clinic repairs car electronics at Premier @ Kaki Bukit. When an ABS or brake warning light will not clear, the unit is tested and repaired on the bench where possible instead of simply replaced. WhatsApp the car model, chassis number and fault code to 9893 1923, or call.

Services

  • ABS pump and module repairABS, ESP and ESC units repaired, or a reconditioned or used unit supplied when that is the better route.
  • Mercedes 7-speed and 9-speed TCU repairGearbox warnings, harsh shifts or a gear that sticks: the transmission control unit is diagnosed and repaired.

Questions

Can you repair my ABS pump or module?

Yes. ABS, ESP and ESC modules are repaired, and used or reconditioned pumps are supplied for makes such as Toyota, Honda, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai, Kia, Volkswagen and Audi.

What should I send on WhatsApp?

The car model, chassis number and fault code. A photo of the scan tool screen or the dashboard warning helps.

Is there a warranty?

Repairs carry a 6-month warranty. New hybrid batteries carry 1 year or 100,000 km.

Do you collect cars?

Pick-up and delivery are available, and after-hours appointments can be arranged.
