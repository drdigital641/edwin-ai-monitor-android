Finzeo Car

Woodlands · North

  • Diagnosis and troubleshooting

Finzeo Car is a car workshop at 60 Jalan Lam Huat, Carros Centre, in Woodlands. It has been running since 2016, and its founder has more than ten years of experience in the trade.

Services

  • Servicing packages (Castrol, Liqui Moly and Motul oils are listed)
  • Brake pad replacement
  • Tyres and wheel alignment
  • Aircon cooling coil and valve replacement
  • Aircon flushing and odour treatment
  • Absorber and suspension replacement
  • Timing belt replacement
  • Commercial van servicing (Hiace, NV200, NV350 and Dyna are listed)
  • Private-hire vehicle servicing

Compiled from SGCarMart and Carousell listings and its Google Maps listing. Please confirm details with the workshop.

Good for

  • All makes, including Japanese and Korean cars
  • Commercial vans: Hiace, NV200, NV350 and Dyna are listed
  • Private-hire vehicles

Location and directions

  • 60 Jln Lam Huat, #03-57, Singapore 737869
  • Getting there: No MRT station is within 2 km. Marsiling MRT (NS8) is about 2.4 km away in a straight line, so check Google Maps for your route.
  • Opening hours: see Google Maps for current hours.

Woodlands is in the Woodlands planning area, part of the North region of Singapore.

Questions

What does Finzeo Car specialise in?

Its listings name diagnosis and troubleshooting, transmission and gearbox work, aircon, and suspension, lower arm and drive shaft repairs.

Which cars does Finzeo Car work on?

Its listings say all makes, including Japanese and Korean cars. Commercial vehicles are welcome too.

Does Finzeo Car service vans?

Its Carousell listing mentions commercial van servicing for the Hiace, NV200, NV350 and Dyna, and private-hire vehicle servicing.

Does Finzeo Car do aircon work?

Yes. Its listings include aircon cooling coil and valve replacement, aircon flushing and odour treatment.

Is diagnosis free?

Its SGCarMart and Carousell listings offer free diagnostics. Please confirm with the workshop when you message.

What are the opening hours?

Sources differ, so please see Google Maps for current hours.
