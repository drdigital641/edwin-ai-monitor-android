What we need from you

  • Your agreement in writing (WhatsApp is fine) and permission to use your photos
  • Business name, area, phone or WhatsApp, and the services you really offer
  • For Verified: your address, opening hours, a few photos of real jobs, and a review source we can link to

What we do not do

We do not rank workshops, use rating or review markup, or list car dealers or insurers. A rating line is shown only when it comes from a source people can open, with its link and the date we checked.

Already listed? Manage your listing

If your workshop is already on SG Car Workshop, message Page One to correct a detail, add your own photos, upgrade to Verified, or remove the listing. We check that you run the business before changing anything.

Remove my listing

Basic listings are compiled from information the business publishes itself (its website, Facebook or WhatsApp Business page) and link to its Google Maps listing. They are not endorsed by or arranged with the business. If you run a workshop and want your listing taken down, send us a message and we will remove it. We check that you run the business first.
