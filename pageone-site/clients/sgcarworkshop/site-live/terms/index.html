What this site is

SG Car Workshop lists car workshops and car services in Singapore. Basic listings are compiled from information the business publishes itself and link to its Google Maps listing. They are not endorsed by or arranged with the business. Premium workshops are marked Featured (paid).

Using the contact buttons

WhatsApp and Call buttons only open a chat or call that you start. We do not send messages to workshops on your behalf. Workshops are separate businesses: agree prices and work with them directly.

Google Maps

Some pages show maps, opening hours or links from Google Maps. Use of Google Maps content is subject to Google Maps Terms of Service.

Removing a listing

If you run a workshop and want a listing removed or corrected, use Remove my listing.

No promises

We take care over the details but cannot promise that a workshop is open, available or suitable. Check with the workshop before you travel.
