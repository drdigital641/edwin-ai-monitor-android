Autotex

Kaki Bukit

Autotex is a parts shop for Kia and Hyundai cars at AutoBay@Kaki Bukit. Pick your model and part on its website, or send your car plate or chassis number on WhatsApp and the shop checks the exact part for your car before you pay. Collection is at the shop, so message first to have the parts ready.

Questions

Do I need the part number?

No. Pick your car model and the part, or send your car plate or chassis number on WhatsApp. The shop checks the exact part for your car before you pay.

Which cars are covered?

Hyundai and Kia only, for models sold in Singapore such as the Avante, Tucson, Kona, Ioniq, Cerato, Sportage, Sorento, Niro and Picanto. Ask if your model is not listed.

Where do I collect parts?

At 1 Kaki Bukit Avenue 6, #01-42, AutoBay@Kaki Bukit, Singapore 417883. Open Monday to Friday 9:30am to 5:30pm and Saturday 9:30am to 3:30pm.

Is the price on the website final?

A listed price is for the part shown. For everything else, ask for the price on WhatsApp. Stock and exact fit are confirmed before you pay.
