How to Apply Nasal Strips for the Best Lift

Four steps, under a minute.

Apply it in four steps

  1. Clean and dry

    No oil or lotion on your nose.

  2. Place it

    Centre it just above the nostril flare.

  3. Press

    Rub along the strip for 10–15 seconds.

  4. Peel off

    Wet it and peel slowly in the morning.

Getting the position right

Where the hard bridge meets the soft sides of your nose.

  • Clean, dry skin
  • Centred and even
  • Pressed 10–15 seconds

Tips and questions

Can I reuse a strip?

No. Use a fresh strip each time.

The strip keeps coming off. What should I do?

Dry, oil-free skin, then press for 10–15 seconds.

Does it hurt to take off?

No. Wet it first and peel slowly from the ends.
