How to Apply Nasal Strips for the Best Lift

Four simple steps. Placement matters most: on the bridge, just above where the nostrils flare.

  1. Clean and dry

    Wash the bridge of your nose and dry it fully. Oil, lotion or moisturiser stops the adhesive from holding.

  2. Find the spot

    Peel off the liner and centre the strip across the bridge of your nose, just above where your nostrils flare.

  3. Press it down

    Press the ends onto the sides of your nose and rub along the strip for 10 to 15 seconds so it bonds.

  4. Peel off gently

    In the morning, wet the strip and peel slowly from the ends. Use a fresh strip each time.

Getting the position right

The strip works by lifting the sides of your nose at the nasal valve. Too high up on the bony part of the bridge and it has nothing to lift; too low and it sits on the soft tip. Feel for where the hard bridge ends and the softer sides of the nose begin, and centre the strip just there.

  • Clean, dry, oil-free skin
  • Centred, with both ends sitting evenly on the sides of the nose
  • Pressed firmly for 10 to 15 seconds

Tips and questions

Can I reuse a strip?

No. Use a fresh strip each night or each session, so the adhesive and the lift work as they should.

The strip keeps coming off. What should I do?

Make sure the skin is clean and completely dry, with no moisturiser or sunscreen on the nose. Press along the whole strip for 10 to 15 seconds after putting it on, and place it on the bridge just above the flare of the nostrils.

Does it hurt to take off?

It should not. Wet the strip with warm water first, then peel slowly from each end towards the middle.

How long can I wear one strip?

The adhesive is designed to hold for up to 12 hours. Use a new strip for each night or each session.
