Free delivery

Free on every kit, with no minimum order. Tracked and insured.

Fast dispatch

Orders before 2pm SGT are dispatched the next business day. Later or weekend orders go out within 1–2 business days.

Singapore only

Arrives typically 2–4 business days after dispatch, to residential, commercial and PO box addresses. No international shipping.

Returns and problems with an order

If your order arrives damaged, or there is a problem with your strips, email sales@cleeneo.co within 14 days of delivery and we will sort it out promptly.

For hygiene reasons, opened packs cannot be returned. Quality issues are always looked after.
