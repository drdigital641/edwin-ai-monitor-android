About Cleeneo

A Singapore brand making premium silk nasal strips for effortless breathing.

Breathe easier, sleep deeper

Cleeneo makes silk nasal strips that gently lift the nasal passages, so you can breathe through your nose more easily. They are made for nighttime congestion, mouth breathing and snoring, and for anyone who wants clearer airflow: sleepers, athletes, travellers and allergy sufferers.

Every strip uses the same four-layer build: three PET spring-tension bars, a soft silk layer and a hypoallergenic, latex-free adhesive. No drugs, no sprays.

Cleeneo is founded and run from Singapore, with one simple mission: help people wake up rested.
