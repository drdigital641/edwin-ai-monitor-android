How a nasal strip works

Just inside your nostrils is the nasal valve, the narrowest part of the airway in your nose. When it narrows, from a cold, allergies, the shape of your nose or simply lying down, breathing through your nose takes more effort and many people switch to breathing through the mouth.

A Cleeneo strip acts like a small spring. Its three PET bars gently lift the sides of your nose, widening the nasal valve the moment it goes on. No drugs, no sprays, nothing to wear off.

Four layers, one strip

Each Cleeneo strip is built in four layers, so it lifts firmly but feels soft on your skin.

  • Silk contact layer for a soft, breathable finish
  • Three PET spring-tension bars for an even lift
  • Hypoallergenic, latex-free adhesive that holds overnight
  • Release liner you peel off before use

On in under a minute

  1. Clean and dry

    Wash the bridge of your nose and dry it fully. Oil, lotion or moisturiser stops the adhesive from holding.

  2. Find the spot

    Peel off the liner and centre the strip across the bridge of your nose, just above where your nostrils flare.

  3. Press it down

    Press the ends onto the sides of your nose and rub along the strip for 10 to 15 seconds so it bonds.

  4. Peel off gently

    In the morning, wet the strip and peel slowly from the ends. Use a fresh strip each time.

Nasal strip FAQ

How do nasal strips work?

A Cleeneo strip has three flexible PET spring-tension bars. Once it is on the bridge of your nose, the bars try to straighten, which gently lifts the sides of your nose and widens the nasal valve, the narrowest part of your nasal airway. More air can then flow through your nose without effort.

Are Cleeneo strips drug-free and latex-free?

Yes. They work mechanically, not chemically, and use a hypoallergenic, latex-free adhesive.

Can I sleep with them on?

Yes. The adhesive and tension bar are made to hold through a full night’s sleep. Put a fresh strip on just before bed.
