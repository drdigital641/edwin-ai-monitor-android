District 14 questions

Which areas are in District 14?

District 14 covers Geylang, Eunos, Paya Lebar, Sims, Aljunied, Ubi, Kembangan and Kaki Bukit (postal sectors 38 to 41).

Which MRT lines serve District 14?

Three lines: the East-West Line (Aljunied, Paya Lebar, Eunos, Kembangan), the Circle Line (Dakota, Mountbatten, Paya Lebar, MacPherson) and the Downtown Line (MacPherson, Ubi, Kaki Bukit).

Which schools are in District 14?

Primary schools include Geylang Methodist School (Primary), Kong Hwa School, Maha Bodhi School and Canossa Catholic Primary. Secondary schools include Geylang Methodist School (Secondary) and Broadrick Secondary. Check the MOE 1 km rule on OneMap for any address.
