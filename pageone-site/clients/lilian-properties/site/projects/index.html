New launch questions

Which new launches are on the 2026–2027 calendar?

16 projects from the PropNex Exciting New Launches 2026/2027 list, from The Serra Residences and Thomson Reserve (October 2026) to Dover Drive GLS and Former Yishun Ten (May 2027). Each has its own fact sheet on this site.

Are the preview dates confirmed?

No. The list is tentative as of 7 October 2026 and units marked * are estimates. Li Lian will WhatsApp you the developers' official dates.

Why is Woodlands Drive 17 EC listed twice?

There are two separate EC sites on Woodlands Drive 17 from two developers. The list shows about 430 units for December 2026 and about 570 units for April 2027; both are covered on one page.

Is Lentor Rise the same as Lentor Signature?

Yes. The PropNex list calls the Lentor Central site Lentor Rise; the developers have named it Lentor Signature.
