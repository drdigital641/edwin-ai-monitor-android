The River Opus review: what to check

  • 999-year leasehold from 1877, so roughly 850 years remain. It is not freehold and not a fresh 999-year lease.
  • Riverside site on River Valley Road, about 750 m straight-line to Great World (TE15).
  • Mixed-use: shares the site with a 184-unit Fraser Suites and a retail podium.
  • Completion targeted for 2031 by the developers.

The River Opus launch date

Estimated preview February 2027. The existing Valley Point buildings are reported to close on 31 March 2027.
