District 16 questions

Which areas are in District 16?

District 16 covers Bedok, Upper East Coast, Bayshore, Eastwood, Kew Drive, Tanah Merah, Bedok Reservoir and Upper Changi (postal sectors 46 to 48).

Which MRT stations serve District 16?

East-West Line: Bedok (EW5) and Tanah Merah (EW4). Downtown Line: Bedok North (DT29), Bedok Reservoir (DT30) and Upper Changi (DT34). Thomson-East Coast Line: Bayshore (TE29). LTA says Bedok South (TE30) and the Sungei Bedok interchange (TE31/DT37) open on 26 December 2026.

Which schools are in District 16?

Primary schools include Temasek Primary, Bedok Green Primary, Red Swastika, Fengshan Primary, St. Anthony's Canossian Primary and Damai Primary. Secondary and JC options include Temasek Secondary, Bedok View Secondary, Bedok Green Secondary and Temasek JC. Check the MOE 1 km rule on OneMap for any address.

Is there a new launch in District 16 in 2027?

Bedok Rise Residences, beside Tanah Merah MRT, has a preview estimated for March 2027. Dates are estimates until the developer confirms them.
