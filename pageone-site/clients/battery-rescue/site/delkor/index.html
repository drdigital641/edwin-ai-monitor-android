Why we fit Delkor batteries

QUICK ANSWER
Every battery Battery Rescue SG fits is a Delkor. Delkor is a Korean battery brand made by Clarios Delkor Corporation (formerly Johnson Controls Delkor Battery Corporation). It has won the Korean Standards Association's Quality Excellence award for car batteries every year since 2006, the 16th straight year in 2021. We give a 1-year warranty on every battery.

Facts about Delkor

  • Maker: Clarios Delkor Corporation, Korea (formerly Johnson Controls Delkor Battery Corporation).
  • Award: Korean Standard Quality Excellence Index (KS-QEI), top car battery, from the Korean Standards Association, 16 consecutive years as of November 2021 (source).
  • History: described as the first automotive battery maker in Korea to supply maintenance-free (MF) batteries (source).
  • Range we stock: 23 sizes, from the NS40ZL for small cars to EFB and AGM batteries for start-stop and European cars. See prices.
