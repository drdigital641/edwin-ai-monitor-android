6 signs your car battery is dying (before it leaves you stranded)

QUICK ANSWER
The six signs are a slow crank on the first start, dim headlights at idle, a battery or charging warning light, start-stop that stops working, crust on the terminals, and a swollen or leaking case. Replace the battery before it fails: at a Motoserv outlet with our website code you pay the Delkor list price minus $20.

By Battery Rescue SG (Motoserv) · Last updated 2 Nov 2026 · First published 26 Oct 2026

A battery rarely dies without warning. Watch for these:

  1. Slow crank on the first start of the day.
  2. Dim headlights at idle that brighten when you rev.
  3. Battery or charging warning light on the dashboard.
  4. Start-stop stops working (the car keeps the engine running at lights): many cars disable start-stop when the battery is weak.
  5. White or green crust on the terminals.
  6. A swollen or leaking case: replace it now and don't jump-start it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know my car battery is dying?

Watch for a slow crank on the first start of the day, dim headlights at idle, a battery or charging warning light, start-stop that stops working, white or green crust on the terminals, and a swollen or leaking case.

Is it the battery or the alternator?

A new or freshly charged battery that keeps going flat, or a battery light that stays on while you drive, points to the alternator. Our battery or alternator guide has a simple voltage check.
