About Partware Auto

Partware Auto Pte. Ltd. was established in 2015 as a retailer, wholesaler and exporter of automobile parts for the Honda make. Our tagline is “Your Honda Parts Specialist”.

What we do

We offer Genuine, OEM and Aftermarket parts across five product groups: Servicing Essentials, Engine Parts, Body Parts, Undercarriage Parts and Interior Parts. We serve drivers at retail, workshops through wholesale, and overseas buyers through export.
