Export Honda parts from Singapore

Partware Auto is an exporter of Honda parts. Paste your parts list, tell us the destination country, and we reply on WhatsApp.

How an export enquiry works

  1. Paste or upload your list

    One line per part: part number or description, then quantity. We match what we can to our catalogue.

  2. Add destination and contact

    Tell us the country and how to reach you. Add chassis numbers (VIN) if you have them.

  3. Send it on WhatsApp

    The form opens WhatsApp with a ready summary. We reply with stock, grade options and a quote.

New to this? Read how to give a parts list with chassis or VIN for an export quote.

Questions

What should I send for an export quote?

Your destination country, the Honda model and chassis number (VIN) for each car, and your parts list with quantities. A photo of the old part helps. Our guide on parts lists explains the details.

Which countries do you export to?

We have not published a country list. Tell us your destination in the enquiry and we will reply on WhatsApp.

What are your shipping and payment terms?

Shipping and payment terms are not published on this site. They are discussed per enquiry, so ask when you send your list.
