How to give a parts list with chassis or VIN for an export quote

What overseas buyers should send so a Honda parts exporter can check fitment and quote without back and forth.

Draft for client review By Partware Auto · 30 Oct 2026

Why send a chassis number or VIN?

Honda models change within a generation. A facelift, engine option or market version can change a lamp, bumper, filter or sensor. The chassis number (or VIN) identifies the exact car, so the seller can confirm the right part instead of guessing from the model name.

Where do you find it?

It is on the vehicle registration or log card, on a plate in the engine bay or at the base of the windscreen, and often on the driver's door pillar. Older Japanese-market cars use a shorter frame number, such as a model code followed by digits. Send whatever is printed.

What should each line of the list contain?

  • Part number if you have it, otherwise a clear description.
  • Quantity.
  • Left or right, for lamps, mirrors and suspension parts.
  • Front or rear, where it applies.
  • The car it is for: model, generation code, year and chassis number.

What else helps?

Tell us the destination country, whether the car is left-hand or right-hand drive, the grade you prefer (Genuine, OEM or Aftermarket), and add a photo of the old part when you can. A simple CSV or text file with one part per line and the quantity after a comma works well.

Where do you send it?

Use the export enquiry form. You can paste your list or upload a .csv or .txt file; lines that match our catalogue are listed and the rest go into your message. Shipping and payment terms are not set on the website, so ask in your enquiry.
