Honda parts for workshops and trade

Wholesale supply of Honda parts from Kaki Bukit. Tell us what your workshop services and what you buy often, and send your parts list.

How a trade enquiry works

  1. Send your details and list

    Fill in the form. A parts list is optional but helps us reply faster.

  2. We check stock and grades

    We confirm what we can supply in Genuine, OEM or Aftermarket grade and the price for each.

  3. Agree supply terms

    Trade terms are agreed with you directly on WhatsApp. None are published here.

Questions

Do you offer trade or wholesale pricing?

Partware Auto describes itself as a retailer, wholesaler and exporter of Honda parts. Trade prices and terms are not published on this site, so ask in your enquiry and we will reply on WhatsApp.

Which parts can a workshop order?

Servicing essentials, engine parts, body parts, undercarriage parts and interior parts for Honda. Genuine, OEM and Aftermarket grades are available to ask about.

Can I send a long list?

Yes. Paste it or upload a .csv or .txt file. Lines we can match to our catalogue are listed; the rest go into your message for our team to check.
