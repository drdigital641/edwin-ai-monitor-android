Questions

Do you sell Genuine, OEM and Aftermarket Honda parts?

Partware Auto offers Genuine, OEM and Aftermarket parts for Honda. What is available differs by part, so send us your list and ask which grades we can offer for your car.

Do I need a part number to order?

No. Pick your Honda model and the part, or send your car plate or chassis number on WhatsApp. If you do have a part number, include it.

Do you supply workshops and overseas buyers?

Yes. Partware Auto is a retailer, wholesaler and exporter of Honda parts. Use the Workshops page for wholesale enquiries and the Export page to send a parts list with your destination country.

Are the prices on this site final?

Not yet. This preview shows sample prices only; Partware Auto will put in its own prices and confirm stock on WhatsApp.

Not sure which part you need?

Send your car plate or chassis number and what you need. We reply with the part, grade options and price.
