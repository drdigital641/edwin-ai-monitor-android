Why sodium-ion beats lead-acid

Carku builds its smart car batteries on sodium-ion cells. Here is what that changes for a driver.

Carku in extreme cold

Built for heat as well as cold

Carku rates its sodium-ion starting batteries from −40 °C to 80 °C. In Singapore the challenge is heat: a car parked in the sun all day. Carku says its sodium-ion chemistry stays stable across that range, and lists self-heating on most models.

Light and sealed

Carku says its sodium-ion starting battery is about half the weight of lead-acid. The case is sealed and maintenance-free, so there is no acid to top up.

Cleaner chemistry

Sodium is an abundant material. Carku describes the chemistry as cleaner, safer and fully recyclable.
