Set up in three steps

No dongle and no workshop visit. The Bluetooth module is built into the battery.

  1. Download the Carku Battery appFree on the App Store and Google Play. Register with a username and password.
  2. Pair your battery over BluetoothTurn on Bluetooth, follow the app's prompts and pick your battery from the list. The app can also read the QR code on the battery.
  3. Check your battery any timeTap your battery's ID to open its dashboard: state of charge, health, voltage, temperature, current and alerts.
