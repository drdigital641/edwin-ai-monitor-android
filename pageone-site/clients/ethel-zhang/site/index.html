My method

The D.R.E.A.M. Alignment Method

Five steps that turn your purpose into a clear 10-year property roadmap.

D

Discover the dream

What must this property do for your life now and over the next 10 years?

R

Review your financial reality

Cash, CPF, loan limits and a buffer for when life changes.

E

Evaluate all viable options

Compare every realistic route, including staying where you are.

A

Align with your whole life

Family, career or business, timing and legacy, pulling the same way.

M

Masterplan the execution

A clear sequence, timeline and next steps before you sign anything.

Questions about working with Ethel

Who is Ethel Zhang?

Ethel Zhang is a Singapore property agent and Associate Division Director with Huttons Asia Pte Ltd (Estate Agent Licence L3008899K). Her CEA registration number is R059986J, which you can check on the CEA Public Register. She has been in full-time real estate since 2018.

What makes Ethel different from other agents?

Ethel starts with purpose, not a project. Using her D.R.E.A.M. Method, she first works out what your next property must do for your life, then checks your finances and every realistic option before recommending a move.

Does Ethel handle commercial property?

Yes. Besides homes, Ethel helps business owners buy, rent or invest in offices and shophouses, and find premises for F&B and other businesses. She runs her own café in a CBD shophouse, so she knows the setup side first-hand.

How much does a first conversation cost?

Nothing. Any commission for a purchase, sale or tenancy is agreed with you in writing before work starts, using the CEA prescribed estate agency agreement.
