The D.R.E.A.M. Alignment Method

How to turn your purpose into a clear 10-year property roadmap.

The D.R.E.A.M. Alignment Method is how I turn your purpose into a clear 10-year property roadmap. We start with the life you want, check what is financially realistic, compare every viable option, make sure the choice fits your whole life, and only then plan the steps.

Why start with purpose?

Is this property meant to improve your family's lifestyle, grow wealth, produce income, support a life transition or become part of your legacy? When the purpose is unclear, even a good property can become the wrong decision.

D: Discover the dream

We talk about where you want to be in 10 years: family, work, lifestyle and what you want property to do for you. This sets the purpose every later step is measured against.

R: Review your financial reality

Cash, CPF, income, existing loans and a buffer for when life changes. We work out a comfortable ceiling, not the most a bank would lend.

E: Evaluate all viable options

Staying, renovating, upgrading, right-sizing, buying a second property or renting: we compare the realistic routes side by side.

A: Align property with your whole life plan

We check the shortlist against your family, career or business, timing and legacy, so the property supports the rest of your plans.

M: Masterplan the execution

A clear sequence, dates and decision points, so you know what happens next and what would make you pause.

My three secrets

  1. Purpose before property. Identify what the property must accomplish at your present life stage.
  2. Connect every decision with D.R.E.A.M. Align property with finances, family, career or business, timing and legacy.
  3. Read the whole market before acting. Connect cooling measures, supply, URA plans, macro trends and the details of each property.

What you leave with

A personalised D.R.E.A.M. Property Plan: the purpose, the budget ceiling, the option we chose and why, and the steps and dates to get there.

Common questions

What is the D.R.E.A.M. Method?

It is Ethel Zhang's five-step way of turning your purpose into a 10-year property roadmap: Discover the dream, Review your financial reality, Evaluate all viable options, Align property with your whole life plan, and Masterplan the execution.

How long does it take?

A first conversation covers the dream and your financial reality. The options, alignment and plan usually follow over one or two more conversations, depending on how complex your situation is.

Is there a fee for the plan?

A first conversation costs nothing. Any commission for a purchase, sale or tenancy is agreed with you in writing before work starts.
