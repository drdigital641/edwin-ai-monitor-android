Finding F&B premises in the CBD: a practical checklist

By Ethel Zhang · Associate Division Director, Huttons Asia Pte Ltd · Updated 16 October 2026

To find F&B premises in the CBD, start with your customers and opening hours, then check that the unit's approved use allows F&B, what licences you will need, the cost and time of fitting out, and the lease terms. Ethel learned these steps first-hand when she opened her own café and workspace in a CBD shophouse in 2024.

1. Start with your customers

Office workers, after-work crowds and weekend visitors want different things. Your opening hours, foot traffic and visibility should match the people you serve.

2. Check the approved use

Not every unit may be used for F&B. Check the zoning and approved use, and whether a change of use approval is needed, before you sign anything.

3. Plan the licences

Food businesses need a licence from the Singapore Food Agency, and some works may need other approvals. See SFA's licences and permits and the GoBusiness licensing portal, and allow time in your plan.

4. Budget the fit-out

Kitchen exhaust, grease traps, electrical load, plumbing and fire safety can change your budget a lot. Ask what the previous tenant left behind and what the landlord will allow.

5. Read the lease carefully

  • Lease length, renewal options and rent reviews.
  • Rent-free fitting-out period.
  • Reinstatement obligations at the end of the lease.
  • Who pays for repairs and building works.

6. Line up the timeline

Licences, renovation and hiring all take time. Work backwards from your opening date and keep a buffer.

Questions people also ask

How long does it take to open a café in Singapore?

It varies with the premises, approvals and renovation. Plan for finding the space, approvals and licences, fit-out and hiring, with a buffer at each step.

Do I need a licence to run a café?

Yes. Food businesses need a licence from the Singapore Food Agency, and the premises must be approved for the use. Check SFA and GoBusiness for the current requirements.
