Renting out your condo in Singapore: a landlord checklist

By Ethel Zhang · Associate Division Director, Huttons Asia Pte Ltd · Updated 16 October 2026

To rent out a condo in Singapore smoothly: prepare and photograph the unit well, set the rent from recent comparable rentals, check the tenant's identity and right to stay in Singapore, use a clear tenancy agreement, and record the unit's condition at handover.

1. Prepare the unit

  • Fix small defects, service the air-conditioning and clean thoroughly.
  • Decide what furniture and appliances stay.
  • Take good photos in daylight.

2. Set the rent

Look at recent rentals of similar units in the same development and nearby, not only asking prices. A realistic rent usually finds a good tenant faster than a high one.

3. Screen tenants carefully

  • Check identity documents and, for foreigners, their pass and its validity.
  • Understand who will live in the unit and for how long.
  • Ask about the reason for moving and the expected start date.

4. Agree clear terms

The tenancy agreement should cover rent, deposit, the lease period, who pays for minor repairs, diplomatic or early-termination clauses and handover condition. Clear terms prevent most disputes.

5. Hand over properly

Do a joint inventory and condition check with photos on handover day, and keep a copy signed by both sides. It makes the end of the lease much easier.

Questions people also ask

How much rent can I ask for my condo?

Base it on recent rentals of similar units nearby. URA publishes private rental statistics, and an agent can pull comparable recent transactions for your development.

Who pays the agent when renting out a condo?

It depends on the arrangement. Fees and who pays them are agreed in writing before the agent starts work.

What should a tenancy agreement include?

Rent, deposit, lease length, repair responsibilities, early-termination terms, and the condition and inventory at handover.
