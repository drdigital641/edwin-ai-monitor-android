Buying a condo as a single in Singapore: what to think about first

By Ethel Zhang · Associate Division Director, Huttons Asia Pte Ltd · Updated 16 October 2026

A single Singapore Citizen can buy a private condo at any age, while HDB sets separate age and flat-type rules for singles. Before choosing a project, decide what the home must do for you in the next 10 years, keep a cash buffer, and favour a unit that is easy to rent or resell if your life changes.

HDB or private: know the rules that apply to you

HDB has specific eligibility rules for singles, including a minimum age under the Single Singapore Citizen Scheme and limits on which flats you can buy. See HDB's page on eligibility for singles for the current rules. Private property has no such age limit for citizens, which is why many high-income singles look at condos first.

What singles often value most

  • Speed and privacy. A clear process and an adviser who respects your time.
  • Freedom. A home that does not lock you in if you move overseas, change careers or start a business.
  • Well-reasoned advice. A recommendation you can follow the logic of, not hype about a launch.

Plan for the life you may have later

Ten years is long. You may marry, want children, care for parents, or move abroad. A two-bedroom near transport is often more flexible than the largest one-bedroom you can afford. Ask: if my life changed in three years, could I rent this out or sell it without loss of sleep?

Keep a buffer

As a single buyer, there is one income behind the loan. Test the instalment at a higher interest rate and keep several months of expenses in cash after paying the down payment, stamp duty and legal fees.

Questions to ask before choosing a project

  1. Why this location, for my work and lifestyle?
  2. Who would rent or buy this unit from me later?
  3. What happens to my plan if my income dips for six months?
  4. Does this purchase affect what I can buy later, for example with a partner?

Questions people also ask

Can a single buy a condo in Singapore?

Yes. Singapore Citizens can buy private condos without an age requirement. Stamp duty and loan limits still apply; check IRAS and your bank for your case.

Should a single buy a one-bedroom or two-bedroom?

It depends on your plans. A two-bedroom often gives more flexibility for a future partner, a home office or renting out a room, but it costs more. Decide based on your next 10 years, not only on today.

Does buying alone affect buying with a partner later?

It can. Owning a property may change the stamp duty and loan limits on a later purchase. Plan the sequence before you buy.

This article is general information, not financial or legal advice. Rules on loans, CPF, HDB eligibility and stamp duty change; check the official sources linked above for the current position.
