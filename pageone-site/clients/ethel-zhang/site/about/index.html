About Ethel Zhang

From dreaming of a condo at 18 to advising more than 100 families.

Ethel Zhang is a Singapore property agent with Huttons Asia Pte Ltd who helps financially capable Singaporeans and PRs connect property decisions with the life they want over the next 10 years. Her background in finance and economics, eight years in full-time real estate and work with more than 100 families shape a practical, whole-life approach.

Why I work this way

I am not the kind of agent who only sells one project, one area or one type of home. What stays constant in my work is people and relationships. I follow my clients through their life stages: renting, buying a first home, upgrading, selling, investing, family arrangements, and sometimes coming back to me many years later.

My own decisions have included moving from one private home to a two-condo structure, an idea first sparked by Rich Dad Poor Dad, while building a business in Singapore. That experience shaped my belief that property must leave room for family, work and future choices.

Homes and business, in two languages

Running my own café since 2024 taught me what business owners worry about: location, approved use, licensing, hiring and cash flow. Today I help families with their homes and entrepreneurs with offices, shophouses and business premises, often both at once. I work in English and 中文, so clients from Singapore, China, Korea, Japan and Indonesia can talk through big decisions in the language they are most comfortable with.

Always learning

I have flown to Taiwan nine times for masterclasses, walked on fire at Tony Robbins' Unleash the Power Within in 2019, and attended Date with Destiny in the USA in 2023. I invest in my own growth because my clients deserve an adviser who keeps getting better.

What I believe

"Your next property decision should not begin with a project. It should begin with the life you want that property to support."
