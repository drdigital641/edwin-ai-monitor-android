10-Year Property Fit Scorecard

Five questions, about two minutes. For Singapore Citizens and PRs at a property crossroads.

Many Singapore buyers start with a project. This scorecard shows what to check before you commit. Rate each statement from 1 to 5, honestly, and see which part of your life, finances and future needs more thought.

Use it in 3 simple steps

  1. Score. Answer five questions honestly.
  2. Spot. Find the weakest part of your plan.
  3. Pause. Do not commit until that gap is clearer.
DYour dream

I can clearly describe what my next home must do for my life over the next 10 years.

RYour financial reality

EYour options

AYour whole life

My family's needs, my career or business and my timing all point to the same decision.

About the scorecard

What is the 10-Year Property Fit Scorecard?

A five-question self-check by Ethel Zhang. It shows which part of your property plan (dream, finances, options, whole life or execution) needs more thought before you commit.

Is the scorecard free?

Yes. Your answers stay in your browser; nothing is sent unless you choose to WhatsApp your result to Ethel.

What should I do with a low score?

Pause before committing and work on the weakest area first. Ethel can talk it through with you.
