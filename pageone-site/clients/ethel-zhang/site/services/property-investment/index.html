Investment and portfolio planning, purpose first.

Whether a second property makes sense for you, which kind, and how it fits with your home, family and finances.

Ethel helps you decide whether an investment property suits you at all, and if so which kind: one that grows wealth, produces rental income or supports a future need. She checks stamp duty, loan limits and holding costs, and reads the whole market before recommending anything.

Start with what the investment is for

  • Long-term wealth growth.
  • Rental income.
  • A future home for children or parents.
  • Part of a retirement or legacy plan.

Read the whole market before acting

Cooling measures, supply, URA plans, macro trends and the details of each unit. A good-looking project can still be the wrong investment for your purpose. Read: Questions to ask before choosing a new launch.

The costs people forget

Stamp duty (including Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty where it applies), loan limits for second properties, maintenance fees, property tax, vacancy periods and repairs. IRAS publishes the current ABSD rules.

A note on returns

No one can promise property returns. Ethel's job is to make sure the decision fits your purpose and finances, with the risks clear before you commit.

Common questions

Should I buy a second property?

Only if it serves a clear purpose and the numbers work with stamp duty, loan limits and a buffer included. For some people, the better choice is to strengthen their own home first.

Is a new launch a good investment?

It can be, but it depends on price, supply nearby, your holding period and exit. Compare it with resale options and your purpose first.

Can Ethel advise on decoupling or ownership structure?

Ownership structure has legal and tax consequences. Ethel will explain the property side and suggest you confirm the legal and tax side with a lawyer and IRAS.
