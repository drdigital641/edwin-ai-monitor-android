Selling your home, with your next move ready.

Pricing, presenting and timing your sale so the proceeds arrive when your next home needs them.

Ethel sells your home with your next move in mind: a realistic price based on recent transactions, good presentation, steady marketing and negotiation, and completion dates that line up with your next purchase or rental.

How we sell

  1. Purpose and plan. Why you are selling and where you are going next.
  2. Pricing. Based on recent comparable transactions, not hopes.
  3. Preparation. Small fixes, styling advice and good photos.
  4. Marketing and viewings. Organised so your home life is disrupted as little as possible.
  5. Negotiation and paperwork. Option to Purchase through to completion.

Sell first or buy first?

The order changes your cash flow, stamp duty and timing risk. Read: Sell first or buy first in Singapore?

Common questions

How do you decide the asking price?

From recent transactions of similar homes nearby, the condition of your home and current demand. We agree the price with you before marketing starts.

What commission do sellers pay?

Commission is agreed with you in writing before work starts, using the CEA prescribed estate agency agreement.

Can I stay in my home while it is on the market?

Yes. Viewings are planned around your schedule, and completion dates can be agreed to suit your next move.
