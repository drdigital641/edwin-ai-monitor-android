Offices and shophouses, for business owners and investors.

Buying, renting or investing in commercial space, with the numbers and the trade-offs clear before you commit.

Ethel helps business owners and investors buy, rent or invest in offices and shophouses. She starts with what the space must do for you, own use, investment or both, then checks the approved use, tenure, costs, tax and financing, and guides you through to completion.

Own use or investment

Owning your premises can give stability and control; renting keeps cash free for the business. As an investment, the tenant, lease and rental demand matter most. Ethel compares the routes side by side with your finances and business plans.

What we check

  • Zoning and approved use, and whether your planned use is allowed.
  • Conservation conditions for conserved shophouses.
  • Tenure and remaining lease.
  • Existing tenancy and rent.
  • Stamp duty, GST and financing.

Costs and tax, plainly

Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty applies to residential property, not to commercial property. Buyer's Stamp Duty still applies, and GST may be charged if the seller is GST-registered. Commercial loans are set by each bank. Ethel lays these out early and works with your lawyer and accountant on the details.

Common questions

Do I pay ABSD on an office or shophouse?

ABSD applies to residential property. For commercial property, Buyer's Stamp Duty still applies and GST may be charged if the seller is GST-registered. Mixed-use property can be treated differently, so confirm with IRAS and your lawyer.

Can Ethel help me rent an office?

Yes. She helps business owners find and negotiate office and shophouse leases, and checks the approved use and lease terms.

Does Ethel work with foreign-owned businesses?

Yes. Many of her clients are business owners originally from Korea, Japan, Indonesia and China. She works in English and Mandarin.
