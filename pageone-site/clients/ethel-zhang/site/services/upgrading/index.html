Upgrading your home without overstretching.

From HDB to private, or to a bigger home for a growing family. The right upgrade fits your finances and your future.

A good upgrade gives your family something the current home cannot, while leaving a buffer for life changes. Ethel helps you decide whether to upgrade at all, how much to spend, and whether to sell first or buy first, then plans the dates so the move goes smoothly.

Should you upgrade at all?

Sometimes staying put or renovating is the better choice. We compare upgrading with the alternatives before looking at any project. Read: Should I upgrade or stay put?

The upgrade plan

  1. Agree the purpose and the budget ceiling as a family.
  2. Value your current home and estimate the proceeds.
  3. Check loan eligibility and stamp duty for your sequence.
  4. Choose the order: sell first, buy first, or a planned overlap.
  5. Line up option, exercise and completion dates on both sides.

Common mistakes we help you avoid

  • Choosing the project before the budget.
  • Using the bank's maximum loan as the budget.
  • Not planning where to live if the sale completes first.

Common questions

When is the right time to upgrade?

When the new home clearly serves your plan and the numbers work with a buffer. Market timing matters less than the fit and your sequence.

Do I need to sell my HDB before buying a condo?

It depends on your situation and the current HDB, loan and stamp duty rules. Ethel will map the options for your case.

How long does an upgrade take?

From first conversation to moving in can range from a few months to longer for a new launch. We plan the timeline before you start.
