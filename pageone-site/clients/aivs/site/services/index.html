Owning, supporting and hosting a machine

AI Vending Solution Pte. Ltd. (AIVS) sells refurbished self-service machines to owner-operators. You buy the machine and keep the sales: there is no franchise fee, AIVS takes 0% royalties, and stock is supplied at cost price. Machines start from S$1,800 for Topi Express and S$5,000 for AI Printbox.

Training is included, and an optional support plan from S$99 a month covers maintenance and system support, with a dashboard that shows sales and inventory on your phone. If you have a site rather than a budget, you can host a machine instead, and a machine can be re-wrapped with your own branding.

Questions about owning a machine

What does AIVS offer owners of a vending machine?

AIVS sells refurbished AI Printbox and Topi Express machines with 0% royalties, supplies stock at cost price, trains you, and offers a support plan from S$99 a month with a dashboard that shows sales and inventory.

Can my shop or mall host a machine?

Yes. Malls, shops, petrol kiosks and workshops can host an AI Printbox or Topi Express at their site. Talk to AIVS about placement on WhatsApp 8335 4865.

Can I put my own branding on the machine?

Yes. A machine can run under the AI Printbox or Topi Express brand, or be re-wrapped with your own name, colours and mascot.
