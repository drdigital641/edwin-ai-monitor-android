About AIVS

A Singapore company that sells self-service machines to owner-operators and stands behind them.

AI Vending Solution Pte. Ltd. (AIVS) is a Singapore company that sells self-service vending machines to owner-operators and places them in malls, the airport and motorcycle shops. It was incorporated on 27 September 2024 (UEN 202439764G) and is based at Carros Centre, 60 Jalan Lam Huat, Woodlands.

What we do

We refurbish and sell two machines, AI Printbox and Topi Express, and support the people who buy them: training, cost-price stock, maintenance plans, 24/7 technical help and a dashboard that shows sales and inventory. Owners pay no royalties. Today 19 AIVS machines run across Singapore.

Mission

In AIVS's own words: to bring smart and convenient shopping to everyone in Singapore with our vending machines. Our franchise partners own their machines with 0% royalties, giving them full control and higher profits. We focus on a fast return on investment and a profitable business model, making vending easier and more rewarding.

Vision

To become Singapore's leading automated vending machine network, offering cashless, hassle-free, 24/7 convenience across the island, and creating more business opportunities for partners who own their machines.

Why "AI"?

In our name, AI stands for Automated Intelligence: smart, automated vending that makes a business simple, efficient and easy to use. It does not refer to artificial intelligence.
