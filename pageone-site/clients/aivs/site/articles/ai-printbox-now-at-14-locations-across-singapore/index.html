AI Printbox is now at 13 locations across Singapore

AI Printbox, the 3-minute custom phone case machine, has grown from four mall locations announced in June 2025 to 13 machines, including two at Changi Airport Terminal 3. The full list with unit numbers is on the AI Printbox locations page.

How it grew

In June 2025 AIVS announced AI Printbox at VivoCity, Sembawang Shopping Centre, Great World and Eastpoint Mall. The current list has 13 machines: AMK Hub, Bukit Panjang Plaza, Changi Airport T3 (Arrival Hall and Basement 2), Changi City Point, City Square Mall, Eastpoint Mall, Hillion Mall, Hougang Mall, Parkway Parade, Sembawang Shopping Centre, Tampines 1 and The Centrepoint.

Why it spreads

A S$15 case printed with your own photo in 3 minutes is an easy gift and an easy treat, and the machine draws a crowd wherever it stands, as it did at a franchise exhibition where visitors queued to try it.

For future owners

Every one of these machines is the same model you can own, from S$5,000 refurbished, with 0% royalties.

What you can do at an AI Printbox

Choose your phone model, pick a design or upload your own photo, pay cashlessly and collect the printed case about 3 minutes later. AIVS publishes the price of a case as S$15. AI Printbox supports the latest iPhone and Samsung models.

Why the location list changes

Machines are placed with the owners of each site, so the list can change over time. Some sites named in the June 2025 announcement, such as VivoCity and Great World, are not on the current list. The locations page always shows the current list with unit numbers, so check it before you travel to a mall.

Planning a visit

Two of the machines are at Changi Airport Terminal 3, one in the Arrival Hall and one at Basement 2, which suits travellers who want a case before a trip or on arrival. The others are in malls across the island, so the nearest one may be on your usual shopping route.

If you want to run a machine yourself

Each of these machines is the same model AIVS sells to owner-operators, from S$5,000 refurbished with 0% royalties and a 1-year hardware warranty (terms apply). Read how to start a vending machine business in Singapore for the steps, or WhatsApp AIVS.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI Printbox at VivoCity?

VivoCity and Great World were announced in June 2025 but are not on the current AIVS location list. Check the locations page for the live list.

Which Changi Airport terminal has AI Printbox?

Terminal 3: the Arrival Hall (#01-VM4) and Basement 2 (iClaw, #B2-45).
