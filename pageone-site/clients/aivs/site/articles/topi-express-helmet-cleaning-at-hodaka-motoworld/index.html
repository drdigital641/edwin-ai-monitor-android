Topi Express runs 24 hours at Hodaka Motoworld

On 22 August 2025 AIVS placed a Topi Express helmet cleaning machine at Hodaka Motoworld Pte Ltd, 10 Kaki Bukit Road 1, #01-09, KB Industrial Building. The machine runs 24 hours a day, so riders can clean and disinfect a helmet in 5 minutes for S$5 even after the shop has closed.

Why Hodaka

Hodaka Motoworld is one of Singapore's best-known motorcycle names, especially among Honda riders, selling motorcycles, gear and accessories. Riders already stop there, which is exactly where a helmet cleaning machine belongs.

What riders get

A 5-minute cycle that disinfects, deodorises and sterilises the helmet with UVC and an eco-friendly disinfectant mist, paid by QR code, at any hour.

Other Topi Express machines

Wheels & Cycles (Woodlands), 345 Wash (Telok Blangah), Candy Motor and Gusto Broz Workshop (Kaki Bukit Road 4) and BW Motorsports (Old Toh Tuck Road).

How a Topi Express cycle works

The machine cleans the helmet in about 5 minutes. It disinfects, deodorises and sterilises the helmet with UVC light and an eco-friendly disinfectant mist, and the rider pays by QR code at the machine. The price published by AIVS is S$5 per clean.

Why a 24-hour machine helps riders

Because the machine runs 24 hours a day, a rider can clean a helmet before an early ride or after an evening one, including outside the shop's own opening hours. Nobody needs to book or wait for a staff member, since the machine takes payment by itself.

A quick refresh, not a replacement for washing

Hand washing the liner and wiping the shell takes time, because the liner can take a day to dry. A machine clean is a quick refresh between washes. Our guide on how to clean and disinfect a motorcycle helmet explains both routes.
