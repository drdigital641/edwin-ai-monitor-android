Topi Express: the 5-minute helmet cleaning machine

Riders drop a helmet in, pay by QR code and ride off fresh 5 minutes later. The lowest-cost machine in the AIVS range, built for places where riders already stop.

What is Topi Express by AIVS?

Topi Express is a self-service machine that disinfects, deodorises and sterilises a motorcycle helmet in about 5 minutes for S$5. Riders place the helmet in the chamber and pay by QR code, and UVC light with an eco-friendly disinfectant mist does the rest. AIVS sells refurbished machines to owner-operators from S$1,800.

  • About 5 minutes per helmet, S$5 a clean
  • UVC light and an eco-friendly disinfectant mist
  • Six machines live at motorcycle shops, workshops and a laundromat, one running 24 hours a day

Is a helmet cleaning vending machine profitable in Singapore?

It depends on the location: how many riders pass the machine and how often they stop long enough for a 5-minute clean. Each clean earns S$5. Against that are the machine (from S$1,800), rent, electricity, disinfectant and the optional support plan from S$99 a month. AIVS does not publish earnings figures, so ask AIVS for real usage numbers from comparable sites before you buy.

See it in action

What riders get

A helmet that is disinfected, deodorised and sterilised in about 5 minutes, using UVC and an eco-friendly disinfectant mist, for S$5. The touchscreen walks them through it and payment is by QR code.

Helmets are worn daily in Singapore's heat, so the machine answers a real need at the places riders visit anyway: workshops, accessory shops, petrol kiosks and even a laundromat.

What you get as the owner

A refurbished Topi Express machine from S$1,800 with a 6-month hardware warranty (terms apply), no royalty fees, and a machine you can self-manage. It plugs into a standard 3-pin socket, so setup is a matter of placing it and switching it on.

AIVS helps with site finding and marketing, tops up the disinfectant liquid, and gives 24/7 technical support. Repairs are covered under the monthly maintenance support plan. You can track sales and inventory live from your phone, and re-wrap the machine with your own branding at your own cost.

Where Topi Express already runs

Wheels & Cycles in Woodlands, 345 Wash in Telok Blangah, Candy Motor and Gusto Broz Workshop on Kaki Bukit Road 4, BW Motorsports at iBiz Centre on Old Toh Tuck Road, and Hodaka Motoworld on Kaki Bukit Road 1, where the machine has run 24 hours a day since 22 August 2025.
