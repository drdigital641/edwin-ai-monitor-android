AI vending machines in Singapore

Self-service machines that do a job for the customer, take cashless payment and report every sale to your phone. AIVS helps you choose one and put it where it earns.

What is an AI vending machine?

An AI vending machine is a self-service kiosk that does more than drop a product: it senses, personalises or processes something for the customer and runs unattended. In Singapore the term covers machines like the AI Printbox, which prints a photo onto a phone case in about 3 minutes, helmet cleaning machines that sterilise with UVC, and smart retail kiosks with cameras and touchscreens. All take QR or card payment and send sales and stock data to a dashboard.

  • Does a task for the customer, not only vends a product
  • Cashless payment, no staff at the machine
  • Live sales and inventory on your phone

AIVS machines are from S$1,800 (Topi Express) and S$5,000 (AI Printbox), refurbished, with a hardware warranty and 0% royalties. Partner suppliers quote their own prices, which depend on the machine type, whether it is new or refurbished, and what the support plan includes. AIVS does not publish earnings figures; ask for real usage numbers from comparable sites before you buy.

What to check before you buy

Three things decide whether an AI vending machine earns: the footfall at the exact spot, the price per transaction, and the uptime. Ask for the payment options (QR, NETS, cards), the remote dashboard, who tops up stock, who fixes the machine and how fast, and what the warranty covers. Visit a live machine first and watch how people use it.

Frequently asked questions

Does AIVS install the machine?

For its own machines, yes: AIVS delivers, sets up and trains you. For partner suppliers' machines, the supplier installs and provides after-sales support.

Can I host an AI vending machine at my shop or mall instead of buying one?

Yes. See the host-a-machine page: AIVS or an owner places the machine and you agree a rent or revenue share.

Is "AI" artificial intelligence?

In the AIVS name, AI stands for Automated Intelligence: smart, automated vending. Some machines on the market do use image recognition or personalisation features.
