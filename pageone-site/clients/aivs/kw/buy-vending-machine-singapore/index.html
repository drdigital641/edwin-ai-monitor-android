How much does it cost to buy a vending machine in Singapore?

AIVS prices: Topi Express helmet cleaning machine from S$1,800, AI Printbox phone case machine from S$5,000, both refurbished. Should I buy a new or a refurbished vending machine?

A refurbished machine from a seller who services it is the lower-risk way to start, because the capital at stake is smaller and the warranty tells you the seller stands behind it. Check the warranty length, what it excludes, who repairs the machine and how fast, and whether the payment system and dashboard are current. AIVS refurbishes its own units and gives a 1-year warranty on AI Printbox and 6 months on Topi Express (terms apply).

What to check before paying

The exact price and what it includes (delivery, installation, GST), the warranty terms, the support plan, the payment options, who owns the branding on the machine, and whether you can move it later. Visit one of the 20 live AIVS machines and watch customers use it before you decide.
