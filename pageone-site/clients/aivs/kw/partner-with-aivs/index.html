Do you have an AI or smart vending machine? Let's collaborate!

Are you a Singapore-based supplier or distributor offering innovative vending machines powered by AI or smart technology?

AIVS.sg is looking for local partners who want to increase their online exposure and reach more potential buyers.

AIVS is expanding beyond its existing AI Printbox and helmet vending machines. Our new direction is to become a multi-brand AI and smart vending machine platform in Singapore, collaborating with local suppliers and distributors offering machines equipped with AI technology or genuine smart features.

We welcome machines featuring technologies such as

  • AI-powered recognition or automation
  • Smart inventory management
  • IoT connectivity and remote monitoring
  • Intelligent or robotic dispensing systems
  • Automated retail with advanced smart features

Traditional vending machines without meaningful AI or smart capabilities are not eligible.
