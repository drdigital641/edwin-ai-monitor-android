Body parts for China EVs in Singapore: what to check before you buy

A buyer's checklist for body parts on China and EV cars in Singapore: model, side, sensors, paint, and what is included.

Why a checklist helps

Body parts look similar across models, but small differences decide whether a part fits. A few checks before you pay save a return trip.

Match the exact model

Give the brand and the model name as written on the car. Parts for two models from the same brand are often different, even when they look alike.

Check the side

Lamps, mirrors, fenders and doors come left and right. Left and right are as seen from the driver's seat.

Sensors, cameras and holes

If your bumper has parking sensors, a camera or other fittings, tell the seller. Ask whether the part you are buying has the same openings.

Paint and finish

Ask whether the part is bare, primed or painted, and who matches the colour. Never assume.

What is included

Ask whether brackets, clips, bulbs or fixings come with the part, and whether the part is new. Ask any seller about returns and warranty, and get the answer in writing. For SH Auto Parts these terms are to confirm.

Send photos

Photos of the old part and the damaged area let the seller compare before you commit.
