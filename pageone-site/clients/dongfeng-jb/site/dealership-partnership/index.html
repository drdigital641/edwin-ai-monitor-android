Partnership FAQ

Who is this page for?

Car manufacturers, OEMs, Chinese auto brands and Malaysian passenger car distributors, including dealer development managers, who are looking for a dealership or 3S partner in Johor Bahru.

What does 3S mean?

3S stands for Sales, Service and Spare parts. A 3S centre sells the cars, services them in its own workshop and supplies parts, all at one site.

Which brand do you represent today?

Dongfeng passenger electric cars. Our showroom has the Dongfeng Vigo electric SUV and the Dongfeng 007 electric sedan, and the Voyah Dream MPV is listed as coming soon.

Are you open to petrol, hybrid and PHEV brands?

Yes. We want to hear from passenger car brands with petrol, hybrid, plug-in hybrid or electric models, including SUVs. This page covers passenger cars, not commercial vehicles.

Where is your centre?

No. 28, Jalan Ekoperniagaan 3/11, Taman Ekoperniagaan, 81100 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia.

Can we visit before discussing terms?

Yes. Arrange a visit with Gavin to see the showroom, workshop and parts set-up. The showroom is open Monday to Saturday 9am to 6pm and Sunday 10am to 4pm.

Who do we contact?

Gavin, Sales Director, MyKar World, on +60 12-551 2820 by phone or WhatsApp. You can also use the enquiry form on this page.

Which languages can we use?

English, Bahasa Melayu or Chinese.
