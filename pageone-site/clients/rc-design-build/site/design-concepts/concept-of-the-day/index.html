Shophouse bakery counter in oak and olive

A long oak service counter with a fluted front and a stone-look top runs along a heritage shophouse unit. Olive wall tiles and warm pendants frame the bread display, while terrazzo-look flooring and timber shutters keep the shophouse character.

  • Fluted oak counter front with a rounded end
  • Glass bread display set into the counter run
  • Olive wall tiles behind the service area
  • Warm pendant lights over the counter
