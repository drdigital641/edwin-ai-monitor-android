Compact boutique with oak perimeter shelving

An AI design concept for a ground-floor shop unit. Oak shelving wraps the perimeter to keep the centre open, cream walls reflect light, and a single display table anchors the room. A slim checkout counter sits toward the back so customers walk past the stock.

Design ideas in this concept

  • Oak veneer perimeter shelving with adjustable shelves
  • Cream painted walls and a light, hard-wearing floor
  • Central display table on a small footprint
  • Track lighting with warm accent spots on shelves
  • Low-volume ceiling speakers for background music
