How it works

Six simple steps, from your first WhatsApp to handover.

  1. WhatsApp usSend your floor plan, unit photos, what the space is for and your target opening date.
  2. Site visitWe measure the unit and check existing services and landlord or mall rules.
  3. Scope & quotationA written scope that shows what is included and what is not.
  4. DesignLayout, carpentry details, finishes, lighting and AVL positions agreed before work starts.
  5. Workshop & installationCarpentry made in our workshop, then trades sequenced on site.
  6. HandoverWalk-through, defects list and the information you need to run the space.

AVL: audio, visual and lighting

Sound, screens and light, planned with the space

Ceiling speakers, display screens and feature lighting need backing, power and cable routes. Planning them with the fit-out keeps them neat and avoids rework.

SoundBackground music and speaker zones planned for each area, so a counter, a seating corner and a fitting room can each have their own level.
ScreensDisplays and digital signage placed for viewing distance, glare and cabling, so messages are easy to read from the shopfront or the queue.
LightAccent and display lighting with dimming scenes, so products, finishes and signage look right from morning to night.

Common questions

What does SG Commercial Fitout do?

SG Commercial Fitout designs and builds retail and commercial spaces in Singapore: custom carpentry, renovation, electrical works, tiling and flooring, painting and finishing, and audio, visual and lighting (AVL) for shops, offices, F&B outlets and salons.

Do you have your own carpentry workshop?

Yes. SG Commercial Fitout has its own carpentry workshop, so counters, cabinets and shelving can be made to your measurements. Workshop photos are on the Projects page.

Are the photos real?

Photos marked "Real project" show SG Commercial Fitout carpentry; decor items in them were added with AI styling. Photos marked "AI design concept" are ideas to discuss, not completed work.

What should I send to start?

WhatsApp a floor plan or unit layout, a few photos of the unit, what the space is for and your target opening date. Landlord or mall fit-out guidelines help too, if you have them.
