Common questions

What is the AI concept of the day?

Every day SG Commercial Fitout publishes a new commercial interior concept for a different kind of Singapore business. Anyone can browse them for design ideas. If you like one, WhatsApp us and we can design and build your new space or revamp an existing one.

Can I get a free design mockup of my space?

Yes. WhatsApp a photo of your current space to SG Commercial Fitout and we will create a few AI 3D mockup designs for you to discuss, free and with no obligation.

What does SG Commercial Fitout do?

SG Commercial Fitout designs and builds retail and commercial spaces in Singapore: custom carpentry, renovation, partitioning, soundproofing, electrical works, tiling and flooring, painting and finishing, and audio, visual and lighting (AVL) for shops, offices, F&B outlets and salons. No job is too small: a single wall, room or counter is welcome.

Do you have your own carpentry workshop?

Yes. SG Commercial Fitout has its own carpentry workshop, so counters, cabinets and shelving can be made to your measurements. Photos of real work are on the Projects page.

Are the photos real?

Photos marked "Real project" show SG Commercial Fitout work. In the five built-in carpentry photos, decor items were added with AI styling; the other real photos are only cropped and colour-corrected. Photos marked "AI design concept" are ideas to discuss, not completed work.

What should I send to start?

WhatsApp a floor plan or unit layout, a few photos of the unit, what the space is for and your target opening date. Landlord or mall fit-out guidelines help too, if you have them.
