How an R&C project is planned to run

The intended process, from first brief to handover.

  1. BriefYour business, how the space will be used, priorities and target opening date.
  2. Site assessmentMeasure the unit, check existing services and landlord or mall requirements.
  3. Scope & quotationA written scope that separates what is included from what is not.
  4. DesignLayout, carpentry details, finishes, lighting and AVL positions agreed before work starts.
  5. InstallationTrades sequenced on site within the building's working rules.
  6. HandoverWalk-through, defects list and the information you need to run the space.

AVL: audio, visual and lighting

Sound, screens and light, planned with the space

Ceiling speakers, display screens and feature lighting all need backing, power and cable routes. Planning them with the fit-out keeps them neat and avoids rework.

SoundBackground music and speaker zones planned for each area, so a counter, a seating corner and a fitting room can each have their own level.
ScreensDisplays and digital signage placed for viewing distance, glare and cabling, so messages are easy to read from the shopfront or the queue.
LightAccent and display lighting with dimming scenes, so products, finishes and signage look right from morning to night.

Questions

What does R&C do?

R&C is a design and build brand for retail and commercial spaces in Singapore. It covers integrated planning, custom carpentry, renovation, electrical works, tiling and flooring, painting and finishing, and audio, visual and lighting (AVL) for shops, offices, showrooms, cafes and salons.

Are the photos on this site completed projects?

No. Every image on this site is an AI design concept, labelled as such, to help discuss ideas. Photographs of actual completed work will only be shown once real projects exist and the owners agree.

What should I send to start?

A floor plan or unit layout, a few photos of the unit, how you plan to use the space, your landlord or mall fit-out guidelines if you have them, and your target opening date. That is enough for a first scope discussion.

Can AVL be planned together with the renovation?

Yes, and it is usually easier that way. Speaker, screen and feature lighting positions affect ceilings, wall backing, power points and cable routes, so planning them during the fit-out avoids rework later.
