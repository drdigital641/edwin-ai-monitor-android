Discuss your space

Send a floor plan, a few photos of the unit, how you will use the space and your target opening date. That is enough for a first conversation about scope, options and next steps.

How to start

The quickest way to a useful first conversation is to share what you already have about the unit and the business.

What to prepare

  1. Floor plan or unit layout, from the landlord, mall or previous tenant if you have one.
  2. Photos of the unit as it is now: shopfront, each wall, ceiling and floor.
  3. Intended use: what you sell or do there, how many staff, what customers do in the space.
  4. Target opening date, plus any handover or fit-out period in your lease.
  5. Landlord or mall fit-out guidelines, if you have them.
  6. Priorities: what must be right on day one, and what can wait.
