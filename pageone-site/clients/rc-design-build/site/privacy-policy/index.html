Privacy

Privacy policy

This website has no forms and no advertising cookies. It records only an anonymous count of WhatsApp button taps. Details you send when you contact R&C are used only to answer your enquiry.

Last updated 24 October 2026.

What this website collects

This website has no forms and sets no advertising cookies. When you tap a WhatsApp button, an anonymous count is recorded (the page you were on, whether you used a phone or computer, and the referring site) so we can see which pages help visitors. It does not record your name, number or the message you send.

Messages you send

If you contact R&C, the details you choose to share (such as your name, number, floor plan and photos) are used only to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Third-party services

WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy. Fonts and scripts on this site are served from this website.

Access, correction and questions

You may ask to see, correct or delete personal data you have shared with R&C.
