Partitioning

Partitioning for Offices, Shops and Showrooms

Partitioning divides one open unit into the rooms your business needs: meeting rooms, offices, storerooms, fitting rooms or back-of-house areas. We build drywall and glass partitions with doors, power points and finishes for Singapore offices, shops, showrooms, F&B outlets and salons.

Common partitioning jobs

  • A meeting room or manager's office in an open-plan unit.
  • A storeroom or pantry behind a shop or cafe counter.
  • Fitting rooms, treatment rooms or private salon rooms.
  • Splitting a large unit into two, or changing an old layout.
  • Feature walls with built-in shelving or display niches.

What a partitioning scope can include

The frame and boards or glass, insulation if needed, doors and locks, skirting, painting, and moving or adding power points, switches and lights for the new room. Air-conditioning and sprinkler positions, and landlord or mall rules, are checked on site before the layout is fixed.

What drives the cost

The length and height of the walls, drywall or glass, insulation, the number and type of doors, electrical changes and finishes. You get a written scope that shows what is included and what is not.

What to send us

WhatsApp your floor plan with a rough sketch of the rooms you want, a few photos of the unit and your target date. No job is too small: one wall or one small room is welcome.

Frequently asked questions

Can you put up just one partition wall?

Yes. No job is too small, from a single wall or storeroom to a full office layout. WhatsApp us your floor plan and photos.

Glass or drywall for a meeting room?

Glass keeps the space bright and open; drywall gives more privacy and is easier to soundproof. Some rooms use both, for example a drywall room with a glass front.

Can partitions be soundproofed?

Yes. Drywall partitions can be insulated, built up to the slab and fitted with sealed doors. See our soundproofing page for the options.

Do I need landlord approval?

Most landlords and malls want to see the layout before partition works start. We check the rules for your unit and plan the work around them.
