Services

Services for retail and commercial spaces

R&C helps Singapore shop owners, office and showroom tenants, and cafe and salon operators plan their spaces. Each service below has its own page covering scope, options, budget drivers and planning factors, so you can see what a trade involves before you enquire. You can ask about a single trade, such as carpentry or flooring, or have the work planned together through design and build. Send us your floor plan and we will discuss your scope.
