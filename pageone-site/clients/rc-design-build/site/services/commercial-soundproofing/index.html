Soundproofing

Soundproofing for Offices, Shops and Studios

Soundproofing cuts the noise that travels between rooms and units, and acoustic treatment cuts the echo inside a room. We plan both into partitions, ceilings, doors and finishes for Singapore offices, shops, studios, F&B outlets and salons.

Who soundproofing suits

  • Offices that need private meeting rooms, phone booths or a quiet director's room.
  • Studios and training rooms for music, recording, fitness or classes.
  • Clinics, salons and spas where clients expect calm and privacy.
  • Cafes and restaurants with hard surfaces that make the room loud.
  • Units next to noisy neighbours, lifts, plant rooms or a busy corridor.

Soundproofing or acoustic treatment?

They solve different problems, and many spaces need a little of both.

SoundproofingAcoustic treatment
Stops sound passing through walls, ceilings and doorsReduces echo and noise inside the room
Mass, insulation and sealing gapsSoft or slatted panels, baffles and ceiling treatment
For privacy and keeping noise in or outFor clearer speech and a calmer room

What a soundproofing scope can include

  • Insulated drywall partitions built full height to the slab.
  • Upgrading an existing wall with insulation and extra board layers.
  • Solid or acoustic-rated doors with seals and drop seals.
  • Sealing gaps around pipes, trunking and ceiling voids, where sound often leaks.
  • Acoustic wall panels, ceiling panels or slatted timber made in our own workshop.

Air-conditioning ducts and landlord rules can affect what is possible, so we check these on site first.

What drives the cost

Room size, wall height, how quiet the room needs to be, the number of doors and glass panels, ceiling access and whether the work is done in an occupied unit. A site visit gives a written scope you can compare line by line.

What to send us

WhatsApp a floor plan, photos of the room and the walls around it, what the noise problem is (for example "voices carry into the next room") and when you need it done. No job is too small: a single meeting room or one wall is welcome.

Frequently asked questions

Can you soundproof just one room?

Yes. Many jobs are a single meeting room, booth or treatment room. No job is too small, so WhatsApp us with photos.

Will acoustic panels stop my neighbours hearing us?

Not on their own. Panels reduce echo inside the room. To stop sound passing through, the wall, ceiling void, door and gaps need treatment too.

Do glass walls work for a quiet meeting room?

They can help, but glass, door gaps and the ceiling void are usually the weak points. We discuss the trade-off between openness and privacy before work starts.

Can soundproofing be done in a running business?

Often, by planning noisy and dusty work outside opening hours. This depends on the landlord or mall rules for your unit.
