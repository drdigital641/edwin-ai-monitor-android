About R&C

R&C - Retail & Commercial Design & Build is a new Singapore brand for business owners and tenants planning shops, offices, showrooms, cafes and salons, covering design and build, carpentry, renovation, electrical, flooring, finishing and AVL.

AI design concept

What R&C is for

R&C is a design and build brand for Singapore business owners and tenants who are opening, moving or refreshing a retail or commercial space. The focus is practical: shops and boutiques, offices and showrooms, cafes and service counters, and salons and beauty studios.

What R&C covers

  • Design & build: integrated planning and coordination from brief to handover.
  • Commercial carpentry: counters, shelving, storage, display fixtures and reception joinery.
  • Renovation, electrical works, tiling and flooring, and painting and finishing.
  • AVL: audio, visual and lighting planned with the space.

How this website handles evidence

R&C is a new brand. This site therefore shows no client list, reviews, project counts or years in business, because none can be verified yet. Every image is an AI design concept and is labelled as one. Photographs of actual completed work, and any credentials, will be added only when they are real and can be checked.
