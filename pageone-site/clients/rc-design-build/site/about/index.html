About SG Commercial Fitout

SG Commercial Fitout is a Singapore design and build company for shops, offices, showrooms, F&B outlets and salons. It has its own carpentry workshop and covers carpentry, renovation, electrical, flooring, painting and AVL.

Who we are

SG Commercial Fitout is a Singapore design and build company that specialises in retail outlets and commercial spaces: shops and boutiques, offices and showrooms, F&B outlets and service counters, and salons and beauty studios. We have our own carpentry workshop, so counters, cabinets and shelving are made to the measurements taken on site.

What we cover

  • Design & build: one point of contact from brief to handover.
  • Carpentry: counters, shelving, storage, display fixtures and reception joinery, made in our workshop.
  • Renovation, electrical works, tiling and flooring, and painting and finishing.
  • AVL: audio, visual and lighting planned with the space.
