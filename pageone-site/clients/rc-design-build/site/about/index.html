Who we are

R&C Spaceworks is a Singapore design and build company for retail and commercial spaces: shops and boutiques, offices and showrooms, F&B outlets and service counters, and salons and beauty studios. We have our own carpentry workshop, so counters, cabinets and shelving are made to the measurements taken on site.

What we cover

  • Design & build: one point of contact from brief to handover.
  • Carpentry: counters, shelving, storage, display fixtures and reception joinery, made in our workshop.
  • Renovation, electrical works, tiling and flooring, and painting and finishing.
  • AVL: audio, visual and lighting planned with the space.
