### Scope boundaries
- Design & Build owns integrated planning and coordination.
- Commercial Renovation owns fit-out/refurbishment work and site execution.
- Commercial Carpentry owns counters, cabinets, shelving, storage, display fixtures and reception joinery.
- Electrical owns power distribution points, wiring and general electrical installation.
- AVL owns experience and equipment integration: sound, screens, feature lighting and controls.
- Link naturally across these pages; do not repeat the same copy with different headings.
- Do not claim kitchen/exhaust, structural, fire-safety submission, specialist certification or other capabilities unless confirmed. Cafe content must not imply an unverified full F&B compliance service.
