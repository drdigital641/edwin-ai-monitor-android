Do you come to Newton?

Yes. We are a mobile service based in Yishun and come to driveways and condo car parks in Newton, around Newton Road, Scotts Road. WhatsApp us your address area.

Can you work in a condo basement car park?

Yes, if your condo allows contractors. Some condos ask us to register at the guardhouse or book a bay, so send us your condo name when you book.

How long is my car out of use for a coating?

From 4-8 h for Sonax Ceramic Lite up to 9-16 h for Gtechniq 5-9 years. Paint correction on its own takes 4-15 h. Every package lists its own downtime.
