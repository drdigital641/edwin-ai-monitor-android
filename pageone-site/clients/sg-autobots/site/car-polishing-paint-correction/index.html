Car Polishing & Paint Correction: common questions

How much is paint correction?

Single to multi-stage paint correction is S$200-S$2,000 and takes 4-15 h. It is free with Sonax CCEVO and Gtechniq ceramic coating.

What can paint correction remove?

Swirl marks, light scratches, oxidation, water spots, bird-dropping and sap etching, and holograms.

How long does the spray ceramic last?

The Detail Car Wash & Polishing & Seal & Sonax Spray Ceramic Coating package lasts about 4 months. It is S$248-S$508 by car size.

Where is it done?

At your car park. We bring the polisher and products to you.
