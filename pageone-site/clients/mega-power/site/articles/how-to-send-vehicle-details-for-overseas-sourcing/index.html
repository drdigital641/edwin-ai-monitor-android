How to send vehicle details for overseas parts sourcing

Mega Power · draft for review by Mega Power's technical team · 29 Oct 2026

Send the VIN or chassis number, the make, model, year and engine, the market the car was sold in, the part needed with any number on its label, a clear photo, the quantity and the destination. With these, our team can identify the part and quote in one reply.

Checklist

  1. VIN or chassis number.
  2. Make, model, year and engine size.
  3. The market the car was sold in, and left- or right-hand drive.
  4. Part needed, with any OEM or part number from the label.
  5. A photo of the old part and its label.
  6. Quantity per part.
  7. Destination country and port or city.

Why the chassis number matters

The chassis number identifies the exact build of the car, which is how parts are matched when the model name alone is not enough. Chassis numbers you send are only seen by Mega Power staff.

Where to send it

Use the export enquiry form or the chassis number form. You can attach a photo or a parts list.

Need a part identified?

Send the chassis number or a photo of the label.
