Common questions

Do you show prices online?

No. Prices depend on the exact part, quantity and destination, so we quote each enquiry.

I don't know the part number. Can you still help?

Yes. Send the make, model and year, or the VIN or chassis number, and our team identifies the part.

Do you supply buyers outside Singapore?

Send an export enquiry with the country and quantity and our team replies with what we can supply.

How do I offer my products to Mega Power?

Use the supplier form to send your company details, range, catalogue and MOQ.
