Questions about these guides

What do I need to send to get the right Hyundai or Kia part?

Your car plate or chassis number, printed on the vehicle log card, and the part you need. For lamps, mirrors and suspension parts, say left or right. A photo of the old part helps.

Can these guides tell me what is wrong with my car?

No. They describe common signs of wear for each part. The same sign can have more than one cause, so have a workshop confirm the fault before you order the part.

Where do I find the part once I know what I need?

Each guide links to the part type for every Hyundai and Kia model we supply. You can also pick your model, search for the part, or ask on WhatsApp for the price and stock.
