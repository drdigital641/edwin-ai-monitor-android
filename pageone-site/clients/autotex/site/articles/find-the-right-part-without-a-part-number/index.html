How to find the right Hyundai or Kia part without a part number

Most owners don't know their part numbers. Here is what to send so you get the right part the first time.

By Autotex · 29 Oct 2026

What do you need to find the right part?

You need three things: the model, the generation (or year) and, for some parts, the engine. The fastest way to give all three is your chassis number, which is printed on your vehicle log card. Send it with the part you need and we match the exact part.

Why does the generation matter?

The same model name covers different cars over the years. A Hyundai Avante AD (2016-2020) and an Avante CN7 (2020 onwards) use different bumpers, lamps and many other parts. That is why every page on our shop shows the generation code and years.

What about facelifts and engines?

A facelift partway through a generation can change lamps, bumpers and grilles. Engines change spark plugs, ignition coils, filters and mounts. Your chassis number settles both.

What to send us on WhatsApp

  1. Your car plate or chassis number.
  2. The part, and left or right if it is a lamp, mirror or suspension part.
  3. A photo of the old part if you have it.
