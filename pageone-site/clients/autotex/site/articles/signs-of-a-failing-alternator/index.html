Signs of a failing alternator on a Hyundai or Kia

A failing alternator usually shows as a battery or charging warning light, dim or flickering lights, a car that is slow to start or keeps flattening the battery, and sometimes a whine from the engine bay. A workshop can test the battery and charging voltage to tell the two apart quickly.

By Autotex · Published 6 Nov 2026 · Last updated 6 Nov 2026

What does an alternator do?

The alternator is driven by the engine and generates the electricity that runs the car's lights, wipers, infotainment and ignition while the engine is running. It also recharges the battery. When it weakens, the car starts to run on the battery alone until the battery goes flat.

What are the common signs of a failing alternator?

  • The battery or charging warning light comes on while you are driving.
  • Headlights and dashboard lights dim or flicker, especially at idle or when the aircon and other electrics are on.
  • The car is slow to start, or the battery goes flat again soon after a jump start or a new battery.
  • A whining or grinding noise from the engine bay that changes with engine speed.
  • Electrical items such as windows, the audio system or the aircon blower behave weakly or erratically.

Is it the alternator or the battery?

Both give a car that will not start, so they are easy to confuse. A weak battery usually shows up when starting, while a failing alternator tends to show up while driving, with the warning light and dimming lights. A workshop can test the battery and the charging voltage in a few minutes. If you keep replacing batteries, have the alternator tested.

Can I keep driving with a failing alternator?

Not for long. The car runs on battery power until it runs flat, and then it can stall without warning, including on an expressway. If the warning light comes on, switch off unnecessary electrics, head for a safe place and have the car checked.

Do all cars have an alternator?

Petrol and diesel cars do. Fully electric cars have no engine-driven alternator, so it is not listed for those models in our shop.

Which alternator do I need?

Alternators differ by model, generation and engine. Send us your car plate or chassis number and we will match the right one. Ask about the drive belt at the same time, because a worn or loose belt can also cause charging problems.
