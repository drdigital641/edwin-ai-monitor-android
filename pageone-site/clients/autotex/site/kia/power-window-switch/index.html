What decides the right power window switch

The power window switch controls the electric windows from the driver door or a passenger door. Which one fits depends on model, generation and the number of windows the switch controls; the master switch on the driver door is different from a single switch.

Signs the Kia power window switch needs replacing

  • A window that works from one switch but not another.
  • Buttons that stick or are intermittent.
  • The auto up or down function that has stopped.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket power window switch

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
