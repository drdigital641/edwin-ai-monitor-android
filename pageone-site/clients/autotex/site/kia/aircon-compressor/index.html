What decides the right aircon compressor

The aircon compressor pumps and pressurises the refrigerant so the aircon can cool. Which one fits depends on engine and compressor type, plus the pulley and plug; two engine options in one model may use different compressors.

Signs the Kia aircon compressor needs replacing

  • Warm air from the vents with the aircon on.
  • A grinding or rattling noise when the aircon is switched on.
  • The clutch plate not engaging or oil spray around the compressor.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket aircon compressor

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
