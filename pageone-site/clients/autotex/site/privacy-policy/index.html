Privacy Policy

What we collect when you use this website or contact Autotex Aircon Enterprise, and what we do with it.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Who runs this site

This website is run by Autotex Aircon Enterprise (UEN 52353300D), 1 Kaki Bukit Avenue 6, #01-42, AutoBay@Kaki Bukit, Singapore 417883.

When you contact us

You can reach us by WhatsApp on +65 9731 9547 or by phone call on 6747 9618. What you send us, such as your name, car plate or chassis number, the part you need and photos of the old part, is used only to reply to you, check the right part for your car and handle your order.

Your cart and order

Your cart is kept in your own browser on your device, not on our server. When you tap Send order on WhatsApp, the order form (your name, car plate or chassis number, collection choice and notes) is put into a WhatsApp message to us. Nothing is sent until you send that message in WhatsApp, and nothing is paid on this website. You can clear the cart at any time.

Prices and stock shown on the site

To show current prices, stock and photos, your browser loads our parts catalogue from our database provider. This only reads the catalogue; it does not send your details.

Counting taps on our WhatsApp buttons

We count how often visitors tap the WhatsApp buttons and send orders on WhatsApp, so we can see which pages help people get in touch. Each tap sends one small record to our dashboard: the page it was tapped on, the button text, whether the device is a phone or a computer, the site you came from and any campaign tag in the link, whether you came from an ad (not the ad click code), how many pages you viewed and how long you had been on the site, your screen size, time zone and browser language, and two random codes: one that lasts only while your browser tab is open, and one kept in your browser so repeat visits from the same browser can be counted. The record is stored with the time of the tap.

The tap counter does not send or store your name, phone number or message. The WhatsApp message itself goes through WhatsApp, not through this counter. The counter does not use cookies.

Fonts and server logs

The text fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to fetch them. Our website host keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

No selling of data

We do not sell your details or give them to advertisers.

Your rights under the PDPA

We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012. To ask for a copy of the details we hold about you, or to have them corrected or removed, WhatsApp us on +65 9731 9547 or call 6747 9618.
