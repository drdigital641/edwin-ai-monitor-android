About Autotex

Autotex Aircon Enterprise is a Kia and Hyundai parts stockist in Kaki Bukit, Singapore. We bring parts in direct from Korea and sell them direct to Singapore drivers.

What we stock

Hyundai and Kia body parts, headlamps and tail lamps, engine parts, suspension, engine mounting, brake pads, brake discs (rotors), cooling and aircon parts, alternators, starter motors, spark plugs and ignition coils.

How we sell

Owners rarely know a part number, so our shop is built around your car: choose the model, choose the part, and send us the order. We match the exact part to your car plate or chassis number before you pay.
