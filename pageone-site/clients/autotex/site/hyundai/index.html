Hyundai parts in Singapore

Autotex supplies Hyundai parts for 25 Hyundai models sold in Singapore, covering 19 model families from Accent, Avante, Avante N and i30 to Venue. Each model page lists every part type we supply for that car, from bumpers, headlamps and tail lamps to brake pads and discs, suspension, engine mounting, spark plugs, ignition coils, cooling and aircon parts. There are also 60 pages that compare one part type across all Hyundai models, with the fitment, years and price for each model. Electric models have no engine, so engine parts are left off their pages. Generations matter: the same model name often has two or three body styles with different parts, so every page shows the generation code and years. If you are unsure, send your car plate or chassis number on WhatsApp and we check the fit before you pay.
