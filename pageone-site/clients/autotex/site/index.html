Questions

Do I need the part number to order?

No. Pick your car model and the part, or send us your car plate or chassis number on WhatsApp. We check the exact part for your car before you pay.

Are the prices on this site the final price?

Where a part has a price on this site, it is the price for the part listed. For every other part, tap Ask for price and we reply on WhatsApp with the price and stock. Either way we confirm stock and the exact fit for your car before you pay.

Where do I collect my parts?

At our shop at 1 Kaki Bukit Avenue 6, #01-42, AutoBay@Kaki Bukit, Singapore 417883. We are open Mon-Fri 9:30am-5:30pm, Sat 9:30am-3:30pm, Sunday closed. Please WhatsApp us first so we can check stock and get your parts ready.

Which cars do you have parts for?

Hyundai and Kia only, for models sold in Singapore such as the Avante, Tucson, Kona, Ioniq, Cerato, Sportage, Sorento, Niro and Picanto. Ask us if your model is not listed.
