What decides the right engine oil (4 L)

The engine oil (4 L) lubricates and cools the moving parts of the engine; the grade and the amount depend on the engine. Which one fits depends on the oil grade (for example 0W-20 or 5W-30) and the capacity for the engine.

Signs the Hyundai engine oil (4 L) needs replacing

  • The oil change interval has passed by distance or time.
  • Oil that is thick, black or gritty on the dipstick.
  • A noisier engine or a rising oil temperature.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket engine oil (4 L)

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
