What decides the right steering rack

The steering rack turns the steering wheel movement into the wheel angle, assisted by hydraulic or electric power. Which one fits depends on model and generation, and whether the assist is hydraulic or electric; left and right hand drive racks differ.

Signs the Hyundai steering rack needs replacing

  • Oil leaking from the rack boots or a wet rack.
  • Heavy steering, a whine on turning or a clunk at the wheel.
  • Play in the steering wheel.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket steering rack

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
