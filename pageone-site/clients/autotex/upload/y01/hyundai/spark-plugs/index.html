What decides the right spark plugs

The spark plugs ignite the fuel and air in each cylinder of a petrol engine, so a set covers every cylinder. Which one fits depends on engine, since plug type, heat range and gap change by engine; the number of plugs is the number of cylinders.

Signs the Hyundai spark plugs need replacing

  • A rough idle or hesitation when you accelerate.
  • Harder starts and higher fuel use.
  • A misfire that sets the check-engine light.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket spark plugs

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
