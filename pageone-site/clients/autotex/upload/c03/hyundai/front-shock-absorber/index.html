What decides the right front shock absorber

The front shock absorber controls the up and down movement of the front wheel so the car stays steady over bumps. Which one fits depends on model and generation, and the left or right side; the part may be sold as the absorber alone or with the spring and mounts as a strut. Because it is a left and right part, say which side you need.

Signs the Hyundai front shock absorber needs replacing

  • A bouncy ride that keeps moving after a bump.
  • Oil leaking down the body of the absorber.
  • A knocking noise over speed bumps or the front dipping hard under braking.

These signs are general to this type of part. A workshop can confirm the cause before you order.

OEM or aftermarket front shock absorber

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
