What decides the right fuel pump assembly

The fuel pump assembly pushes fuel from the tank to the engine at the right pressure, usually mounted inside the tank. Which one fits depends on model, generation and fuel (petrol or diesel); the module may include the filter and level sender.

Signs the Hyundai fuel pump assembly needs replacing

  • A whine from the rear seat area.
  • The engine cutting out under load or struggling to start.
  • A long crank when the tank is low.

OEM or aftermarket fuel pump assembly

OEM means made to the carmaker's own specification. Aftermarket means made by another maker to fit the same car, usually at a different price. Our catalogue does not label each listing as one or the other, so ask on WhatsApp and we tell you what we quote for your car before you pay.
