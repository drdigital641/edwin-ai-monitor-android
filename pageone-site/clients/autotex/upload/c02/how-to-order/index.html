How to order Hyundai and Kia parts from Autotex

Find the part, add it to your cart, and send the cart to our WhatsApp. We check the fit before you pay.

  1. Find your part

    Use the three boxes on the home page (brand, model, part) or type your car and the part into search, for example “Kona spark plugs”. You can also browse by Hyundai or Kia model, or by part type.

  2. Check the model and years

    Each page shows the generation code and the years it fits, like Avante AD 2016-2020. If you are not sure, carry on: we check before you pay.

  3. Add to cart

    Pick left or right where asked, choose how many, and tap Add to cart. Your cart stays on your phone until you send it.

  4. Send the order on WhatsApp

    Open your cart, add your name and car plate or chassis number, and tap Send order on WhatsApp. The order arrives at +65 9731 9547 as a ready-made message.

  5. We confirm and you collect

    We reply with stock, the exact part and the total. Collect at 1 Kaki Bukit Avenue 6, #01-42, AutoBay@Kaki Bukit, Singapore 417883, or ask us about delivery.

Questions

Do I pay online?

No. Your cart is sent to us on WhatsApp. We confirm stock, the exact part for your car and the total, then tell you how to pay and collect.

What if I don't know my car's generation?

Send your car plate or chassis number. The chassis number is printed on your vehicle log card. We use it to find the exact part.

Can I order parts for left and right sides?

Yes. Lamps, mirrors and suspension parts ask you to pick left or right. Add both if you need a pair.

Can I change my order after sending it?

Yes. Just reply in the same WhatsApp chat before we confirm.
