Website privacy notice

How this website handles the details you send us when you enquire.

Who we are

Ape Champs Swim is run by Ape Swimming Pte Ltd (UEN 202600447R), Singapore.

What you send us

When you WhatsApp or email us, you may share your name, phone number, your child's age, your area and condo, and your child's swimming experience. The enquiry form on our contact page does not store anything on this website: it only prepares a WhatsApp message that you choose to send.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry and arrange lessons. We do not sell your details.

Button tap counts

This website counts taps on its WhatsApp and email buttons, with the page, the button and the device type, so we know which buttons parents use. It does not record your IP address or the contents of your messages.

Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act you may ask to see or correct the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you.
