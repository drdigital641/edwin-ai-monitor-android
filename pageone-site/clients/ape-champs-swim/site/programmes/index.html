Kids' swimming programmes

Four levels, from first splashes to all four strokes. Your child's starting level is confirmed after the trial.

The four levels

  • ApeStart™ Water Confidence

    0.5 to 4 years30-minute lessons

    Gentle, play-based introduction to the water.

    • Bubbles, floating and kicking
    • Basic water safety
    • Parents can be poolside

  • ApeCore™ Learn-to-Swim

    Starting level confirmed after the trial45-minute lessons

    Learn-to-swim with strong foundations for freestyle and backstroke.

    • Freestyle and backstroke foundations
    • Aligned with SwimSafer® stages

  • SwimSafer® Programme

    6 to 12 years45-minute lessons

    Follows Sport Singapore's SwimSafer® Stage 1 to 6 syllabus.

    • Water safety and survival skills
    • Assessment-based progression

  • Stroke Refinement

Our in-house confidence awards

These are Ape Champs Swim's own milestones for younger swimmers. They are not official SwimSafer® certificates.

Water Explorer Award

Recognises swimmers who have developed early water confidence and comfort, and are ready to progress into structured learn-to-swim programmes.

What the coach looks for

  • Blow bubbles with face comfortably in the water for at least 5 seconds
  • Float independently (front or back) for at least 5 seconds
  • Willingness to place face in water
  • Readiness to progress into ApeCore™ Learn-to-Swim

ApeCore Ready Award

Recognises swimmers who have developed stronger independence, control, and foundational movement skills in the water, and are ready for assessment-based progression.

What the coach looks for

  • Blow bubbles and hold breath with face in water for at least 5 seconds
  • Front float and back float independently for at least 5 seconds each
  • Streamline rocket kicks for at least 2 metres independently and comfortably
  • Strong foundation in water confidence and independence
  • Consistent body control and propulsion skills
  • Readiness to progress into SwimSafer® 2.0 Stage 1

Exact award checks may vary depending on starting level, attendance and practice.
