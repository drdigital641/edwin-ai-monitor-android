Swimming lesson FAQ for parents

Straight answers. Where we can't answer here yet, WhatsApp us and we'll confirm for your child and your condo.

Who are the lessons for?

Children, from about 6 months to about 12 years old. Most of our families live in condos. We are not an adult swim school.

Where do lessons happen?

At your condo pool only. We cover Punggol, Sengkang, Hougang, Tampines, Bukit Timah and Upper East Coast. We do not teach at landed homes or public pools. WhatsApp us your condo name to check availability.

What age can my child start?

Children from 6 months old can be introduced to the water through gentle water orientation. Most children start structured lessons around 3 to 4 years old, when they can follow simple instructions.

How do we start?

Book a trial class. It lasts 30 to 45 minutes, includes a water assessment and ends with a programme recommendation. If it is a good fit, regular weekly lessons follow.

Trial price: WhatsApp us to confirm.

Does my child keep the same coach?

Yes. After the trial, your child keeps the same coach every week. We have about 6 coaches on the team.

What happens if the coach is sick?

WhatsApp us to confirm how cover is arranged.

Do we need condo management approval, or a resident to book the pool?

WhatsApp us to confirm, with your condo name. The same applies if you are not sure whether your condo allows outside coaches.

Is it the SwimSafer syllabus?

Yes. Our lessons follow Sport Singapore's SwimSafer® Stage 1 to 6 syllabus, with water safety, survival skills and assessment-based progression.

Do you run the official SwimSafer test? Are there test or certificate fees?

WhatsApp us to confirm. Our Water Explorer and ApeCore Ready awards are our own in-house confidence milestones, not official SwimSafer certificates.

How long is each lesson?

30 minutes for ApeStart™ (the youngest children) and 45 minutes for ApeCore™, SwimSafer® and Stroke Refinement. Lessons are once a week, in blocks of 4.

How much are lessons?

Kids' programmes are from $70 per lesson. Group classes for 2 to 4 children sharing a coach work out to $45, $33 or $28.75 per child per lesson. See the full rates.

How and when do we pay?

Fees are billed per month or per block of lessons, with no hidden registration or admin fees. For payment methods and timing, WhatsApp us to confirm.

Can we ask for a female coach?

Yes, for an extra $10 per lesson.

Do you teach children with special needs?

Yes. Special needs swimming lessons are 30 minutes each, at an extra $10 per lesson. Tell us about your child when you WhatsApp us.

What should my child bring?

Swimwear, goggles or a swim cap if needed, a towel and a change of clothes. Bring floatation aids only if the coach recommends them.

What if it rains or there is lightning?

Light rain: lessons continue. If there is lightning or heavy rain that affects visibility, the lesson pauses or is postponed, and coaches may switch to dryland training. Each lesson package includes bad weather cancellations. See our terms.

What if my child is unwell or we need to cancel?

Each lesson package includes 2 bad weather, 2 MC and 1 discretionary cancellation. The full rules are on our terms and cancellation page.
