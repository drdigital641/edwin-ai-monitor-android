3How it works

  1. Book a trial class

    30 to 45 minutes at your pool, with a water assessment and a programme recommendation.

  2. Keep the same coach

    After the trial, your child keeps the same coach every week. We have about 6 coaches on the team.

  3. Swim once a week

    Lessons run in blocks of 4: 30 minutes for the youngest, 45 minutes for older programmes.

7Questions parents ask

Who are the lessons for?

Children, from about 6 months to about 12 years old. Most of our families live in condos. We are not an adult swim school.

Where do lessons happen?

At your condo pool only. We cover Punggol, Sengkang, Hougang, Tampines, Bukit Timah and Upper East Coast. We do not teach at landed homes or public pools. WhatsApp us your condo name to check availability.

What age can my child start?

Children from 6 months old can be introduced to the water through gentle water orientation. Most children start structured lessons around 3 to 4 years old, when they can follow simple instructions.

How do we start?

Book a trial class. It lasts 30 to 45 minutes, includes a water assessment and ends with a programme recommendation. If it is a good fit, regular weekly lessons follow.

Trial price: WhatsApp us to confirm.

Does my child keep the same coach?

Yes. After the trial, your child keeps the same coach every week. We have about 6 coaches on the team.
