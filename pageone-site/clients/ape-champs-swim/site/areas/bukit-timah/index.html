How lessons work in Bukit Timah

  • Lessons happen at your own condo pool in Bukit Timah. We don't teach at landed homes or public pools.
  • Start with a trial class of 30 to 45 minutes, with a water assessment and a programme recommendation.
  • After the trial, your child keeps the same coach every week.
  • Lessons run once a week in blocks of 4: 30 minutes for ApeStart™, 45 minutes for older programmes.
  • Kids' programmes are from $70 per lesson. Siblings or close friends can share a coach from $28.75 per child per lesson (1 to 4). See rates.

Ages and levels

From about 6 months (ApeStart™ water confidence) to about 12 years (Stroke Refinement), following the SwimSafer® syllabus. See programmes.

Is my condo covered?

We teach at about 7 condos regularly across our six areas. WhatsApp us your condo name in Bukit Timah and we'll confirm availability, along with any condo pool rules.

Other areas we cover: Punggol, Sengkang, Hougang, Tampines, Upper East Coast.
