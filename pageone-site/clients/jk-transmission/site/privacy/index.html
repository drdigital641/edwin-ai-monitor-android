What we collect

This website has no account or login and does not store enquiry forms. When you tap WhatsApp, call or email, you contact us through that app, and we receive what you choose to send (such as your name, number, car details and photos).

To understand which buttons people use, the site counts taps on its WhatsApp, call, email, map and Facebook links. That count records the page, the button, the device type and a random visit id. It does not record your name, number or message.

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and to arrange and carry out work on your car. We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices
