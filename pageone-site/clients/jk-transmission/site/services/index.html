Choose a symptom

Jerking or slipping

A jerk between gears, or revs that climb without the car pulling, can start in several places: the fluid, the clutch packs or the valve body. Guessing gets expensive, so we check first and repair only what is needed.

Tell Jason when it happens: pulling off, at one gear change, or all the time.

WhatsApp Jason about this

Slow to engage D or R

A pause before Drive or Reverse catches is worth checking early. Fluid condition, solenoids and wear-and-tear parts are all on our list, and the checks come before any parts.

Mention if it is worse on a cold start or after a long drive.

WhatsApp Jason about this

Warning light or limp mode

A gearbox warning light or limp mode means the car has flagged a problem it does not like. Reading what the car reports is the first step. TCU / TCM repair and programming is one of our services.

A photo of the dashboard light on WhatsApp helps.

Hybrid DCT shudder

Shudder or judder on take-off in a hybrid double-clutch gearbox is work we do. We have overhauled a Honda Vezel Hybrid double-clutch gearbox, and DCT repair and replacement is one of our services.

Send Jason the model, year and whether it shudders cold, warm or both.
