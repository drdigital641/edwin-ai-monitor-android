Who we are

J & K Transmission Specialist Pte Ltd is a small gearbox repair shop in the west of Singapore. In our own words: we are new on Facebook, but definitely not new to this industry.

16years of combined experience across the team

How we work

We only repair what is needed. Every job starts with an honest, targeted diagnosis. We fix the parts that are worn or faulty, not the whole gearbox by default.

Cars we work on

Hybrid, Japanese, Korean and Continental cars, with automatic, double-clutch (DCT) and hybrid transmissions.

Work we have done

  • Honda Vezel Hybrid: double-clutch gearbox overhaul
  • Audi A6: reconditioned Transmission Control Unit, programming and transmission fluid
