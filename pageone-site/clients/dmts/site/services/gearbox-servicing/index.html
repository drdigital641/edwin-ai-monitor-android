Why the right ATF matters

Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF) cools and lubricates the gearbox and carries the hydraulic pressure that makes it shift. Different gearboxes need different fluids. We only use the manufacturer’s recommended or gearbox-specific brand of ATF, filled to the correct level, for longer life of bearings and gears with less wear.

Servicing prevents breakdowns

Good maintenance and periodic inspection catch small issues, such as a slow leak or old, worn fluid, before they turn into slipping, harsh shifting or a full overhaul. Follow the transmission service interval in your car’s service schedule, or ask us to check it for you.

Gearbox Maintenance & Servicing: common questions

What are the signs my gearbox needs attention?
A transmission check light, hesitation or refusing to go into gear, being stuck in one gear, jerking or slipping between gears, vibration at low RPM, red ATF stains under the car, or a loud noise from the gearbox. Any of these is worth a diagnostic before it gets worse.
