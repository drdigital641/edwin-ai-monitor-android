When an upgrade makes sense

A gearbox is designed for a certain amount of power (bhp) and torque (Nm). If the engine has been tuned or modified, the original clutches and torque converter can be pushed past what they were built for. A strengthening upgrade matches the gearbox to the engine, so it can handle the extra load reliably.

What we upgrade

  • Clutch packs
  • Valve body
  • Torque converter
  • Lubrication of bearings and gears, set up for the new performance level

Every upgrade starts with an assessment and a complete diagnostic of your current gearbox, so the upgrade is designed for your car and how you drive it.
