Gearbox problems we fix

Noticed one of these? Book a diagnostic before a small fault becomes a full overhaul.

Transmission check light on

The engine or transmission check light comes on while driving. Some cars also hold one gear (often called “limp mode”) to protect the gearbox.

What it means →

Lack of response

You press the accelerator and the car hesitates, or there is a delay before it goes into Drive or Reverse.

What it means →

Can’t change gear

The car stays in first gear, or only shifts up to third, when moving off or while driving.

What it means →

Jerking or gears slipping

The car jerks when shifting up or down, slips in and out of gear, or pops into neutral while driving.

What it means →

Vibration

The car vibrates at high speed on low RPM, or when accelerating hard from a standstill.

What it means →

ATF leaking or low

Bright red oil (ATF) stains on the floor after the car has been parked for a while.

What it means →

Gearbox noise

A loud noise from the gearbox once the engine starts or while the car is moving.

What it means →

Gearbox repair FAQ

How much does a gearbox repair cost in Singapore?
It depends on the car and the fault, so we don’t quote blind. We start with a pre-repair diagnostic (from S$30) to find the actual cause, then give you a clear quote before any work starts. WhatsApp us your car model and symptoms to get started.
How much is the diagnostic?
Our pre-repair diagnostic starts from S$30. We read the transmission fault codes and check the gearbox, then tell you the fault and the repair price before any work starts.
My car can’t move. Can you tow it?
Yes. We provide free pick-up to our workshop for gearbox repair during office hours, and if the car can’t move because of transmission failure we send a tow truck, free of charge. WhatsApp 8430 9665 or call 6352 8832.
Which car brands do you work on?
All types of vehicles: continental (Audi, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen, Mini), Japanese (Toyota, Lexus, Honda, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Nissan, Subaru, Suzuki), Korean (Hyundai, Kia) and other makes such as Land Rover, Jaguar, Volvo, Ford, Renault, Peugeot, Citroën, Chevrolet and Opel.
Is there a warranty after repair?
Yes. All repairs come with a standard 6-month warranty unless otherwise stated.
What are the signs my gearbox needs attention?
A transmission check light, hesitation or refusing to go into gear, being stuck in one gear, jerking or slipping between gears, vibration at low RPM, red ATF stains under the car, or a loud noise from the gearbox. Any of these is worth a diagnostic before it gets worse.
Where are your workshops and when are you open?
Our HQ is at 71 Woodlands Industrial Park E9, #01-25 Wave 9, Singapore 757048, and our branch is at 60 Jalan Lam Huat, #05-48, Singapore 737869. We are open Monday to Friday 9am–6pm and Saturday 9am–3pm, and closed on Sundays and public holidays.
