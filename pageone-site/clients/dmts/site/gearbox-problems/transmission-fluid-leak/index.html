Transmission Fluid Leak in Singapore: Red Oil Under the Car

Bright red oil (ATF) stains on the floor after the car has been parked for a while.

Common causes

These are the usual reasons for this symptom in an automatic gearbox. More than one can be involved, which is why we diagnose before quoting.

  • Worn or leaking gearbox seals
  • A leaking transmission pan gasket
  • Damaged transmission cooler lines
  • Loose or damaged drain or fill plugs

What to do now

  • Don’t just keep topping up: find and fix the leak
  • Watch for slipping or harsh shifts, signs the fluid is already low
  • Book a check before low fluid overheats the gearbox

Why it pays to check early

An automatic gearbox rarely fixes itself. A fault that starts as a sensor, a solenoid or low fluid can, if the car keeps being driven, wear the clutches and other internal parts, and a simple repair can turn into a full overhaul. Finding the cause early usually means a smaller, cheaper fix, which is why we diagnose first and quote before any work starts.

Questions about this problem

Is it safe to drive with a transmission fluid leak?
A small leak can be driven to a workshop, but low fluid makes a gearbox overheat and slip, which can lead to a much bigger repair. Get it checked soon, and don’t ignore it.
