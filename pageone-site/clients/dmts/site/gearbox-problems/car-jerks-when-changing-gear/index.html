Car Jerks When Changing Gear or Slips Out of Gear: Singapore Repair

The car jerks when shifting up or down, slips in and out of gear, or pops into neutral while driving.

Common causes

These are the usual reasons for this symptom in an automatic gearbox. More than one can be involved, which is why we diagnose before quoting.

  • Worn clutch packs or bands inside the gearbox
  • Low or degraded transmission fluid (ATF)
  • Valve body or shift solenoid faults
  • Torque converter problems

What to do now

  • Avoid hard acceleration and towing loads
  • Check for fluid leaks where you park
  • Book a diagnostic: slipping usually gets worse with time

Why it pays to check early

An automatic gearbox rarely fixes itself. A fault that starts as a sensor, a solenoid or low fluid can, if the car keeps being driven, wear the clutches and other internal parts, and a simple repair can turn into a full overhaul. Finding the cause early usually means a smaller, cheaper fix, which is why we diagnose first and quote before any work starts.

Questions about this problem

Will changing the gearbox oil fix jerking?
Sometimes, if the fluid is low or worn out. But jerking can also come from worn clutches or a faulty valve body, which fresh fluid won’t fix. A diagnostic tells you which it is before you spend money.
How much does it cost to fix?
