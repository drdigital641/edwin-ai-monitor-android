About Dynamic Motoring (DMTS)

“15 years of combined industry experience working on gearbox repair and overhaul.”

Transmission specialists, not general mechanics

DMTS’ team of specialists have over 15 years of combined industry experience in gearbox repair and overhaul. With wide and in-depth knowledge of different transmission systems and models, we follow a rigorous, stringent process to solve any gearbox issue. Our transmission specialists are constantly trained and kept up to date on the latest developments in the auto transmission industry.

What we believe

We believe in giving every customer the most direct and accurate gearbox solution for their specific problem, to save them time and money. That is only possible with an experienced team of transmission specialists and an extensive range of special tools and equipment.

Our focus on customers’ needs, and meeting their expectations, is why drivers choose us as their trusted workshop for gearbox repair.

Our strengths

  • Specialist in auto transmission repair & overhaul of all types of vehicles
  • Experienced & well-trained mechanics in gearbox repair
  • Competitive and direct pricing for transmission issues
  • Warranty after repair for peace of mind
  • High quality parts and Automatic Transmission Fluid (ATF)
  • Comfortable waiting lounge with a clear view of your car
