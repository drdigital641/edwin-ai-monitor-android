A 2019 **Honda Freed Hybrid** came in, and Jacky's scan of the transmission control module showed **P2857: Odd clutch pressure engagement performance**. It is a common code on Honda's hybrid dual-clutch gearbox, and it is a good example of why this gearbox needs someone who knows it well: the code names the clutch, but the cause is not always the clutch.

## Which Honda hybrids use this gearbox?

Honda's i-DCD hybrid system pairs the engine and an electric motor with a **7-speed dual-clutch transmission (DCT)**. In Singapore you will mostly find it in the hybrid versions of the:

- **Honda Freed** and Freed+
- **Honda Vezel**
- **Honda Shuttle**
- **Honda Fit / Jazz**

Jacky works on these Honda hybrid DCT gearboxes regularly.

## What does P2857 mean?

A dual-clutch gearbox has two clutches. One drives the **odd gears** (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th) and the other drives the even gears. P2857 means the control module asked the odd clutch to engage and the result, measured through clutch pressure and movement, was not what it expected.

On the scanner in the photo the code showed as **Temporary**. That means the module has seen the fault but may not have confirmed it yet. It is an early warning worth acting on, because the odd clutch also handles first gear when you pull away.

## What owners notice

- Jerking or shuddering when pulling away from a stop
- A clunk or harsh shift at low speed, or in stop-start traffic
- Hesitation or a flat spot before the car moves
- A gearbox or check engine warning light, sometimes with limp mode
- Symptoms that come and go, often worse when the gearbox is hot

These signs overlap with other faults, so they are a reason to test, not a diagnosis.

## Why it is not always the clutch

On the Honda hybrid DCT, an odd clutch code can come from several parts that work together:

- **The clutch itself.** Wear or slip on the odd clutch.
- **The actuators.** Electric actuators engage the clutches and move the gear selectors. If an actuator is weak or sticking, the clutch cannot engage the way the module expects.
- **The first gear hub.** Wear here can affect how first gear engages, which is where the odd clutch works hardest.
- **Internal bearings and shafts.** Worn bearings or shaft play change how the clutch and gears line up.
- **Wiring, sensors and software.** Clutch position sensors, connectors and the module's learned values also play a part.

Replacing the clutch alone does not fix a worn actuator, hub or bearing, and the code can come back. That is why this gearbox needs a specialist who tests each part before deciding what to repair.

## Questions
Q: What does P2857 mean on a Honda Freed Hybrid?
A: P2857 means "odd clutch pressure engagement performance". The control module saw that the clutch for the odd gears, including first, did not engage the way it expected.
Q: Is P2857 always a clutch problem?
A: No. On the Honda hybrid DCT it can also come from the actuators, the first gear hub, internal bearings and shafts, or wiring and sensors. Testing finds which part it is.
Q: Which Honda models use the hybrid DCT gearbox?
A: The i-DCD hybrid with a 7-speed dual-clutch gearbox is used in hybrid versions of the Honda Freed, Vezel, Shuttle and Fit / Jazz.
Q: Can I keep driving with code P2857?
A: Drive gently and get it checked soon. If the car jerks badly, will not pull away or goes into limp mode, stop and ask for a tow so the fault does not spread to other parts.
