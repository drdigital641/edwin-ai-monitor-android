**Repairing the gearbox on an older car is usually worth it** when the repair costs clearly less than what you would lose by giving up the car, the rest of the car is in good condition, and you plan to keep driving it for the remaining COE period. It is usually not worth it when the COE is about to run out and you don't plan to renew it, or when the engine and body need major work too.

## What should I find out first?

Get the actual fault before deciding. A gearbox that slips, jerks or shows a warning light does not always need a full overhaul: sometimes the cause is a sensor, a solenoid, wiring or old fluid. ## How does the remaining COE affect the decision?

In Singapore a car's Certificate of Entitlement (COE) runs for 10 years. When it ends, you either renew it and keep the car, or deregister the car. That makes the remaining COE period the most useful yardstick:

- **Several years of COE left:** a repair spreads its cost over a long period, and the alternative (buying another car) is expensive. Repair usually makes sense if the rest of the car is sound.
- **Less than a year left, and you won't renew:** a large repair is hard to justify. Ask whether a smaller repair can keep the car running safely until deregistration.
- **Planning to renew the COE:** treat the car as a longer-term car. A proper repair or overhaul, plus regular [gearbox servicing](/services/gearbox-servicing/), protects that decision.

## What else should I weigh?

- **The rest of the car:** engine, suspension, aircon and body condition. A sound car with one failed gearbox is a good candidate for repair.
- **What the car is worth to you:** if you would have to replace it straight away, compare the repair quote with the full cost of changing cars, not just the car's resale value.
- **The warranty:** all repairs come with a standard 6-month warranty unless otherwise stated.

## Is it safe to keep driving while I decide?

If the gearbox is slipping, flashing a warning light or refusing to move in Drive or Reverse, don't keep driving it: it can strand you and turn a smaller repair into a bigger one. If the car can't move, we arrange free towing or pick-up to our workshop during office hours.

A: Only if you plan to renew the COE or the repair is small. If you will deregister the car, ask for the smallest safe repair rather than a full overhaul.
