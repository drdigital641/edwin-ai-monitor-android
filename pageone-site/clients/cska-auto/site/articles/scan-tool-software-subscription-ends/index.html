What happens to a scan tool when the software update subscription ends?

By CSKA Auto Enterprise · Updated 31 October 2026

In general the tool keeps working with the software already installed, so you can still read codes and run the functions you had. What stops is new software: coverage for newer cars, fixes and added functions. Each maker sets its own terms, so check your model's update end date and what renewal covers before it expires.

Does the scan tool stop working when the subscription ends?

Usually no. The software on the tool at the end of the update period stays on it. The change is that the tool no longer receives new versions. Some online functions can depend on a current subscription, and the rules differ between makers and models, so read the terms for your tool rather than assume.

What do you lose without updates?

A workshop feels the gap on newer cars and newer systems rather than on the cars it already services:

  • Newer model years cars released after your last update may not be listed or may have fewer systems covered.
  • Fixes bugs found after your last update stay in your version.
  • New functions added service resets, tests or coding options reach you only with a current update.
  • Protocol support newer communication standards such as DoIP and CAN FD arrive through updates, so a tool that is not kept current falls behind as these cars become more common.

What should you check before the period runs out?

Do these while the subscription is still active:

  • The end date find it in the tool's settings or account page, or ask your supplier.
  • What renewal covers ask whether it is all brands or only some, and whether it covers any online programming or extra modules you use.
  • Your own car mix if most of your work is on older cars, you may need updates less often than a workshop that services new models every week.
  • A backup plan note which jobs you could not do on an out-of-date tool, such as a model-year gap, so you can decide with facts.

How do makers handle this?

Each maker sets its own update terms. For example, Autel states one year of free software updates and a one-year limited warranty for the MaxiSYS MS909 S2. After that, ask for the renewal for your model. THINKCAR, TOPDON and the other makers have their own terms, so confirm yours instead of using another maker's as a guide.

Questions

Can I still use my scan tool after the free update period ends?

Generally the tool keeps the software it already has. Check the terms for your model, because online functions and some features can depend on a current subscription.

How often should a workshop renew software updates?

It depends on your cars. A workshop that services recent models every week needs current coverage more than one that works mostly on older cars. Compare the jobs you turn away with what renewal covers.

How do I renew updates for a tool I bought from CSKA?

Is the renewal the same for every brand?
