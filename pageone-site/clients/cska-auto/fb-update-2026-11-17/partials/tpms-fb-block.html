Questions

What does a TPMS tool do?

It wakes up each tyre pressure sensor to read its ID, pressure and battery, programs replacement sensors, and runs the relearn so the car recognises the new sensor.

Do you have a TPMS tool that programs sensors?

Yes. The Autel MaxiTPMS TS508WF programs Autel MX-sensors, and the THINKTOOL Master X2 has a TPMS module that programs and relearns sensors.

Is there a quick way to check tread depth for customers?

The laser tyre tread and brake disc examiner reads wear without taking the wheel off and shares a report by QR code. Ask us for a demo.

Can one tool activate, program and relearn?

Yes. The Autel MaxiTPMS TS508WF and the TPMS module in the THINKTOOL Master X2 both activate sensors, program them and run the relearn, so the car recognises the new sensor.

Does CSKA deliver TPMS tools to the workshop?

Yes. Each tool is delivered to your workshop and handed over in person.

When does a car need a TPMS relearn?

After a replacement sensor is fitted so the car recognises it, and on many makes after a tyre rotation too. It depends on the make and the TPMS type, so check the car's service information. The Autel MaxiTPMS TS508WF and the THINKTOOL Master X2 TPMS module run relearns.
