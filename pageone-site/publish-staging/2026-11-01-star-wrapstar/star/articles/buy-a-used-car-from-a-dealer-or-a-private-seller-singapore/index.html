Should you buy a used car from a dealer or a private seller in Singapore?

Both routes can work. With a private seller you deal with the owner directly and arrange the viewing, checks and transfer paperwork yourself. A dealer has cars on display, can walk you through the paperwork and is a business you can go back to after pickup. Either way, have the car inspected at a workshop you trust before you pay in full.

What do you handle yourself with a private seller?

You arrange the viewing and the test drive, ask for the registration date, the COE expiry date and any service records, agree the price and the terms of any deposit, and organise the ownership transfer with the seller. Nothing about this is wrong, but every step is yours to check, so allow time for it.

What does a dealer add?

A dealer gives you one place to see the car, drive it and ask questions, and a business to go back to after pickup. At Star Performance Motor you can view and drive the car at our Yishun showroom, have it inspected at a workshop of your choice before you pay in full, and we explain the paperwork and hand the car over when the transfer is done. If you have a car to trade in, tell us its model, registration year and mileage and we will make an offer that can go towards the car you buy.

Is one cheaper than the other?

Do not assume so. Compare cars of the same model, age, mileage and remaining COE, and look at the yearly depreciation figure rather than the asking price alone; our guide to depreciation on a car listing shows how.

What should you check whichever way you buy?

Ask for the COE expiry date and work out how long you can drive the car before renewal (see what a COE is). Book a pre-purchase inspection and use our test drive checklist. Get the terms of any deposit in writing before you pay it. After you buy, what to do after buying a used car covers the next steps.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safer to buy a used car from a dealer?

Not automatically. What protects you is what you check: an independent inspection, the documents, and the deposit terms in writing. A dealer makes some of those steps easier, but you should still do them.

Can I have a dealer's car inspected before I buy?

At Star Performance Motor, yes. You can book a pre-purchase inspection at any workshop you trust before you commit.

Can I trade in my car when I buy from a dealer?

At Star Performance Motor, yes. Tell us your car's model, registration year and mileage and we will make you an offer, which can go towards the car you buy.
