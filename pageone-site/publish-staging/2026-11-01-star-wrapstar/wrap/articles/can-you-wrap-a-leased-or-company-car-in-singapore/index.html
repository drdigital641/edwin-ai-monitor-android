Can you wrap a leased or company car in Singapore?

By Wrapstar Autostyle · 1 November 2026

The short answer

Often yes, but ask the car's owner first. A colour change wrap is a film laid over the paint, so the factory paint stays underneath and the film can be removed later, which is why many owners wrap cars they plan to return or resell. Whether your lease or company allows it depends on your contract, not on the studio.

Why does a wrap suit a car you will have to return?

Because it does not change the paint. The film sits over the original colour, and when it is peeled off the original colour is still there. A respray cannot be undone in the same way, which is why a wrap is the usual choice when the car is not fully yours.

What should you check before you book?

  • Your lease or company car policy: does it allow a colour change or any modification, and must the car go back in its original colour?
  • Who has to give permission, and get that permission in writing.
  • Who pays for the wrap and who pays for removal at the end.
  • Your insurer: tell them about the change and ask what they need from you.
  • The current requirements for a colour change on LTA's OneMotoring website, because the rules are set by LTA.

What should you do before the job?

Take dated photos of the car as it is, so there is a record of the paint before the film goes on. A good studio checks the paint before it starts: badly resprayed panels can lift paint when film is removed, so tell the studio if any panel has been repainted. Our guide to preparing your car for a wrap or PPF job lists what to tell them.

What about taking the wrap off at the end?

Ask at the start who will remove the wrap, when, and what the surface should look like afterwards, because removal is a job for a studio, not a quick peel at home. Plan the removal date before the car is due back. The same question comes up when selling a car, covered in should you remove a wrap before selling.

For other questions to put to a studio, read what to ask before booking a car wrap. To see what we do, read about our colour change wrap or WhatsApp us your car model and a photo.

Questions

Can I wrap a leased car in Singapore?

Possibly. The wrap itself can be removed and the factory paint stays underneath, but whether you may do it is decided by your lease or the leasing company. Ask them and get the answer in writing before you book.

Will the original paint be safe under a wrap?

A good film on healthy factory paint comes off cleanly. Panels that were badly resprayed can lift paint when film is removed, so we check the paint before we start and tell you if there is a risk.

Who removes the wrap when I return the car?

Ask a studio to do it. Agree at the start who will remove it and when, so it is done before the car is due back.
