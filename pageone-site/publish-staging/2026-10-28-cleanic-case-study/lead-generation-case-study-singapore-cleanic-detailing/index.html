Lead Generation Case Study Singapore: How a Mobile Car Detailer Gets Found on Google

A real Singapore case study: how a mobile car detailer with no shopfront gets found on Google, with Search Console figures you can check yourself.

Published 28 October 2026 · By Page One Singapore

Most small business owners in Singapore start online the same way: a Facebook page, an Instagram account, maybe a Google Business Profile with a handful of good reviews. Customers who already know you can find you. The harder question is how strangers find you, the ones typing a problem into Google at night and messaging whoever shows up first.

This lead generation case study looks at one real Singapore business that Page One runs: Cleanic Detailing, a mobile car grooming and fumigation service. Every figure below comes from Google Search Console, Google's own report of how a website performs in search, and the website is live for you to check at cleanicdetailing.com.

What does this lead generation case study show?

It shows how a service business with no shopfront went from no Search Console data to appearing on page one of Google for 1,210 different searches in the 28 days to 21 October 2026, with 480 clicks from Google in that period. It also shows what was built to get there, and what the numbers do and do not prove.

Key takeaways

  • Cleanic Detailing is a mobile business: customers can't walk past a shop, so being found online is how new customers arrive.
  • Its Search Console data starts on 20 June 2026. From August it was getting about 500 clicks a month from Google.
  • In the 28 days to 21 October 2026 it appeared on page one for 1,210 searches, 582 of them in the top 3.
  • The work was ordinary and steady: one page per service, guides on the problems customers search for, Google Business Profile and Search Console set up, and new articles chosen from real search data.
  • Clicks are visits from people who were searching, not guaranteed enquiries. A WhatsApp button on every page is what turns a visit into a message.
  • These are one business's results. Yours will depend on your trade, area and competition.

Who is Cleanic Detailing?

Cleanic Detailing is a Singapore car grooming and fumigation service run by Colin Loo. It comes to the customer, so there is no shop for anyone to walk into. Its services include car grooming, steam cleaning, odour removal and MIRAKN dry gas fumigation for pests such as cockroaches and flies inside a car.

Its prices are stated plainly on the site. For example, the MIRAKN Express treatment is S$118 and takes about 1 hour, and the full MIRAKN fumigation starts from S$180 and takes about 1.5 hours.

That makes it a good example for owners who run a mobile or home-based service, or who sell mainly through social media. Without a shopfront, a customer's first impression of you is whatever they find on their phone.

What was built for Cleanic?

Page One built and runs the website and the Google listings. Nothing here is unusual. The difference is that it was done completely and then kept going every day.

  1. One page for each service. Car grooming, steam cleaning, fumigation and odour removal each have their own page, so each one can be found on its own search.
  2. Guides on the problems customers actually search for. Cockroaches in the car, vomit in the car, houseflies in the car. A worried car owner searching at 11pm finds a clear answer and a way to book.
  3. Google Business Profile, Search Console and Bing set up. So Google Maps, Google Search and Bing all have the same correct business details.
  4. Structured data. Code that tells search engines and AI assistants the business name, services and contact details in a form they can read.
  5. New articles picked from real search data. Each new topic comes from searches Google already shows the site for. By 24 October 2026 the site had 84 articles.
  6. A WhatsApp button on every page. One tap opens a chat with Cleanic, so a reader can ask a question or book without filling in a form.

The daily part is handled by a set of AI tools we designed to optimise SEO every day: a technical audit with fixes, and new content on a schedule. A person checks the facts. For Cleanic, details such as treatment times are confirmed with Colin before they go on the site.

What happened on Google, month by month?

Here is Cleanic's Search Console data for each month. A lower average position is better, and positions 1 to 10 are page one.

Month (2026) Clicks from Google Times shown on Google Average position
July 188 16,872 9.4
August 500 31,417 13.5
September 505 39,551 10.4
October (1 to 21 Oct) 370 29,634 7.8

Two things stand out. First, clicks jumped from 188 in July to 500 in August, then held at that level. Second, the average position dipped in August as the site started to appear for many more searches, then improved again in September and October.

In the 28 days to 21 October 2026, compared with the 28 days before:

  • 480 clicks from Google, up from 465.
  • 40,625 times shown on Google, up from 34,408.
  • Average position 8.3, improved from 11.0.
  • On page one for 1,210 different searches, 582 of them in the top 3.

Does this mean more enquiries?

Honestly, the Search Console figures show visits, not bookings. A click means someone searched, saw Cleanic and chose to open the site. What happens next depends on the page: is the answer clear, is the price shown, is it easy to message?

That is why every Page One site is built for the second step too. Each page answers the usual questions, shows prices from, and has a WhatsApp button with a pre-filled message. Page One also counts WhatsApp taps on the sites it runs, so it can see which pages lead to messages and improve those first. We don't publish Cleanic's enquiry numbers, and we won't promise you a number of enquiries. Nobody honestly can.

What can you learn from Cleanic if you have no website yet?

If you are a new business, or you're busy on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Carousell or Google Maps but have no website, here is what Cleanic's results suggest:

  1. Social pages and a Business Profile are a start, not the whole job. Google Search mostly shows web pages for searches like 'car fumigation singapore'. A profile or a post can't hold a page for each service.
  2. Write for the problem, not just the service. Many of Cleanic's visitors arrive from a problem they typed into Google. Think about what your customers worry about before they call you.
  3. One page per service beats one page that lists everything. Each page can match its own search.
  4. Give it months, and keep going. Cleanic's clicks grew over the first three months, not the first week. Steady daily work is what keeps a site moving.
  5. Make enquiring one tap away. A visit is wasted if the visitor can't see how to reach you.
