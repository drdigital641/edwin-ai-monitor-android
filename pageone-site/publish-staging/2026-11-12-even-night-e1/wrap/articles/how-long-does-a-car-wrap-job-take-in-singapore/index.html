How long does a car wrap take in Singapore?

By Wrapstar Autostyle · 12 November 2026

The short answer

A full vinyl car wrap takes days, not weeks. At Wrapstar Autostyle you drop the car at the studio on the agreed day and collect it when it is done. The exact time depends on your car, the film and how much of the car is covered, so we confirm it when we quote.

What happens from first message to collection?

  1. Send a photo. WhatsApp us your car model and a photo, with the colour or film you have in mind.
  2. Get a quote. We reply with the film options and a price for your car, and you can ask for the time then.
  3. Drop the car. Bring it to our studio at North Spring Bizhub, Yishun, on the agreed day.
  4. Collect and check. We walk you round the finished car, and you can come back for a check if anything bubbles or lifts.

What changes how long a wrap takes?

These are general factors, not promised times:

  • The size of the car. More paint to cover means more film to lay.
  • How much is covered. A full wrap with door shuts and edges covered takes longer than a partial job.
  • The film and finish. Ask us about your choice when we quote. See how to choose your wrap finish.
  • The paint underneath. We check the paint before we start, and tell you if there is a risk. See car wrap or respray.
  • The studio's diary. The drop-off day is agreed with you on WhatsApp.

How do you get the car ready?

A clean car with nothing left inside makes the day smoother. Our checklist is in how to prepare your car for a wrap or PPF.

When are we open to drop off and collect?

Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm and Saturday 9am to 3pm. We are closed on Sunday. Ask us when to bring the car and when to collect it.

What after you collect it?

Give the film a few days to settle before the first wash, then hand wash gently. See how to wash and care for a wrapped car. For how long the film itself lasts, see how long a car wrap lasts in Singapore.

How do you start?

See our colour change wrap service, or WhatsApp a photo of your car for a quote and the time it will take. Before you book, what to ask before booking a car wrap is a useful checklist.

What makes a wrap take longer?
