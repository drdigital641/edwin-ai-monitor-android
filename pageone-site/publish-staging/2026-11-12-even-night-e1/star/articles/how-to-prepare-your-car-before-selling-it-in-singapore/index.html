How to prepare your car before selling it in Singapore

Before selling your car in Singapore, gather the service records and spare key, note the mileage, COE expiry and any loan balance, clean the car, take clear photos, and write down any faults you know about. Then ask for an offer. You do not need to spend money on repairs first.

A little preparation makes the sale quicker and the offer easier to explain. This is general advice; it does not promise a higher price.

What should you gather first?

  • Service records and receipts. They show how the car was looked after. See how to check mileage and service history.
  • The spare key and manuals, so the car is handed over complete.
  • The car's details: model, registration date, mileage and COE expiry date. See what a COE is.
  • Your loan balance, if there is one. The loan is settled with the bank when the car is sold.
  • Your paperwork, as listed in what you need to sell your car in Singapore.

Should you clean the car?

Yes, a basic clean is worth doing. A tidy, vacuumed car with no clutter is easier to photograph and easier to inspect, and it lets the buyer see the car as it really is. A tidy cabin and clean boot also make it easier to spot anything that needs mentioning. You do not need an expensive detail.

How do you photograph it for an offer?

  1. Park in daylight, in a clear space.
  2. Take the front, rear, both sides and the front corners.
  3. Add the interior, the dashboard with the mileage showing, and the boot.
  4. Photograph any scratches, dents or marks up close, so there are no surprises later.

Should you fix things before you sell?

Be careful before spending money. A dealer inspects the car in any case, and what needs doing, and what it costs, is part of how an offer is worked out. Note down any warning lights, noises, scratches or repairs you know about, and tell the buyer. Being upfront makes the inspection smoother. If you are unsure whether a repair is worth doing, ask for the offer first and ask what the dealer would do. For how offers are formed, see how much your used car is worth.

Which route should you use to sell?

Preparing helps whichever way you sell. If you are choosing between a quick dealer sale, consignment or selling directly, compare them in trade-in, consignment and direct sale, and see how long consignment takes if you can wait for the best buyer.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do before selling my car in Singapore?

Gather your service records and spare key, note your COE expiry, mileage and loan balance, clean the car, take clear photos and write down any faults you know about. Then ask for an offer.

Should I repair my car before selling it?

Ask the dealer first. A buyer or dealer looks at the whole car at inspection, so tell them about known faults and let them say what needs doing before you spend money on repairs.

Do I need to clean my car before selling?

It isn't required, but a clean car is easier to photograph and easier to inspect, so the condition you show is the condition they see.

What information do I give a dealer to get an offer?

The model, registration year, mileage, COE expiry and a few photos. Tell us your loan balance too if there is one, because a loan is settled with the bank as part of the sale.

Can I sell a car that still has a loan?

Yes. The loan has to be settled with the bank as part of the sale. When you sell to Star Performance Motor, we settle any outstanding loan with the bank as part of the deal.
