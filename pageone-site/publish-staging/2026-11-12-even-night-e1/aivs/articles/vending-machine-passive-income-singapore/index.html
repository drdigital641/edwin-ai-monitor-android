Vending machine business in Singapore: is it really passive income?

A vending machine is one of the more hands-off small businesses in Singapore, but it isn't fully passive. The machine sells on its own, and you still restock it, keep an eye on it and deal with the odd problem.

This guide explains what "passive" really means for a vending machine owner, what you still do each week, and how AIVS's setup keeps that work small.

What runs without you?

  • Selling. Both AIVS machines are self-service, so there are no staff. AI Printbox prints a custom phone case in 3 minutes for S$15. Topi Express cleans and disinfects a helmet in 5 minutes for S$5.
  • Opening hours. The machine works whenever the location is open. Topi Express at Hodaka Motoworld has run 24 hours a day since 22 Aug 2025.
  • Payment. It's cashless, so there's no cash to collect. See how cashless vending machines work.
  • Tracking. A live dashboard shows sales on your phone. See monitoring a vending machine from your phone.

What do you still do?

  • Restock blank phone cases or disinfectant before they run out. AIVS supplies stock at cost.
  • Check the machine and keep it clean. See weekly vending machine maintenance.
  • Handle customer issues like a refund request. AIVS's support team helps on the support plan.
  • Look after the host relationship with the mall, shop or workshop.
  • Watch the numbers and decide if a quiet machine should move to a better spot.

That's why "low-hands-on" is a more honest description than "passive". See running a vending machine business part-time.

How does AIVS keep the work small?

  • You own the machine: no franchise fee and 0% royalties, so you keep the sales.
  • Training included, so you know how to restock and fix common issues.
  • Support plan from S$99/month for help when something goes wrong. See support and maintenance.
  • Refurbished machines from S$1,800 (Topi Express, 6-month hardware warranty) and S$5,000 (AI Printbox, 1-year hardware warranty).
  • 19 machines already live in Singapore, at 13 AI Printbox sites (malls and Changi Airport T3) and 6 Topi Express sites (motorcycle shops).

Is it worth it as extra income?

That depends on the location and how many people use the machine. AIVS doesn't publish income figures and doesn't promise earnings. Read is a vending machine profitable in Singapore? to work out your own low, middle and high estimate first.

Frequently asked questions

Is a vending machine business passive income in Singapore?

Partly. The machine sells by itself, but you still restock, check it and handle the occasional customer issue. It's low-hands-on rather than fully passive.

How much time does a vending machine take each week?

It depends on how busy the machine is and how far it is from you. Restocking and a quick check are the main jobs, and the dashboard shows sales without a visit.

Do I need staff?

No. AIVS machines are self-service and cashless.

Do I pay royalties?

No. With AIVS you own the machine, with no franchise fee and 0% royalties.
