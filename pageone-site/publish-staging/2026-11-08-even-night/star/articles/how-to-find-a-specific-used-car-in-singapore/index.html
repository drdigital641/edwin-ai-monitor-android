How to find a specific used car in Singapore

Write down the model, year range, budget and must-haves, then search listings with limits on mileage and remaining COE, and ask dealers who can search their own network for you. Whichever route you take, view the car, test drive it and have it inspected at a workshop you trust before you commit.

What should you decide before you search?

Decide what the car must have before you open a single listing, so you can say no quickly to the rest. A short written brief also makes it easy to hand the search to a dealer later.

  • The model, and the year range you will accept.
  • Your budget, including the loan if you need one. See how much car loan you can get for a used car.
  • The colour, specification and anything you will not compromise on.
  • How long you plan to keep the car, because that decides how much COE you need left. See buying a used car with little COE left.

Where can you look for a specific used car?

  1. Online listings. Search by model and year, then sort out the ones that match your brief on mileage and COE.
  2. Dealers' own stock. Star Performance Motor lists its live cars on its sgcarmart dealer page and in the showroom in Yishun.
  3. Private sellers. You can find a good unit, but the checks and paperwork are yours. See buying from a dealer or a private seller.
  4. A dealer's network. A dealer can ask other dealers and sellers on your behalf and bring you a shortlist.

How do you judge a car you find?

Treat every candidate the same way, however rare the model is. Check the remaining COE, the mileage against the service records, how it drives, and then what a workshop says about it.

Can a dealer find the car for you?

Yes, if you give a clear brief. Star Performance Motor describes its car sourcing like this: you tell it the model, budget and must-haves, and it searches its dealer network and private sellers for a well-maintained unit. It shares the best options with photos and history, tells you what to look out for, and brings the car to you for viewing and inspection before you commit. It then completes the purchase, the transfer and any trade-in.

Examples it has posted include a Volkswagen Tiguan 2.0 R-Line in August 2026 and a Honda Civic Type R FD2R. See finding a specific car with Star Performance Motor.

What if the exact car does not turn up?

Relax one condition at a time, and keep the ones you listed as non-negotiable. Widen the year range first, then the colour, then the mileage limit, and ask your dealer which cars come closest. A car that is a little different but well kept is a better buy than the perfect spec with a poor history.

Frequently asked questions

Can a dealer find a specific used car for me?

Yes. Star Performance Motor searches its dealer network and private sellers when you give it the model and budget, shortlists options with photos and history, and lets you view and inspect the car before you commit.

What should I tell a dealer when I ask for a specific car?

The model, the year range, your budget, the colour and anything you will not compromise on. Also say how long you plan to keep the car, because that affects how much COE you need.

Can I have a sourced car inspected before I buy?

Yes. At Star Performance Motor you can view and test drive the car and have it inspected at a workshop of your choice before you commit.

What if I cannot find my exact car?

Widen one thing at a time: the year range, the colour or the mileage limit. Keep the items you will not compromise on and ask the dealer which cars come close.
