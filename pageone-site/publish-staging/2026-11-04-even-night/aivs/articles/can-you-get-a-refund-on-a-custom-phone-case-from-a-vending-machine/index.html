Can you get a refund on a custom phone case from a vending machine in Singapore?

No. AI Printbox purchases are non-refundable and non-exchangeable under the AIVS terms, because each phone case is printed to your order. If the machine itself has a fault, WhatsApp AIVS technical support and refund enquiries on +65 8878 4061. Check your phone model and design on the screen before you pay.

Why is a printed phone case non-refundable?

Each case is printed to your order with your phone model and your design or photo, so it cannot be sold to someone else. That is the reason AIVS gives in its customer terms: all purchases are non-refundable and non-exchangeable. You are also responsible for the content you choose to print.

What should you check before you pay?

  • Your phone model. The supported models are shown on the machine screen at the time of your order. See which phones a case printing machine can print for.
  • Your design or photo, so it is the one you want on the case.
  • The price shown on the machine. AIVS publishes S$15 for a custom case; check the machine for the current price.

What if the machine has a problem?

Technical support and refund enquiries go to AIVS on WhatsApp, +65 8878 4061. Tell them which machine you used and roughly when, and describe what happened. AIVS decides each case under its terms, so this page does not promise a refund.

Is the photo you upload kept?

No. The AIVS terms say uploaded images are deleted from the system within 48 hours and are not stored, shared or used for any other purpose.
