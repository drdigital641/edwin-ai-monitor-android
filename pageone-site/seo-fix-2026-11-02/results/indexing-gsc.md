## Limits of this check

- The API gives per-URL status only. It has no indexed-vs-submitted counts, no "Crawled - currently not indexed" report totals, and no Request Indexing. Those need the Search Console UI.
- Bing Webmaster Tools is not reachable from this session, so Bing status is not covered.
- Page choice: sggarage.com and pageonesingapore.com used their live sitemaps. The other sites used common paths (/services/, /articles/, /about/, /contact/, /prices/). "URL is unknown to Google" can mean the page does not exist, not only that Google has not seen it. Several sites use different paths: sofacaresg.com and theecuclinicsg.com have article URLs ending in .html or other slugs, and dannychuafinancial.com's referring pages point to /about-danny and /disclaimer. Those pages were not checked.
- pageonesingapore.com /services/ and /prices/ are not in its live sitemap (which lists /pricing/).
- Sitemap "no" means the per-URL result did not list a sitemap, not that the sitemap is missing. For sggarage.com /car-aircon-repair/ the URL is in the live sitemap, but the API did not report it.
- Crawl times are as reported by the API, in UTC.
- Pages in "Discovered - currently not indexed" or "Blocked due to access forbidden (403)" were not crawled recently. cleanicdetailing.com /about/ returned 403 to Google on its last crawl (2026-07-16) and needs checking.
