About XADO

European engine technology since 1991

XADO is a car-care company with headquarters in Germany, the Netherlands and Ukraine. It makes revitalizants, oils, greases and oil and fuel additives, and sells more than 1,000 products in over 100 countries.

Who makes XADO?

XADO was founded in 1991 in Kharkiv, Ukraine. Production now runs under XADO Germany and XADO Lube B.V. (Netherlands) licences. In Singapore, XADO is distributed by Rev Solutions Pte Ltd and fitted by 11 authorised workshops.

What is XADO best known for?

Its patented Revitalizant® technology, which XADO says forms a protective ceramic-metal layer on worn friction surfaces. The other main lines are XADO Atomic Oils and VeryLube car-care products. See how revitalizant works or find the right product for your car.

XADO in motorsport

XADO runs its own XADO Motorsport team and has partnered with the Kamaz motorsport team. It has also sponsored boxer Nikolai Valuev and the esports team Pro100!XADO.

Company facts are as published by XADO Singapore on shop.xado-shop.sg.
