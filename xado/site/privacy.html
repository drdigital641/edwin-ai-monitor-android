Privacy

Privacy notice

This website is run for XADO Singapore by its distributor, Rev Solutions Pte Ltd, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

What we collect

This site has no forms and no log-in. When you tap a WhatsApp button, we record an anonymous count of the tap: the page, the time, phone or desktop, and a random session code. No name, number or message is recorded. Your WhatsApp chat goes straight to the workshop you chose.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the explainer video is embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. These services may see your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Workshops

Each authorised workshop handles any details you send it under its own obligations under the PDPA.
