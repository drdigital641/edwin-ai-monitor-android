The problem nobody sees

Every drive in Singapore is wearing your engine — a few atoms at a time

Stop-start jams on the PIE, 33°C afternoons, short school runs and long idling in car parks are some of the hardest conditions an engine can face. Each cold start and every hot crawl rubs microscopic metal from cylinder walls, piston rings, bearings and cam lobes.

XADO's lab data puts it bluntly: used oil can carry up to 3 grams of worn-off metal, and an engine that loses around 60 grams of its friction surfaces is effectively worn out. That wear shows up as lost compression, more noise, higher fuel use and oil burning — usually long before your COE runs out.

Seeing is believing

“Metal can be healed”

In XADO's own lab test, engineers deliberately carved a defect into a bearing race, applied a revitalizant carrier and ran it under an 8,000 N load at 1,000 rpm. Within 15 minutes the scratch was filling in; after an hour it was practically invisible under the microscope.

That is the idea behind every XADO product: protect the parts you can't see, so the car you paid six figures for keeps running smoothly.

Frequently asked questions

What is XADO?

XADO is a European lubricant and engine-treatment brand founded in 1991, now produced under XADO Germany and XADO Lube B.V. (Netherlands) licences and sold in more than 100 countries. It is best known for its patented Revitalizant® technology, which is designed to form a protective ceramic-metal layer on worn friction surfaces.

Is XADO an oil or an additive?

Both. XADO Atomic Metal Conditioners and EX120 Revitalizants are treatments added to your existing oil or fluid. XADO also makes fully formulated engine oils (Atomic Oil, Luxury Drive and LX AMC Black Edition) with revitalizant already blended in, so you get the treatment with every oil change.

Does XADO change my oil's viscosity?

According to XADO, the revitalizant does not change the viscosity or characteristics of the oil — the oil simply carries it to friction surfaces such as cylinder walls, piston rings, bearings and cam lobes.

Where can I get XADO put into my car in Singapore?

At any of the 11 authorised XADO workshops across Yishun, Ang Mo Kio, Kaki Bukit, Ubi, Bukit Batok, Jurong East and Woodlands. See the workshop finder and WhatsApp the one nearest you.

Will XADO fix a broken engine?

No treatment can repair broken, cracked or heavily damaged parts. XADO is designed for wear protection and restoring worn surfaces; a good workshop will check your engine first and tell you honestly whether a treatment or a mechanical repair is the right call.
