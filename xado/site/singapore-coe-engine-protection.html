Protecting your engine in the $130,000 COE era

When the certificate costs more than the car, the engine has one job: last the full 10 years. Here's how to help it.

Key takeaways

  • October 2026 COE: $132,004 (Cat A) and $131,901 (Cat B), per LTA.
  • Singapore's heat, jams and short trips are classic high-wear conditions.
  • A worn engine costs you twice: higher running costs now, and a weaker case for COE renewal later.
  • Adding XADO at your normal oil change is a low-cost layer of protection for a six-figure asset.

The maths every Singapore car owner knows

With COE quota premiums at around $132,000, you are effectively paying more than $13,000 a year just for the right to drive. Whether you keep the car for 10 years, renew for 5 or 10 more, or sell early, the engine's health directly affects how much value you get back.

An engine that starts burning oil at year six, fails emissions at inspection, or needs a top overhaul eats into that investment fast — and makes renewing your COE a much harder decision.

Why Singapore is tough on engines

  • Stop-and-go traffic. Idling and crawling on the CTE, PIE and AYE means low airflow, high temperatures and thin oil films.
  • Short trips. School runs, market trips and HDB car park hops mean the engine rarely reaches full temperature — cold-start wear is highest.
  • Heat and humidity. 30°C+ ambient temperatures push oil and cooling systems harder all year round.
  • Small turbo engines. Many modern Cat A and Cat B cars use downsized turbo engines with high specific power — more heat in a smaller package.

Where XADO fits in your maintenance

XADO doesn't replace regular servicing — it adds protection to it. At your normal oil change, your workshop adds the right XADO treatment (or fills up with XADO oil containing revitalizant). As you drive, the treatment works on the friction surfaces that matter most for compression, power and oil consumption.

Ask a workshop

Thinking of renewing your COE?

Before you pay the Prevailing Quota Premium to keep your car another 5 or 10 years, ask a workshop to check compression, oil consumption and engine noise. If the engine is mechanically sound but showing wear, a XADO treatment plan alongside proper servicing is a cost-effective way to support the next chapter of the car's life.

FAQs

Why does COE make engine protection more important?

With Cat A and Cat B COE above $130,000 in October 2026, most of what you pay for a car is the right to drive it for 10 years. Keeping the engine healthy for the full period — or longer if you renew — protects that investment.

When should I start using XADO?

XADO makes a 'New Car' formula for engines under 20,000 km to protect them from the break-in period onwards. For older cars, the Maximum or Twin Turbo conditioners are designed to restore worn surfaces.

Is XADO worth it if I plan to renew my COE?

If you plan to keep your car past 10 years, engine condition decides whether renewal makes sense. Ask a XADO workshop to check compression and oil consumption, then recommend a treatment plan.
