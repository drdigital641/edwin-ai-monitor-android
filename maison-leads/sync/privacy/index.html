Privacy policy

How Maison Interior Design handles the details you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you send the enquiry form we receive your name, your mobile number, the project or interest you typed, the page you sent it from and the time. When you tap a WhatsApp or call button we count the tap (page, time and device type) without your name or number.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry about interior design and renovation, usually by WhatsApp, and to see which pages help visitors find what they need.

Who sees it

Maison Interior Design and the team that runs this website for us, only to handle your enquiry. We do not sell or rent your details.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it to answer your enquiry and follow up on your project.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or tell us to stop contacting you, at any time: WhatsApp +65 9339 1185 or email maisoninteriordesignsg@gmail.com.
