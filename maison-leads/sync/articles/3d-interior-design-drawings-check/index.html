3D interior design drawings: what to check before you approve

17 November 2026·3 min read·Maison Interior Design

3D interior design drawings show each room as it will look before any work starts, so you can change a cabinet, a tile or a colour on screen instead of on site. They only help if you know what to look for. This note sets out what to check in a set of perspective 3D drawings, room by room, and which questions to ask before you approve the design.

In this article

  1. What 3D drawings are for
  2. What to check in each view
  3. How to review the drawings step by step
  4. What 3D drawings cannot show
  5. How Maison does it

01What 3D drawings are for

Perspective 3D drawings turn a floor plan into views you can read at a glance. Instead of imagining a layout from lines on paper, you see where the cabinets, walls and furniture sit and how the colours work together. This is the easiest time to change your mind, because nothing has been built yet.

02What to check in each view

  • Layout: do the doors, walkways and furniture match your floor plan, and is there enough room to move around?
  • Carpentry: is every cabinet, feature wall and built-in you discussed drawn, with the right position and height?
  • Finishes: are the materials and colours the ones you chose, and do they match the colour proposal?
  • Lighting and electrical points: are the lights, switches and sockets where you will use them?
  • Things you will actually use: shoes, laundry, appliances and storage should each have a place in the drawing.

03How to review the drawings step by step

Sit down with the floor plan, the colour proposal and the drawings together, and go through one room at a time.

  1. 01

    Start with the floor plan

    Confirm the layout first. If a wall or door is in the wrong place, every view of that room will be wrong too.

  2. 02

    Walk through each room

    Follow your daily routine in the drawing: coming home, cooking, working, sleeping. Note anything that feels tight or missing.

  3. 03

    Compare the finishes

    Check each material and colour against the colour proposal, so what you approve is what is written down.

  4. 04

    Write down your changes

    List every change you want, room by room, so nothing is lost between a conversation and the revised drawing.

  5. 05

    Approve the final set

    Once the drawings are right, they become the reference for the quotation and for the build team on site.

04What 3D drawings cannot show

A drawing is a picture, not the finished room. Real materials can look a little different under your own lighting, so ask to see samples of key finishes. The drawing also cannot replace a measured site check, which is why the layout should be confirmed against your actual floor plan before you approve.

Good to know

Quick answers

What are 3D interior design drawings?

Perspective views of each room, drawn before any work starts, that show the layout, carpentry, finishes and colours as they will look. They help you decide on the design without building anything first.

Can I ask for changes after I see the 3D drawings?

Yes. At Maison you can ask for revisions to the drawings before the quotation is final, so changes are agreed on screen rather than on site.

What should I check first in a 3D drawing?

Start with the layout against your floor plan, then the carpentry, finishes, and the lighting and socket positions. A wrong layout affects every other view of the room.

Do I still need to see material samples?

It is a good idea. A drawing shows the look, but real materials can appear different in your own lighting, so ask to see samples of the key finishes.
