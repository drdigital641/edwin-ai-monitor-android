Good to know

About these guides

What is the difference between TOP and key collection?

TOP (Temporary Occupation Permit) means the building can be occupied. Key collection comes after, on a date your developer or HDB gives you. We never treat a TOP date as your key date.

When should I contact an interior designer?

One to two months before keys for a BTO or condo, and before you sign the lease for an office or shop. That leaves time for design, quotation and approvals so work starts on day one.

Are you the appointed interior designer for these projects?

No. Maison is an independent firm, not appointed by or affiliated with any developer, HDB or building owner. We write these guides to help new owners and tenants plan.

Where do the project facts come from?

Only from public sources linked on each page, such as HDB, the developer's own site or published reports. If we could not confirm a fact, we left it out or marked it as reported.
