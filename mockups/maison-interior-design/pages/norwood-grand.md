## FAQ and detailed planning notes

### Which unit or plate do these concepts use?
2-bedroom Type B1, 58 sqm / 624 sq ft, PDF p.16 in the [developer/landlord source](https://www.cdlhomes.com.sg/norwoodgrand/brochure.pdf). The selected scheme does not represent all project types. Confirm your exact stack, floor and any mirrored version.

### What layout facts were used?
- living-dining: Central living/dining with balcony directly beyond living, bedroom2 left and master right. Sofa/TV on opposite side walls as suggested plan; ceiling2.70m, living fan and porcelain floor specified.
- kitchen: Open kitchen at entry adjacent foyer; compact sink/hob run and fridge/washer-dryer provision near entry. B1 uses induction hob, laminate/melamine cabinets, quartz top and porcelain kitchen tiles; ceiling2.40m.
- master-bedroom: Master on plan-right, entry toward dining, bathroom above, window on outer/bottom wall above RC ledge. Vinyl bedroom floor, melamine built-in wardrobe;2.70m ceiling.
- study: Bedroom2 on plan-left converted with movable desk; top entry and wardrobe, bottom window above A/C ledge. No ensuite added; supplied bedroom vinyl/wardrobe retained conceptually.

### Which finishes are supplied, and which are proposals?
Official specifications give porcelain living/kitchen tiles, vinyl bedrooms, laminate/melamine kitchen cabinets with quartz tops, melamine wardrobes, B1 induction appliances,2.70m living/bedroom and2.40m kitchen ceilings. CDL names Bosch/Samsung appliances and Geberit/Hansgrohe bathroom fittings. Exact colours, models, cabinet internals and as-built locations need handover verification. [Finishes/developer source](https://www.cdlhomes.com.sg/norwoodgrand/floorplans/specifications.pdf). Maison-inspired colours, furniture, lighting and joinery treatments do not prove supplied finishes or a previous Maison commission.
