# CanningHill Piers Interior Design & Renovation Ideas

The four theme pictures on this page are AI-generated style concepts by Maison for a CanningHill Piers renovation. They are not photos of an actual unit, not completed Maison projects and not surveyed layouts; layouts, fittings and views are illustrative. Each look leans on built-in carpentry, the kind of River Valley condo renovation Maison plans for homes between Clarke Quay and Fort Canning. The floor plans are copied from the official [CanningHill Piers e-brochure](https://canninghillpiers.com.sg/floorplans/CanningHill-Piers-e-brochure.pdf) (crop and scale only). Plans are subject to change, so confirm your own unit plan, stack and any mirroring before adapting an idea.

## FAQ and detailed planning notes

### When did CanningHill Piers get TOP?
CDL lists TOP obtained in October 2026; the expected vacant possession date of 30 June 2027 is a separate contractual date. Key dates are per buyer. [Source: CDL construction page](https://www.cdlhomes.com.sg/construction/).

### Which unit are the concepts based on?
Type B1(S) 2-bedroom + ensuite study, 77 sq m / 829 sq ft (e-brochure p31). They are style concepts, not your actual unit.

### What does the developer supply?
CDL names Ernestomeda kitchen systems, Miele and De Dietrich kitchen appliances, and Gessi and Laufen bathroom fittings. Check your own handover list. [Source: CDL news release](https://www.cdl.com.sg/newsroom/capitaland-and-cdl-to-launch-canninghill-piers-tallest-residential-development-along-the-singapore-river-sales-previews-commence-from-friday-5-november-2021).

### Where can I get the full floor plan?
The official [e-brochure](https://canninghillpiers.com.sg/floorplans/CanningHill-Piers-e-brochure.pdf) and [storey key plans](https://canninghillpiers.com.sg/floorplans/storey-key-plans.pdf) on canninghillpiers.com.sg; use your sales documents for your exact unit.

### Can I renovate right after keys?
After defects inspection and management approval; condo rules differ from HDB rules.
