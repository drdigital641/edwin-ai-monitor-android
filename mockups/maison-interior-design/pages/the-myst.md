## FAQ and detailed planning notes

### When did The Myst get TOP?
CDL lists TOP obtained in September 2026; expected vacant possession date is 31 March 2029. Key dates are per buyer. [Source: CDL construction page](https://www.cdlhomes.com.sg/construction/).

### Which unit are the concepts based on?
Type B1 2-bedroom, 63 sq m / 678 sq ft (brochure p8). They are style concepts, not your actual unit.

### What does CDL supply in the kitchen and bathrooms?
CDL names Bosch and SMEG kitchen appliances and Hansgrohe, Geberit and Kohler bathroom brands. [Source: CDL news release](https://www.cdl.com.sg/assets/en-newsroom-press-release-pdf/CDL-News-Release-The-Myst-Preview-web.pdf).

### Where can I get the full floor plan?
The public simplified brochure (pages 8-10) shows six types and their variants; use your own sales documents for your exact unit.

### Can I renovate right after keys?
After defects inspection and management approval; condo rules differ from HDB rules.
