## FAQ and detailed planning notes

### Which unit or plate do these concepts use?
Typical 2-bedroom Type B1 (not B1(d)), 63 sqm / 678 sq ft, PDF p.8 in the [developer/landlord source](https://www.cdlhomes.com.sg/themyst/assets/pdf/The%20Myst%20Simplified%20Brochure_Final.pdf). The selected scheme does not represent all project types. Confirm your exact stack, floor and any mirrored version.

### What layout facts were used?
- living-dining: Central living/dining ends at living-width balcony; bedroom2 flanks left and master right. Kitchen at entry-left behind dining; selected typical B1 not double-volume variant.
- kitchen: Open L kitchen at entry-left, hob side return and sink run toward dining; entry corridor right. Bosch/SMEG appliances named by CDL; exact models/cabinet colour not verified.
- master-bedroom: Master plan-right; window on bottom exterior wall above RC ledge; bath/entry toward top. Bed headboard along right wall; no private bedroom balcony.
- study: Bedroom2 plan-left with bottom window above A/C ledge and access from dining; study is proposed reuse not separate unit study. No exterior side window or balcony access assumed.

### Which finishes are supplied, and which are proposals?
CDL names Bosch and SMEG kitchen appliances and Hansgrohe, Geberit and Kohler bathroom products. Exact flooring materials, finishes/colours, individual room sizes and ceiling height were not established in the reviewed simplified brochure. Any timber-look floor, colour and light fitting shown is a design proposal, not a developer-supply assertion. ### Are the diagrams and renders exact or dimensioned plans?
No. The diagram is an original room-block schematic, not a trace. Individual room dimensions are not stated, so none are invented. Door/window symbols are approximate. The AI perspectives interpret selected anchors; door geometry, joinery details and apparent room sizes may differ. Use the approved plan and measured site survey for construction. Compass facing and real views are not implied.

TOP obtained September 2026. Individual key/access appointments remain unconfirmed. Building TOP does not authorise tenant/owner renovation. [Timing source](https://www.cdlhomes.com.sg/construction/).

### What must happen before move-in or occupation?
Inspect and log defects; agree rectification and access; survey and freeze scope; obtain management/landlord requirements and work-specific authority approvals; procure, build, test and inspect; confirm completion/occupation requirements and moving arrangements. Condo/EC and office rules differ from HDB residential rules. No exact approval lead time, deposit, permitted hours or move-in date is verified.
