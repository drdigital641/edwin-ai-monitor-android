Services · New condo renovation

New condo renovation, lighter work, sharper detail

A new condo or executive condominium usually comes with floors, kitchen and bathrooms already finished, so the work is lighter than a BTO. The detail is in what you add: storage, lighting and furniture that fit the layout. The rules come from the condo's management, not HDB, and the developer's defects period is your best chance to get things fixed for free.

What new condo owners usually change

  • Wardrobe internals, extra storage in the bedrooms and a full-height shoe and utility cabinet at the entrance.
  • Lighting: cove lights, track lights or pendant points to replace the basic ceiling lights.
  • Feature walls and a TV console sized to the living room, with hidden cabling.
  • Window treatments: curtains, blinds or sheers sized to the full window.
  • Balcony: flooring tiles or decking, and a balcony screen only if it follows the design the condo management allows.
  • Owners renting out a unit often keep it simple: durable finishes, good storage and furniture that suits tenants.

Defects check at handover

  • Developers' sale and purchase agreements usually include a defects liability period of 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours for the exact terms.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new defects are not blamed on your renovation.
  • Check the floors for hollow tiles, scratches and gaps in the timber or vinyl, and the walls for cracks and paint stains.
  • Test kitchen appliances, the hob and hood, every tap, the water heater, the floor traps and the air-conditioning.
  • Check doors, locks, windows, the balcony glass, and the sealant around the kitchen and bathrooms.
  • List everything with photos and submit it through the developer's defects process. Keep a copy.

What condo rules allow

  • Renovations are approved by the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST once it is formed). Expect an application form, a deposit, contractor registration and lift protection.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are often shorter than HDB's, with no works on Sundays and public holidays. Check your condo's rules before booking work.
  • Anything that changes the building's look from outside, such as balcony screens, window grilles or the main door, usually needs the management's approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural work, such as removing walls, needs a professional engineer and building approvals. Most new-condo work avoids it.
  • Executive condominiums follow the condo's rules, not HDB's renovation permits.

A realistic timeline

  1. 01

    Before keys 2 to 6 weeks

    Measurements from the floor plan, 3D drawings, quotation and the condo renovation application.

  2. 02

    Keys + defects 1 to 2 weeks

    Defects inspection and report, site measurements, MCST approval.

  3. 03

    Carpentry and electrical 3 to 4 weeks

    Built in the factory while lighting and power points are done on site.

  4. 04

    Install and finish 1 to 2 weeks

    Carpentry installation, curtains, lights and cleaning.

Most new condo renovations take about 4 to 8 weeks from keys to move-in, because there are few wet works. Condo hours and lift bookings can stretch this during busy handover months.

Good to know

Questions owners ask

How long does a new condo renovation take?

Usually 4 to 8 weeks from keys to move-in, since the floors, kitchen and bathrooms are already done. It can be longer if many owners are renovating at once and the lift is busy.

Do I need HDB approval for an EC renovation?

No. An executive condominium is managed like a condo, so renovation approval comes from its management, not HDB.

Can I add a balcony screen?

Usually only in the design the condo management has approved. Ask the management office for the approved design before ordering one.

What does the developer fix for free?

Defects covered by your sale and purchase agreement during the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Report them before renovation starts.
