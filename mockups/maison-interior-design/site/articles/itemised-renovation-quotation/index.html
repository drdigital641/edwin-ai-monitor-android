Reading an itemised renovation quotation: what each line should tell you

15 November 2026·3 min read·Maison Interior Design

An itemised renovation quotation breaks the job into lines you can read, instead of one lump sum. That makes it easier to see what is included, compare one designer with another, and agree changes before work starts. This note sets out what a good quotation should show, how to read it room by room, and what to ask about before you sign.

In this article

  1. Why itemised matters
  2. What a good quotation should show
  3. How to read it room by room
  4. How Maison does it
  5. Questions to ask any designer

01Why itemised matters

A single total tells you nothing about what is in it. An itemised quotation lists the work in separate lines, so you can ask about each one, remove or change a line, and see exactly what a comparison with another designer is comparing.

02What a good quotation should show

  • Work grouped by area or trade: hacking and alterations, flooring and tiling, carpentry, electrical, painting, and furnishing or decor where included.
  • A clear description for each line: what is being built or done, and where in the home.
  • Quantity and unit where they apply, such as metres of cabinetry or the number of points, so lines can be checked against the plan.
  • What is included and what is not, written down, so nothing is assumed.
  • The drawings it refers to, so each carpentry line matches something you can see.

03How to read it room by room

Take the floor plan and the 3D drawings next to the quotation and go one room at a time.

  1. 01

    Match lines to drawings

    Every cabinet, feature wall or built-in in the drawings should have a line, and every line should be something you can find in the drawings.

  2. 02

    Check the finishes

    Materials, colours and finishes should be named in the quotation, matching the colour proposal.

  3. 03

    Look for gaps

    Ask about anything you expected but cannot find, such as electrical points, lighting, or clearing and cleaning at the end.

  4. 04

    Note the applications

    For HDB flats, ask who applies for the HDB renovation permit and who handles the renovation loan paperwork.

  5. 05

    Ask about changes

    Ask how a change after signing is recorded, so any new line is written down and agreed before it is done.

05Questions to ask any designer

  • Is every line described, so I can see what it covers?
  • Do the lines match the 3D drawings room by room?
  • What is not included in this quotation?
  • Who manages the site, and how will I get updates?
  • How are changes after signing written down and agreed?

Good to know

Quick answers

What is an itemised renovation quotation?

A quotation that lists the renovation work in separate lines, each described and grouped by area or trade, instead of one lump sum. It lets you see what is included and ask about each line.

How do I compare two renovation quotations?

Put both next to the floor plan and go room by room. Compare what each line covers, the materials named, and what is listed as not included. Two totals only compare fairly when the lines cover the same work.

Does Maison give an itemised quotation?

Yes. After the concept consultation and layout proposal you receive an itemised quotation with perspective 3D drawings and a colour proposal, and you can ask for revisions before it is final.

Who applies for the HDB renovation permit?
