Bedok Reservoir Blk 715

A whole-home design and build at Bedok Reservoir Road: one calm palette of stone, oak and charcoal, lit so the textures do the talking.

Type
HDB home
Scope
Design & build
Status
Completed
01

A rock wall to dine by

The dining area is anchored by a full-height rock-textured feature wall, washed from above by a hidden light line. A veined stone island and table sit in front, softened by tan leather chairs.

02

Oak, stone and soft light

Oak-tone panelling wraps the living room and hides the doors. The TV sits on a stone-look panel framed by a warm glow, over a floating console, with herringbone flooring running through the home.

03

Light where you need it

Strip lights sit under every wall cabinet and inside each niche, so the kitchen, wardrobe and bathrooms read as quiet, evenly lit spaces rather than bright boxes.
