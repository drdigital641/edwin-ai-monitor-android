Four The Myst interior design themes

Four rooms, four looks, each built around bespoke carpentry. We adapt every idea to your actual unit.

All four are styled on Type B1 typical 2-bedroom, 63 sq m / 678 sq ft (CDL simplified brochure p8; standard ceiling, not the B1(d) 4.5m-void variant).

Misty Forest Modern · Living room

The Myst living room interior design — Misty Forest Modern

A fluted pale-oak feature wall, a floating moss-green stone TV console, a built-in window bench and soft cove lighting bring the green outlook indoors. A calm starting point for your The Myst renovation.

Champagne Marble Deco · Kitchen (close-up)

Upper Bukit Timah condo kitchen design idea — Champagne Marble Deco

Handleless champagne lacquer cabinets with brass edges, a calacatta-look sintered-stone counter and backsplash, and fluted bronze-glass wall cabinets give the kitchen a light, polished finish. Adapt the finishes and storage to your actual unit.

Mocha Limewash Contemporary · Main bedroom

The Myst main bedroom design — Mocha Limewash Contemporary

A full-width limewash headboard wall with backlit floating shelves, a built-in platform bed and a ribbed walnut full-height wardrobe keep the main bedroom warm, quiet and uncluttered.

The Myst common bedroom renovation idea — Navy Oak Heritage

The Myst project facts

  • 408 units in two 24-storey towers at 800 and 802 Upper Bukit Timah Road. Developer: CDL Aries Pte. Ltd., wholly owned by CDL. Land area approx. 179,008 sq ft. CDL news release
  • 99-year lease from 11 May 2023. TOP obtained September 2026; expected vacant possession 31 March 2029. CDL construction page
  • CDL describes a 5-minute walk to Cashew MRT (Downtown Line) and 75% of the footprint as landscaping and communal facilities. CDL news release

Maison is an independent interior design firm, not affiliated with CDL. Key dates are set per buyer by CDL.

About these concepts and plans

The four theme pictures on this page are AI-generated style concepts by Maison for a The Myst renovation. They are not photos of an actual unit, not completed Maison projects and not surveyed layouts; layouts, fittings and views are illustrative. Each look leans on built-in carpentry, the kind of Upper Bukit Timah condo renovation Maison plans for homes near Cashew. The floor plans are the original pages 8 to 10 of The Myst simplified brochure by CDL, scaled only. They are subject to change and final survey, so confirm your own unit plan, stack and any mirroring before adapting an idea.

Layouts and renovation ideas

1-bed + study (A1Sa)

  • Build the study as a slim desk-and-shelf wall; keep the living open.

2-bed (B1) / 2-bed + study (B3S)

  • Full-height feature wall with hidden storage; bedroom 2 doubles as a study with a built-in desk.

3-bed (C1)

  • Plan storage room by room; built-in TV console and window benches free floor space.

4/5-bed (D2, E1)

  • Separate dry/wet kitchen zones (per plan) suit stone counters and a display wall; (d) units’ 4.5m void suits full-height fluted or veneer feature walls.

Ideas only; check walls, services and management rules before work.

Views tips

CDL describes “picturesque views of its lush surroundings”, but no unit’s view is guaranteed. Check your stack, floor and facing on the brochure key plan, then plan blinds, glare control and window benches around your actual outlook.

Details, caveats and sources

When did The Myst get TOP?

CDL lists TOP obtained in September 2026; expected vacant possession date is 31 March 2029. Key dates are per buyer. Source: CDL construction page.

Which unit are the concepts based on?

Type B1 2-bedroom, 63 sq m / 678 sq ft (brochure p8). They are style concepts, not your actual unit.

What does CDL supply in the kitchen and bathrooms?

CDL names Bosch and SMEG kitchen appliances and Hansgrohe, Geberit and Kohler bathroom brands. Source: CDL news release.

Where can I get the full floor plan?

The public simplified brochure (pages 8-10) shows six types and their variants; use your own sales documents for your exact unit.

Can I renovate right after keys?

After defects inspection and management approval; condo rules differ from HDB rules.
