The Myst renovation guide

The Myst is a 408-unit CDL condominium on Upper Bukit Timah Road, with homes from a 1-bedroom plus study of about 517 sq ft to a 5-bedroom of about 1,690 sq ft. That range means very different renovations under one roof.

What to know about The Myst

A 517 sq ft 1-bedroom plus study and a 1,690 sq ft 5-bedroom need different plans. In the smaller units the study often becomes a guest room or home office with a wall bed and hidden storage. In the larger units the focus moves to family storage, a proper study and lighting that makes big rooms feel warm.

The Myst sits in a green part of Upper Bukit Timah, and owners often want interiors that bring that calm indoors: natural timber tones, stone-look surfaces and soft lighting. We keep the developer's good finishes and spend the budget where you will feel it every day.

What owners usually change

  • For 1- and 2-bedroom units: a study that works as a home office or guest room, and storage built into every wall.
  • For 3- to 5-bedroom units: full-height wardrobes, a family study and an entrance utility cabinet.
  • Warm lighting in place of the basic ceiling lights.
  • Timber and stone-look finishes for the TV wall and feature areas.
  • Curtains or blinds sized to the full window.

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.

Rules change, so we check the current version for your unit before we quote. Full new condo renovation guide

A realistic timeline

  1. 01

    Before keys 2 to 6 weeks

    Measurements from the floor plan, 3D drawings, quotation and the condo renovation application.

  2. 02

    Keys + defects 1 to 2 weeks

    Defects inspection and report, site measurements, MCST approval.

  3. 03

    Carpentry and electrical 3 to 4 weeks

    Built in the factory while lighting and power points are done on site.

  4. 04

    Install and finish 1 to 2 weeks

    Carpentry installation, curtains, lights and cleaning.

Most new condo renovations take about 4 to 8 weeks from keys to move-in. Smaller units at The Myst can be quicker; larger units with more carpentry take longer.

Questions owners ask

When is TOP for The Myst?

We could not confirm a TOP date on an official page. CDL's construction page that we read showed an indicative TOP of Q1 2027. Your developer's notice is the date that counts.

How do I make a 1-bedroom plus study at The Myst work harder?

Treat the study as a flexible room: a wall bed or daybed, a fold-down desk and full-height storage let it work as an office, a guest room or a nursery.

Who approves renovations at The Myst?

The condo's management, under its by-laws. HDB permits do not apply to a private condo.
