Planning your Norwood Grand renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.

Norwood Grand renovation FAQ

Which unit types do the floor plans cover?

Pages 16 to 21 of the CDL brochure show every type from 1-Bedroom + Study (A1S, 46 sqm / 495 sq ft) to 4-Bedroom Premium + Study (D3PS, 124 sqm / 1,335 sq ft), with the (P) variants on the same pages. The brochure notes that areas include the balcony and PES where applicable and that plans are subject to final survey. Confirm your exact stack, floor and any mirrored version.

Which finishes are supplied, and which are proposals?

Official specifications give porcelain living/kitchen tiles, vinyl bedrooms, laminate/melamine kitchen cabinets with quartz tops, melamine wardrobes, B1 induction appliances, 2.70m living/bedroom and 2.40m kitchen ceilings. CDL names Bosch/Samsung appliances and Geberit/Hansgrohe bathroom fittings. Exact colours, models, cabinet internals and as-built locations need handover verification. Finishes/developer source. Maison-inspired colours, furniture, lighting and joinery treatments do not prove supplied finishes or a previous Maison commission.

Are the pictures exact reconstructions?

No. They are AI-generated style concepts, not photos of a Norwood Grand unit and not completed Maison projects. Room shapes, fittings, furniture and window views are illustrative. Use your approved plan and a measured site survey for construction. Compass facing and real views are not implied.

What is the confirmed timing?

TOP obtained August 2026. Individual key/access appointments remain unconfirmed. Building TOP does not authorise tenant/owner renovation. Timing source.

What must happen before move-in or occupation?

Inspect and log defects; agree rectification and access; survey and freeze scope; obtain management/landlord requirements and work-specific authority approvals; procure, build, test and inspect; confirm completion/occupation requirements and moving arrangements. Condo/EC and office rules differ from HDB residential rules. No exact approval lead time, deposit, permitted hours or move-in date is verified. A moderate home scope may use 6–10 on-site weeks, or modest office 8–12 weeks, only as an editorial planning allowance after access/approvals—not a guarantee.

What other project details should I check?

Project-wide facilities, amenities and the original unit mix do not establish your own view or unit details. Ask for your approved owner documents before committing to works.
