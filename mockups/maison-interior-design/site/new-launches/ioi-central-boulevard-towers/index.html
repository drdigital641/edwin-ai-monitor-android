Planning your IOI Central Boulevard Towers interior

This page is for office fit-out, not for home renovation. In a CBD tower that is already completed, the usual brief is a reception or pantry joinery run, workstation storage, a meeting-room wall, and a pantry or copy-room cabinet line, designed to the approved floor plate and the landlord’s fit-out manual. Maison specifies built-in carpentry for those interiors, not loose, flat-pack furniture, and we can work in a wide floor view or in closer studies of a reception desk or a pantry run. We have no official floor plate on file and will not invent a typical tower typologies mix or a headcount.

Keppel South Central, Newport Tower and 39 Robinson Road are nearby office or central links; Shaw Tower, already live, is another. They are not a typologies reference for this building. We will not invent walking times to Raffles Place or to the waterfront, and we will not treat 2026 leasing news as a construction milestone.

After access is agreed, record handover conditions, freeze the layout, obtain landlord consent and any fire-safety or workplace approvals that the scope requires, then fabricate. A moderate office joinery scope is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after approvals; that is an editorial allowance, not a guarantee, and a multi-floor workplace can take longer. Planning your IOI Central Boulevard Towers fit-out

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the space against it.
  • Check ceilings, raised floors, air-conditioning outlets, sprinkler heads, power and data provisions.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before your contractor starts.
  • Agree how and when landlord defects will be fixed so they do not hold up the fit-out.

Rules and approvals

  • The building's fit-out guidelines: approved contractors, insurance, deposits, hours and drawing submissions.
  • Changes to sprinklers, fire alarms or escape routes usually need a qualified person and SCDF approval.
  • Structural changes need BCA approval. If your use differs from the approved use, check URA's change-of-use rules.
