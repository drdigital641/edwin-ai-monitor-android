Planning your Chong Pang City integrated development interior

This page is for future shop, supermarket and F&B fit-out, not for home renovation. Until a tenancy plan and a permitted trade are in your hands, typologies and shop sizes stay blank. When they are, the usual fit-out brief is built-in: a shopfront and signage line that the landlord will accept, a counter, display and storage joinery, and a back-of-house run. Food and wet trades will need a different services design and should not be copied from a dry-shop layout. Maison specifies custom carpentry for those interiors, not loose, flat-pack fixtures, and we will not invent a typical Chong Pang shop plan because no official tenancy drawing is on file.

Yishun Beacon and Chencharu Acres are nearby Yishun housing pages and are not a commercial typologies reference. We will not invent walking times to Chong Pang or to Yishun town, and we will not treat a 2027 targeted completion as an opening day or as a key date for any home.

Operators who secure a unit should work backwards from the actual handover: landlord fit-out rules, trade licences, fire-safety classification where it applies, then fabrication. A modest non-food shop fit-out is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after access and approvals; that is an editorial allowance, not a guarantee. WhatsApp Maison with your tenancy plan and permitted trade when you have them. We are not affiliated with SLA.

Planning your Chong Pang City integrated development fit-out

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the unit against it.
  • Check the shopfront, floor, ceiling, power supply, water and drainage points, and any exhaust provision.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before work starts.
  • Agree how landlord defects will be fixed so they do not delay opening.

Rules and approvals

  • The landlord's fit-out guidelines: contractors, hours, deposits, signage and drawing submissions.
  • Food businesses need the right SFA licence; plan the kitchen with it in mind.
  • Fire safety changes need a qualified person and SCDF approval; structural changes need BCA approval.
