What to know about Pollen Collection

A new landed home is not like a new condo. There are more floors to furnish, more stairs and landings to light, and outdoor spaces such as a garden, a car porch or a roof terrace that need finishes able to take Singapore's sun and rain.

In a landed development with shared facilities, the management usually controls anything that changes the outside of the house, such as the gate, facade or roof. Inside, you have more freedom. Our builder team mainly builds landed houses, so we plan the interior with how the whole house works.

What owners usually change

  • Built-in storage on every floor, including under the stairs.
  • A family room or study upstairs, separate from the formal living room.
  • Lighting for the staircase, landings and outdoor areas.
  • Weather-resistant finishes for the porch, garden and any roof terrace.
  • Air-conditioning and ceiling fans planned floor by floor.

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period. Check it before work starts.
  • Walk every floor, the roof and outdoor areas. Look for water stains, cracks, hollow tiles and blocked drains.
  • Test the gate, doors, windows, sanitary fittings, water heaters and every electrical point.
  • Submit defects with photos to the developer and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Interior work usually needs no planning approval, but structural changes and extra floor area usually need plans by a qualified person (URA and BCA).
  • In a landed development with shared facilities, the management controls changes to the facade, gates and roof.
  • Protect shared walls, drains and neighbours' property, and agree access before work starts.

Rules change, so we check the current version for your unit before we quote. Full landed renovation guide

A realistic timeline

  1. 01

    Design and survey 4 to 8 weeks

    Site survey, layout, 3D drawings and an itemised quotation.

  2. 02

    Approvals (if needed) 4 to 12 weeks

    Plan submissions by the qualified person for additions and alterations.

  3. 03

    Building and wet works 6 to 16 weeks

    Structure, roofing, waterproofing, wiring and plumbing.

  4. 04

    Interior and finishing 6 to 10 weeks

    Carpentry, kitchen, bathrooms, painting and handover.

An interior renovation of a new landed home usually takes about 3 to 4 months. Anything that changes the outside needs approval first and takes longer.

Questions owners ask

Can I change the outside of my Pollen Collection home?

Usually only with the management's approval and within its rules, because the look of the development is shared. Inside, most changes are yours to make.

How long does a new landed home renovation take?

About 3 to 4 months for interior work. Work that changes the outside takes longer because it needs approval first.
