Planning your Newport Plaza retail podium interior

Because this is a city-fringe podium, the useful conversation with Maison is a shop or F&B fit-out, not a home renovation. Incoming tenants typically need a shopfront and signage package the landlord will accept, fitted joinery for display, a counter, and a compact back-of-house, designed to the approved tenancy plan rather than to a guessed bay size. Food and beverage trades at level 1 will need coordinated extraction, grease, water and electrical capacity, and should not be designed from a dry-retail layout. Maison specifies built-in carpentry for those interiors, not loose, flat-pack fixtures. No official tenancy floor plan is on file.

Newport Residences, packed earlier, and Newport Tower, in this batch, are the correct cross-links. 39 Robinson Road and Shaw Tower are nearby central links only. We will not invent walking times to Tanjong Pagar or to any station, and we will not assign a view or a frontage to a unit we have not seen.

Once a lease and an indicative handover are in writing, work backwards: defects and landlord inspection, a measured survey, landlord and trade approvals, then fabrication. A modest non-food shop fit-out is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after approvals; food trades can take longer. That is an editorial allowance, not a guarantee. Planning your Newport Plaza retail podium fit-out

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the unit against it.
  • Check the shopfront, floor, ceiling, power supply, water and drainage points, and any exhaust provision.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before work starts.
  • Agree how landlord defects will be fixed so they do not delay opening.

Rules and approvals

  • The landlord's fit-out guidelines: contractors, hours, deposits, signage and drawing submissions.
  • Food businesses need the right SFA licence; plan the kitchen with it in mind.
  • Fire safety changes need a qualified person and SCDF approval; structural changes need BCA approval.
