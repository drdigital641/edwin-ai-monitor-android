Planning your Lentor Modern mall interior

Because the mall is already open, the useful conversation with Maison is a shop, services or F&B fit-out, not a home renovation. A new or incoming tenant typically needs a shopfront and signage that the landlord will accept, fitted joinery for display and storage, a counter or checkout run, and a back-of-house cabinet line that can take stock, bags and cleaning. Food trades have a different services brief — extraction, grease, water and electrical load — and should not be designed from a generic lifestyle-shop layout. Maison specifies built-in carpentry for those interiors, not loose, flat-pack fixtures, and we will work from your tenancy plan, permitted trade and the landlord’s fit-out manual, not from a guessed shop size. No official tenancy floor plan is on file for this pack.

Lentor Gardens Residences, packed earlier, is a nearby residential page in District 26 and is not a typologies or lease reference for a mall unit. We will not invent walking times beyond the file’s note that the mall is integrated with Lentor MRT, and we will not imply that every unit has the same frontage or the same services.

After a lease is in hand, the sober order is: record handover defects, freeze a layout against the approved tenancy plan, obtain landlord consent and any trade-specific or fire-safety approvals, then fabricate. A modest non-food shop fit-out is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after access and approvals; that is an editorial allowance, not a guarantee, and food or services-heavy work can take longer. WhatsApp Maison with your tenancy plan and permitted trade. We are not affiliated with GuocoLand or with the named anchors.

Planning your Lentor Modern mall fit-out

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the unit against it.
  • Check the shopfront, floor, ceiling, power supply, water and drainage points, and any exhaust provision.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before work starts.
  • Agree how landlord defects will be fixed so they do not delay opening.

Rules and approvals

  • The landlord's fit-out guidelines: contractors, hours, deposits, signage and drawing submissions.
  • Food businesses need the right SFA licence; plan the kitchen with it in mind.
  • Fire safety changes need a qualified person and SCDF approval; structural changes need BCA approval.
