Planning your The Botany at Dairy Farm interior

Dairy Farm Walk sits against a greener, hillier edge of the island. That is pleasant, and it also has practical design consequences: more reason to plan window treatments for weather and glare, to think about a bench or low cabinet under a living-room window, and not to assume every stack has the same outlook. We will not assign a view to your home without your key plan and a site visit. What we can prepare, once the approved floor plan is in hand, is the joinery that makes a new condo liveable: an entry shoe cabinet, a living-room storage wall, full-height wardrobes, and a fitted kitchen with a tall pantry. If a common bedroom will be a study, a built-in desk and wardrobe along one wall is usually cleaner than a loose table.

Maison is an interior designer. The carpentry is built in, detailed and photographed in the way a professional interior is finished, including closer shots of a wardrobe, a headboard wall or a kitchen counter when that is the decision you need to make. We do not use loose, flat-pack furniture as the main look, and we have no official floor-plan image on file to publish here.

Planning your The Botany at Dairy Farm renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.

Rules change, so we check the current version for your unit before we quote. Full new condo renovation guide
