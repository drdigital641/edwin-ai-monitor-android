Planning your 8@BT interior

A smaller Bukit Timah condominium is usually bought as a long-stay home rather than a short let, which is why the interior is worth specifying carefully. With fewer neighbours in the development, the design brief tends to be more personal: a living room that can take both quiet evenings and guests, bedrooms that are properly fitted, and a kitchen that does not look temporary. The work Maison would start with, once your approved plan is in hand, is built-in carpentry — an entry cabinet, a full living-room joinery wall, handleless kitchen cabinets with a tall pantry, and full-height wardrobes, including a headboard wall in the main bedroom if the plan allows it. We do not specify loose, flat-pack furniture as the main look. Close-up photographs of stone, veneer and door profiles are as legitimate as a wide corner-to-corner view.

Because no official floor plan is on file, we will not list typologies or invent a typical square footage. District 21 is a settled, green, low-rise residential belt; your actual outlook, traffic noise and afternoon sun depend on stack and floor, which only your key plan and a site visit can settle. Use those before you decide on blinds, a window seat or a desk under a window.

An expected TOP in late 2027 is enough time to design properly and not so far off that the decision can drift. The sequence after keys remains: defects inspection, measurement, management approval, then fabrication. A moderate, non-structural carpentry scope is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after access and approvals — an editorial figure, not a promise. We are not affiliated with Bukit Sembawang Estates. Send your plan and unit type when you have them.

Planning your 8@BT renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.

8@BT renovation FAQ

When is TOP or key collection at 8@BT?

Our project file records: 2027-Q4 expected TOP. Where the note says indicative or expected, treat it as a developer estimate, not an obtained TOP and not your personal key date. Check with the developer for your own appointment.

Do you have the 8@BT floor plan?

No official floor-plan or site image is on file for this page, so none is published here. Please send your approved unit plan.

Can I renovate right after keys?

After handover, inspect defects and obtain the management corporation’s renovation approval first. A moderate carpentry scope is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after access and approvals; that is an editorial allowance, not a guarantee.
