Planning your Keppel South Central interior

Because the building is already complete, the useful conversation with Maison is an office fit-out for a new or remaining tenant, not a home renovation and not a construction-progress story. Typical work, once a floor plate and the landlord’s manual are in hand, is a reception joinery wall, workstation storage, meeting-room carpentry, and a pantry run, specified as built-in work rather than as loose, flat-pack furniture. We can present that in a wide floor view or in closer shots of a reception or a pantry. No official floor plate is on file, so typologies and typical floor areas are left blank.

Newport Tower, IOI Central Boulevard Towers and 39 Robinson Road are nearby office links; The Island Residence and CanningHill Piers are nearby residential links only. We will not invent walking times to Tanjong Pagar MRT or to the city, and we will not treat a divestment date as permission to start works.

After a lease or access letter is in hand, record handover conditions, freeze the layout, obtain landlord consent and any fire-safety approvals the scope requires, then fabricate. A moderate office joinery scope is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after approvals — an editorial allowance, not a guarantee. WhatsApp Maison with your floor plate and permitted use. Planning your Keppel South Central fit-out

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the space against it.
  • Check ceilings, raised floors, air-conditioning outlets, sprinkler heads, power and data provisions.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before your contractor starts.
  • Agree how and when landlord defects will be fixed so they do not hold up the fit-out.

Rules and approvals

  • The building's fit-out guidelines: approved contractors, insurance, deposits, hours and drawing submissions.
  • Changes to sprinklers, fire alarms or escape routes usually need a qualified person and SCDF approval.
  • Structural changes need BCA approval. If your use differs from the approved use, check URA's change-of-use rules.
