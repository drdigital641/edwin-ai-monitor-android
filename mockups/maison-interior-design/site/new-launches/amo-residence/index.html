What to know about AMO Residence

Upgraders often bring furniture from their HDB flat, and condo rooms are shaped differently. Before buying anything new, measure what you are bringing and plan the built-ins around the pieces you want to keep.

Moving from HDB to a condo also changes the rules: renovation approval comes from the management, and the hours and deposits are set by the condo's by-laws. The developer's defects period is your chance to get things fixed before renovation starts.

What owners usually change

Common requests at AMO Residence:

  • Built-ins planned around furniture you are bringing from your current home.
  • Wardrobe internals and an entrance cabinet.
  • A study corner or home office.
  • Lighting upgrades in the living and dining areas.
  • Curtains and blinds for every window.

Planning your AMO Residence renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.

Most new condo renovations take about 4 to 8 weeks from keys to move-in.

AMO Residence renovation FAQ

When will AMO Residence be completed?

Stacked Homes reported an expected TOP in 2026. We could not confirm this on an official page, so check your developer's notice.

I'm moving from an HDB flat. What changes?

Approval comes from the condo's management, not HDB, and working hours are set by its by-laws. Check the developer's defects process too.

Should I keep my old furniture?

Keep the pieces that fit. Measure them against the new floor plan before designing the built-ins, so everything works together.
