Layouts and fit-out ideas

Ideas only; check the tenancy plan, landlord fit-out rules and approvals before work.

  • Side core keeps the window line free: run workstations and benches along the glazing, put meeting rooms and storage near the cores.
  • 3m ceiling and 150mm raised floor: use the height for cove lighting and acoustic baffles; route power through the floor to built-in desks.
  • Knock-out panels allow vertical links between floors (landlord feature); any opening needs landlord and authority approval.

Views tips

The landlord markets high-zone offices facing Marina Bay and facing the city. No view is guaranteed for any floor or suite: check your actual floor and side (the base plan marks the Beach Road and Nicoll Highway frontages and a north point), then plan glare control and screen positions around it.

Planning your Shaw Tower fit-out

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the space against it.
  • Check ceilings, raised floors, air-conditioning outlets, sprinkler heads, power and data provisions.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before your contractor starts.
  • Agree how and when landlord defects will be fixed so they do not hold up the fit-out.

Rules and approvals

  • The building's fit-out guidelines: approved contractors, insurance, deposits, hours and drawing submissions.
  • Changes to sprinklers, fire alarms or escape routes usually need a qualified person and SCDF approval.
  • Structural changes need BCA approval. If your use differs from the approved use, check URA's change-of-use rules.

Shaw Tower fit-out FAQ

When did Shaw Tower get TOP?

Lendlease announced on 6 July 2026 that Shaw Tower secured its Temporary Occupation Permit. Tenant handover dates are set per lease. (Lendlease)

How big are the office floors?

The landlord lists High Zone (L21-L31) at 19,000-20,000 sq ft and Low Zone (L7-L18) at 17,000-18,000 sq ft, with 3m floor-to-ceiling height. (Shaw Tower plans)

What are the concepts based on?

The landlord's published side-core floor plate and typical high-zone test fit. They are style concepts, not your actual office.

Where do I get my exact floor plan?

From the Shaw Tower leasing team; the public site shows base plans and an example test fit only.

Do I need approval for an office fit-out?

Yes, landlord consent, and SCDF plan approval where works affect fire safety such as escape routes. A qualified person classifies the works. (SCDF plan approval)
