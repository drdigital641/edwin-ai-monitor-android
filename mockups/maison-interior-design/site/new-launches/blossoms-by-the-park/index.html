Planning your Blossoms by the Park interior

District 5 has a mixed character: research and business parks, older west-coast housing, and newer apartments close to one-north. Owners here often need a home that can absorb weekday work as well as ordinary family life. That tends to mean a built-in desk or study wall in a common bedroom or a living alcove, a living-room joinery wall that hides screens and cables, and a kitchen planned for weekday cooking rather than a show-flat overlay of loose appliances. Maison will design those pieces as built-in carpentry, not as IKEA-style furniture, and we will not assume a wall can be moved until your approved plan and a site survey say so.

No official floor plan is on file, so this page does not list typologies, room sizes or a sample layout. Slim Barracks Rise is a real street, but walking times to any MRT station, park or food centre are not something we will invent. Check your own stack, facing and the management corporation’s rules on balcony and window treatments before you commit to blinds, a bench or a screen.

If your keys are expected in the 2027 window, use the lead time. Share the approved plan when you have it, freeze the carpentry drawings, and only then book fabrication around the actual handover, defect rectification and renovation approval. A moderate carpentry scope is often planned at six to ten weeks on site after access and approvals; that is a planning allowance, not a guarantee. WhatsApp Maison with the plan and any key date you have in writing. We are an independent designer and are not affiliated with the developer.

Planning your Blossoms by the Park renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.

Rules change, so we check the current version for your unit before we quote. Full new condo renovation guide
