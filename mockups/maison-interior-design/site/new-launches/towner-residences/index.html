Planning your Towner Residences renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Report defects within 30 days of key collection, before renovation starts (HDB move-in guide). The Defects Liability Period runs 1 year from key collection.
  • Check floor and wall tiles for hollow or chipped pieces, plaster for cracks, and paint for stains.
  • Test every door, window, lock, tap, floor trap, light point and power socket.
  • Report through the QR code in your Welcome Kit or your project's Building Service Centre, with photos.

What the rules allow

  • Use a contractor listed in HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors; they can apply for the permit for you.
  • A permit is needed for works such as removing floor finishes, raising floor levels in wet areas, or hacking or altering walls.
  • For the first 3 years, toilet wall and floor finishes in a new BTO flat cannot be removed. New tiles can be overlaid.
  • General works: Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Noisy works: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. No works on Sundays or public holidays.
  • Household shelter walls must not be hacked or drilled. Windows need a BCA-approved window contractor; air-conditioners need a BCA-trained installer.

Rules change, so we check the current version for your unit before we quote. Full bto key collection renovation guide

Towner Residences renovation FAQ

Which floor areas are correct?

HDB’s brochure states 3-room: approx. 68 sqm including the air-con ledge (65 sqm internal), and 4-room: approx. 93 sqm including the air-con ledge (90 sqm internal). The ledge is not usable living space. Printed p.12 shows two 4-room arrangements; confirm your own stack and any mirroring.

How does the first 4-room arrangement work?

In the first arrangement on p.12, three bedrooms line the plan-top edge. Living/dining sits plan-left, the shelter beside the lower-left entrance, kitchen at the bottom and service yard to its right; bathrooms are beside this service zone. These are diagram-relative directions, not compass facings.

Which finishes are supplied?

The specifications name aluminium-framed tinted windows, glazed porcelain kitchen floor/wall tiles and an aluminium-framed glazed service-yard door. Polished porcelain living floors and vinyl bedroom floors are optional OCS items, not universal supply. Render colours, cabinetry, counters, furniture and light fittings are design proposals.

Are the pictures exact reconstructions?

No. They are AI-generated style concepts, not photos of a Towner Residences unit and not completed Maison projects. Room shapes, fittings, furniture and window views are illustrative. Individual room dimensions were not published, so check your approved owner plan and a measured survey before construction.

Are keys officially confirmed for a particular month?

No project-wide current key date was confirmed. Historical third-party estimates are not an appointment. Confirm your own HDB handover and defects process.

How should renovation be sequenced?

Inspect and report defects, coordinate rectification, survey the home, confirm the HDB renovation and permitted-work requirements, then procure and build. Any 8–12-week example is a conditional planning allowance, not a Maison guarantee. Never alter the household shelter or structural elements based on a concept image.
