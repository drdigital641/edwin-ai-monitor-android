Four CanningHill Piers interior design themes

Four rooms, four looks, each built around bespoke carpentry. We adapt every idea to your actual unit.

All four are styled on Type B1(S) 2-bedroom + ensuite study, 77 sq m / 829 sq ft (official e-brochure p31).

Riverside Teak Modernist · Living room

CanningHill Piers living room interior design — Riverside Teak Modernist

A vertical teak slat TV wall with a floating granite console, a built-in L-shaped window bench, a fluted teak dining banquette and cove lighting. Styled on the B1(S) living and dining, which run in one line from the kitchen to the living balcony.

Ivory Travertine Minimal · Kitchen (close-up)

River Valley condo kitchen design idea — Ivory Travertine Minimal

Handleless ivory cabinets, a travertine-look sintered-stone counter with a waterfall end and full-height backsplash, and an oak appliance tower. Styled on the B1(S) galley kitchen beside the entrance, open to dining; the developer names Ernestomeda kitchen systems with Miele and De Dietrich appliances (models not shown).

Oxblood Walnut Nocturne · Main bedroom

CanningHill Piers main bedroom design — Oxblood Walnut Nocturne

An oxblood upholstered headboard wall framed in walnut, a built-in platform bed with bedside shelves and a full-height lit walnut wardrobe. Styled on the B1(S) master bedroom, which has its own balcony and ensuite.

Sage Linen Contemporary · Common bedroom (close-up)

CanningHill Piers common bedroom renovation idea — Sage Linen Contemporary

A sage lacquer desk-and-shelf run under the window flows into a fluted ash wardrobe, with a linen headboard panel. Styled on B1(S) Bedroom 2, so a compact room works for both study and rest.

CanningHill Piers project facts

  • 696 apartments in 48- and 24-storey towers linked at Level 24; joint development by CapitaLand Development and City Developments Limited; direct link to Fort Canning MRT. CDL news release
  • 99-year lease from 17 September 2021. TOP obtained October 2026; expected vacant possession 30 June 2027. CDL construction page
  • Level 24 facilities include pools, a yoga deck, two gyms and a Flexi Lounge; Level 45 has lounges. CDL news release

Maison is an independent interior design firm, not affiliated with the developers. Key dates are set per buyer by the developer.

About these concepts and plans

The four theme pictures on this page are AI-generated style concepts by Maison for a CanningHill Piers renovation. They are not photos of an actual unit, not completed Maison projects and not surveyed layouts; layouts, fittings and views are illustrative. Each look leans on built-in carpentry, the kind of River Valley condo renovation Maison plans for homes between Clarke Quay and Fort Canning. The floor plans are copied from the official CanningHill Piers e-brochure (crop and scale only). Plans are subject to change, so confirm your own unit plan, stack and any mirroring before adapting an idea.

2-bed + study / ensuite study

Views tips

The developer describes a riverside 48-storey tower and a 24-storey tower facing Fort Canning Hill, linked at Level 24. No unit’s view is guaranteed: check your stack on the storey key plan, then plan blinds and glare control around your real outlook.

Details, caveats and sources

When did CanningHill Piers get TOP?

CDL lists TOP obtained in October 2026; the expected vacant possession date of 30 June 2027 is a separate contractual date. Key dates are per buyer. Source: CDL construction page.

Which unit are the concepts based on?

Type B1(S) 2-bedroom + ensuite study, 77 sq m / 829 sq ft (e-brochure p31). They are style concepts, not your actual unit.

What does the developer supply?

CDL names Ernestomeda kitchen systems, Miele and De Dietrich kitchen appliances, and Gessi and Laufen bathroom fittings. Check your own handover list. Source: CDL news release.

Where can I get the full floor plan?

The official e-brochure and storey key plans on canninghillpiers.com.sg; use your sales documents for your exact unit.

Can I renovate right after keys?

After defects inspection and management approval; condo rules differ from HDB rules.
