What layout facts were used?

  • living-dining: Living/dining on plan-left; living balcony at top, kitchen near entrance at bottom. Bedroom 2 top-middle, main suite to right with a separate balcony.
  • kitchen: Compact open L kitchen lower-left beside entry and DB/storage, opens into dining. Ernestomeda kitchen system with Miele/De Dietrich appliances named by developer.
  • master-bedroom: Main bedroom upper-right with its own balcony at outer/top edge; study below and bath to right. Bed along right wall; no claimed hill/river outlook.
  • study: Ensuite study below master, bounded by suite circulation/wardrobe and bathroom zone; no external window indicated. Proposed compact desk in internal study, not invented third bedroom.

What must happen before move-in or occupation?

Inspect and log defects; agree rectification and access; survey and freeze scope; obtain management/landlord requirements and work-specific authority approvals; procure, build, test and inspect; confirm completion/occupation requirements and moving arrangements. Condo/EC and office rules differ from HDB residential rules. No exact approval lead time, deposit, permitted hours or move-in date is verified. A moderate home scope may use 6–10 on-site weeks, or modest office 8–12 weeks, only as an editorial planning allowance after access/approvals—not a guarantee.
