What to know about CanningHill Piers

City-centre condos have two kinds of owners: people moving in, and investors preparing a unit for tenants. The renovation is different for each. Owners usually want personal storage and lighting; investors want durable finishes, simple furniture and a unit that photographs well for listing.

Compact units of about 409 sq ft reward careful planning: built-in storage that reaches the ceiling, a dining table that folds or extends, and lighting that separates living and sleeping areas without walls. In a building this size, book your renovation slot with the management early, because many owners will be working at the same time.

What owners usually change

  • For owners: full-height storage, a feature wall and warm, layered lighting.
  • For landlords: hard-wearing finishes, simple built-ins and furniture packages that suit tenants.
  • Space-saving furniture for the 1- and 2-bedroom units.
  • Blinds or curtains that control afternoon light in city-facing units.
  • Smart locks and lighting that the management's rules allow.

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.

Rules change, so we check the current version for your unit before we quote. Full new condo renovation guide

A realistic timeline

  1. 01

    Before keys 2 to 6 weeks

    Measurements from the floor plan, 3D drawings, quotation and the condo renovation application.

  2. 02

    Keys + defects 1 to 2 weeks

    Defects inspection and report, site measurements, MCST approval.

  3. 03

    Carpentry and electrical 3 to 4 weeks

    Built in the factory while lighting and power points are done on site.

  4. 04

    Install and finish 1 to 2 weeks

    Carpentry installation, curtains, lights and cleaning.

Most new condo renovations take about 4 to 8 weeks from keys to move-in. A rental-ready furnishing package can take about 3 to 5 weeks.

Questions owners ask

When is TOP for CanningHill Piers?

We could not confirm a TOP date on an official page. CDL's construction page that we read showed an indicative TOP of Q4 2026 and an expected vacant possession date of 30 June 2027, which is a contractual date, not your key-collection day.

Can you prepare my CanningHill Piers unit for renting?

Yes. We plan durable finishes, built-in storage and a furniture package so the unit is ready for viewings soon after handover.

How do I make a 409 sq ft 1-bedroom feel larger?

Use full-height built-ins, mirrors, light colours and lighting zones instead of walls. Every piece of furniture should earn its space.
