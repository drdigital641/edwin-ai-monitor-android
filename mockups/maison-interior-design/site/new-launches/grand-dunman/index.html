What to know about Grand Dunman

When around a thousand homes hand over within weeks of each other, lifts, loading bays and the management office are in demand. Owners who have their design, quotation and renovation application ready before keys are the ones who start first. Owners who wait often lose weeks in the queue.

The unit mix is wide. A 1-bedroom investor unit, a 3-bedroom flex unit and a dual-key unit all need different plans, so we start from your floor plan and how you will use the home, not a package.

What owners usually change

Common requests at Grand Dunman:

  • Investor units: durable finishes and a furniture package for tenants.
  • Flex and study units: a room that changes use, with a wall bed or fold-down desk.
  • Dual-key units: storage and soundproofing for both parts.
  • Family units: full-height wardrobes and an entrance utility cabinet.
  • Lighting upgrades throughout.

Planning your Grand Dunman renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.

Rules change, so we check the current version for your unit before we quote. Full new condo renovation guide

From keys to move-in

  1. 01

    Before keys 2 to 6 weeks

    Measurements from the floor plan, 3D drawings, quotation and the condo renovation application.

  2. 02

    Keys + defects 1 to 2 weeks

    Defects inspection and report, site measurements, MCST approval.

  3. 03

    Carpentry and electrical 3 to 4 weeks

    Built in the factory while lighting and power points are done on site.

  4. 04

    Install and finish 1 to 2 weeks

    Carpentry installation, curtains, lights and cleaning.

Most new condo renovations take about 4 to 8 weeks from keys to move-in. At a 1,008-unit handover, add time for lift bookings in the first weeks.

Grand Dunman renovation FAQ

When will Grand Dunman be completed?

The developer lists expected completion as 2028. We could not confirm an earlier TOP date on an official page.

How do I avoid delays when everyone renovates at once?

Have your design, quotation and renovation application ready before keys, and book your lift slots with the management as early as allowed.

Can you plan a dual-key unit at Grand Dunman?

Yes. We plan storage, kitchenettes and soundproofing so both parts work on their own or together.
