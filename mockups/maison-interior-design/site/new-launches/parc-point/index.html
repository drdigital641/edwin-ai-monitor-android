Parc Point Fit-Out & Interior Design

Parc Point is an HDB neighbourhood centre at 302 Tengah Park Avenue, in Tengah, not a residential condominium. Our project file, citing an NUHS/NUP release, records a progressive opening on 28 February 2026, including FairPrice, the Gourmet Paradise food court and Tengah Polyclinic, consistent with HDB’s earlier first-quarter 2026 planned opening. Research already on file also records, from HDB and CNA reporting, a retail centre of about 16,000 sq m with more than 40 shops, adjacent to Parc Residences @ Tengah. Those are scheme facts. We have not audited the current trading status of every tenant, we will not invent individual shop sizes by dividing the centre area, and no official tenancy floor plan is on file. This page sets out what is on file and how to plan your Parc Point fit-out.

Planning your Parc Point interior

This page is for shop and F&B fit-out, not for home renovation. A supermarket, a food court, a clinic and a small shop each have a different services and licensing brief; a generic lifestyle-retail layout is not a substitute for any of them. For an ordinary shop unit, Maison would start from the approved tenancy plan and the landlord’s fit-out rules, then specify built-in shopfront joinery, a counter, display and storage, and a compact back-of-house run, not loose, flat-pack fixtures. Food trades need coordinated extraction, grease management and water and electrical capacity before any carpentry is fixed. HDB’s commercial renovation route, as already recorded in our research file, requires prior approval for fitting-out, usually through a qualified person; classification of the actual work must be checked, and we will not promise a processing time.

Plantation Creek, Garden Waterfront I & II, Plantation Verge, Brickland Weave, Plantation Edge I & II and Plantation Dew are nearby Tengah housing pages; they are not a shop-unit typologies list. We will not invent walking times to any present or future MRT station, and we will not publish generic shop concepts, which are not plans of this centre.

If you have a unit, send the tenancy plan, permitted trade, measured area and any landlord manual. After handover, record defects, obtain approvals, then fabricate. A modest non-food shop fit-out is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after approvals — an editorial allowance only. WhatsApp Maison at https://wa.me/6593391185. Planning your Parc Point fit-out

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the unit against it.
  • Check the shopfront, floor, ceiling, power supply, water and drainage points, and any exhaust provision.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before work starts.
  • Agree how landlord defects will be fixed so they do not delay opening.

Rules and approvals

  • The landlord's fit-out guidelines: contractors, hours, deposits, signage and drawing submissions.
  • Food businesses need the right SFA licence; plan the kitchen with it in mind.
  • Fire safety changes need a qualified person and SCDF approval; structural changes need BCA approval.

Parc Point fit-out FAQ

Is Parc Point already open?

Our project file records: 2026-02-28 opened. Check your own lease and landlord handover for unit access. This is not a home key-collection date.

Do you have a floor plan for Parc Point?

No official floor plan or tenancy / floor-plate drawing is on file for this page, so none is published here. Please send your approved tenancy or office plan.

Is this a home-renovation page?

No. The copy is angled at shop, office or F&B fit-out. Send a tenancy plan and permitted trade, not a residential brief, unless you actually have a home in a related development — in which case use that development’s own page.

What should I send Maison?
