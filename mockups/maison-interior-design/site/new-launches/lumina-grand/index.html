What to know about Lumina Grand

Lumina Grand's homes are family sizes, from about 936 sq ft for a 3-bedroom to about 1,496 sq ft for a 5-bedroom. In a family home the work usually goes into storage, a study corner and rooms that can change as children grow, more than into hacking.

As an executive condominium, Lumina Grand follows the condo's own renovation rules, not HDB's renovation permits. Approval comes from the management, and working hours are set by its by-laws. With most units handed over, the defects period is running now, so report defects before your contractor starts.

What owners usually change

  • Full-height wardrobes and a study area that can become a child's room later.
  • A shoe and utility cabinet at the entrance, with space for a stroller or bicycles.
  • Kitchen storage: tall larder units and appliance cabinets around the developer's kitchen.
  • Lighting upgrades, usually cove lights in the living room and warm lights in the bedrooms.
  • Window treatments and balcony flooring that follows what the management allows.

Defects check at handover

  • Your sale and purchase agreement sets the defects liability period, commonly 12 months from vacant possession. Check yours.
  • Inspect before your contractor starts, so new marks are not blamed on the renovation.
  • Check floors, walls, doors, windows, balcony glass, sealant, appliances, taps, water heater and air-conditioning.
  • Submit a list with photos through the developer's defects process and keep a copy.

What the rules allow

  • Renovation approval comes from the condo's management (the managing agent, then the MCST). Expect a form, a deposit and contractor registration.
  • Working hours are set by the condo's by-laws and are usually shorter than HDB's. Check them before booking work.
  • Changes seen from outside, such as balcony screens, grilles and the main door, need approval and must follow the approved design.
  • Structural changes need a professional engineer and building approvals.
  • Executive condominiums follow the condo's rules, not HDB's renovation permits.

A realistic timeline

  1. 01

    Before keys 2 to 6 weeks

    Measurements from the floor plan, 3D drawings, quotation and the condo renovation application.

  2. 02

    Keys + defects 1 to 2 weeks

    Defects inspection and report, site measurements, MCST approval.

  3. 03

    Carpentry and electrical 3 to 4 weeks

    Built in the factory while lighting and power points are done on site.

  4. 04

    Install and finish 1 to 2 weeks

    Carpentry installation, curtains, lights and cleaning.

With the kitchen and bathrooms already done, most Lumina Grand renovations take about 4 to 8 weeks from keys to move-in, plus time for the management's approval.

Good to know

Questions owners ask

Do I need an HDB permit to renovate at Lumina Grand?

No. Lumina Grand is an executive condominium, so renovation approval comes from its management, under the condo's by-laws, not from HDB.

When did Lumina Grand get TOP?

CDL's 1H 2026 results release says Lumina Grand obtained TOP in April 2026 and most units have been handed over. Your own key date is in your developer's notice.

What is the most useful upgrade for a Lumina Grand family home?

Storage. Full-height wardrobes, an entrance cabinet and kitchen larder units make the biggest daily difference in a family-sized EC.
