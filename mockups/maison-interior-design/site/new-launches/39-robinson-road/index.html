What to know about 39 Robinson Road

In a refurbished office building the core services are usually upgraded, but floor plates, columns and window lines often stay as they were. A fit-out that works with those fixed points, rather than against them, costs less and gets approved faster.

CBD tenants often move on a tight timeline between leases. Ask the landlord for the fit-out guidelines and the handover condition early, so your design starts from what will actually be on site.

What tenants usually change

Common requests at 39 Robinson Road:

  • Layouts planned around existing columns and window lines.
  • Glass meeting rooms that keep daylight in the open office.
  • Pantry and collaboration areas for hybrid teams.
  • Data and power planned for flexible desks.
  • Acoustic treatment for meeting rooms and phone booths.

Planning your 39 Robinson Road fit-out

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the space against it.
  • Check ceilings, raised floors, air-conditioning outlets, sprinkler heads, power and data provisions.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before your contractor starts.
  • Agree how and when landlord defects will be fixed so they do not hold up the fit-out.

Rules and approvals

  • The building's fit-out guidelines: approved contractors, insurance, deposits, hours and drawing submissions.
  • Changes to sprinklers, fire alarms or escape routes usually need a qualified person and SCDF approval.
  • Structural changes need BCA approval. If your use differs from the approved use, check URA's change-of-use rules.

39 Robinson Road fit-out FAQ

When will 39 Robinson Road be ready?

EdgeProp reported an expected TOP in 2026. We could not confirm this on an official page, so check with the landlord for your handover date.

What should we ask the landlord before designing?

The fit-out guidelines, the handover condition of the space, the approved working hours, and the submissions they need before work starts.

Do we need SCDF approval?

Only if the works change fire safety items such as sprinklers, alarms or escape routes. These need a qualified person and SCDF's approval.
