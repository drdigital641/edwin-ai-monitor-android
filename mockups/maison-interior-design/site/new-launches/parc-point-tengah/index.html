What to know about Parc Point

A neighbourhood centre in a young town serves residents who have only just moved in. The shops that do well are easy to find, quick to walk through and open when the estate needs them. That shapes the fit-out more than the look of the shopfront.

HDB is the landlord, so its tenancy terms apply to what you can change, how signage is approved and when work can happen. Read those terms before you commit to a design, and plan the fit-out period so the shop opens while residents are still choosing their regular places.

What tenants usually change

  • A clear shopfront and signage that can be read from the walkway, within the landlord's signage rules.
  • Counters and checkout placed so a queue does not block the entrance.
  • Storage sized for deliveries, since small units fill up fast.
  • For food businesses: kitchen, exhaust and wash areas planned with the SFA licence requirements in mind.
  • Durable floors and wall finishes that survive heavy foot traffic and daily cleaning.

Handover check

  • Ask the landlord for the handover condition in writing and walk the unit against it.
  • Check the shopfront, floor, ceiling, power supply, water and drainage points, and any exhaust provision.
  • Photograph existing marks and defects before work starts.
  • Agree how landlord defects will be fixed so they do not delay opening.

Rules and approvals

  • The landlord's fit-out guidelines: contractors, hours, deposits, signage and drawing submissions.
  • Food businesses need the right SFA licence; plan the kitchen with it in mind.
  • Fire safety changes need a qualified person and SCDF approval; structural changes need BCA approval.

A realistic timeline

  1. 01

    Design and quotation 2 to 4 weeks

    Layout, 3D views, materials and an itemised quotation.

  2. 02

    Landlord approval 1 to 4 weeks

    Drawing submission, plus licences for food businesses.

  3. 03

    Build 3 to 6 weeks

    Shopfront, M&E, flooring, counters and fixtures.

  4. 04

    Fit and open 1 week

    Signage, final checks, stock and opening.

For a small shop, plan on about 6 to 12 weeks from design to opening, longer for food businesses with kitchen works.

Good to know

Questions owners ask

Who approves a fit-out at Parc Point?

HDB is the landlord of HDB neighbourhood centre shops, so check your tenancy agreement for what needs HDB's approval before work starts. Food businesses also need the right SFA licence.

When did Parc Point open?

HDB announced that Parc Point would open in 1Q 2026, starting with the FairPrice supermarket and Gourmet Paradise food court, with the remaining shops opening progressively. Individual shop opening dates vary.

Can Maison design my shop at Parc Point?

Yes. We design and fit out shops, cafés and food outlets. We are an independent firm, not appointed by HDB, and we work to your tenancy terms.
