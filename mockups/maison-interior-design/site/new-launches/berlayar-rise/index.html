Planning your Berlayar Rise interior

Bukit Merah is a southern, city-fringe town, with a different grain from a new-town BTO in the north or west. Buyers here often already work in the city and want the future flat to feel considered rather than temporary. You still have time before keys. When the official plan is issued, the joinery that typically earns its keep is a fitted kitchen, full-height wardrobes, an entry cabinet, and a living-room storage wall, with a built-in desk if a common bedroom will be a study. Maison specifies this as built-in carpentry, not as IKEA-style furniture, and we can present the look in a wide view or in closer shots of a counter or a wardrobe.

No official floor plan is on file, and we will not invent typologies or a typical Berlayar Rise layout. We will also not invent walking times to the city, to the harbour, or to any MRT station, and we will not assign a view from the project name. Nearby Maison pages in the wider central and southern catchment include Towner Residences and Kent Heights, already live in Kallang/Whampoa, and CanningHill Piers; those are internal links, not typologies references.

After keys, inspect defects, then apply for the HDB renovation permit before fabrication. A moderate carpentry scope is often planned at about six to ten weeks on site after approvals — an editorial allowance, not a promise. WhatsApp Maison with your official plan when you have it. Planning your Berlayar Rise renovation

Defects check at handover

  • Report defects within 30 days of key collection, before renovation starts (HDB move-in guide). The Defects Liability Period runs 1 year from key collection.
  • Check floor and wall tiles for hollow or chipped pieces, plaster for cracks, and paint for stains.
  • Test every door, window, lock, tap, floor trap, light point and power socket.
  • Report through the QR code in your Welcome Kit or your project's Building Service Centre, with photos.

What the rules allow

  • Use a contractor listed in HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors; they can apply for the permit for you.
  • A permit is needed for works such as removing floor finishes, raising floor levels in wet areas, or hacking or altering walls.
  • For the first 3 years, toilet wall and floor finishes in a new BTO flat cannot be removed. New tiles can be overlaid.
  • General works: Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Noisy works: Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. No works on Sundays or public holidays.
  • Household shelter walls must not be hacked or drilled. Windows need a BCA-approved window contractor; air-conditioners need a BCA-trained installer.
