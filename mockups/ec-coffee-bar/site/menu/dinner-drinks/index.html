Common questions

Does EC Coffee Bar serve dinner?

Yes. Dinner runs until closing (9pm Monday to Saturday, 8pm Sunday), with mains, pasta and dinner sets that include wine.

Is there a dinner set for a group?

Yes. The Signature Spread wine set feeds 4 to 6 people with six mains and a bottle of house pour wine for $219.90 (July 2025 menu).

Which wines do you serve?

House pour red and white, plus bottles from France, Italy and other European countries.
