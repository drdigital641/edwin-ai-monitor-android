Common questions

Does EC Coffee Bar serve breakfast all day?

Yes. EC Coffee Bar is an all-day café, and breakfast is on the menu from opening at 8.30am.

What is in the EC Signature Breakfast Platter?

Scrambled eggs, grilled sausage, oven-baked potato, roasted tomato, sourdough with butter and a cup of mushroom soup. It is $19.90, or $21.90 with a drink.

What is the cheapest breakfast set?

The classic croissant with a drink at $9.90 (July 2025 menu).
