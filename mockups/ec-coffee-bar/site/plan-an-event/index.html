Questions office managers ask us

Is there a minimum spend, room charge or deposit?

There is no minimum order for a table. For the VIP room or a whole-café booking, the team confirms any room terms, deposit and cancellation policy when it confirms your date. Ask on WhatsApp and you will have it in writing in the chat.

Can the café invoice my company?

Ask the team when you book. For your records, the café is EC Coffee Bar Pte. Ltd., UEN 202416189N, at 40 Carpenter Street, #01-01, Singapore 059919.

Can we make a speech or show slides?

A short speech or toast is no problem. For a screen, projector or microphone, tell the team what you need when you enquire and they will confirm what is available for your date.

How do you handle dietary needs?

List allergies and dietary needs in your booking message. The menu is Western café food with chicken, beef, lamb, fish, pasta and rice dishes, and the kitchen will advise which suit your guests.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. A table for ten needs less notice than the VIP room or the whole café. Send your date first and the team will tell you what is open.

Can we order a cake for the occasion?

Yes. Whole cakes come in 4, 6 and 8 inch sizes from $19.90 and need to be reserved ahead. If you would rather bring your own cake, ask the team first.

How do guests get there?

Clarke Quay MRT is a short walk away, and so are the Raffles Place offices. Use the Google Maps link on this page for directions, and ask the team about parking nearby.

What time can an evening event run until?

The café is open until 9pm Monday to Saturday and 8pm on Sunday. Ask the team if your plan needs a different time.
