Farewell lunch ideas for a colleague

Short answer: Book a relaxed venue close to the office for a long lunch, collect a group card and a small gift, order a cake to arrive at the end, and keep speeches to one or two short ones. Ask the leaving colleague about food preferences without spoiling the surprise.

Pick a place they would choose

Think about what the colleague enjoys: a quiet café, a lively bar or a long lunch with wine. A walkable venue makes it easy for everyone to come.

Card, gift and cake

Pass a card around a week ahead. Order a whole cake in advance so it is ready when you arrive; a 6 or 8 inch cake usually covers a team.

Keep the speech short

One manager and one close teammate is plenty. A short story beats a long list of achievements.

Make it easy to join

Choose a time that suits most people, share the address and map link, and offer non-alcoholic drinks.
