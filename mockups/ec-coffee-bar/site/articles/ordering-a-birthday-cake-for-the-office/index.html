Ordering a birthday cake for the office

Short answer: As a rough guide, a 4 inch cake suits 2 to 4 people, a 6 inch cake 6 to 10 and an 8 inch cake 12 to 16, depending on slice size. Order a few days ahead, check for allergies, and arrange for the cake to come out at the end of the meal.

Rough size guide

Cake portions depend on how thin you slice, but the rough guide above works for most offices. If in doubt, size up; leftovers rarely go to waste in an office.

Pick a flavour most people like

Cheesecake and chocolate are safe choices. A fruit cake is lighter after a big lunch.

Order ahead

Whole cakes at EC Coffee Bar need a reservation. They come in 4, 6 and 8 inch sizes from $19.90 (July 2025 menu).

Plan the surprise

Tell the venue the name to write or the moment to bring the cake out, and have someone ready with a lighter and a phone camera.
