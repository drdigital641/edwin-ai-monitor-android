The VIP room for about 10 guests

A private room for client lunches, partner meetings and small celebrations, when you want the food of a café and the privacy of a meeting room.

What the room is for

Client lunches, partner and board meetings, interview lunches, small team dinners and private birthday dinners. It seats about 10 guests, so a full team or a client group can sit together.

Food that suits a meeting

Order individual mains such as the Kindfire striploin or lamb rack, or share the Signature Spread across the table. Wine is served by the glass or the bottle, from France, Italy and other European countries.

Booking the room

The room is popular for lunch and dinner, so message us early with your date, time and number of guests.

Popular for groups

Prices in Singapore dollars from EC Coffee Bar's July 2025 menu. Prices and items can change, so please check with the team on WhatsApp before you book.

Common questions

Does EC Coffee Bar have a private room?

Yes. The VIP room seats about 10 guests and suits client lunches, meetings and small private dinners.

How do I reserve the VIP room?

WhatsApp 9365 6626 with your date, time and number of guests.

Can we order wine in the VIP room?

Yes. House pour wine, bottles from France and Italy, cocktails and beer are on the menu.

Is the VIP room good for a client lunch near Raffles Place?

Yes. It is a short walk from Raffles Place and Clarke Quay, and the room is private enough to talk business over lunch.
