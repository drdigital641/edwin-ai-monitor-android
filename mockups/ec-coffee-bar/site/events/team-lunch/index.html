Team lunches and team bonding

Get the team out of the office for an hour or an afternoon. Good food, a calm room and sharing sets that make ordering for a group easy.

Lunch with the whole team

Team lunches work best when nobody has to organise a complicated order. Sharing sets such as the EC mixed lunch set cover the table, and individual lunch sets (a main with a drink or cake) start from $17.90.

Team bonding without the hard sell

Not every team wants an escape room. A long lunch or an afternoon tea session gives people time to talk. Book a breakfast set for an early team huddle, an afternoon tea for a quarterly catch-up, or after-work drinks to end a project.

Groups of every size

Small teams fit into the VIP room for about 10. Whole departments of up to about 50 can fill the café.

Popular for groups

Prices in Singapore dollars from EC Coffee Bar's July 2025 menu. Prices and items can change, so please check with the team on WhatsApp before you book.

Common questions

Is EC Coffee Bar good for a team lunch in the CBD?

Yes. It is a short walk from Raffles Place and Clarke Quay, seats about 50, and has sharing and lunch sets that make group ordering simple.

How much does a team lunch cost?

Lunch sets with a drink or cake start from $17.90 per person, and the EC mixed lunch set for sharing is $100.90 (July 2025 menu). Ask on WhatsApp for current prices.

Can we book a table for a team of 10 to 20?

Yes. WhatsApp 9365 6626 with your date, time and headcount so the team can hold the right tables for you.

Do you do team breakfasts?

Yes. The café opens at 8.30am, and sharing breakfast sets come with four drinks.
