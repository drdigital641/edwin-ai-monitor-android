Birthdays, farewells and office celebrations

Mark the moment with the team: a farewell lunch, a surprise birthday cake, a promotion toast or a project wrap party.

Celebrations we help with

Farewell lunches, office birthdays, promotion and anniversary dinners, baby showers for colleagues, and team wins worth toasting.

Whole cakes by reservation

Order a whole Silk Whisk Cheesecake, Hazelnut Truffle Delight, Berry Fruit Cake or Golden Biscoff Dream in 4, 6 or 8 inch sizes, from $19.90 for 4 inch. Reserve ahead so it is ready when your group arrives.

Afternoon tea for a softer celebration

The EC afternoon Glow Tea Set comes on a tiered stand and is made to order, so reserve it ahead. The menu also offers afternoon tea with low-sugar cakes, a good fit for colleagues who prefer something lighter.

Popular for groups

Prices in Singapore dollars from EC Coffee Bar's July 2025 menu. Prices and items can change, so please check with the team on WhatsApp before you book.

Common questions

Can I order a birthday cake for the office?

Yes. Whole cakes come in 4, 6 and 8 inch sizes from $19.90 and need a reservation. WhatsApp 9365 6626 with the cake, size and date.

Where can we have a farewell lunch near Clarke Quay?

EC Coffee Bar at 40 Carpenter Street seats about 50, has a VIP room for about 10, and offers sharing sets for groups.

Can we bring our own decorations?

Ask the team on WhatsApp when you book so they can plan the table for you.

Do you serve wine and cocktails for celebrations?

Yes: wine by the glass or bottle, a set of six signature cocktails, and beer packages.
