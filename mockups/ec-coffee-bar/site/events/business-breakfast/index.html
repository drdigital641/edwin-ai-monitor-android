Business breakfasts and meetings

A quiet table, proper coffee and a good breakfast from 8.30am, a short walk from the Raffles Place offices.

Breakfast before the day starts

The café opens at 8.30am every day. Breakfast sets pair a dish with a coffee or tea, from $9.90 for a classic croissant and drink, up to the EC Signature Breakfast Platter set at $21.90.

Meetings that need a little privacy

For a client or partner meeting, book the VIP room for about 10. For a casual catch-up, any table works, and the room is calm rather than loud.

Built for professionals

EC Coffee Bar was set up as a place for the business community to meet, recharge and rise together. The name says it: E for Entrepreneurs, C for Chick, and the house motto is "Where Chicks Grow into Eagles".

Popular for groups

Prices in Singapore dollars from EC Coffee Bar's July 2025 menu. Prices and items can change, so please check with the team on WhatsApp before you book.

Common questions

What time does EC Coffee Bar open?

From 8.30am every day. It closes at 9pm Monday to Saturday and 8pm on Sunday.

How much is breakfast?

Breakfast sets with a drink start from $9.90 (classic croissant and drink) on the July 2025 menu.

Is it a good place for a business meeting near Raffles Place?

Yes. It is a short walk from Raffles Place, opens at 8.30am, and has a VIP room for about 10 when you need privacy.

Can I book a table for a breakfast meeting?

Yes. WhatsApp 9365 6626 with your time and number of guests.
