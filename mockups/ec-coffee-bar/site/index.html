Quick answers

How many people can EC Coffee Bar host?

About 50 guests at full capacity, and the VIP room seats about 10. Groups of 10 to 30 get reserved tables in the café.

Is there a minimum order or minimum spend?

No. Everyone is welcome, from one person dropping in for coffee to a whole company. For the VIP room or a whole-café booking, ask the team on WhatsApp for the current terms.

Roughly how much should I budget per person?

From the July 2025 menu: lunch sets $13.90 to $27.90, dinner sets with a glass of wine $25.90 to $48.90, and the Signature Spread for 4 to 6 people $219.90. See the event planner page for the full guide.

How do I book an event for my office?

WhatsApp 9365 6626 with the date, time, number of guests, the occasion and any dietary needs. The team replies with availability and menu options. There is a ready-made message on the event planner page.

Where is EC Coffee Bar, and how do we get there?

At 40 Carpenter Street, #01-01, Singapore 059919, a short walk from Clarke Quay MRT and the Raffles Place offices.

What are the opening hours?

Monday to Saturday 8.30am to 9pm, and Sunday 8.30am to 8pm.
