Wiper blade size guide

Three ways to find your size. The fastest is a call.

Call Kohden 6966 6565

  1. Read the box

    The old blade's box or the blade itself shows the length, often in inches.

  2. Measure tip to tip

    Measure the rubber from end to end. Driver and passenger sides are often different, so measure both, plus the rear.

  3. Call Kohden

    Give your car's make, model and year. Kohden will match the size and type.

Inches to millimetres

Fu-Gang, the maker, lists sizes in inches with millimetres, for example 12" (300mm) and 28" (700mm). The pairs below use the same step of 25mm per inch.
