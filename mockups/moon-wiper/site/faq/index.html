Moon wiper questions

Short answers from Kohden's published facts.

Call Kohden 6966 6565
Which MOON blade fits a Japanese car?

Start with the Silicone Hybrid M1001. Kohden sells it from $30 to $80 depending on size.

Which MOON blade is for Continental cars?

Silicone Advance, the higher-end line. It comes with an adapter set. Kohden gives the price.

How much are MOON wiper blades?

Kohden's published prices: Hybrid M1001 $30 to $80, Flat M-102 $30 to $80, Japan Flat M-105 $30, rear M-GRA and M-GRB $20, rear M-FR $22.

Do they really last twice as long?

That is the maker's claim from laboratory testing: the MOON silicone blade lasted 2X longer than traditional natural rubber.

Where are MOON blades made?

In Taiwan, by Fu-Gang, which holds ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949:2016.

How do I find my size?

Read the old box or blade, measure tip to tip, or call Kohden with the make, model and year. The size guide has the full table.

Anything to do before first use?

The MOON leaflet says to clean the glass, then let the blades wipe dry glass for 5 minutes or more.

Which blades use the adapter chart?

Only Silicone Advance. The adapter chart does not apply to the Hybrid, flat or rear blades.
