Short answers. Call Kohden for anything else.

Which MOON blade should I get for a Japanese car?

Start with the MOON Silicone Hybrid M1001. Kohden sells it from $30 to $80 depending on size.

Which MOON blade is for Continental cars?

Silicone Advance, the higher-end line. It comes with the adapter set. Call Kohden for the price.

How much are MOON wiper blades in Singapore?

Kohden's published prices: Silicone Hybrid M1001 $30 to $80, flat M-102 $30 to $80, M-105 Japan Flat $30, rear M-GRA and M-GRB $20, rear M-FR $22.

Do MOON blades really last twice as long?

That is the maker's claim from laboratory testing: the MOON silicone blade lasted 2X longer than traditional natural rubber.

Where are MOON wiper blades made?

In Taiwan, by Fu-Gang. Fu-Gang is certified ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949:2016.

How do I find my wiper size?

Read the old blade's box, measure the rubber tip to tip, or call Kohden on +65 6966 6565 with your make, model and year.

Is there anything to do before first use?

The MOON leaflet says to clean the glass, then let the blades wipe dry glass for 5 minutes or more.
