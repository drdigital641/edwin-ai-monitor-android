DENSO questions

Where can I get DENSO spark plugs fitted in Singapore?

At any workshop on our authorised workshop list. They buy direct from KohDen, so the parts are genuine.

KohDen's list price is S$24 per plug for the Iridium Power range, S$22 to S$42 for Iridium SR and S$26 to S$36 for Iridium TT. Fitting is charged by the workshop.

How do I know my DENSO plugs are genuine?
