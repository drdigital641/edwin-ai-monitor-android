MOON questions

How often should I change wiper blades in Singapore?

When they streak, chatter or leave patches, often every 6 to 12 months in our sun and rain.

Flat or hybrid blade?

Flat blades sit low on curved screens; hybrid blades add a spoiler shape against wind lift. The workshop will match the fitting to your car.

What does the MWW-30 washer additive do?

MOON states it removes oil, soot and road film and is safe for paint, chrome, rubber and plastics.
