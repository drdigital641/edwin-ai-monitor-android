EXN questions

Which EXN oil suits a hybrid?

EXN lists Extentios Hybrid 0W-16 (API SP) for hybrids. Always follow the grade in your owner's manual.

Is there a CVT fluid?

Yes, ATFIOS CVTF, fully synthetic, 4L, list price S$80. Ask the workshop to confirm it matches your gearbox.
