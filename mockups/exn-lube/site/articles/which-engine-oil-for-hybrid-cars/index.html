Which Engine Oil Should You Use in a Hybrid Car?

By the EXN Lube technical team · 8 November 2026

Most hybrid cars use thin, low-viscosity engine oil, usually 0W-20 or 0W-16, with an API SP rating. The exact grade depends on your model, so always follow the owner's manual. Using the grade your maker specifies helps protect the engine and keeps fuel economy as designed.

What does 0W-16 mean?

0W-16 is a SAE viscosity grade for a very thin engine oil. The "0W" shows how easily the oil flows when cold. The lower the number before the W, the better it flows at start-up. The "16" describes thickness at normal running temperature. A lower number means a thinner oil when the engine is hot.

Thin oil is not weak oil. It is made with modern additives and base oils so it still protects parts. It is simply designed for engines built with very small clearances.

Why do hybrid cars use low-viscosity oil?

Hybrids use thin oil because their engines start and stop many times and are built to save fuel. Several things work together here.

  • Frequent start-stop. The petrol engine restarts often during one trip. Each start needs oil to reach moving parts quickly, and a thin oil with a "0W" cold rating flows fast.
  • Cooler running. When the car drives on electric power, the engine is off and can cool down. On short trips it may not stay at full temperature for long.
  • Fuel economy. Thinner oil creates less internal drag, so the engine wastes less energy moving through the oil.
  • Engine design. Many hybrid engines are made for thin oil, with tight clearances and parts matched to it.

Which oil grades do hybrids use?

Most hybrids use 0W-16 or 0W-20, although some models call for other grades. The table below shows the usual picture. Your manual has the final say.

GradeWhere it is commonly usedNote
0W-16Newer hybrids designed for very thin oilUse only if the manual lists it
0W-20Many hybrids and fuel-efficient petrol carsA very common specification
0W-30 or 5W-30Some older or specific modelsUse only if the manual lists it

Can I use thicker oil in a hybrid?

You should not use thicker oil unless your maker allows it. Thicker oil can add drag and reduce fuel economy. It may also flow more slowly at start-up, which matters in an engine that restarts often. Using an oil outside the specification could also cause problems with a warranty claim.

What does API SP mean?

API SP is a service category from the American Petroleum Institute for petrol engines. It was introduced in 2020 and sits above older categories such as SN. It covers things like deposit control, wear protection, and protection against low-speed pre-ignition in turbocharged direct-injection engines. Many modern hybrid specifications ask for API SP or an equivalent. If your manual lists an older category, an SP oil is generally suitable too, but check your owner's manual.

How do I choose the right oil for my hybrid?

Choose the oil by matching your manual, not by habit. Follow these steps.

  1. Open the owner's manual and find the engine oil section.
  2. Write down the viscosity grade, such as 0W-16 or 0W-20.
  3. Write down the service category, such as API SP, and any extra approval the maker names.
  4. Check the oil capacity, including the filter, so you buy enough.
  5. Pick an oil that matches the grade and category exactly.
  6. Follow the maker's change interval. Short trips and heavy stop-go traffic may call for earlier changes, so ask your workshop.

Do hybrids still need regular oil changes?

Yes. A hybrid still has a petrol engine, and its oil still ages. Fuel dilution, heat cycles and condensation from short trips can all affect it. The engine may run less than a normal car, but oil still needs changing by time as well as distance. Follow the interval in your manual.

Questions and answers

Can I use 0W-20 instead of 0W-16 in my hybrid?

Only if your owner's manual allows it. Some makers list both grades, while others name just one. Do not switch grades on your own, because the engine was designed and tested for the specified oil. If you are unsure, ask your workshop to confirm.

Why do hybrids use such thin engine oil?

Hybrid engines restart often, may run cooler on short trips, and are built to save fuel. Thin oil flows quickly at start-up and creates less drag. The engine is also made with tight clearances matched to thin oil.

What does API SP mean on hybrid oil?

API SP is a petrol engine oil category introduced in 2020. It covers wear protection, deposit control and protection against low-speed pre-ignition. Many hybrid specifications ask for it. Always match the category your owner's manual lists.
