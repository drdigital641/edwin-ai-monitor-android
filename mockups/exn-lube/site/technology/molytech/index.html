Molytech: how EXN's nano technology works

Molytech is EXN's friction technology. It is based on a 60-atom sphere called a buckyball that looks like a soccer ball at atomic level. The nano particles act like billions of ball bearings that fill gaps in metal surfaces, turning sliding friction into rolling, which EXN says lowers heat, wear and engine drag.

What Molytech does, step by step

  1. Nano particles spread through the oil and settle into the tiny peaks and valleys of metal surfaces.
  2. They make the surface more even at atomic level.
  3. Moving parts roll over the particles instead of grinding against each other.
  4. Less friction means less heat, which helps the oil last longer, and less engine drag, which helps fuel mileage.

The eight benefits EXN lists

  • Cleans out deposits in the piston
  • Prevents sludge from clumping in the oil tank
  • Forms a film layer that stops metal-to-metal friction and wear
  • Works as an anti-foaming agent
  • Holds the oil's viscosity across temperatures, lowering shear and extending the drain interval
  • Nano particles spread into curved surfaces
  • A tackifier helps the oil stick to metal while the engine runs
  • Raises TBN to fight corrosion from combustion acids

The 100 km "without engine oil" test

In 2020 EXN added Carios Molytech to a Toyota Vios engine, flushed it out and drove from Klang to Rawang via Damansara and back. EXN reports that the engine "remained in excellent condition, with no scratches or damage". Watch the video on YouTube. This is a demonstration of film strength, not advice: never drive without oil.

EXN also holds a Certificate of Achievement from the Malaysian Motorcycle Association for its "Zero Engine Oil Run".
