Marine lubricants

EXN marine lubricants are made for fast and stationary diesel engines. EXN says they are formulated with detergent and dispersant properties, resist oxidation, protect against wear and have favourable alkalinity (TBN) to neutralise acids from combustion.

EXN: "Specially formulated with detergent and dispersant properties, these marine lubricants are designed to cater to the needs of fast and stationary diesel engines. They exhibit excellent resistance to oxidation, anti-wear capabilities, and possess favorable alkalinity properties."

Questions and answers

What engines are EXN marine oils for?

Fast and stationary marine diesel engines. Contact EXN with your engine maker and model for the right grade.

Why does alkalinity matter in marine oil?

Alkalinity, shown as TBN, neutralises acids formed when diesel fuel burns, which helps protect engine metal.
