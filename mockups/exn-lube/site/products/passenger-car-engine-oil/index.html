Passenger car engine oil

EXN makes three passenger car engine oil families: Extentios Hybrid 0W-16 (fully synthetic, API SP) for hybrids, Extentios Molyracing in 0W-20, 5W-30, 5W-40 and 5W-50 (fully synthetic PAO and ester) for modern and performance engines, and Carios Molytech Nano in 5W-30 and 10W-40 with EXN's Molytech nano technology.

EXN describes its car range like this: "Our range of engine lubricants has been meticulously formulated to withstand even the most arduous conditions, ensuring peak performance. We take pride in offering an array of engine oil solutions tailored to various form of car types, from commercial cars, diesel cars to high-performance racing engines."

Every oil carries a dual API rating (SN/CF) or the latest API SP, plus ACEA sequences where listed, so a workshop can match it to the owner's manual.

Questions and answers

Which EXN oil is for a hybrid car?

Extentios Hybrid 0W-16, a fully synthetic oil rated API SP and sold in 4L. Use it where your owner's manual calls for 0W-16.

Which EXN oil suits a turbo or performance car?

Extentios Molyracing, a fully synthetic PAO and ester oil in 0W-20, 5W-30, 5W-40 and 5W-50. The 5W-30 and 5W-40 also carry ACEA C3. Pick the grade in your owner's manual.

What does API SN/CF mean on EXN oils?

SN is an API petrol engine category and CF a diesel category, so the oil is rated for both. See our guide to reading an oil label.
