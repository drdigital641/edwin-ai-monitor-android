Passenger car engine oil

Questions and answers

Which EXN oil is for a hybrid car?

Extentios Hybrid 0W-16, a fully synthetic oil rated API SP and sold in 4L. Use it where your owner's manual calls for 0W-16.

Which EXN oil suits a turbo or performance car?

Extentios Molyracing, a fully synthetic PAO and ester oil in 0W-20, 5W-30, 5W-40 and 5W-50. The 5W-30 and 5W-40 also carry ACEA C3. Pick the grade in your owner's manual.

What does API SN/CF mean on EXN oils?

SN is an API petrol engine category and CF a diesel category, so the oil is rated for both. See our guide to reading an oil label.
