Questions and answers

Can I mix DOT 4 and DOT 5.1 brake fluid?

Both are glycol-based and compatible, but it is best to flush and fill with one type. Never mix them with silicone DOT 5.

What does Super Engine Flush do?

An engine flush is added before an oil change to help loosen deposits so they drain out with the old oil. Follow the label's instructions.
