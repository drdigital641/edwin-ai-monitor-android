Heavy duty diesel engine oil (HDDEO)

EXN Heavy Duty Diesel Engine Oil (HDDEO) is made for the diesel engines of heavy trucks, commercial vehicles and farm machinery. EXN says it is blended to meet and exceed major truck and engine makers' requirements, with advanced additives for wear protection, soot control and bearing corrosion protection.

EXN's tagline for the range is "Built To Endure, Designed To Excel". The company describes it as "specialized for the Heavy Duty diesel engines of vehicles blended design for Heavy Truck, Commercial and Farm lubricants".

Fleet operators can contact EXN for drum and bulk supply and for the specification sheet of each grade.
