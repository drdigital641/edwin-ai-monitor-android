Questions and answers

Which Molyracing grade should I use?

The one in your owner's manual. 0W-20 suits many modern Japanese petrol engines; 5W-30 and 5W-40 with ACEA C3 suit many European cars; 5W-50 is for engines that call for a thicker hot grade.

What does ACEA C3 mean?

C3 is a European low-ash (mid-SAPS) category for engines with diesel particulate filters and catalytic converters.
