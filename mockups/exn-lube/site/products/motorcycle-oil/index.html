Motorcycle oil (Motios)

EXN's motorcycle range is the Motios series, launched between 2019 and 2021. EXN says these oils are made for high-output, high-temperature motorcycle engines by blending synthetic and petroleum-based oils, with good clutch engagement, engine protection and extreme pressure protection for all types of transmissions.

In EXN's words: "specifically designed for high output and high temperature motorcycle engines which are created through blending synthetic and petroleum-based oils. Our products excels when comes to clutch engagement, superior engine protection and capable to withstand extreme pressure protection for all types of transmissions."

EXN has supported motorcycle events such as PortBike Motoday Kedah and the Malaysia Cub Prix, and holds a Certificate of Achievement from the Malaysian Motorcycle Association for its "Zero Engine Oil Run".

Questions and answers

Is Motios oil safe for wet clutches?

EXN states the Motios series excels at clutch engagement. Check the JASO grade on the bottle against your bike's manual before use.

Which Motios grade do I need?

Follow the viscosity in your motorcycle's owner's manual, then ask an EXN stockist for the matching Motios bottle.
