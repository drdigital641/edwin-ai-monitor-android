Transmission and gear oil (Atfios, Garios)

EXN's gearbox range has two families: Atfios transmission fluids, which include a fully synthetic ATF for conventional automatic gearboxes and a fully synthetic CVTF for CVT gearboxes (both 4L), and Garios gear oils for manual gearboxes and differentials. ATFIOS CVTF

ATF and CVT fluid are not interchangeable. A CVT needs a fluid with the right friction for its belt and pulleys, while a conventional automatic uses ATF. Read our guide to ATF vs CVT fluid before changing either.

Questions and answers

Can I use ATF in a CVT gearbox?

No. A CVT needs CVT fluid such as Atfios CVTF. Using ATF can make the belt slip and damage the gearbox.

Is Atfios fully synthetic?

Yes. Both Atfios ATF and Atfios CVTF are listed as fully synthetic, in 4L bottles.
