EXN Lube products

EXN makes lubricants in eight groups: passenger car engine oil, motorcycle oil (Motios), heavy duty diesel engine oil, industrial lubricants, marine lubricants, grease, transmission and gear oil (Atfios and Garios) and the +Care car care series. Each group has its own page below.

Passenger car engine oil

Fully synthetic and Molytech oils for daily cars, hybrids, diesels and performance engines.

More details

Motorcycle oil (Motios)

Motios oils for high-output, high-temperature bike engines and wet clutches.

More details

Heavy duty diesel engine oil (HDDEO)

HDDEO for trucks, buses, commercial and farm diesel engines.

More details

Industrial lubricants

Hydraulic, turbine, compressor, bearing and gear oils for plants and factories.

More details

Marine lubricants

Marine diesel engine oils for fast and stationary diesel engines.

More details

Grease

Lithium and lithium complex greases for bearings, gears and sliding parts.

More details

Transmission and gear oil (Atfios, Garios)

Atfios ATF and CVT fluid, and Garios gear oil.

More details

+Care car care series

Brake fluid, power steering fluid, flushes, injector cleaners and treatments.
