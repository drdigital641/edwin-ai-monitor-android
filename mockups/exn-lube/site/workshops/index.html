EXN oils for Singapore workshops

Workshops in Singapore can stock EXN engine oils, gearbox fluids and +Care treatments through KohDen International Pte Ltd, EXN's authorised distributor. Every EXN label prints the viscosity and the API and ACEA ratings, so your team can match the owner's manual at the counter.

  • Grades for most cars on the road: 0W-16, 0W-20, 5W-30, 5W-40, 5W-50 and 10W-40.
  • Gearbox fluids: Atfios ATF and CVTF, both fully synthetic, 4L.
  • +Care add-ons: DOT 4 and DOT 5.1 brake fluid, power steering fluid, ATF and CVT treatments, injector cleaners, engine flush.
