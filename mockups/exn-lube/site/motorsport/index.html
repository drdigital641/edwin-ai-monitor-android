EXN Lube in motorsport and the community

EXN Lube has sponsored the Malaysia Speed Festival (MSF), a MAM and FIA-sanctioned racing championship, every year since 2017. EXN also backs drift, motorcycle, car show and badminton events and runs the EXN Lube Racing Team.

Malaysia Speed Festival partnership

YearEXN-backed series
2017Max Impact
2018MSF Fap Master
2019Official Partner, MSF Series
2020 to 2024MSF Superturismo, Cyberturismo, Supermoto, Touge, Merdeka Race and SuciMuci Motorsports

MSF, in EXN's words, "aims to promote grass roots as well as the general motorsport scene in Malaysia".
