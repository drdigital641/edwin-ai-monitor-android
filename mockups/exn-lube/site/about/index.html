About EXN Lube in Singapore

EXN Lube makes engine oils, gearbox fluids, grease and the +Care car care series under the slogan "The Lubricant Expert". In Singapore, EXN is distributed by KohDen International Pte Ltd in Kaki Bukit, an authorised distributor of DENSO, EXN, MOON and Hyper Power.

Where EXN comes from

EXN is a Malaysian lubricant brand, owned by EXN Lube Marketing Sdn. Bhd. and founded in 2015 in Petaling Jaya by Marc Ee. EXN says it now supplies more than 10 export markets. Singapore is one of them, served by KohDen International Pte Ltd.

What EXN stands for

  • Lubricants. "Our focus is on lubricants and we provide solutions for all issues and applications in this field."
  • Value. Technically advanced products that generate added value.
  • Technology. Molytech, EXN's nano friction technology.
