About EXN Lube

EXN Lube is a Malaysian lubricant brand owned by EXN Lube Marketing Sdn. Bhd. (registration 201201003247 (976772-K)). Founded in 2015 and based at Metropolitan Square, Damansara Perdana, Petaling Jaya, EXN supplies engine oils and lubricants to more than 700 workshops, spare parts shops and retailers in Malaysia and to over 10 export markets.

EXN timeline

  1. 2015 First headquarters opens in Petaling Jaya.
  2. 2016 to 2018 Expands to Sabah and Sarawak, first export to China, sponsors the Kepong FC badminton team. Certificate of Achievement from the Malaysian Motorcycle Association for the "Zero Engine Oil Run".
  3. 2019 to 2021 Launches the Motios motorcycle series and the MolyRacing series for premium cars, sponsors the Malaysia Speed Festival and rebrands with a new logo.
  4. 2022 to 2023 Debut at the TAPA 2023 expo and a fifth straight year with the Malaysia Speed Festival.
  5. 2024 to 2025 Exhibits at trade shows including Automechanika Dubai and Automechanika Shanghai; 50 EXN-wrapped cars drive across Klang Valley from December 2024.
