Molytech: less friction, less heat, less drag

Molytech is EXN's nano technology. Its particles act, in EXN's words, "like billions of ball bearings" between metal surfaces, turning sliding friction into rolling. Lower heat helps the oil last longer and less drag helps fuel mileage.
