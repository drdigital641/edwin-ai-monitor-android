Volvo T8 ERAD Motor Recond in Shah Alam: Symptoms and 1 Year Warranty

Rasa Sayang Automotive offers a recond electrical ERAD (electric rear axle drive) motor for Volvo T8 hybrids, with a 1 year warranty.

Service details, photos and booking are on our page for Volvo T8 ERAD motor recond in Shah Alam.

Which Volvo T8 models

The recond ERAD motor job covers these four models:

  • Volvo XC90 T8
  • Volvo XC60 T8
  • Volvo S90 T8
  • Volvo S60 T8

Symptoms to look out for

Our post lists three symptoms for this job:

  • Gear Shift Not Smooth
  • Axle Knocking Noise When Driving
  • Jerk When Driving With Electric Mode Shift To Engine Hybrid Mode

If your T8 shows any of them, send us the model, year and a short video on WhatsApp so we can advise the next step.

What recond means

Recond is short for reconditioned. This offer is a recond electrical ERAD motor for Volvo T8 hybrids.

1 year warranty

The recond ERAD motor comes with a 1 year warranty. Ask us on WhatsApp for the details when you enquire.

ERAD work we have done before

One of our posted hybrid jobs is an ERAD motor and rear anti-roll bar replacement on a Volvo XC90 T8. The photos show the car on our lift with the rear subframe down on stands, and the ERAD motors and anti-roll bars laid out on the floor.
