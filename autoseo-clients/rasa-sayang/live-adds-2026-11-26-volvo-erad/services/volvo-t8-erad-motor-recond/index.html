Volvo T8 ERAD motor recond in Shah Alam

Recond electrical ERAD motor for Volvo T8 hybrids, with a 1 year warranty.

1 year warranty

What we do

We recond the electrical ERAD (electric rear axle drive) motor on Volvo T8 plug-in hybrids. Recond is short for reconditioned.

1 year warranty

Our Volvo T8 recond electrical ERAD motor comes with a 1 year warranty. Please contact us for enquiry and appointment.

Volvo T8 models

This recond ERAD motor job covers four Volvo T8 models:

  • Volvo XC90 T8
  • Volvo XC60 T8
  • Volvo S90 T8
  • Volvo S60 T8

Signs to look out for

These are the symptoms we list for this job. If your Volvo T8 shows any of them, WhatsApp us the model and year.

  • Gear Shift Not Smooth
  • Axle Knocking Noise When Driving
  • Jerk When Driving With Electric Mode Shift To Engine Hybrid Mode

Frequently asked questions

Which Volvo models does the ERAD motor recond cover?

The Volvo XC90 T8, XC60 T8, S90 T8 and S60 T8.

Is there a warranty on the recond ERAD motor?

Yes. It comes with a 1 year warranty.

Which symptoms are listed for this job?

Gear shift not smooth, axle knocking noise when driving, and a jerk when driving with electric mode shifting to engine hybrid mode.
