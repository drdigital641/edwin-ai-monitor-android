Accident repair, panel beating and bodywork

From minor dents to major accident damage, we provide professional bodywork and repair services.

No matter the size or type of vehicle — sports cars, luxury cars, everyday cars, SUVs, MPVs or electric vehicles (EVs) — we are here to help.

From minor dents to major accident damage, we provide professional bodywork and repair services.

Our services include:

  • Panel beating and dent repair
  • Accident damage repair
  • Welding and cracked panel repair
  • Body panel alignment and restoration
  • Damaged body panel replacement
  • Bumper repair and restoration
  • Body kit installation and modification
  • Cash repairs and insurance claims

From colour chat to collection

Four steps, the same for a scuffed bumper or a full colour change.

  1. 1

    WhatsApp colour consult

    Send photos of the car and the colour or damage. We talk through the finish and what the job needs.

  2. 2

    Body prep and repair

    Dents and damaged panels are repaired, sanded and masked so only the right surfaces get paint.

  3. 3

    Spray booth and clear coat

    Colour goes on inside the spray booth, then a clear coat for depth and gloss.

  4. 4

    Collect your car

    Pick up your car at Jalan Indah 16/11 and look over the finish with us.

Repair first, replace only when needed

Whenever possible, we repair and restore your original parts instead of replacing them unnecessarily. Severely damaged parts can be replaced when required for safety and proper restoration.

At MCS, we focus on workmanship, quality and attention to detail, restoring your vehicle as close to its original condition as possible.

Common questions

Should I repair or replace a damaged panel?

It depends on how deep the damage goes. Send photos from a few angles on WhatsApp and we will tell you what can be repaired and what needs replacing.

Can you help with an insurance claim for the accident?

Yes. Insurance claims are one of our services. Tell us your insurer when you WhatsApp, and we will explain the next steps.

Will the repaired panel match the rest of my car?

Yes. Repaired panels are colour matched and finished with clear coat so they blend with the rest of the car.

Do you publish body repair prices?

No. Every accident is different. Photos with your car model and year let us tell you what the repair involves.
