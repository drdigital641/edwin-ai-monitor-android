Choosing a custom colour without guessing

A colour looks different on a phone screen, under the workshop lights and in the midday sun. That is why MCS keeps a colour showroom with walls of real spray-out samples: solid colours, candy, pearl, chameleon colour-flip, holographic, carbon-weave and wood-pattern panels. You can sit at the table, hold the samples up and compare them before deciding.

Candy and pearl finishes are built in layers, so the base colour, the effect layer and the clear coat all affect the final look. Two-tone schemes, such as a different roof or bonnet colour, are also possible. Our posted jobs include candy-cyan, candy-red and blue colour-flip finishes. Bring photos of colours you like, or send them on WhatsApp first.

Common questions

Can I see the colour before you spray my car?

Yes. Our colour showroom has walls of real spray-out samples. You can compare them in person before you choose.

Do you do two-tone paint?

Yes. Two-tone schemes, such as a contrasting roof or bonnet, are part of our custom colour work. Our posted jobs include a two-tone Honda Accord.

Is a candy or pearl colour harder to repair later?

Layered finishes take more care to match, because the base, the effect layer and the clear coat all change the look. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will explain what that means for your car.

How do I get a quote for a colour change?

WhatsApp 016-556 2998 with your car model and year, photos of the car and the colour you have in mind. We do not publish prices because every colour change is different.
