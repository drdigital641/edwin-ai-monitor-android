MCS Auto Spray Painting works from one multi-bay workshop at No.57, Jalan Indah 16/11, Taman Bukit Indah, Iskandar Puteri. Every job is done there, so the question is simply how far you are from Bukit Indah. The places below are all within about 20 km, sorted by straight-line distance from the workshop.

If you live in Taman Bukit Indah itself, you are in our home township: see the home page and our services.

Further out?

All of our work is done at the Bukit Indah workshop, so if you are outside these places, for example in Kulai, Pasir Gudang or Pontian, you are still welcome. WhatsApp us a photo first and we will tell you what the job involves before you make the trip.
