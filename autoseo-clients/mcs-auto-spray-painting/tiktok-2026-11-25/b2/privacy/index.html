Privacy

How MCS Auto Spray Painting handles the details you send when you contact us.

What we receive

When you WhatsApp, call or email us, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, number, car model and photos of the car.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to you, quote and carry out work on your car. We do not sell your details.

This website counts taps on its contact buttons (WhatsApp, call, email, map) to see which buttons are used. It does not record your name or number.
