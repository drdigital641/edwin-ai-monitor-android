Send photos from a few angles

After a knock, send us photos of the damage from a few angles. Our workshop then looks at what can be repaired and what needs replacing, and preps and resprays the car in the booth.

After a minor accident, the damage you can see is not always all of it. A bumper can crack behind the cover, and a door can bend at the edge. Photos from a few angles help judge what can be knocked out and repaired, and what is better replaced.

How a dented panel is handled

Our page sets out the order of work:

  1. Dent knocking reshapes the metal first.
  2. The panel is then filled, sanded and primed.
  3. It goes into the spray booth for colour and clear coat.

The same page lists four steps from colour chat to collection, the same for a scuffed bumper or a full colour change:

  1. WhatsApp colour consult: send photos of the car and the colour or damage. We talk through the finish and what the job needs.
  2. Body prep and repair: dents and damaged panels are repaired, sanded and masked so only the right surfaces get paint.
  3. Spray booth and clear coat: colour goes on inside the spray booth, then a clear coat for depth and gloss.
  4. Collect your car: pick up your car at Jalan Indah 16/11 and look over the finish with us.

Panel spray or full respray

Our guide says most paint jobs do not need the whole car resprayed. If the damage is limited to one or two panels and the rest of the paint is in good condition, it says a panel spray is the sensible choice. In that case the damaged panel is repaired, sanded, primed, painted in the car's colour and finished with clear coat. The new colour is then blended so it sits naturally next to the original paint on the neighbouring panels.

The guide also says a cracked plastic bumper can often be repaired and repainted instead of buying a new one, which also avoids waiting for parts. For the full comparison, read Full respray or panel spray? A guide for JB drivers.

Questions about this topic

Is a panel spray cheaper than a full respray?

Usually, because less of the car is painted, but the cost depends on the car, the colour and how much preparation is needed. We do not publish prices; WhatsApp photos for a quote.

Will a resprayed panel match the rest of the car?

The colour is matched and blended into the neighbouring panels so it sits naturally. Metallic, pearl and candy colours take more care than solid colours.

Do I need to tell JPJ after a respray?

Only if the colour changes. The owner updates the record at a JPJ branch using form JPJ K8, and JPJ has said there is no fee.
