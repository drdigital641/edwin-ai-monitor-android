What a respray at MCS involves

A good respray is mostly preparation. Old paint is sanded back, small dents and chips are filled, and everything that should stay unpainted (glass, rubber seals, trims, lights) is masked with paper and tape. Only then does the car go into the spray booth on Jalan Indah 16/11, where the base colour and a clear coat are applied in a closed, cleaner space than an open bay.

You can choose between a single panel, a few panels or a full-car respray. Panel work is colour matched to the rest of the car, so a new bumper or door does not stand out. For a full colour change, you can pick from the real spray-out samples in our colour showroom before the job starts.

Common questions

Do you publish spray painting prices?

No. The cost depends on how many panels, the condition of the old paint, any dents and the colour you choose. WhatsApp photos with your car model and year to 016-556 2998 and we will tell you what the job involves.

Can you match my car's original colour?

Yes. Panel work is colour matched to the rest of your car so the repaired part blends in. Send a photo in daylight to help us see the current shade.

Do I need to change the whole car's colour?

No. Many jobs are one or two panels, such as a scraped bumper or a faded roof. A full-car respray is only needed if you want a new colour or the paint is worn all over.
