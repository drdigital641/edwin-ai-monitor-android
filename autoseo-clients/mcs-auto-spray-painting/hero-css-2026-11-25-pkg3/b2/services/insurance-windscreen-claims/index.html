Insurance and windscreen claims, step by step

An insurance claim usually means paperwork, photos and back-and-forth with the insurer before the repair can start. As a workshop that handles insurance claims and windscreen claims, MCS can help you through that process and then carry out the body and paint work at the same place on Jalan Indah 16/11.

Start by sending us photos of the damage and telling us your insurer on WhatsApp. We will explain what is needed for your claim. Approval times are set by the insurer, so we do not promise a fixed time, but we keep you updated as the claim moves.

Common questions

Can MCS handle my insurance claim?

Yes. Insurance claims and windscreen claims are services we offer. WhatsApp us your insurer and photos of the damage to start.

How long does insurance approval take?

Approval times are set by your insurer, so we do not promise a fixed time. We keep you updated on WhatsApp as the claim moves.

Can I claim for a cracked windscreen?

Windscreen claims are one of our services. Check your policy covers it, then WhatsApp us a photo of the crack.

Is the repair done at the same workshop?

Yes. The body and paint work is done at our workshop on Jalan Indah 16/11, Taman Bukit Indah.
