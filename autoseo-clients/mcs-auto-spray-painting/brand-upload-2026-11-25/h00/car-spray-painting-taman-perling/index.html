Coming over from Taman Perling

Taman Perling sits between Bukit Indah and the city, around Perling Mall and the Sungai Danga. Drivers from Perling usually come over along Jalan Persisiran Perling or the Coastal Highway (Lebuhraya Iskandar).

Around Taman Perling: Perling Mall, Taman Perling shoplots, Sungai Danga. Main roads: Jalan Persisiran Perling, Jalan Skudai, Coastal Highway. The workshop is about 2.7 km away in a straight line.

Paint and body care for Taman Perling cars

Perling is an established township, so many cars here are older family cars with faded roofs and bonnets from years of street parking. A roof and bonnet respray, or a polish and coating, often brings them back without a full-car job.

Questions from Taman Perling drivers

How far is MCS from Taman Perling?
