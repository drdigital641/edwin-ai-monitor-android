How Nippon AI-PPS protection works

Nippon AI-PPS is a paint protection film that is sprayed on rather than wrapped. Because it goes on as a spray, there are no seams to lift and no glue line at the edges. It can be clear, to keep the original colour, or coloured, to change the look and protect it at the same time.

The film is there to take the stone chips and light scratches that daily driving brings, so the paint underneath stays intact. Light marks on the film can be polished out. Our Facebook demo shows the process on a Toyota Alphard. If you want to compare it with a conventional PPF wrap or a ceramic coating, ask us and we will explain the difference for your car.

Common questions

What is Nippon AI-PPS?

It is a spray-on paint protection film. It goes on as a spray, so there are no seams or glue lines, and it can be clear or coloured.

Can AI-PPS change my car's colour?

Yes. It comes in clear or coloured versions, so it can keep your current colour or give the car a new look while protecting the paint.

What does it protect against?

It helps resist stone chips and light scratches from daily driving, and light marks on the film can be polished out.
