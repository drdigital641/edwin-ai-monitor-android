Carbon-fibre repair, remake and upgrade

Carbon-fibre parts such as bonnets, spoilers, mirror covers and lips can crack, chip or lose their clear finish over time. Depending on the damage, a carbon part can be repaired and refinished, remade, or upgraded. We look at photos of the part first, because the right fix depends on where the crack is and how deep it goes.

Custom body kits are fitted and painted to suit the car, either matched to the body colour or in a contrast finish such as a carbon-look or two-tone scheme. Tell us the kit you are planning and your car model and year, and we will explain what fitting and painting involve.

Common questions

Can a cracked carbon-fibre part be repaired?

Often, yes, depending on where the crack is and how deep it goes. Send close-up photos so we can advise whether to repair, remake or upgrade it.

Can you paint a body kit to match my car?

Yes. Custom body kits are fitted and painted to match the body colour, or in a contrast finish if you prefer.

Can you refresh faded carbon?

Yes. Carbon parts that have lost their gloss can be refinished with a fresh clear coat.
