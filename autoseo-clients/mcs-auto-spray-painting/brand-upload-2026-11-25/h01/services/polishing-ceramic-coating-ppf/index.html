Polish first, then protect

Johor sun, rain and road grime leave paint looking dull and covered in fine swirl marks. Polishing removes a thin layer of that haze and brings back gloss. Once the paint is clean and corrected, it can be protected with a ceramic coating, a PPF paint protection film wrap or a simple wax, depending on how much protection you want.

These services are listed on the services board in our shop, alongside wrapping and deep cleaning, and are handled at the same workshop as our paint jobs. A fresh respray or panel repair is also a good time to add protection, while the paint is at its best.

Common questions

What is the difference between ceramic coating and PPF?

A ceramic coating is a thin hard layer that adds gloss and makes washing easier. PPF is a physical film that takes stone chips and scratches. Ask us which suits how you use your car.

Should I polish before coating?

Yes. Polishing removes swirl marks and haze first, so the coating seals in a clean, glossy finish rather than the marks.

Can I add protection after a respray?

Yes. A fresh respray or panel repair is a good time to add a coating or PPF while the paint is at its best.
