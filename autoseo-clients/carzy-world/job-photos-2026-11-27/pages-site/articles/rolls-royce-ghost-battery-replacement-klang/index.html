What we did

We replaced the car battery. Luxury cars carry a lot of electronics, so the battery has a big job to do every time the car starts and even while it is parked.

Signs your battery may need replacing

  • The engine cranks slowly or struggles to start
  • The battery warning light comes on
  • The battery keeps going flat after the car sits for a while

A flat battery is not always the battery's fault. The charging system, or something drawing power while the car is off, can drain a good battery too. That is why it is worth checking the cause and not just swapping the battery. Read more in Car battery keeps going flat? The causes besides the battery.
