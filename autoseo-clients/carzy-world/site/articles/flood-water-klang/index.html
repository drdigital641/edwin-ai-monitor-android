Flood season in Klang: why sedans should not wade through deep water

Heavy-rain flooding is a normal part of life in Klang. In a workshop video recorded in November 2026, Carzy World explains a simple rule for driving through flood water, and what to do if the water rises while you are still in the car.

Short answer

In the video, SUVs and pickups may cope with some depth, but sedans — especially Mercedes and BMW — should never wade through deep water. Their electronic boards sit low, and water damage can mean a five-figure repair. If the water rises while you are inside, switch off, open the door and get out.

SUVs and pickups, versus sedans

The video points at tyre depth to show how high flood water can sit. It says SUVs and pickups may get through some of that depth, because they sit higher. Sedans sit lower, so the same water reaches more of the car.

Why Mercedes and BMW are called out

The presenter opens the bonnet and points at an exposed electronic control board. In the video he says that on sedans, especially Mercedes and BMW, these boards sit low. If flood water reaches them, the repair can run to five figures. That five-figure figure is the claim made in the video; we are not quoting a price list from the workshop.

If the water rises while you are inside

The last part of the video is filmed in the driver's seat, with a rising-water graphic. The advice is short: switch off, open the door, and get out. Do not stay in the car hoping the water will go down.

FAQ

Questions about this topic

Should I drive a sedan through deep flood water?

The video says no, especially for Mercedes and BMW. Sedans sit lower, and their electronic boards sit low, so deep water can reach them.

Can an SUV or pickup go through flood water?

The video says SUVs and pickups may cope with some depth. It does not give a safe depth, so treat any flood water with care and do not assume a higher car is always safe.

What should I do if the water rises while I am in the car?

The video says: switch off, open the door, and get out.
