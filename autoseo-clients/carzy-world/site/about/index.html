A workshop that does things properly

Carzy World Sdn Bhd is an automotive service centre in Pusat Perniagaan Bestari, Taman Perindustrian Air Hitam, Klang.

We service and repair all car brands: everyday cars, pickups, continental cars, EVs and classics. Recent jobs include a Tesla Model 3, an Isuzu D-Max, a Mercedes-Benz C230 and a 1988 Mercedes 190E 2.5-16.
