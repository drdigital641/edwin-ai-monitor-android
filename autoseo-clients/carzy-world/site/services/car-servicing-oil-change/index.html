FAQ

Questions about Servicing & oil change

What's the difference between a normal service and a major service?

A normal service is mainly the oil and oil filter change with basic checks. A major service usually adds more items such as other filters, fluids and spark plugs, depending on your car's schedule. WhatsApp us your car model and mileage and we'll tell you which one is due.

Do you service pickups and 4x4s?

Yes. All car brands are welcome, and we recently did a major service on an Isuzu D-Max.
