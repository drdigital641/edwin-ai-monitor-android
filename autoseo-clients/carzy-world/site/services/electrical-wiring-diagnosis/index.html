FAQ

Questions about Electrical & wiring

Other workshops couldn't find the fault. Can you help?

Difficult wiring and electrical problems are what we're known for. WhatsApp us what you've already tried and what the car is doing, and we'll take a look.

My battery keeps going flat. Is it the battery?

Not always. A flat battery can also come from a charging problem or something drawing power when the car is off. A check finds the cause, so you don't replace parts for nothing.
