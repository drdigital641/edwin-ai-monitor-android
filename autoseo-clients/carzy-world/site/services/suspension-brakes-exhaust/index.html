Signs to get checked

  • Squealing or grinding when you brake
  • The car pulls to one side or the steering shakes
  • Knocking over bumps or a bouncy ride
  • A louder exhaust, rattles or exhaust smell in the cabin

FAQ

Questions about Suspension, brakes & exhaust

My brakes squeal. Is it urgent?

A squeal can mean worn brake pads, and grinding usually means they're worn right down. Get them checked soon, because worn brakes take longer to stop the car.

Why is my exhaust suddenly louder?

A louder exhaust usually means a leak or a damaged part in the exhaust system. It's worth checking, especially if you can smell exhaust inside the car.
