FAQ

Questions about Engine & gearbox

Can I keep driving with the engine warning light on?

If the light is flashing, or the car feels very different, stop driving and get it checked, because it can mean a serious fault. If it's steady and the car drives normally, book a check soon. Either way, WhatsApp us and we'll advise you.

Do you fix oil and coolant leaks?

Yes. We recently fixed coolant and oil leaks on a Mercedes-Benz C230 and checked the warning lights it was showing.
