How the send-in service works

Five steps from your bench to ours and back. You always know the fault and the cost before we repair anything.

  1. 01

    WhatsApp first

    Send the car model, the fault you see and the part number on the module label. A photo of the label helps.

  2. 02

    Send or drop the module

    Courier the module to us or drop it off in Johor Bahru.

  3. 03

    Diagnosis before quote

    We check the module on the bench and tell you the fault and the cost before any repair starts.

  4. 04

    Repair and bench test

    Once you agree, we repair at component level and test the module on the bench.

  5. 05

    Return

    The module goes back to your workshop, ready to refit in the car.

Why repair

Why workshops repair modules instead of replacing them

01

The fault is often one part

A module that has stopped working usually has a failed component, a cracked joint or a damaged track, not a board that is gone completely.

02

Your customer keeps the original unit

Repairing the module that came out of the car avoids sourcing a replacement unit that may then need setting up for the car.

03

You decide with the facts

Diagnosis before quote means you get the fault and the cost first, then decide with your customer whether to go ahead.

Questions workshops ask

Do you repair car ECUs for workshops in Johor Bahru?

Yes. EMATS is a send-in service for workshops. You WhatsApp the fault and part number, send or drop the module, and we check it before quoting.

What does component-level repair mean?

We repair the board inside the module: the failed chip, component, solder joint or track. We do not just swap the whole unit.

Will I know the cost before you repair?

Yes. We check the module on the bench and tell you the fault and the cost before any repair starts.

Which modules can you look at?

ECU/ECM boards, ABS modules, instrument clusters and other car electronic boards and control modules. We have also repaired marine (boat) electronic controls. Send the part number and we will tell you if we can check it.

Can car owners send a module too?

Our main customers are workshops, but car owners can also message us. Your workshop will usually need to remove and refit the module.
