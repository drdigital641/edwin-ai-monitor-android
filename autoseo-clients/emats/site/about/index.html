About

About EMATS

EMATS TECHNOLOGY SALES AND SERVICE is a Johor Bahru business that repairs car electronic control units at component level. Most of our work comes from car workshops that send in ECU/ECM boards, ABS modules, instrument clusters and other control modules.

WhatsApp us Call +60 10-820 2063

Engineering-trained, bench-based

Repairs are done by an engineering-trained technician. EMATS is run by Jackie Sky, who holds a Graduate Diploma in Engineering. The work happens at the bench: opening modules, testing them, and repairing the board itself.

How we work

Diagnosis before quote: we check the module and tell you the fault and the cost before any repair. Repair, not replace: we fix the failed part on the board rather than swapping the whole unit. Send-in service for workshops: you send or drop the module, and we return it after repair and bench test.

Beyond cars

The same bench skills have also been used on marine (boat) electronic controls.
