ABS Module Repair in Malaysia

ABS module repair in Malaysia for workshops, done on the bench in Johor Bahru. When the ABS warning light stays on and the sensors, wiring and pump have checked out, the fault is often inside the ABS control module. EMATS checks the module and repairs the board at component level, so the answer does not always have to be a new unit.

WhatsApp us Call +60 10-820 2063

Signs the ABS module itself is at fault

We see ABS modules from workshops after the usual checks. Typical reasons they come in:

  • ABS or traction warning light that stays on after sensor and wiring checks
  • No communication with the ABS module on the scan tool
  • Module-related fault codes that return after clearing
  • Intermittent faults that come and go with heat or vibration

How we repair an ABS control module

The electronic part of the ABS unit is checked on the bench. We look for failed components, cracked solder joints and damaged tracks on the board, then tell you the fault and the cost before any repair.

Once you agree, the board is repaired at component level and the module is tested on the bench before it goes back to your workshop.

Remove and refit stays with your workshop

Your workshop removes the module, sends it in and refits it after the repair. Ask us on WhatsApp first with the part number, and we will tell you exactly what to send.

Questions workshops ask

Do you repair the whole ABS unit?

We work on the electronic control module, the board that controls the ABS. Send the part number first and we will confirm what to send.

Can you check an ABS module before I buy a new one?

Yes. That is what diagnosis before quote is for: you learn the fault and the repair cost first, then decide.
