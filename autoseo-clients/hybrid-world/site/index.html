Hybrid car specialist in Johor Bahru

Hybrid World Specialist (HWS) is a hybrid car specialist in Johor Bahru, at Taman Bukit Indah in Iskandar Puteri. We work on Toyota, Lexus and Honda hybrids: hybrid battery replacement, hybrid battery reconditioning, hybrid system and inverter faults, and the everyday maintenance every car needs.

For hybrid battery replacement in JB we fit new original/OEM battery sets. They come only as complete assembled units, never as single cells. If your pack can be saved, we can recondition it on our battery rack instead.

We also do aircon service (including aircon system flushing), auto transmission repair, engine rebuilds and general car maintenance, so one workshop can look after the whole car.

Hybrids we work on

  • Toyota Prius / Prius C
  • Toyota C-HR
  • Toyota Camry Hybrid
  • Toyota Corolla Cross Hybrid
  • Toyota Estima / Alphard / Voxy Hybrid
  • Lexus CT200h / ES / NX
  • Honda hybrids, e.g. Shuttle Hybrid

Questions owners ask

Which hybrid cars do you work on?

Toyota and Lexus hybrids such as the Prius and Prius C, C-HR, Camry Hybrid, Corolla Cross Hybrid, Estima, Alphard and Voxy Hybrid, Lexus CT200h, ES and NX, and Honda hybrids such as the Shuttle Hybrid.

Do you replace single cells in a hybrid battery?

No. For battery replacement we fit new original/OEM hybrid battery sets, and they are sold only as complete assembled units. If your pack is still worth saving, ask us about reconditioning.

How much does a hybrid battery cost?

It depends on the model and on what the check finds. WhatsApp us your car model and year and we will tell you the options and the price before any work starts.

Where is the workshop and when are you open?

No 5, Jalan Indah 16/6, Taman Bukit Indah, 79100 Iskandar Puteri, Johor Bahru. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm, and closed on Sunday.
