When a hybrid battery needs replacing

A tired hybrid battery usually shows up as a hybrid system warning on the dashboard, the engine running more than it used to, or the car feeling weak when you pull away. These signs can have other causes too, so we check the car before we recommend a new battery.

If the check shows the pack can still be saved, we will tell you, and hybrid battery reconditioning may be the better choice.

New original/OEM battery sets, complete units only

We fit new original/OEM hybrid battery sets. They come as complete assembled units, ready to go into the car, never as loose single cells. That keeps the whole pack matched from day one.

You can see brand-new battery units and a part label close-up in our reels on the home page.
