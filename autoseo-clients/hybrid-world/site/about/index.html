About Hybrid World Specialist

Hybrid World Specialist (HWS) is a hybrid car specialist in Johor Bahru, run by HYBRID WORLD (M) SDN BHD. Our workshop is in Taman Bukit Indah, Iskandar Puteri.

What we do

We focus on Toyota, Lexus and Honda hybrids: hybrid battery replacement with new original/OEM battery sets, hybrid battery reconditioning, hybrid system and inverter faults, and general maintenance. We also do aircon service, auto transmission repair and engine rebuilds.

Our workshop

The workshop front carries the HYBRID WORLD (M) SDN BHD banner over the door. Inside are our lifts and a separate battery room with our battery conditioning rack.
