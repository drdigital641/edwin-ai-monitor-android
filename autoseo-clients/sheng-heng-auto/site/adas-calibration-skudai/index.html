ADAS Calibration in Skudai, Johor Bahru

Camera and radar calibration, done in Skudai

We calibrate ADAS cameras and radar when a warning comes on or after windscreen work. Our workshop is at 17, Jalan Hang Lekir 12, Taman Industri Jaya, Skudai.

We work on BMW, Toyota, Honda, Nissan and Porsche.

When you need it

A calibration may be needed when a safety warning lights up. It may also be needed after a windscreen is changed.

  • Pre-collision system warning
  • Camera warning after windscreen work
  • Radar or sensor warnings

Cases we have done

Our recent work covers many makes.

Questions drivers ask

What is ADAS calibration?

It sets the car's camera or radar so the driver-assist systems read the road correctly.

Do I need it after changing the windscreen?

Often, yes, if the car has a windscreen camera. We have worked on a Nissan Navara windscreen camera.

Which cars have you calibrated?

BMW, Toyota (including the Alphard), Honda CR-V, Nissan Navara, and Porsche Cayenne and Panamera.

Can I send a photo of the warning first?

Yes. WhatsApp us a photo of the dashboard warning and your car model.
