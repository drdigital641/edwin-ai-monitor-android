About Sheng Heng Auto Service Centre

Our workshop since 2010. Make The Quality.

Sheng Heng Auto Service Centre is a car workshop in Skudai, Johor. Our workshop has been running since 2010.

You can find us at 17, Jalan Hang Lekir 12, Taman Industri Jaya, 81300 Skudai.

The kind of work we do

We do servicing, diagnostics and electronic work, plus gearbox and engine jobs.

  • Car service and maintenance
  • Computer diagnostic
  • ECU repair, programming and coding
  • Smart key programming
  • ADAS calibration
  • Instrument cluster and coding
  • Gearbox and transfer case overhaul
  • Hybrid car maintenance

Makes

European makes: BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, Volkswagen, MINI and Peugeot. Asian makes: Toyota, Lexus, Honda, Nissan, Hyundai and Ford.
