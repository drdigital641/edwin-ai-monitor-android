Signs your discs need changing

  • Steering wheel or pedal shakes when braking
  • Deep grooves or a lip on the disc edge
  • Blue heat marks on the disc face
  • The disc is thinner than the minimum stamped on it

Brakes are a safety part. Always confirm the exact part for your car with us or your workshop before fitting.

Questions drivers and workshops ask

Why are Monaer discs silver?

The silver layer is a protective coating that helps prevent rust and corrosion, useful in Malaysian rain and humidity.

Do I need new discs every time I change pads?

Not always. Your workshop measures the disc. If it is above the minimum thickness and smooth, new pads alone are fine.
