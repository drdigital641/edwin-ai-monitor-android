More than 10 years on Continental cars

CCH Automotive Sdn Bhd is a car workshop in Likas Baharu on Jalan Tuaran, Kota Kinabalu, in the Jalan Tuaran Bypass area. We work on Continental cars: Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi and Volkswagen, along with the other makes Sabah drivers bring in.

The workshop has more than 10 years of experience. Bring us a warning light, a gearbox that feels wrong, an aircond that has stopped blowing cold or a car that is simply due for its service. We start with troubleshooting and computer diagnostics, then repair.
