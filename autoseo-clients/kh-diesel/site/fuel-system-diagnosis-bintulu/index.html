KH Diesel Fuel Pump checks diesel fuel systems to find out whether the problem is the pump, the injectors or the turbo. The workshop is at Batu 10 on Jalan Bintulu–Miri, Bintulu.

Updated: 23 Nov 2026

When to ask for a diagnosis

  • Smoke, power loss or hard starting and you do not know why
  • A part was already replaced and the problem is still there
  • The engine runs but uses more fuel than before
  • You want to know what needs fixing before you spend

Pump, injector and turbo faults often look alike from the driver's seat. Testing each part tells them apart.

One workshop for pump, injector and turbo

Because the pump test benches, the common-rail test room and turbo work are under one roof at Batu 10, each part can be checked in the same place and you get one answer.

How a job runs

  1. 01ReceivedWhatsApp a photo first, then bring the part, vehicle or machine to Batu 10.
  2. 02Stripped and inspectedThe pump, injector or turbo is opened and checked. You get the findings and a quote before any repair starts.
  3. 03Clean-room rebuildOnce you say go ahead, pumps and injectors are rebuilt in the dust-controlled clean room.
  4. 04Bench calibrated and testedIt is set and tested on the bench, and you see the result.
  5. 05Fitted and road-testedThe part goes back on and the vehicle is road-tested.
