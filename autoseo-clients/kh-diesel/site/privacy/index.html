Privacy policy

This page explains what we collect when you visit this website or contact KH DIESEL FUEL PUMP SDN BHD, why we collect it, and your choices. We follow the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia.

What we collect

  • When you contact us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email: your name and number or email address, and what you tell us about your car or your request (for example the model, the problem, photos you send).
  • When you use the website: when you tap a contact button (for example WhatsApp, call or email) we record which button, the page, the time, how you reached the site (the referring site or ad campaign), how many pages you viewed and for how long, device type, browser language, time zone and screen size. To count visits without knowing who you are, a random ID is kept in your browser's local storage. This record does not include your name, number, messages or IP address, and we use no cookies for it. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Our web host keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and to keep the site running.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you, give you a quote, book your visit and do the work you ask for.
  • To follow up on work we have done for you, if you want us to.
  • To see which pages and buttons help customers, so we can improve the website.
  • To keep the website safe and working.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not send you marketing messages unless you ask for them, and you can tell us to stop at any time.

Who else sees it

We only share what is needed with these services:

  • WhatsApp (Meta), when you message us on WhatsApp. WhatsApp handles your messages under its own privacy policy.
  • Google, which runs search, our Google Business Profile and reviews.
  • Our website hosting and web service providers, who store the website, its server logs and the anonymous tap records.
  • Google Maps, when you tap to open the map or directions.

We may also share data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your contact details and messages only for as long as we need them to answer you, serve your car and keep the records the law requires. After that we delete them. Tap records carry no name, number or message and are used only as statistics.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent and ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will reply as soon as we can.
