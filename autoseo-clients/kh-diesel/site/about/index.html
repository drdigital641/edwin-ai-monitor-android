KH DIESEL FUEL PUMP SDN BHD is a diesel workshop at Batu 10 on Jalan Bintulu–Miri, Bintulu. The work is diesel fuel pumps, common-rail pumps, injectors and turbos.

The Batu 10 workshop

The workshop is a cream two-storey building with a yard and a front canopy, near Kampung Penan Muslim Batu 10 and Jalan Nyabau. Inside there is a clean room with pump test benches on a green floor behind strip curtains, a separate common-rail test room, racks for turbos and spare parts, and a bay at the back for heavy engines.

The kind of work

Pumps, injectors and turbos are tested before they are repaired, and tested again after. Customers bring diesel pickups, light trucks, lorries, coaches and heavy plant.

Who runs it

The business is run by Hii Wei Soon. You can reach the workshop on WhatsApp at 019-854 5433 or on the landline 086-340356.

How a job runs

  1. 01ReceivedWhatsApp a photo first, then bring the part, vehicle or machine to Batu 10.
  2. 02Stripped and inspectedThe pump, injector or turbo is opened and checked. You get the findings and a quote before any repair starts.
  3. 03Clean-room rebuildOnce you say go ahead, pumps and injectors are rebuilt in the dust-controlled clean room.
  4. 04Bench calibrated and testedIt is set and tested on the bench, and you see the result.
  5. 05Fitted and road-testedThe part goes back on and the vehicle is road-tested.
