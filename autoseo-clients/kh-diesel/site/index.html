How a job runs

  1. 01ReceivedWhatsApp a photo first, then bring the part, vehicle or machine to Batu 10.
  2. 02Stripped and inspectedThe pump, injector or turbo is opened and checked. You get the findings and a quote before any repair starts.
  3. 03Clean-room rebuildOnce you say go ahead, pumps and injectors are rebuilt in the dust-controlled clean room.
  4. 04Bench calibrated and testedIt is set and tested on the bench, and you see the result.
  5. 05Fitted and road-testedThe part goes back on and the vehicle is road-tested.
