About Benz World, a car workshop in Permas Jaya

Benz World (M) Sdn Bhd is a car workshop at No. 66, Jalan Permas 9/13, Bandar Baru Permas Jaya, Masai, just beside Johor Bahru. The company was registered in 2020.

We focus on European and American luxury makes and other passenger cars. That includes Mercedes-Benz and BMW, British makes such as Jaguar, Land Rover and Bentley, and American makes such as Ford, Chevrolet and Jeep. Core work covers routine servicing and computer diagnosis, engine and transmission repair, chassis and suspension, aircon and electrical, and performance and special services, including vehicle pick-up and drop-off.

On site there is an air-conditioned customer lounge, free Wi-Fi and dedicated free parking. Look for the white BENZ WORLD letters on the teal roof, above the PUSAT SERVIS KERETA BENZWORLD banner. We are open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm, and closed on Sunday.

Team trained and certified: Kemahiran Malaysia, City & Guilds, IMI, Bosch, Beissbarth and more.
