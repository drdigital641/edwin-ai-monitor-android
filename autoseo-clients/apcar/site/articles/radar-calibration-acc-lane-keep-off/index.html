Radar calibration in Kuantan: ACC or lane keep switched off?

12 Nov 2026 · 2 min read
WhatsApp usCall

Looking for radar calibration in Kuantan? It usually starts with one thing: ACC or lane keep has switched off. Here is what that means and what we do.

Short answer

When the front radar or camera is out of line, ACC and lane keep switch off until it is aimed again. We read the code, aim it in our bay with Autel / MaxiSys equipment, then road test.

See the service page: ADAS calibration →

Signs it needs aiming

  • ACC or lane keep switched off
  • "Check System" on the dash
  • Pre-crash system malfunction
  • After bumper, radar or windscreen work
  • Lane keep pulls the wrong way

Why it switches off

ACC, lane keep (LKAS), lane departure (LDW) and pre-crash use a front radar and camera that must point exactly right.

When a code such as "misalignment millimeter wave radar" appears, the system switches off until it is aimed again.

Do I need calibration after bumper or windscreen work?

If ACC or lane keep stops working after bumper, radar or windscreen work, the radar or camera may need aiming. WhatsApp us the car model and what you see.

Yes. Every calibration we post ends with a road test.
