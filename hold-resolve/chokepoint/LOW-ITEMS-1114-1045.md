## 1. mkdir is limited to site roots plus private folders (cpanel.ts)
`api2('mkdir')` now succeeds only when the new folder is:
- inside a site root, or
- inside `~/bluehost-bridge-private` or `~/backups-private`, and nowhere else. This is a fixed list and is never widened at run time.

**Security review GUARD FOLDER NAMES 1114-1110 removed the four `*-private` entries** (alicia-, danny-, sofacare- and sofacare-production-publisher-private). The Bridge has no reason to create folders inside the Alicia, Danny or SofaCare publisher folders.

How the site roots are handled:
- The site roots are the cPanel document roots of `SITES`. `loadRoots()` in bridgeOps.ts registers them through `setSiteRoots()`, which is its only caller.
- Until the roots are loaded, no site folder can be made (fail closed).
- A root equal to `~/public_html` is never accepted, so a folder can never be made at public_html level.
- The site root itself can't be made.

What is refused (each one is a test):
- `%`, `..`, a backslash or NUL in the path, and a `/` in the name
- home itself, `~/.ssh`, `~/etc`, `~/mail`, `~/public_ftp`, and anything outside home
- `~/alicia-publisher-private`, `~/sofacare-production-publisher-private`, `~/alicia-private`, `~/danny-private`, `~/sofacare-private`, and any unknown `*-private` folder
- making a new `~/x-private` folder
- look-alike folders such as `~/backups-private2` and `~/bluehost-bridge-private-x`
