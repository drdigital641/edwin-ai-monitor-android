Understanding Insurance Claim vs Private Settlement

When an accident occurs, you generally have two choices:

  • Insurance Claim: Filing a claim through your or the other party’s insurance company.
  • Private Settlement: Directly agreeing on a compensation amount with the other driver, without involving insurance.

Both options have their place, but the right choice depends on the accident's severity, who’s at fault, repair costs, and your insurance excess. Let’s dive deeper.

Knowing which category your accident falls into is the first step in deciding whether an insurance claim or a private settlement makes more sense.

When Should You File an Insurance Claim?

Insurance claims are the go-to path for more severe accidents or when the repair costs are substantial. Here are some red flags that mean you should consider going the insurance route:

  • Significant damage to the car (e.g., bumper crushed, headlights shattered).
  • Injury to drivers, passengers, or pedestrians.
  • Multiple vehicles involved in the accident.
  • Unclear liability or fault.
  • Repair costs exceed your insurance excess.

For example, a Mitsubishi Outlander recently came into our Ang Mo Kio workshop after a collision near Hougang. The front bumper and radiator were heavily damaged, with repair costs exceeding $3,500. For such cases, filing an insurance claim is often the most practical approach. Our team handled the claim documentation, liaised with the insurer, and ensured everything was processed smoothly, minimizing hassle for the customer.

When Does a Private Settlement Make Sense?

Private settlements can save time and avoid potential insurance premium hikes. However, they’re only practical for minor accidents. Here’s when they might work:

  • Superficial damage, like scratches, dents, or minor paint scuffs.
  • Both parties agree on liability and compensation.
  • Repair costs are lower than your insurance excess.
  • No injuries or third-party claims are involved.

Factors That Impact Repair Costs

In Singapore, repair costs vary based on several factors:

  • Extent of Damage: Minor scratches are a much smaller job than severe damage involving part replacements; at Edwin Garage, every repair is quoted after inspection. For example, replacing a bumper or repairing a crushed radiator can significantly impact the bill. Consider potential damage like radiator coolant leaks, which complicate repair efforts.
  • Car Make and Model: Parts for luxury cars like Audis and BMWs are significantly pricier compared to Toyotas or Mazdas.
  • Parts Replacement vs Repair: Replacing bumpers, panels, or radiators often costs more than repairing the original parts. However, in some cases, repairs may not restore structural integrity.
  • Spray Painting: Full-panel repainting and precise colour matching add to the bill depending on the area involved, and are quoted after inspection.
  • Insurance Excess & NCD Impact: Typical insurance excess ranges from SGD 500 to SGD 1,500, depending on your policy. If repair costs are near your excess, paying out of pocket might be more economical. Additionally, a claim may reduce your No-Claim Discount (NCD) by 10-30%, increasing future premiums.

We also guide customers on negotiating private settlements. For minor bumper or light repairs, get an inspection quote first so the settlement figure matches the actual damage.

Common Misconceptions About Insurance vs Private Settlement

Myth #1: "Private settlements are always cheaper." Not necessarily. While private settlements avoid premium hikes, repair costs may exceed the settlement amount, leaving you to cover the shortfall out of pocket. Always consult a professional for an accurate repair quote before agreeing.

Myth #2: "You don’t need to report an accident if you settle privately." This is false; you are legally required to report all accidents to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day, even if you don’t file a claim. Failure to report can void your policy.

Myth #3: "Insurance always covers the full repair cost." This is not true in cases where your policy excess applies. For example, if repairs cost SGD 1,800 and your excess is SGD 1,500, your insurer only covers the difference of SGD 300. Always review your policy terms carefully.

Protecting Yourself Before and After Any Accident

You can't always avoid an accident, but you can control how well-prepared you are when one happens. A few habits go a long way for drivers around Ang Mo Kio, Hougang, Yio Chu Kang and Serangoon:

  • Install a dashcam. Front and rear footage is often the deciding factor in disputed liability cases, and insurers increasingly expect it.
  • Photograph everything at the scene. Wide shots of both vehicles, close-ups of the damage, the road markings, and any relevant signage.
  • Exchange particulars properly. NRIC, driving licence, vehicle registration number, and insurance details — for every party involved, not just the other driver.
  • Avoid admitting fault at the scene. Stick to the facts; let the insurers or a professional assessment determine liability.
  • Get a repair estimate before agreeing to any settlement figure. A quick assessment at our workshop can tell you within the hour whether a private settlement offer is fair.
  • Keep your workshop's contact handy. The sooner we see the car, the easier it is to document pre-existing damage versus accident damage — which protects you if there's ever a dispute.

These small habits cost nothing but can save you hundreds — sometimes thousands — of dollars and a lot of unnecessary back-and-forth.

FAQs

Should I report all accidents to my insurer?

Yes, even if you settle privately, you’re legally required to report all accidents to your insurer within 24 hours or by the next working day. Failing to do so can void your policy.

Can I still claim insurance if I initially agreed to a private settlement?

No, once you settle privately and sign a waiver of claims, you cannot file an insurance claim for the same accident.

Will my premiums increase if I file a claim?

Yes, your No-Claim Discount (NCD) may be affected, depending on who’s at fault and your policy terms. Speak to your insurer for details.

Can I decide to settle privately after getting a repair quote?

Definitely. At Edwin Garage, we can provide you with a detailed repair estimate to help you decide whether private settlement or insurance is more cost-effective.

What happens if the other driver refuses to settle privately?

If private settlement fails, you can proceed with an insurance claim or seek legal advice. Our team can assist you with insurance claim processing and documentation.
