When should you stop driving instead of coming in later?

Not every workshop visit is an emergency. A scheduled service, a minor cosmetic scrape or a weak aircon may usually be arranged at a convenient time. Drive carefully and seek an early assessment if a warning light appears, the car behaves differently, the aircon cycles repeatedly or an unfamiliar noise develops.

Stop in a safe place and seek assistance if the engine temperature is rising, a red warning message is displayed, braking performance changes markedly, there is smoke or a burning smell, or there is a serious fluid leak. Continuing to drive in these situations can turn a smaller repair into a larger one and may be unsafe.
