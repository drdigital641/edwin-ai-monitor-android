Why Does My Car Aircon Smell?

Bad smells commonly come from:

  • Bacteria growing on the evaporator coil
  • Mould and mildew inside the air ducts
  • Moisture trapped inside the air conditioning system
  • Dirty cabin air filter
  • Dust and organic debris inside the ventilation system

Ordinary air fresheners only mask the smell temporarily. The bacteria causing the odour remain inside the air conditioning system.
