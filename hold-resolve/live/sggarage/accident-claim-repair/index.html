Insurance claim or private settlement?

An insurance claim may be appropriate when the circumstances and coverage support it. For smaller damage, private settlement can sometimes be a simpler route. Every accident is different; we help you understand the repair process and documentation, without making assumptions about your coverage.

Accident repair FAQ

What should I do after an accident?

Prioritise safety, exchange necessary details, document the scene and vehicle damage, then book online and WhatsApp us for repair or claim-process enquiries.

Do you provide towing?

How much does car accident repair cost in Singapore?

Every accident repair at Edwin Garage is quoted after inspection. Dent size, panel material, parts, sensors, colour matching and insurance requirements all change the price, so we give you an itemised estimate before any work starts.

Can I use an insurance claim workshop in Singapore after a minor accident?

That depends on your insurer’s approved-workshop conditions, policy and claim circumstances. Photograph damage, exchange details and report within your insurer’s deadlines; do not assume every repair is claimable. Edwin Garage can help assess repairs and coordinate claim submission, but the insurer decides coverage.

What is panel beating and when is it needed?

Panel beating reshapes damaged metal body panels after a dent or collision. A technician checks whether reshaping, replacement or additional structural and safety-system work is appropriate, then prepares the surface for finishing and paint. We assess the damage before recommending the repair route.
