Signs your aircon needs attention

Not cold enough

Cooling that has become weak can point to low refrigerant, a leak or a component issue.

Unpleasant smells

Musty odours often suggest a filter, evaporator or moisture-related concern.

Strange noise

Clicking, rattling or whining when the aircon runs deserves a professional check.

Blowing warm air

Warm air may be caused by refrigerant loss, compressor trouble or electrical faults.

Humid interior

Excess cabin humidity or misting can indicate poor aircon performance.

Why a specialist approach matters

Car aircon systems are interdependent. Edwin Garage checks the system rather than guessing at a single part, helping you understand the repair recommendation. Where suitable, remanufactured compressors can offer a practical repair option.

How we diagnose car aircon at our Ang Mo Kio workshop

  1. System pressure check: gauges on the high and low sides show whether the system is low, overcharged, blocked or has a compressor fault.
  2. Leak detection: if pressure is low, we trace the leak with UV dye or an electronic leak detector. Common leak points are the compressor seal, hose joints, the condenser and the cooling coil behind the dashboard.
  3. Electrical and compressor check: clutch engagement, pressure switches and relays. An electrical fault can stop the compressor and look like a gas problem.
  4. Airflow check: blower motor, cabin filter and cooling coil, for weak airflow or musty smells.
  5. Clear recommendation: we explain what we found and in what order to fix it. We recharge with the correct refrigerant (R134a or R1234yf) only after the leak is repaired.

Car aircon FAQ

How often should car aircon be serviced?

Service needs vary by vehicle and performance. If cooling weakens, smells develop or noises appear, arrange an inspection.

How much is a gas top-up?

The cost depends on the refrigerant type and whether the system has a leak. A diagnostic check helps avoid topping up a leaking system without addressing the cause.

What is a cooling coil?

The cooling coil, also called the evaporator, absorbs heat from cabin air. A failed or leaking coil can reduce cooling performance.

Can I drive with a broken compressor?

The car may still move, but aircon performance and related components can be affected. Arrange a check before the issue worsens.

How much does a car aircon gas top-up cost in Singapore?

At Edwin Garage a gas top-up is from $20 after a leak check, final price after inspection. If cooling has faded because of a leak, a top-up alone will not fix the underlying fault. Ask for a diagnostic assessment and quote first.

Why is my car aircon not cold in Singapore traffic?

Common possibilities include low refrigerant from a leak, a weak condenser fan, a blocked condenser, compressor trouble or a cooling-coil fault. In Singapore’s heat and stop-go traffic, poor airflow can make cooling worse at idle. A pressure and electrical check can identify the cause before parts are changed.

What does car aircon servicing in Singapore include?

It depends on the symptoms and vehicle: a technician may check vent temperatures, refrigerant pressures, fan operation, filters, leaks and compressor engagement. Cleaning or a gas top-up is recommended only where inspection indicates it will help. Book an inspection online for a model-specific quote.
