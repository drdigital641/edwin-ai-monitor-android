How we work

Edwin Garage is an owner-operated car workshop in Ang Mo Kio Industrial Park 2. We keep the process simple: you book a slot, we find the cause of the problem, and you approve the quote before any work starts.

Diagnosis before parts

We test before replacing. A car that won't start may need a battery, or the alternator or starter may be the real fault.

Quoted before work

Prices are "from" prices that include parts, labour and GST. The final figure is agreed with you after inspection.

By appointment

Online booking lets us give each car the time it needs instead of rushing a queue of walk-ins.

What we repair

Servicing & mechanical repair

Engine oil servicing, major servicing, engine diagnosis, brakes, suspension, batteries, alternators, overheating and transmission checks.

Car aircon repair

Aircon not cold, gas leaks, cooling coils, compressors, condensers, blowers and smells.

Accident claims & spray paint

Accident repair with insurance claim support, panel beating and spray painting.

We work on common Japanese, Korean and Continental cars in Singapore, including Toyota, Honda, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Hyundai, Kia, BMW and Mercedes-Benz, and we also service Tesla. Our car repair guides explain common faults by symptom and by model.
