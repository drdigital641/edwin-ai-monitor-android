Autel MaxiSYS MS909 S2 vs MS908S3: which tablet suits your workshop?

By CSKA Auto Enterprise · Updated 3 November 2026

Choose the MS908S3 if your workshop needs a 10.1-inch Autel tablet with DoIP, CAN FD and everyday service functions for newer cars. Choose the MS909 S2 if you also program modules: it adds an 11-inch screen, 256 GB, Android 13, the MaxiFLASH VCI2 with J2534 pass-thru, Topology 3.0 and 45+ service functions, and it can be upgraded for ADAS calibration.

What do the two tablets list side by side?

These are the specifications CSKA posts for each tool, taken from Autel's own descriptions:

  • MaxiSys MS908S3 10.1-inch, Android 10, DoIP and CAN FD, Auto Scan 2.0, 40+ service functions.
  • MaxiSYS MS909 S2 11-inch, Android 13, 256 GB, MaxiFLASH VCI2 with J2534 pass-thru, Topology 3.0, 45+ service functions, ADAS upgrade available.

When is the MS908S3 enough?

The MS908S3 suits a general workshop that diagnoses and services newer cars. DoIP and CAN FD are the protocols newer cars increasingly need for a full scan, and 40+ service functions cover the resets and relearns a busy bay runs every week. If your jobs are diagnosis, live data, active tests and service resets, you may not need to pay for programming hardware you will not use.

When is the MS909 S2 worth it?

The MS909 S2 is the Autel tablet to look at when you program or replace modules. Its MaxiFLASH VCI2 provides J2534 pass-thru, the interface used to flash ECUs with the carmaker's own programming software, and Topology 3.0 maps the modules in a car so you can see where a fault sits in the network. It also has the larger screen and 256 GB of storage, and it can be upgraded with the ADAS package if you plan calibration later.

What should you check before you decide?

Decide on your jobs, not the model number:

  • List the jobs you turn away if they are programming or module replacement, lean to the MS909 S2.
  • Check the makes you service ask CSKA to confirm coverage for them on the model you are considering.
  • Ask about J2534 on the MS908S3 the CSKA listing names J2534 for the MS909 S2 only, so confirm before assuming it.
  • Ask about updates each maker sets its own update terms, so check what your model includes and when it ends.

Where can you compare them?

The Autel MS908S3 and MS909 S2 are both listed on the CSKA diagnostic scanners page, with the other makers' models beside them. For which one fits your workshop, WhatsApp CSKA with the cars you service and the jobs you want to do.

Questions

What is the difference between the Autel MS909 S2 and the MS908S3?

Per CSKA's listing, the MS909 S2 has an 11-inch screen, Android 13, 256 GB, a MaxiFLASH VCI2 with J2534 pass-thru, Topology 3.0 and 45+ service functions. The MS908S3 has a 10.1-inch screen, Android 10, DoIP and CAN FD, Auto Scan 2.0 and 40+ service functions.

Which Autel tablet can program ECUs?

The listing names J2534 pass-thru on the MaxiSYS MS909 and MS909 S2. Ask CSKA to confirm what you need for the makes you program.

Can the MS909 S2 do ADAS calibration?

It can be upgraded for ADAS calibration with the software and the target package. WhatsApp CSKA with your tablet model and the jobs you plan.

Do both tablets support DoIP and CAN FD?

The MS908S3 lists DoIP and CAN FD. For the MS909 S2, ask CSKA to confirm the protocols for the cars you service.
