Hybrid car aircon service: why POE oil matters and how to avoid mixing it with PAG

By CSKA Auto Enterprise · Last updated 2 November 2026

Many hybrid cars use an electric aircon compressor whose motor windings sit in the oil, so the oil must not conduct electricity. POE oil is insulating; PAG oil is not. Putting PAG into a hybrid system can reduce the compressor's insulation and cause a fault. Always use the oil the carmaker specifies, and keep PAG out of the equipment you use on hybrids.

Why does a hybrid aircon need a different oil?

In a conventional car the aircon compressor is turned by the engine belt. Many hybrids use an electric compressor instead, driven by a high-voltage motor, so the aircon can run with the engine off. The motor's windings are in contact with the compressor oil, which therefore has to be a good electrical insulator as well as a lubricant.

What is the difference between PAG and POE oil?

PAG is the common oil in belt-driven compressors. POE (polyolester) oil is the type specified for hybrid electric compressors because it insulates. Delphi Technologies states that as little as one percent of PAG oil in a hybrid system can affect its insulating properties, and that some carmakers require the parts to be replaced after cross-contamination. Never assume two oils are interchangeable: the carmaker's specification decides, including the viscosity.

What goes wrong if the wrong oil gets in?

Contaminated oil can lower the insulation between the compressor motor and the housing. The system can detect the leak and shut the compressor down, and the car may show an aircon fault. In the worst case a damaged compressor has to be replaced. High-voltage components should only be worked on by technicians trained for them, following the carmaker's procedure.

How does a workshop avoid mixing the oils?

Treat hybrid work as a separate process:

  • Check the label or manual find the specified oil and refrigerant for that exact car before you start.
  • Use a machine and oil injection that suit hybrids the oil you add must be POE, and nothing that has held PAG should touch a hybrid system.
  • Keep the tools separate use clearly labelled hoses, injectors and containers for PAG and for POE.
  • Do not top up from memory add the amount the procedure gives, and record what you used.

Questions

Can I use PAG oil in a hybrid car?

Not if the carmaker specifies POE for the electric compressor. PAG does not insulate, so it can affect the compressor motor. Follow the specification for that car.

How do I know which oil a hybrid needs?

Check the compressor or under-bonnet label and the carmaker's service information for that model. Do not rely on another car's specification.

Does every hybrid use POE oil?

No. It depends on whether the car has an electric compressor and on the carmaker, so always read the specification for the exact model.

Can one machine service both normal and hybrid cars?

Only if it lets you choose the oil and you keep PAG out of the hybrid circuit. The SPZ-FA1 lets you select PAG or POE oil.
