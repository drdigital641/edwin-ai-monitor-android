Car consignment in Singapore: we sell your car for you

Consignment suits owners who want a better price than a quick sale and can wait for the right buyer.

Consignment means Star Performance Motor sells your car for you. We photograph and list it, answer enquiries, show it to buyers and handle the paperwork. You agree the selling price with us and are paid when the sale completes. Terms are agreed with you before we list the car.

How does car consignment work?

  1. Agree the plan. We look at your car and agree a target price and the terms with you.
  2. Listing. We photograph your car and list it, including on our sgcarmart dealer page and social media.
  3. Viewings. We take the calls and arrange viewings and test drives with serious buyers.
  4. Sale and paperwork. When a buyer commits we handle the deposit, the loan settlement and the ownership transfer.
  5. You get paid. You receive the agreed amount when the sale completes.

Why owners consign

  • Time. No calls from strangers or weekend viewings for you.
  • Reach. Your car is shown to our buyers and our followers.
  • Track record. Our March 2022 consignment posts show cars sold in one to three viewings.

What should you ask before you consign your car?

Before you consign, ask who sets the price, what the terms are, how viewings work, how any car loan is settled and when you are paid. Terms are agreed with you before we list the car, so get each answer clear first.

  1. Price. Who agrees the selling price with you?
  2. Terms. What are the current terms? WhatsApp +65 9742 7491 and we will go through them before we list your car.
  3. Viewings. How are viewings and test drives arranged, and can you still use the car? Ask us when we agree the terms.
  4. Loan. If the car has a loan, how is it settled? When a buyer commits we handle the deposit, the loan settlement and the ownership transfer.
  5. Payment. When are you paid? You receive the agreed amount when the sale completes.
