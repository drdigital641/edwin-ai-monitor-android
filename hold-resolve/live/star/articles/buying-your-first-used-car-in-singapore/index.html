Buying your first used car in Singapore: a checklist

For a first used car, set a budget that includes depreciation, insurance, road tax and running costs, not only the price. Check how much COE is left, take a proper test drive, book a pre-purchase inspection at a workshop you trust, and make sure the ownership transfer is done through LTA before you drive off.

1. Set the full budget

Look at the depreciation figure on each listing (the yearly cost of owning the car), then add insurance, road tax, parking, fuel and servicing.

2. Check the COE

How many years are left on the COE decides how long you can drive the car and how much its depreciation is.

3. Car loan

MAS sets the maximum loan amount and tenure for car loans. Check the current limits on the MAS website and ask the dealer or your bank for the rate.

4. Test drive and inspection

Drive the car on roads you know and book a pre-purchase inspection before you pay in full.

5. Paperwork

The ownership transfer is done on LTA's OneMotoring. Make sure insurance starts from the day you take the car.

Questions to ask the dealer

Ask for the registration date, the COE expiry date, the number of previous owners and the service records. Ask whether the car has any outstanding loan and how the deposit works if the inspection finds a problem. A dealer who answers these clearly and in writing is easier to trust than one who rushes you.

Mistakes first-time buyers make

The most common one is looking only at the monthly instalment instead of the total cost of owning the car. Others are ignoring how much COE is left, skipping the inspection because the car looks clean, and paying in full before the inspection is done. Taking your time on each step avoids most of them.

On the day you collect the car

Confirm that the ownership transfer has been completed, that your insurance starts from that day, and that you have received the keys and documents. Take photos of the car at handover. Our guide on what to do after buying a used car lists the follow-up steps, and our pre-purchase inspection guide explains the check to book beforehand.

Take it one step at a time

Buying a first car feels like many decisions at once, but they come in a clear order: decide what you can afford each year, shortlist cars with enough COE left, test drive, inspect, then agree the price and paperwork. You do not have to decide everything on the first visit. Many first-time buyers visit more than once, bring a family member or friend, and ask questions by WhatsApp in between. Frequently asked questions

How much should a first-time buyer budget for a used car?

Start from the depreciation you can afford each year, then add insurance, road tax and running costs. A dealer can show you cars that fit that number.

Do I need a PPI for my first car?

It is strongly recommended. It tells you the real condition of the car before you commit.

Can a dealer help a first-time buyer?

Yes. Many of our Facebook reviews are from first-time owners who were guided step by step.
