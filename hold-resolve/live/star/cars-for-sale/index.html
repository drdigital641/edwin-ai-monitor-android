Before you buy

How buying from us works

  1. Choose. Browse our live stock on sgcarmart or tell us on WhatsApp what you need: model, budget, how long you plan to keep it.
  2. View and test drive. Come to the showroom in Yishun. We walk you round the car and you take it for a drive.
  3. Inspect. Book a pre-purchase inspection (PPI) at any workshop you trust. Customers often ask us to come along, and we do.
  4. Deposit and paperwork. Once you are happy, a deposit holds the car while we prepare the transfer and any trade-in.
  5. Handover. You collect the car and the keys. Questions after pickup go to the same person you dealt with.
