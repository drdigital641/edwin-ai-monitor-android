Buying an EV in Singapore through a dealer: new or used?

Buying an EV through a car dealer in Singapore means one place can source the car, take your petrol car in trade and handle the paperwork. You can choose a brand-new EV or a used one, and the right choice depends on your budget, where you will charge it and how long you plan to keep it.

Why buy an EV through a dealer?

A dealer sees many cars across many brands. At Star Performance Motor we supply brand-new EVs as well as used cars. We can source brands such as BYD, Zeekr, XPENG, AION, Tesla, Dong Feng and Denza, and we can handle your trade-in in the same deal. That saves you from selling your old car on your own while you wait for the new one.

New or used EV: how to decide

Neither is right for everyone. Here is a plain way to compare them.

  • A brand-new EV comes with a fresh warranty and no unknown history. You pay more upfront, and the car starts losing value from day one.
  • A used EV usually costs less to get into. Part of the early loss in value has already happened, but you need to ask more questions about the battery, the warranty and how the car was charged.
  • Availability differs. A new model may have a waiting list, while a used unit is ready to view and drive now.

If you plan to keep the car for many years, a new one can feel simpler. If you change cars often, a used one with low depreciation can make more sense. Our guide to what depreciation means on a car listing explains how to read that number.

Questions to ask before you buy any EV

  • Can I see a battery health report? Ask who produced it and when.
  • Does the manufacturer warranty transfer to me, and what does it cover, including the battery?
  • How many owners has the car had, and how was it usually charged?
  • Has the car been serviced on time, and where?
  • Can my own workshop inspect it before I pay in full? Our guide to the pre-purchase inspection applies to EVs too, and a workshop that knows the brand helps.
  • What is the exact variant on the log card, and does it match the listing?

Write the answers down. If the dealer promises something, ask for it in the sales agreement. We explain how in our article on what to put in a used car sales agreement.

Think about charging first

Before you fall for a model, think about where you will charge it. If you live in a landed home, home charging may be simple. If you live in an HDB flat or a condominium, find out what charging is available near your block, and how far you will drive each day. This matters as much as the car itself.

Incentives, taxes and COE rules for EVs change from time to time. We do not quote figures here because they may be out of date. Please check the current details on LTA OneMotoring before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Can a dealer in Singapore supply a brand-new EV?

Yes. Star Performance Motor supplies brand-new EVs as well as used cars, and can source brands such as BYD, Zeekr, XPENG, AION, Tesla, Dong Feng and Denza.

Can I trade in my petrol car when I buy an EV?
