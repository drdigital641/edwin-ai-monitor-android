Buying a used BMW 5 Series (G30) in Singapore: what to check

A used BMW 5 Series (G30) can be a sound buy in Singapore if you pick the right unit with the right history. Check the service records, ask what has been modified, and have a workshop you trust inspect the car before you pay in full.

The G30 is the BMW 5 Series generation you will see on many used car listings. It comes mainly as the 520i and the 530i. Both share the same body and cabin. The difference lies under the bonnet and in how the car feels on the road.

520i or 530i: what is the difference?

The 520i is the more common version and the easier-going one. It suits a driver who wants a comfortable, relaxed executive saloon. The 530i is the stronger-engined version. It suits someone who wants more pull and is happy to pay for it, both at purchase and when running the car.

We are not quoting engine specs here, because they differ by unit. Check the exact variant on the log card and in the listing. Inside, the two feel very alike, so the test drive is where the difference shows.

Is a used BMW really unreliable?

Many people stereotype BMWs as unreliable. We see it differently. A well-maintained BMW is a different story. The badge tells you little. The history tells you a lot.

Buying the right unit with the right history makes all the difference. A car with regular servicing, sensible owners and clear records is a very different purchase from one with gaps and no explanation. Treat a missing record as a question to answer, not a detail to skip.

What to check on a used 5 Series

  • Service history. Ask for invoices, not just a stamped book. Our guide on how to check a used car's mileage and service history shows what to look for.
  • A workshop inspection. Use a workshop that knows BMWs. Read our explainer on what a pre-purchase inspection is before you book one.
  • The test drive. Listen for noises, feel the gearbox, and check that every electronic feature works. Our test drive checklist covers the basics.
  • Paperwork. Confirm the remaining COE and any other rules that apply to the car on LTA OneMotoring. We do not quote figures because they change.
  • Tyres, brakes and the interior. These show how the car was used, whatever the odometer says.

Buying a modified 5 Series

Many G30 cars in Singapore have been modified. A tasteful set-up is not a problem in itself. The risk is not knowing what was done.

Ask what was changed, who did the work and when. Ask for the receipts and keep copies. Then have your workshop look at the parts that were touched. A modified car with a clear paper trail is often easier to trust than a standard one with no records.

Frequently asked questions

Is a used BMW 5 Series expensive to maintain?

Running costs depend on the unit and how it was looked after. Ask for the service invoices and have a BMW-savvy workshop inspect the car, so you know what to expect before you buy.

What is the difference between the 520i and the 530i?

They share the same body and cabin. The 530i is the stronger-engined version, while the 520i is the more common and easier-going one. Check the exact variant on the log card and drive both if you can.

Can I buy a modified 5 Series safely?

Yes, if you know what was changed. Ask who did the work, get the receipts and have your workshop inspect the modified parts.

Can I take the car to my own workshop before paying?

With us, yes. We drove one buyer's car to his preferred workshop the day after he paid a deposit, and he collected it 3 days later.
