Hydroponics Singapore guide: what it costs, what grows and how it works

Hydroponics grows plants soil-free, with their roots in a nutrient solution. In Singapore, an indoor grow cabinet with its own LED light needs no garden or sunny window: the Urban Eco package is from S$1,300 and costs about S$22–25 per cycle to run.

Updated: 18 Nov 2026

How hydroponics works indoors in Singapore.

The roots sit in water with a chemical hydroponic nutrient (fertiliser) solution instead of soil. No pesticides are used in growing.

  • Full-spectrum LED lights draw about 120 W and run about 14 hours a day, so the cabinet does not need a sunny window.
  • Seeds start in sponge cups, about 2 seeds per cup, on the 76-position seedling layer.
  • Seedlings move up to 4 planting layers of 18 plants each.
  • About 10 minutes a week goes on checking water and nutrient levels.

For background on soil-free farming, see the Singapore Food Agency's hydroponics myths explainer.

What hydroponics costs in Singapore.

The package is from S$1,300 and includes delivery, installation, training and one servicing. After that, each growing cycle costs about S$22 (4 weeks) or S$25 (5 weeks) to run.

We trialled 20 crops on this system. Pak choy and bayam took about 4 weeks; lettuces, choy sum and kale about 5 weeks; basil, shiso and parsley about 7 weeks.

Hydroponics in Singapore: FAQ.

Does hydroponics work in a Singapore flat?

Yes. The grow cabinet has its own full-spectrum LED light that runs about 14 hours a day, so it does not need a garden or a sunny window. It needs a footprint of 811 × 433 mm and a height of 1,805 mm.

How much does it cost to run hydroponics in Singapore?

About S$22 for a 4-week cycle and S$25 for a 5-week cycle, all in: electricity (S$15–18), nutrient (about S$4.40), sponges (about S$1.80), water (under S$0.50) and seeds (about S$0.30). The package itself starts from S$1,300.

What can I grow with hydroponics in Singapore, and how fast?

We trialled 20 crops on this system. Pak choy and bayam were ready in 4 weeks; lettuces, choy sum and kale in 5 weeks; herbs such as basil, shiso and parsley in 7 weeks.

Are pesticides used?

No pesticides are used in growing. The plants are grown soil-free with a chemical hydroponic nutrient (fertiliser) solution.

Will hydroponics save me money?

Honestly, not quickly. Produce alone is unlikely to pay back the system fast, and the value depends on the crop you grow and whether you would otherwise buy it. We don't promise savings or a payback period. People buy it for fresh pesticide-free greens, learning and engagement.
