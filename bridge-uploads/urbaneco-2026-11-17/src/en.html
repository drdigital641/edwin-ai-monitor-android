School garden and hydroponics grants in Singapore: every fund compared

Published 17 Nov 2026 · Checked 17 Nov 2026

Islandwide funds, North West District funds and what each can pay for, in one table. Schools and preschools apply themselves.

{{SVG}}WhatsApp Elaine

The short answer

A Singapore school or preschool can fund an indoor edible garden or hydroponics corner from several funds: SG Eco Fund Sprout (up to $30,000, at up to 80% of supportable costs), the SG Partnerships Fund Seed tier (up to $5,000, open year-round), the Environment Fund for Schools (up to $1,500) and, for North West District schools only, the GIRAFFE Fund (up to $10,000). In every case the school, preschool or community group applies itself; Urban Eco Solutions is a vendor, not a grant body.

Each fund covers different costs and has its own window, so the table below puts them side by side, grouped by where they apply. We checked every figure on the official page on 17 Nov 2026. Figures can change, so confirm on the linked page before you apply.

Which fund may suit which school

Preschools

A preschool in the North West District may suit Little Greenies @ North West: up to $800 per financial year as a top-up for a Green Learning corner. A preschool may also look at the SG Partnerships Fund Seed tier (up to $5,000, year-round) for a non-commercial green programme; check eligibility on the SGPO page.

Primary and secondary schools

The Environment Fund for Schools may suit a first trial as a top-up of $500 to $1,500. A larger grow corner may suit your school's White Area Grant allocation, if it is still available, or SG Eco Fund Sprout as a community project. Schools in the North West District may also look at the GIRAFFE Fund (up to $10,000).

Junior colleges and Millennia Institute

JCs and MI may suit the White Area Grant, if the allocation is still available, and the SG Eco Fund (Sprout, or the Climate Adaptation Package for a larger local-produce project).

No fund guarantees approval. Each fund decides which costs it supports.

SG Eco Fund Sprout in brief

Sprout gives up to $30,000, at up to 80% of supportable costs, for projects that involve the community. The current window closes on 31 Dec 2026. For the dates, eligibility and what a proposal needs, see SG Eco Fund Sprout closes 31 Dec 2026.
